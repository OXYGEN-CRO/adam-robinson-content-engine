# Composition with substance

Use this for implementation graphics, richer visual explorations and critiques of simplistic work. The goal is a recognisable graphic that teaches a real relationship. Extra decoration is not a proxy for sophistication.

## Art direction before construction

Write a compact direction: reader, takeaway, concrete visual object, composition, identity cues, evidence status. For an exploration, consider materially different silhouettes before choosing. For ordinary production, make that choice without requiring a style-selection round.

Useful compositions include:

| Relationship | Composition | Detail that earns its place |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A request becomes a useful result | Oversized input above a worked output, joined by a trace | Carry the same field or record through the operation |
| Implement a method | Main artifact with numbered annotations in a side rail | Show inputs, the rule, output fields and one exception |
| Compare a changed decision | Shared baseline with asymmetric before/after space | Mark the exact change and retain comparable units |
| Explain a system | Large main route with a quieter feedback/exception branch | State what moves, why it branches and where it ends |
| Explain evidence | Large receipt or faithful product crop with a focused callout | Preserve source, date and the caveat next to the claim |

A terminal border need not enclose every thought. Mix open typography, rules, one purposeful surface and tangible content. Vary scale and grouping instead of arranging every item in equal rectangles. Semantic icons can make operations faster to recognise; use one coherent family and preserve actual vendor marks when relevant.

## Make the identity work harder

Read the current guide for approved versus proposed choices. Reuse its type family, colour roles and characteristic geometry. Express identity through emphasis, frame treatment and motion behaviour as well as the logo. An illustration-specific motif may recur when it fits the information; it does not become a universal watermark.

For the current MoltSets kit, the productive ingredients are terminal typography, coral selection, mint results, square geometry and hard pixels. A travelling pixel can indicate a field moving through checks; a mint edge can identify a completed result. Oversized type and a restrained stack of output sheets can add scale and depth without neon glow, glass panels or a new palette. These are proposed composition extensions, not source-site rules.

Show concrete implementation details that help the reader act: real field names, example input, the matching rule, the output shape, missing-data handling. Label invented examples as examples. Do not fabricate executable commands, schema support, live product screens, customer identities or performance receipts. A proposed recipe can teach a method without claiming a shipped integration.

## Craft review

Inspect native and phone-size renders, then answer with observations:

- What attracts attention first, and is it the main idea?
- Can the reader explain the operation from the large labels alone?
- Does the visual show a transformation or merely name several steps?
- Is there enough concrete information to make the graphic useful after the animation stops?
- Does the focal point have room, and do secondary elements look intentionally subordinate?
- Would this still resemble the client's work if the logo were covered?

Fix the largest failure first. A passing bounds or font report establishes export integrity, not design quality. If the explanation needs tiny text, reduce its scope or split it; do not shrink the entire composition.
