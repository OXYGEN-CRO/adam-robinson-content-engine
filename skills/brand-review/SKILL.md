# Review the visual

Read `brand/BRAND.md`, the asset brief and only the supporting context needed to assess the work. If the brand guide is blank, review clarity and execution; do not invent brand-compliance failures.

Inspect the actual rendered artifact at the intended size and a small viewing size. With only a screenshot, distinguish visible findings from checks requiring editable source.

Assess:
- Meaning: the takeaway, reading order, truthful relationships and evidence.
- Identity: consistency with approved rules, where rules exist.
- Craft: text size, line breaks, alignment, spacing, contrast, clipping and connector collisions.
- Delivery: dimensions, loaded fonts, linked assets and editable/export files when supplied.

For implementation graphics, check whether the visual shows enough concrete input, rule and output detail to teach the method. Assess the focal point, variation in scale and the client's recognisable treatments separately from mechanical correctness. More decoration or more frames is not evidence of stronger branding.

For animated graphics, inspect opening, central action, completed hold and reset at native and phone sizes. Watch encoded playback when available; sampled stills cannot establish pacing. Check object continuity, competing motion, reading time, loop seam, complete static companion and reduced-motion HTML. Report GIF/MP4 dimensions, duration and actual size when delivery is in scope. Distinguish a working export from a successful creative concept.

Give the few highest-impact findings with a location, effect on the reader and concrete correction. Separate functional defects, conflicts with adopted rules and subjective preferences. Avoid numerical quality scores without a defined rubric.

Review is report-only unless fixes were requested. For fixes, stay within the asset's scope, preserve user edits, rerender and inspect the result. Reviewing a single asset does not authorize global brand changes, Notion edits or publication.

Lead with the assessment and state inspection limits.
