## Exact post text

MESSAGE TO FOUNDERS: You are not allowed to want to make money. 

In tech, only the VC’s are allowed to desire money, set up funds whose only purpose is to make money, and get incredibly rich at the expense of the power-law model that pushes 80% of founders to the point of failure (making no money at all).

All to then tell you, that in order to succeed, you need to be mission driven and not desire money.

Felix Dennis is a publishing legend who wrote a book called “How to Get Rich”, that anyone going into any kind of business should read. 

He insists - and in my experience, I agree - that becoming very rich is so incredibly difficult and requires so much focus over so much time that the people who get rich are obsessed with being rich, because there’s no other way to get there.

“People who get rich are the ones utterly determined to, whatever the cost”, he says. “Desire is insufficient, compulsion is mandatory.”

I’m betting that some time between when Tim Draper started his career and when he wrote this post, he had that compulsion too.

But if you are a tech Founder in 2026, you must listen to VCs and ignore all of that…

YOU’RE not allowed to want to make money.
