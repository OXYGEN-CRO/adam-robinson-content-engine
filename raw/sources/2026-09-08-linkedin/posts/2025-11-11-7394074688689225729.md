## Exact post text

4 things that make me feel like shit about myself when I see them on LinkedIn:

1. Fyxer AI adding $1m/week every week

REACTION:
- Did I not meet these guys at $1m ARR like 6mo ago?
- Am I not trying hard enough?
- Should I somehow build somehow build something in a stickier category with real NRRs?

2. HeyReach.io adding $1m every month

REACTION: 
- Why are they getting 3k signups/mo and I’m only getting 2k/mo?
- Should I be selling something that is broader than website visitors (only marketers, US-based, smb)
- How can I tap into the agency ecosystem for UGC like they are doing masterfully?

3. Deel CEO posting about the 10 $100m ARR businesses they have and $1b ARR.

REACTION: 
- Am I not aiming high enough?
- What if I started in a stickier space?
- Are those guys really that much better than me?

4. Clay raises another $100m at $3.2b.

REACTION: 
- I need to build towards some type of network effect.
- Varun’s BHAG is just so much more audacious than mine.
- Am I not thinking big enough?

Seven years ago I told myself that if I ever got to $2m/mo MRR that would be it. 

I would never want or need a larger or faster growing business, ever. 

$2m MRR ($24m ARR), lean and mean, and I would care less about what anybody else was up to. 

Guess what. 

I hit that goal about a year ago. 

And in some ways, it did alleviate some of my biggest anxieties and insecurities. 

But as I sit here today, doom scrolling Linkedin … 

I’m coming to a PAINFUL realization. 

No matter how far I go, how big we get… 

I’ll never conquer FOMO. 

How does the old saying go… 

Compare and despair?
