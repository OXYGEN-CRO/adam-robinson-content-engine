## Exact post text

RANT: The biggest problem I have with the VC path is Founders put themselves on a path to “change the world” (while telling me it’s cute that I have a lifestyle biz that makes money), set out to create inter-generational wealth, but don’t realize that the reason they want all of that in the first place is because they want freedom. 

Entrepreneurism can be one of the purest expressions of freedom, but the VC shackles for 9/10 Founders end up being the exact opposite of that. 

The best case scenario is you’re in prison for 10 years, sell, then have no idea what to do with yourself. 

If you go down the road I went down, eventually you get enough momentum and resources where you are truly free - you and your team answer to no one.

As the great Warren Buffett once said - the happiest people in the world are those who enjoy the lifestyle of running their businesses.

Note to other Founders:

This should not be a low-brow pursuit.

Remember what you really wanted when you started doing this in the first place…

FREEDOM
