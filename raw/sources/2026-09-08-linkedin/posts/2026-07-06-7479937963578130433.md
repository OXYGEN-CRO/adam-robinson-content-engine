## Exact post text

10 THINGS I HATE ABOUT MOLTSETS:

1. For some reason we can’t get the APIs to spit out the data in our B2B database

2. I’m risking my reputation w/ people I barely know, selling the idea of 100% valid records and their lists are coming back 11% and 40% valid

3. We’re trying to line up an email vendor waterfall for higher coverage and tiny accounts chew up more than their plan in external API charges in 5min

4. We have over 4,500 people on a waitlist who I told we’d have a product 3 weeks ago, and we still have nothing

5. People are somehow finding a path to sign up, buying the product, and getting shitty email coverage, and deciding MoltSets sucks without me being able to intervene

6. I lined up a bunch of UGC creators, one of whom just dropped a post (as I told him to 2 weeks ago) about a product that does not yet exist 

7/ I have no idea how to create a “normie” onboarding experience for an API set that requires using some other sort of UI 

8. We have to figure out GDPR compliance, and EMEA/APAC/LATAM datasets, or the product sucks

9. Some guy told me our industry tagging is the only reason he wouldn’t switch from Apollo today

10/ Clay told me to pound sand when I asked for an integration (TASTE IT ADAM!!!)

TAKEAWAY

Startups are HARD. 

I thought MoltSets would be easier, since: 

1. There is no UI, and
2. We already had the b2b contact database

I’ve missed my “soft launch” by three weeks, and STILL have no idea when the day will come where we actually drop a signup link on the site. 

Am I annoyed? Tremendously. 

But I’m also encouraged by all this. 

If this thing crushes, you can all go back to this post and see what it was like just before it worked. And if it doesn’t…

The haters will love telling me how stupid I am for trying to disrupt a wildly competitive and saturated space dominated by two entrenched incumbents. 

And I’ll be right here on LinkedIn ready to take my licks.

Stay tuned.
