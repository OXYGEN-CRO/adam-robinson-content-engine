## Exact post text

Here are my 4 core ideas for my new business, MoltSets.

How stupid is each one of them? Rate them 1-10 (10 most stupid)

IDEA 1: Build an app with no UI/UX, whole app in Claude.

- All existing competitors have robust, highly functional UI’s.

IDEA 2: Have the marketing website be a terminal. 

- Tiny amount of space, user has to type things into a command line.

IDEA 3: Only do four things. 

- Prioritize quality data at a great price. Only do email & phone enrichment, people & company search. Nothing else.

IDEA 4: Enter a hyper-competitive space with LESS than everyone else.

- Build features agents want and forget literally everything that makes the human experience good (90% less than competition)

TAKEAWAY 

I have no idea if this idea will work or not. 

What I do know is that I’m trying to do everything in the EXACT OPPOSITE way that it’s been done until now. 

Why? 

I love to say this about marketing (and all of business): 

“It’s better to be different and worse than the same and better” 

I’m going for pattern-interrupt, nails on chalkboard. 

So that leaves me wondering … 

How stupid am I? 

(genuinely looking for smart negative feedback)
