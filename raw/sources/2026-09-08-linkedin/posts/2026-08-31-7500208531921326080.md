20 APIs that turn Claude Code into a GTM engine:

Here's the entire stack from Michel Lieben 👇

1. Signal layer

PredictLeads - monitors hiring spikes, funding rounds & tech adoption across 110M+ companies.

Explorium - agent-ready B2B data. Feeds companies, contacts & buying signals straight into agentic workflows.

Claap - records & analyzes every sales call. Turns conversations into structured data, insights & follow-ups.

Knock2.ai - de-anonymizes website visitors & surfaces buying intent.

2. Data layer

MoltSets - Enrich your prospect lists with verified emails, mobile numbers, and LinkedIn data, right inside your AI stack from only $27/mo!

Prospeo - 98%+ verified emails. Person & company enrichment, domain search, 200M+ contacts.

FullEnrich - one API cascading through 20+ providers until it finds verified emails & phones. 80%+ find rates.

Openmart - 200M+ local business records. Filter by category & location, enrich with verified contacts.

Apollo.io - B2B contact & company data. Search, filter & enrich prospects at scale.

GetLeads - unlimited B2B data. 402M verified contacts with people search, email & phone endpoints.

LeadsFactory - real-time LinkedIn data. Finds decision-makers by job title from any company URL.

Limadata - real-time data layer covering 50+ GTM use cases.

3. Action layer

Instantly.ai- cold email at scale: campaigns, warmup, analytics & reply webhooks.

lemlist - multichannel sequences: email + LinkedIn + calls. Routes each lead to the right channel.

Expandi - LinkedIn outreach: connection requests, DMs & account targeting.

4. Automation layer

ColdIQ - unified GTM API. One key routing Claude Code (& other agents) to 40+ tools, autonomously picking the best provider per task.

5. System of record

Attio - AI-native CRM with full API access: people, companies, deals, custom objects + real-time webhooks.

6. Conversion layer

cobl - AI sales proposals. Drafts, sends & tracks them for you.

7. Revenue layer

Hyperline - billing, subscriptions, usage metering & invoicing. Payment webhooks + ARR, MRR & churn analytics.

Dreamdata - B2B revenue attribution. Tracks every touchpoint from first signal to closed deal.
