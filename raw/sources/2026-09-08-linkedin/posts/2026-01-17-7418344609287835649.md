## Exact post text

I’m the CEO of a $28m+ ARR SaaS. 
How did I do last year? Grade me - A to F. 
(real metrics below)

1. TOP LINE: $28.2m Run Rate (+11.2%) 

- The story here is Retention.com was flat and RB2B grew from 4 to 6.5m ARR.
- I take full responsibility for not investing in innovation on the Retention side (we have changed that this year)
- We need to add a LOT to RB2B in 2026 to not experience a similar plateau
- If I’m being honest I get massive FOMO when I see AI startups going $0-$100m ARR in 2 years or less

2. BOTTOM LINE: $1m/mo 6mo Average Profitability (+45%)

- Our goal in running this metric is to substantially grow this metric over time
- We chose looking at the 6mo average
- Our goal is to continue to grow without adding meaningful headcount, so profitability growth will continue to outpace revenue growth

3. CHURN: 5%/mo for Retention, 9%/mo for RB2B 

- We sell a high churn product to an even higher churn buyer persona
- I have spoken to many founders of similar businesses - nothing helped until they added completely different tangential products
- In 2025, we acknowledged the solution, but have not shipped it yet

4. HEADCOUNT: 33 FTE, flat

- We love running lean, and growing without adding headcount
- We are not in a hyper-growth situation at the moment … if we were, I would feel differently about managing to a flat headcount

5. INNOVATION: Hired Phil, put 5 FTE’s on something COMPLETELY new

- I believe my biggest mistake of 2023-24 was not doing this sooner
- I now realize that growth is about “S-Curves”
- If you hit PMF once, you better be working on your next PMF, or you will slide

6. CULTURE: WAY better than 2023, LOTS of room to improve

- Quarterly employee surveys indicate we are managing better and communicating clearer
- We prioritize delivering financial, intellectual, and interpersonal capital to our team
- Our goals seem attainable, we focus on doing fewer things, but better
- Voluntary churn is near zero

7. VISION: $28m >> $33m ARR in ‘26, Retention2.0 MVP in-market, RB2B multi-product

- This is not as exciting as sending humanoid robots to Mars (which is happening)
- It’s very exciting to me - we have a bunch of irons in the fire, and some might work!
- If we can nail 2026 and 2027, this business will be completely transformed

TAKEAWAY

All you armchair quarterbacks out there…

As a CEO, how did I do last year? 

Give me a letter grade, A through F, but most importantly … 

TELL ME WHY!!! 

I need feedback.
