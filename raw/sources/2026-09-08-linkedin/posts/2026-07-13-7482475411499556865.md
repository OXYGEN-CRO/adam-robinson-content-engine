I hate going to conferences. But if you're an early-stage founder, they're insanely powerful. Tomorrow we're showing you how to use Claude + MoltSets to automate your conference prep in 5 minutes:

Here's the problem. Before an event, an SDR or a marketer normally spends hours digging through LinkedIn. Who's actually attending? Which of those people are real buyers for us? It was brutal manual work.

Here's what the Claude workflow does:

1. It asks one question: am I prepping before the conference, or following up after?
2. It searches LinkedIn (using Apify) for people publicly posting that they're attending or speaking.
3. If it finds nothing, it falls back. It looks for ICP who attended last year, so you can reach out and ask: "hey, are you going again this year? I'm hosting a happy hour."
3. It matches everyone against your ICP, enriches the top fits with MoltSets, and pushes them into HubSpot.

Tomorrow at 3pm ET Oleg Lysiak (founder of Beezi AI) is going to walk us through how to set it up and give you the free Claude skills so you can plug them in and go.
