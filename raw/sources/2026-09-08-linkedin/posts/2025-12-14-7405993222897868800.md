## Exact post text

From 2016-2020, my email marketing startup was stuck at $3M ARR. Literally *nothing* I did moved the needle. I was completely demoralized. 

Here's the story of how we hit $20M ARR (and my message to founders who feel like quitting):

BACKSTORY

In 2016, I hit $3M ARR in the highly competitive Email Marketing SaaS space.

To do this, we used a creative data mining/cold calling strategy.

It seemed like leads would flow forever.

Until they didn't...

Things got so bad I was forced to scale back the team. 

It was the hardest day of my life.

I tried to remain optimistic.

But I had no idea what to do.

No move I made worked.

As I scrambled, two unfortunate patterns emerged:

1. I would meet someone who did X and they would convince me that X was my problem and the reason I was stuck. So, I would spend thousands of $ on FB ads or a new website or SEO or a partner program, which never worked.

2. I would meet someone I saw as an expert and they would tell me to sell the company

The problem was no matter who I met, I took their advice WAY too seriously. 

That's how desperate I was.

I had constant anxiety.

I wanted to quit.

My dream-life was collapsing before my eyes.

Then one day I learned something new...

I heard you could resolve anonymous website visitors to an email address. 

This was mind blowing.

Our current customers biggest problem was shrinking email lists. 

"Damn," I thought.

If I can turn anonymous web traffic into emails, I can sell it to anyone. 

It took us 18 months to figure out how to do it. 

I thought it would be a cool feature inside our app.

But then we noticed something...

People were signing up for our app, using the new anonymous ID feature, downloading the data, and using it in another email app and saying it was amazing. 

I read once that if someone is willing to endure a brutal user experience and still give you a high NPS score, that’s an early sign of product/market fit.

So we made a major pivot.

We spun out the anonymous ID feature as its own product.

I got the UI and front-end of v1 done on Upwork. 

Then I ran a $5,000 Facebook ad campaign to promote it.

And something amazing happened...

We got $10,000 back in MRR!

That was the moment Retention.com was born.

Since then, we’ve had plenty of bumps in the road.

But it’s been mainly up-and-to-the-right.

Why am I sharing this?

Because when I was stuck, I would have done ANYTHING to hear from a founder who was going through the same.

To read their words, what they were trying, what was working and not. 

I would have done ANYTHING to learn how to get unstuck.

That's why I create all of this content on Linkedin about my business.

That's why I share about the journey.

I’m doing it for the founders who are stuck

I'm doing it for a younger version of myself.

If you're out there and you're stuck... 

Know this:

The breakthrough is around the corner.

It may not feel like it.

You may want to quit.

But just keep building.
