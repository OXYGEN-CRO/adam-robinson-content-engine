## Exact post text

In the last 30 days, RB2B’s churn has gone from 9.1%/mo to a STILL HORRIBLE 8.8% (down from 15%/mo last September). I’m incredibly confused about what to do next. Here’s why:

1. RB2B is a high-churn product category with a high-churn persona and a high-churn GTM motion (freemium, no sales, no onboarding, etc)

2. The category is also plagued with issues:

- You can’t book a demo by spamming a site visitor
- You can’t book a demo from a site visit if you can’t outbound in the first place
- A low % of traffic is ICP, for most people it’s not a channel on its own

Product improvement is the only REAL way to reduce churn.

So we need to keep improving.

But, how?

———

Product improvement for RB2B is what I’m confused about.

There’s an obvious feature set that the product person in me wants to build:

It’s “RB2B handles the whole site visitor workflow”.

Meaning - someone hits the site, we:

1. Filter for ICP using AI
2. Provide additional qualified contacts at the account
3. Do AI research for personalization
4. AI writes the copy for the sequence
5. We send the LI connection and messages

And if we built that, it would be better to rebuild RB2B from scratch.

———

I think it’s easy to understand why I think that’s what RB2B “should” be.

Here’s why I’m confused:

If you properly filter for ICP, it will eliminate 90% of site visitors.

For MOST of our customers, that would cut lead count.

For the few who are larger, the workflow described above COULD work…

But high-traffic sites want a bunch of OTHER signals to get demos.

Then once we added the OTHER signals and a workflow builder, we would be in direct competition with (and trying to sell to the same GTM teams as) Common Room, Unify, Warmly…

…and the scariest of all, Clay.

No thank you.

———

So what are we going to do?

Rather than compete with the companies who are owning workflows, we are going to try to power them.

1. We’re creating an OEM product where you can put our pixel inside your own and have RB2B’s identity inside your own app

- One pixel for all your customers, one account/credit balance, you route contacts however

2. We’re going to create a set of “Identity APIs”

- There have been IP > company API’s for years, but there has never been an IP > person API. We’re going to build that and others

At the same time, we’re going to improve the experience of RB2B.

- We’re improving the daily email to be meaningful and interesting
- We’re going to provide additional contacts from the account
- We’re going to replace standard lead info w/ session descriptions and source

Things like that.

———

It’s been 565 days since we launched RB2B.

It’s been more of an emotional yo-yo than I could have imagined.

As horrible as it feels, as I stare at that almost double-digit churn, and realize the implications for future growth (or lack thereof...)

$6.6m ARR in less than 2 years w/ 3 people working on it?

I’LL TAKE IT.

PS - Want to play with our API’s? We’re looking for beta testers. DM me.
