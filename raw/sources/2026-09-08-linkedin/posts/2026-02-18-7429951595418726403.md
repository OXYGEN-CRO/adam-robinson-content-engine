## Exact post text

Worst ways to build wealth

- thinking a $100M valuation means you have $100M
- raising money. period
- adding features for 5 years instead of admitting your product sucks
- telling yourself "we just need more time" at year 7
- calling yourself a CEO but asking a board for permission 

Best ways to build wealth 

- bootstrap to $10M ARR and own every cent
- pay yourself more than your VC-backed competitors raised
- build something so lean that AI can run it for a week (or forever)
- have the freedom to move to Aspen and ski on Fridays
- never ask permission from anyone ever again

Study the founders who got called “not ambitious enough” and ended up more successful than everyone who said it.
