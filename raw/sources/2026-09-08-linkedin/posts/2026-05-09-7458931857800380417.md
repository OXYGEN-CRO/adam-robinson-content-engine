## Exact post text

Stop overcomplicating Linkedin. If I didn't have 150,000 followers, I'd spend 80% of my time commenting. In Q1, Michael Kurson booked 299 meetings + $2m in pipe from this strategy. Here's his social selling playbook (and how he tracks it):

Commenting is the biggest pillar of social first GTM. 

It's the best way to build 1:1 awareness before you make an ask. 

You're running outbound in public and it compounds.

Your prospects see your comments. Your connections see your comments. They become familiar with you. They start associating you with the rest of their industry. The engine warms up week by week, in front of everyone.

The next step is THEN to post about what your prospects care about.

His care about HubSpot, RevOps, pipeline, GTM. So that's what he writes about. Top of funnel for the broad audience. Middle for the practitioners trying to build pipeline through social. Bottom of funnel to drive inbound.

Then, of course, he treat his profile like a landing page.

When a prospect lands on his LinkedIn it's clear what he does and who it's for. No AI nonsense, just the specific problem they solve.

That's the basic methodology.

Then he uses his own product FirstTouch as the system of action to run it. They capture every like and comment across their team's posts, qualify against ICP using 50+ data sources, automate the connect and DM motion, and push everything back into HubSpot so meetings and pipeline get attributed to social.

On Tuesday, he's going to walk us through his full social selling playbook. Step-by-step walkthroughs of language, cadences, targeting and how they operationalize it and tie it back to revenue.
