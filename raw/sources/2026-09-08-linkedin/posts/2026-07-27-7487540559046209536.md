## Exact post text

AI comments are ruining LinkedIn. Take it from someone who has 150k+ followers. Two years ago, I replied to every single comment. It was the highlight of my day, and it was the #1 differentiator for my community building and audience growth. Today, I look at my comments for 2 seconds and it makes me want to vomit. Even worse. It crushes my soul. 

I used to be excited about comments because a comment meant someone had spent the time to take in my thoughts (and this is my art) and wanted to share a meaningful insight that sometimes even led to discussions between commenters. 

Today it’s, “The interesting part is … “ and “The X is the real story here ….” and “__________ is the real shift (or takeaway) here is …”. Not only do I hate reading the same comment OVER AND OVER again, for some reason I feel deeply insulted that I’m taking the time to read a comment written by a person who didn’t even read my post. 

Let me be clear here - If you are leaving AI comments on my posts, I don’t blame you, because someone dumb told you this is how you grow a linkedin following and you have no idea how to think for yourself or get anything to actually work. I’ve been there. What I can tell you is that I’m disgusted by the look of your headshot and your name, and even more disgusted when I write something stupid back to your AI comment and your AI (or even worse, you) says “NOPE, NOT AI, IT’S REAL HUMAN ME HERE”. I’m not that fucking stupid. 

So LinkedIn - coming from a creator that has probably done more to get Founders posting content on your platform than literally anyone, I am begging you:

PLEASE STOP THE AI COMMENTS

If you don’t know how to, DM me, I will give you some ideas. 

Oh yea - can’t wait for what the AI bots do with this one…
