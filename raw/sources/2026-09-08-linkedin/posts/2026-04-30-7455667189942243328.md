## Exact post text

I spoke at SaaStock last week in front of 250 founders and execs about “What to Build When Building is Free”. Here’s what I said:

1. I don’t love trying to build agents that do work and charge on outcomes like Intercom. 

People already ran their SaaS like complete financial idiots and burning tokens will make that worse. Intercom was special and built major internal data and expertise. Claude will copy anything that works. 

2. We’re entering a world of more competition than we could ever imagine. 

The AI PR cycle will bring in 10k times more founders, and a few Elon’s. The combination of new super-talented builders is why SaaS is getting smoked, not vibe coding (copying Hubspot) itself. 

3. If $10k/mo is life-changing money for you, NOW is your moment. 

The rise of the tiny, content-led, niche SaaS is upon us. Attention that leads to audience that leads to trust that leads to a business is going to be one of the only ways. 

4. Data plays - especially for AI Agents - are a viable path. 

I’m launching MoltSets: Contact Data for Claude, a 100% verified email and phone number enrichment database for AI Agents with no UI/UX. Super simple. 

5. Founder Brand is more important than ever. 

You may say I am talking my book but I think it’s hard to argue that AI-generated slop makes what people like me write (who do not use AI) much more trusted.

6. Network effects are the holy grail. 

Claude Code can’t create the value of Uber or Instagram. 

TAKEAWAY 

I closed the presentation by saying you should: 

- Learn how to create high-quality organic social 
- Experiment with running your businesses as lean as possible (like RB2B)
- Continue doing customer discovery, even though prototyping is quick

…and included tactical videos and how-to email courses for each.

You can get the videos and emails through the QR code at the end. 

Hope you enjoy ✌️
