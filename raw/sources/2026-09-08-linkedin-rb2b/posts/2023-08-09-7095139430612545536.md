## Exact post text

Here's the sales playbook that allowed Retention.com to grow to $14M ARR with only 6 employees:

1. We ran a cold email operation to tech lists from BuiltWith. 
2. We had people book demos in a link in the email, and on the website.
3. We only touched customers that were 15k ACV and above, all “inbound”.
4. Our product was so good that we got tons of word-of-mouth.
5. We didn’t hire more salespeople even when everybody said we should.

My advice: 

Stay way too lean for way too long. 

You’ll thank me later.
