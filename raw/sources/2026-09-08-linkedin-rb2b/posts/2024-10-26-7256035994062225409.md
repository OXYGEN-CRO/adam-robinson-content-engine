## Exact post text

I bootstrapped 3 startups over the last 10 years. Here's the 5 non-secret secrets to scaling revenue when you don't have big VC money:

1. Stop focusing on revenue

- Only companies who are financially free can go for total domination
- Always have a few experiments running that can be 1000x-ers
- You need to be working on your next “S” curve

2. Keep making your core product better

- You can always get faster, easier, quicker time-to-value
- Competitors are always nipping at your heels
- Your work is never done

3. Come up with even more ways to deliver perceived value

- Hit people over the head with the value you generate
- Make sure they are seeing at it the same way you are
- There are other ways to provide value than $$$
- Can you educate them? Entertain them? Make something beautiful?

4. Be the leanest possible version of your current stage

- More people doesn’t mean faster - it usually means slower
- Lean has SO many benefits, culturally, financially, otherwise
- I don’t know how many times it will take me to learn this lesson

5. Invest in RevOps early

- You need to understand your metrics as you scale
- Where you deploy capital will soon become the most important thing
- I got this one completely wrong

You don't need to raise a billion dollars to build a great company.

In fact, that often makes it much harder.

Ignore the pomp and circumstance.

Commit to the basics and then execute.

Every single day.
