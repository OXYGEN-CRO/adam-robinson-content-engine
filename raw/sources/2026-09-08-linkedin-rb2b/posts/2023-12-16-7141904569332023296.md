## Exact post text

In 2023, we spent $750,000 on 3 large trade shows trying to generate leads. Here's the harsh truth I learned about trade shows at that rate of spend (and how it's driving our entire event playbook for 2024):

The truth is... 

I wish we hadn’t spent $1 on trade shows!

The ROI was minimal and the time-suck was huge. 

Why? 

Because trade shows (at least for us) don't work. 

There are TOO MANY people and the environment is awful:

- Bad coffee
- Loud music
- Bright lights
- Crappy food
- Air conditioning
- People droning on onstage
- Overloaded with pushy vendors

It’s such a nightmare that some brands walk around with their badges turned around so they don’t get attacked by vendors.

What we learned from this is simple:

We do FAR better on small to medium-sized events (where WE control the list).

That's the GTM event playbook our team has been perfecting this year:

Our journey started with a few small dinners with other vendors where we both brought customers and prospects, and split the bill. 

It then progressed to an “NYC Pizza Tour” where we got prospects and customers together and took them around to all of the best pizza spots in NYC.

Then we decided that we wanted to try something BIGGER.

An event that fixed all of the problems we have with traditional trade shows. 

Trade shows wear you down.

You come home exhausted and hungover (physically and emotionally).

What we wanted to do is create an event that did the EXACT opposite. 

So last month the Retention.com team rented a mansion in the Hollywood Hills, packed the rooms with different wellness experiences (breath work, cold plunge, iv and b12 injections, yoga, sound healing) and a steady flow of fireside chats from known DTC operators.

It was an invite-only experience. 

Big enough to not feel cramped, small enough where you could speak to everybody.

And as a bonus, we hosted a sushi dinner and a crazy DJ party.

It was an incredible success (see video below).

Here are the results:

- 200+ founders & brands in attendance (35 from our Top Accounts List)
- $2.2B in combined annual revenue in attendance
- 64 Agency Partners & Sponsors
- 20+ Investors & Influencers
- 15.8% Engagement Rate (300%+ increase)

We’re betting BIG on these type of events in 2024.

This is exactly what our GTM event playbook will look like.

Not just for DTC, but for B2B too.

Why?

Because true connection & community is formed outside the convention center.
