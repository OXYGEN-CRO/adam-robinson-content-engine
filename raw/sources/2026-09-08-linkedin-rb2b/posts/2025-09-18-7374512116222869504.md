## Exact post text

Over the past 6 weeks, I’ve done 15min calls with 72 customers and over 20 of them asked me how the most successful people use RB2B. I had no answer, but now I do. Here’s the email copy I used to book these calls + the questions and tools I used to get incredible testimonials:

1. Do 12 x 15min customer calls every Wed afternoon. 

These are valuable for so many reasons. I now know why people hate and love our product, and what we need to build. Here’s the copy I use, sent to every +$249/mo user after 30 days: 

==================

Subj: Can we hop on a quick call?

Body: 

Adam Robinson here, founder and CEO of RB2B.

First off - thank you.

You’re one of our most valued customers, and I’d love the chance to personally learn from you.

I’m setting aside time over the next few weeks for short 15-minute conversations with a handful of our top users. I want to understand exactly how you're using RB2B, what’s working well, what’s not, and how we can improve the product to serve you even better.

If you're up for a quick call, you can grab a time that works best here:

[CALENDAR LINK HERE]

I genuinely appreciate your time.

Your feedback will directly influence where we take RB2B next.

Looking forward to connecting,

Adam

Founder & CEO

RB2B

================

2. Ask happy customers if they will do “workflow testimonials” 

I use Simple Testimonial for this. It makes it VERY easy for users with two clicks to answer a series of question on video. Mine are: 

- What is a 10sec summary of who you are, what your company is, and what you do?
- How are you actioning RB2B data, step-by-step?
- Can you share the message copy you using to reach your site visitors?
- Are you reaching out to other people at the visitors’ company, or just the visitor?
- What is the tangible result you’re seeing from RB2B?
- What would you say to a friend about why they should choose RB2B?

3. Edit each testimonial separately, and make one large mash-up. 

John Eley is helping me out here.

4. Send the long mashup to customers who ask how people are winning w/ RB2B.

5. Make posts and put adspend behind each of the short testimonials. 

Understory is running these to our founder, marketing, and sales personas. The customer wins because they get visibility from my account, we win because people are learning how to use RB2B. 

TAKEAWAY

Six weeks ago I had no idea how our best customers were winning. 

Now I have a 47-minute video compilation of them telling me exactly how they are crushing it (below is a shortened version).

I guess sometimes being clueless is the best starting point.
