## Exact post text

I actually say that Retention.com is spliced into two different eras.

Before October 24th, 2022, and AFTER October 24th, 2022.

We went from struggling to close deals, having too many options, having some tough clients, struggling to retain customers…

To having a machine that delivers insane results for high-quality clients and generates good revenue for us at the same time.

On October 4th, we switched to annual pricing. And it changed everything.

Let’s talk about why we did it, and why you should probably do it too.

When we had monthly pricing, it was really confusing.

It was based on how many unique US visitors a brand was bringing in.

That changes all the time, which means that customers would come in and downgrade when their traffic went down.

My partner and I both had a limiting belief that we weren’t going to be able to sell an annual plan.

It’s very expensive for the leads we were getting. We imagined that if people were churning from the monthly because it was too costly, the annual plan DEFINITELY wouldn’t make sense.

We began testing a product that was more specific. Instead of just capturing web visitors, we were capturing abandoned cart data.

But because this product was so stable, we knew that no one would cancel it. It was too good.

We changed our offer:

An annual deal with a 60-day trial period, using the more specific product as the flagship (because it has a 5x ROI), and then throw in everything else that we offer “for free”.

With the combination of moving upmarket in terms of our demographic (Shopify Plus only), and this new offer, this was one of the best things we’ve ever done.

The first day we tried this new offer, my partner closed 3 out of 3 deals that were on the calendar that day. October 4th.

We knew we were going to move toward a higher-revenue, lower-churn machine that was going to completely take over the Shopify Plus ecosphere.

Here are some key takeaways:

1. The velocity is insane.

We’ve had AEs with no prior sales experience close 5 for 5 in their first 3 weeks.

If you know anything about enterprise software sales, this is crazy.

It’s just such an easy offer to sell.

2. We closed 81 deals in December.

That’s $3M ARR.

3. We closed 92 deals between January 1-20th.

That’s around $3.3M ARR. Average contract value of $30,000.

This has been a huge success.

The lesson here is this:

As a founder, you probably have a ton of limiting beliefs around what people will be willing to buy/spend money on.

Diana and I certainly felt the same way.

If people don’t like our monthly plan, how could they possibly opt for our annual plan?

It goes to show that 1+1 does not always equal 2.

Hope this story helps.
