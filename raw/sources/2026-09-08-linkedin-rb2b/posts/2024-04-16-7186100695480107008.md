## Exact post text

45 days ago, we launched our new startup RB2B. We have driven 4,757 signups with an ~80% install rate, but only have 13 paying customers! Here are the 3 most embarrassing pricing mistakes I've made (and how to fix it):

To set the stage: 

- We launched our Person-Level Website Visitor Identity solution on March 1
- It’s a free/unlimited push to Slack… I wanted to charge for other integrations
- What I didn’t realize is you can parse the Slack block and push for free 
- Feedback is great, signups are great, installs are great
- We’re just not getting paid!

What are the three biggest mistakes I’ve made with pricing?

1. Our pricing is broken for smaller B2B companies

- Smaller, low-traffic B2B companies aren’t paying $495 for this no matter what
- Even if they would, the jump from zero just feels like too much

2. Our pricing is broken for larger B2B companies

- At 100+ leads a day there is too much noise, we need a filter (don’t have yet)
- Larger, higher-traffic B2B also want a Hubspot/SF integration (don’t have yet)

3. There’s too much value in the free plan, not enough in paid

- We’ve observed willingness to pay drop when people see what they’re getting
- People can also just take the data out of Slack and do what they want with it
- I suspect that even when we have filters/integration people still won’t pay

How do we plan to fix the problem? 

1. Gate the free plan for the top 20% of signups

- I didn’t want to do this, but I think we have to because of the Slack parse
- After X leads/mo, we’ll serve a headshot (will be totally free for 80% of users)
- We’ll still do a 30 day full-featured unlimited trial for everyone
- We’ll have a much lower-priced entry point than $495

2. We need to build three major features THIS QUARTER

- We’re starting with Hubspot integration and ICP filtering first, SF next. 

3. We're going to try adding one hyper-productive AE for a 2-touch sales cycle

- We want to segment our high value customers during onboarding and get them on the phone to make sure they are using the tool correctly
- We’ll then reach out at the end of their trial to see if they booked any demos

4. We’re focusing on our B2B Agency motion

- We’re getting lots of traction with B2B demand and lead gen agencies
- The HubSpot agency community is excited about what we’re doing too 
- We’re extending preferential pricing to agencies that is a win/win for both side 
- DM me if you want the details!

TAKEAWAY:

I was so confident at launch that we could PLG our way to the $495 plan. 

That has happened ZERO times in 45 days and almost 5,000 signups. 

I’m realizing that:

a) Our product isn’t good enough 
b) My LinkedIn community was right about pricing (THANK YOU!)

Too much value in the free plan, not enough in paid. 

The longer I do this, the more times I re-learn the universal truth of startups.

Things never look like you think they will when you start.

But that’s okay, if that’s what you expect. 

You just have to keep building.
