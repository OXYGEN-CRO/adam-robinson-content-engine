## Exact post text

99% of companies are TERRIBLE at transparency. I want to change that.

Here are 14 things we changed this year to make Retention.com the most transparent and trustworthy company in our industry:

1) Full financial updates quarterly, and MRR updates monthly, along with a full monthly recap.

2) Creating my Founder Brand content:

- Producing a a docu-series called "Billion Dollar Challenge"
- Hosting a brand new podcast series (coming soon)
- Posting 2x daily on Linkedin
- Posting 4x daily on Twitter
- Engaging with followers

3) Sponsoring every major trade show that caters to the Shopify Plus audience. I'm speaking at several of them.

4) Creating high-quality video testimonials of customers

5) Establishing agency partnerships. We hired Shopify ecosystem operators to represent our brand in the agency community.

6) Turbo-charging our company LinkedIn page. We are publishing employee profiles, customer success stories, and "what it's like to work here" content daily. 

7) Sharing our revenue growth projections with literally everybody I talk to, and explaining what exactly we're doing to get there.

8) Sponsoring the industry's best newsletters

9) Sponsoring the industry's best podcasts

10) Writing a book. It's about how I see business and entrepreneurship as a creative act that is spiritual in nature. This will be a great asset for recruiting, to hand out at shows, etc.

11) Building personal relationships with major influencers in the ecosystem so they clearly understand our value and how we can help the brands they speak to.

12) Speaking to Forrester about ways to collaborate around possible category creation and GTM strategy.

13) Creating 100% free high-value content like 'The Five Fundamental Flows' (link in the comments)

14) Systematizing case studies by implementing a request and production into our customer success motion.

Will it be enough?

I don't know.

But we are living our values.
