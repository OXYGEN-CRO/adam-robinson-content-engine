## Exact post text

Daniel Murray has built The Marketing Millennials audience of over 600,000 people.

And he let me in on a secret to making LinkedIn content go more viral:

Dumb it down.

Let’s take email marketing for example.

Explaining something about email marketing to an audience of agency owners will look a lot different than explaining that same thing to an audience of people like your mother.

See, you have a bias that makes you think you need to sound smarter than everyone else.

You believe that the best way to get ahead is to be so much wiser and more profound that everyone will trust you more. That may be true for some audiences.

But you need to understand that the world we exist in is only a tiny fraction of the population. 

Less than 0.1%.

If you want to make content for as many people as possible (and there’s nothing wrong with that), you absolutely *cannot* go ultra-meta and talk about niche things that only a handful of people understand.

You need to take more cues from someone like MrBeast.

I talk about MrBeast in this newsletter all the time, because I think he is one of the best examples of what “greatness” looks like.

MrBeast makes content for everyone.

He gets billions of views because his videos are about something everyone understands and loves: handing out money to strangers.

There’s not a whole lot of talking. It’s very visual. It’s like a kids’ show for adults.

Couple this with the fact that he has dozens of other channels for different languages, and you suddenly have an asset that most of the world can enjoy.

How does this apply to you?

Dumb. It. Down.

Make content for more and more people.

That’s how you grow your audience, and become a thought leader for people who are outside of your bubble.
