## Exact post text

In 2023, I had to spend $100,000 on speaking engagements to get in front of my target market. In 2024, I’ll spend $0 to speak in front of 10x as many people. Here's what changed:

I achieved what I call “influencer/market fit”. 

BACKSTORY:

In September of 2022, with $12m ARR and 6 FTE, we decided to hone in on our ICP of large Shopify stores.

At the time, we believed we had single digit penetration into the Shopify Plus ecosystem, and a product that crushed for all of them. 

I viewed this as an awareness problem. 

A friend recommended I read "Founder Brand" by Dave Gerhardt.

I loved what he said, but since I’d never posted on social, I was scared to start with ZERO idea of how the game was played. 

I was stuck. 

Shortly thereafter I met Tommy Clark.

He thought I should start posting and that LinkedIn was the place to start.

Most importantly, he said he could write for me.

So began my journey as a CEO making content.

This was October 2022.

We got some pretty early traction. 

I wanted to use video and ended up putting out one per day for 8 months.

People were starting to recognize me at trade shows…

Saying that they watched all of my videos every day.

This was very cool, but there was a fundamental problem:

I didn’t have much to say to ecommerce operators!

I wasn’t one of them.

I didn’t know what to talk about other than my own business, and when aimed at ecom founders, it resulted in “meh” engagement.

—

In mid-August, we started beta testing a B2B SaaS tool that identified website visitors at the PERSON-level, which was novel, as vendors like 6Sense, Clearbit, and Demandbase weren’t doing it.

Around Labor Day, with encouragement from Alec Paul, I decided to start writing entirely to B2B SaaS revenue leaders instead of ecomm founders. 

But there was a catch...

I had to write myself.

What happened next? 

I dropped 4x posts in a row that had 50x the engagement from the prior six months.

The rest is history...

Why was it such a dramatic change?

Because I was speaking to an audience I was a part of:

- My perspective became more relevant and valuable
- The pain I was articulating was IDENTICAL to the pain they were experiencing
- As a $22m bootstrapped SaaS founder, I had a level of success other SaaS founders hoped to achieve

Last but not least, SaaS founders and revenue leaders LIVE on LinkedIn, unlike the ecomm ecosystem (who live on X/Twitter)

All of the above is why I believe that “influencer/market fit” is a massive moat. 

My content works because of who I am and where I’m coming from.

I know because it DIDN’T work nearly as well when I was writing to ecomm.

This is why I’m now getting invited to speak at conferences that I would have had to pay BIG $$$ for before.

I'll be keynoting the next @SaaSOpen in NYC Sept 5th. 

See you there: https://lnkd.in/gTA45pXh
