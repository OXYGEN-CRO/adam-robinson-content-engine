## Exact post text

In the last 12 months, I screwed up a lot of things. In January of 2022, I told the Retention.com team (and posted *frequently* on LinkedIn) we would hit $50M ARR by the end of the year. 

Why?

Because at the end of last year we had this insane euphoria.

We started aggressively scaling the team. 

I told myself ours was a super fast sale. 

We didn't need experienced AEs, I said.

It was easy to onboard new customers. 

We had an incredible product that JUST WORKED. 

In hindsight...

This seems incredibly stupid. 

Our $50m ARR goal, while at the time seemed achievable, led to a series of poor decisions. 

Here's the TAKEAWAY for founders:

Ground your optimism.

If anything, try to be a little conservative.

And be ready to admit your mistakes. 

As a founder, it's easy to get caught up.

You have to BELIEVE when no one else does.

We're in the business of making big bets.

Some will work out, others won't.

The key is to never make the same mistake twice.

We get into all of this on today's episode of BILLION DOLLAR CHALLENGE. Watch it below 👇.

Of course, it's not all doom and gloom.

We still managed to grow 75% this year!

Going from $12M to $20M ARR.

But I know we can do better.

Hope you'll stay tuned-in for the ride.

I'll keep sharing what we're learning.
