## Exact post text

You cannot in good conscience rely solely on social media platforms to build a successful brand.

Don’t believe me?

Just earlier this year:

→ Instagram suspended thousands of accounts.
→ TikTok is facing consistent rumors of being banned.
→ Twitter is making sweeping changes post-Elon takeover.

So, ask yourself:

If any one of these platforms were to nosedive… 

Would your business be able to survive?

Gets you thinking, right?

And before you start yelling in the comments, let me be clear. 

I’m not painting this as a ‘social vs email’ argument.

You should be focusing on building + optimizing your owned audiences — Email and SMS — in conjunction with strong social media marketing.

Both are necessary. And ideally both channels work together the fuel the marketing engine behind your brand (while making your brand algorithm-proof).

See how it works?
