## Exact post text

After 28,750 RB2B signups said it was unusable because they couldn’t identify and route leads, today we have FINALLY solved our two biggest problems leading to churn. 

Here’s our new 5-step guide to setting up your Hubspot integration to filter and route leads to your team:

1. Get your TAM in Hubspot

For some, this may be challenging. For others, it may already be done. I get pushback that “there are leads we want outside of our TAM”… I encourage you to start here, just with your TAM.

2. Set up our new Hubspot integration

Connect your RB2B account to Hubspot. Our old integration added a marketing contact ($$), the new one does not. The new one adds pageviews to the timeline, and passes lead source, LinkedIn URL, and firmographic info. 

3. Let Hubspot route the leads to the appropriate rep

Before this integration filtering and routing RB2B signals was impossible. Our new documentation (see below) shows you how to use workflows to route leads to a given rep based on industry, size, lead source, last seen or timeline activity.

4. Set up real-time notifications or engagement workflows

Set up tasks for reps to follow up with visitors to key pages within 24 hours, OR increase the visitor’s “lead score” by a designated amount. Trigger targeted emails or sequences with personalized content based on website activity. Notify reps of repeat visitors.

5. OR … Push to Slack with the rep tagged.

We got negative feedback from people who have big traffic about how noisy the Slack channel becomes. This integration solves that.

TAKEAWAY

Last week I had one of the hardest days of my career.

We had $6,720 of churn in a single day. 

And we’re $5k LESS MRR than a week ago.

I felt like my face was a punching bag. 

Reading churn reasons made me feel sick. 

I was legitimately scared of what's coming.

I’m never scared. 

But I got a good night’s sleep and listened to an inspiring book with a quote from a billionaire oil trading legend: 

He said that business is about problems. 

And you must run towards problems, because problems are the only thing separating you from the success you are striving for.

If you ask Diana, she’d say the reason people are churning is because: 

1. We’re selling to people too small to make it work 
2. We’re telling them to do the wrong thing with the data

We’ve been saying you should run our visitors through Clay and send out automated messaging… 

But we’re learning that the companies who are getting the most value from the product are doing something VERY, VERY different. 

They’re doing what I talked about in this post. 

Diana is joining us full time to fix problem #1. 

This Hubspot integration (and soon a SF integration) fixes problem #2. 

I humbly approach this next chapter of building RB2B recognizing that v1 missed the mark.

But we’re only just beginning. 

Keep building.
