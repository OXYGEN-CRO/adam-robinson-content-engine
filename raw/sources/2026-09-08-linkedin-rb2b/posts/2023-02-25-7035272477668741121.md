## Exact post text

Today I want to reveal the best subject line I’ve ever written — and give you 3 easy tricks to write subject lines that your customer can’t help but open…

So what was it?

At my last startup, Robly (an ESP), I set up this automation that triggered when customers sent their first email using our platform.

After sending their first campaign using Robly — customers would get a plain text email with the subject line:

“Hi [NAME]. Just poking around. Saw you just hit send… All okay?”

Forget open rate… the REPLY rate was over 70% 🤯🤯🤯

Why was the subject line so awesome?

Simple:

1) TIMING — the customer got this email with this message at the perfect time while intent was super high

2) PERSONALIZATION — that email had personalization almost to the point where it was creepy. Lol.

3) MAGICAL — if you can create ‘magical’ experiences for customers (meaning they elicit this positive emotion and don’t know how it happened), your emails will crush

That’s what I’ve got today. What do you think of the subject line I shared?
