## Exact post text

In 14 months, I bootstrapped my SaaS Startup from $10M-$20M ARR (half the time it took me to go from $1M-$10M ARR). Here's my 6-step framework for scaling up without raising VC money:

Step 1: Bring on an advisor to give us the unicorn playbook

- I needed help from someone experienced for the next phase
- Santosh put ZoomInfo and Apollo on the unicorn path
- He acted as an advisor for 12 months
- Then joined us full time as COO

Step 2: Grow the team

- We got to $10M ARR with six full-time employees
- We needed people and processes to push to the next level
- We started with go-to-market, then built out an engineering and product
- We (sadly) went too far too fast… we were 55 at one point, now we’re 45

Step 3: Build an in-real-life sales motion

- We sell to primarily large Shopify merchants
- They tend to buy at at dinners, events, and shows
- We hired a partnership team to build an agency and affiliate motion

Step 4: Build out a proper product and engineering team

- Tate had built the entire app, Anne and I had done product
- It was incredibly fast and surprisingly high quality, but high key-man risk
- We hired Michael to learn the product and app before we hired engineers
- We now have a very strong team in product and engineering

Step 5: Dial in reporting and RevOps.

- This still hasn’t completely happened yet
- We went from spreadsheets to Salesforce starting last summer
- We’re attempting to have Salesforce be the source of truth
- We have a complicated business to report on (annual deals w/ opt-out dates)
- We continue to make progress… but still have no idea what our CAC/LTV is

Step 6: Expand TAM, keep building cool stuff.

- We’re going downmarket, in beta with a $77/mo Shopify app. 
- We’re going upmarket, uncapping our $5k unlimited deal, getting SOC.
- We’re going B2B, in beta with an individual-level ID product for sales teams

TAKEAWAY:

What got you here won’t get you there.

You can only get so far with a team of highly productive individual contributors.

But, with the foundation we put in place we 2x'd revenue with a small increase in headcount.

Happy to answer questions in the comments! 👇

TL;DR

Step 1: Bring on Unicorn Advisor
Step 2: Grow the team
Step 3: Build IRL sales motion
Step 4: Build out engineering
Step 5: Dial in RevOps
Step 6: Expand TAM
