## Exact post text

99% of salespeople think the next generation of $1M/year earning AE’s are current entry-level sellers at top-tier startups learning the ropes as BDRs. They’re wrong. The next generation of 7-figure sellers are aspiring YouTubers:

Here's the breakdown:

1) We’re moving into a sales world where the best “influencers” will have incredible power

Generative AI will increase the amount of content created by a factor of 100x over the next several years. Fewer voices will break through, but those who do will break through so spectacularly because we will crave authenticity and perspective that great thought leaders bring. The POV that the leader brings is what makes great content… AI doesn’t have perspective.

2) Few on LinkedIn understands how powerful video is

I’ll make two exceptions - Chris Walker and Morgan J Ingram. The talking head itself is completely hypnotic. Anybody who says anything even semi-intelligent and can get people watching them talk every day and will have a power over their audience that text simply cannot create. It’s a weakness that we have as humans.

3) “Influencer Selling” is infinitely more capital efficient than sales teams

Whether it be with founder brand or a non-founder becoming an influencer, you can create and capture WAY more demand with WAY less time and energy than a traditional marketing and sales motion. That’s in addition to the insane top-of-funnel awareness you create for you and your company.

4) Aggregating audience (which really means BUILDING COMMUNITY) through video is a learned - and INCREDIBLY valuable - skill

This is MOST VALUABLE thing any company can be doing over the next decade. Community and brand are the only two moats that exist in a world where competitors can ask ChatGPT to copy your entire website and product and spit it out in 5 minutes.

5) Sales teams will have 5% of the headcount, 50x the overall follower count.

Assembling and managing a sales team will be more like putting together a team of elite athletes. But, unlike sports, there isn’t a fixed amount of players that can be on the field. I think we’re going to see some crazy new playbooks built during the next VC bubble when money is flowing freely.

6) This new generation of rep is going to be PAID.

As eyeballs on the social platforms continue to grow, so does the value of the athletes (or sellers) who know how to attract the most eyeballs. Will these types of reps be paid multiples of what today’s highest paid reps are being paid? Definitely. Will we hear about insane “contracts” that these types of reps move jobs for? Without a doubt. Will these reps be paid more than CEOs? 100%. They will make or break a business.

TAKEAWAY:

The next generation of $1M W2 sellers won't follow the SDR to AE path.

They’ll be YouTubers who decide they want to make REAL money.

When this happens, sales teams are going to get smaller.

But much, much more powerful.

Want to be a “super-rep” paid millions?

Start sharpening your video skills.
