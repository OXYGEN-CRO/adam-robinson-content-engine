## Exact post text

In 6 months, I grew my startup from $0-$1M ARR with six people and zero funding. I’m about to launch a new startup and plan to hit $1M ARR in 50% of the time (with even fewer people). Here’s my 6-step playbook for building a lean, mean, high-velocity growth machine:

To set the stage:

- Our current startup is $22m ARR, bootstrapped, we sell to ecommerce
- We are launching a NEW product for B2B that identifies your anonymous web traffic, then sends their LinkedIn profiles to a Slack channel, in real-time
- I’m totally addicted to watching these leads roll in.

Now, to the 6-step playbook to get from $0-$1M in 90 days: 

Step 1) Have insane product-market fit.

- If you don’t have strong word-of-mouth, nothing else will work
- If you do, you can accelerate things in so many different ways
- We have a product that when I show people what it does, their jaws drop 

Step 2) Make it free, unlimited use

- It’s 2024. Buyers don’t want to talk to salespeople
- They want FREE, with MASSIVE value. Don’t hold back
- Figure out how to get on millions of sites, then gate features later somehow

Step 3) Create virality through all available channels

- Social is everything. Influencers are everything. Founders are the best.
- Build systems that create user-generated content - reviews, social posts, etc
- Create viral loops that turn 1 free user into 2 more

Step 4) Start investing in community Day 1

- Community will be your biggest driver of user-generated content
- Community is your only true moat. Anyone can copy software and GTM
- Deliver content that builds community around expertise, persona-by-persona

Step 5) Build in Public. Be ridiculously transparent

- This just works. Trust me. I've been doing it for over a year. Look at my posts
- Share as much as you can. The more naked you get, the more eyeballs
- If you can, make it a reality show. I literally did that last year. It’s on my profile

Step 6) Charge a throw-away price to larger businesses

- To start, sales velocity and market share are more important than revenue
- Gate things at low price points that are obvious value
- Take the pricing discussion off the table.

TAKEAWAY:

Every business is different. 

What I’m trying this time wouldn’t have worked for me last time. 

(It may not work this time… We’ll see!!!)

What’s the same is I’m starting with a product that makes people’s jaws drop.

But unlike last time, I’m making the product 100% free. 

Free is the global domination play. 

And, if it catches fire, it's the quickest way to $1m ARR. 

PS.

DM me if you want to: 

1. identify the anonymous people on your website, and 
2. Get their LinkedIn profiles sent to a Slack channel, in real-time

…It’s 100% free. 

No, I am not kidding.
