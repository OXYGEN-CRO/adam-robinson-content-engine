## Exact post text

I lost $8.5 million on my P&L in a single day, back when I was working on Wall Street. Quick storytime.

Act One: I was suuuper long on this one company called Beazer.

Act Two: I saw a headline that read “Fraud at Beazer”.

Act Three: I lost $8.5 million on my P&L in a single day.

I thought my life was over, considering I’d only made $600k the year prior.

That was one of the worst days ever.

Funny enough, that event made me more risk-tolerant than before, because I realized that money ain’t a thing.

Right now, I’m really going for it with Retention.com.

Genuinely. All in. Nothing left on the field.

Because of that, I feel very comfortable putting all of my chips on the table, because if it doesn’t work out, I won’t feel it like someone else who’s never been exposed to this kind of risk would feel it.

I’m in my element because of it.
