## Exact post text

I've talked to 47 Founders and CEOs of DTC brands in the last 6 weeks.

Here are the 12 most controversial and surprising things I learned:

1. Past a certain point, brands are having to focus more on retail instead of DTC because of the Apple privacy changes that keep coming.

2. CAC is up 50% or more since iOS14. Nobody knows what to do about it.

3. Brands would love to spend more on ads across the board if they could get their CAC down. They just don’t know how to.

4. CAC to LTV, blended CAC, and MER are the three metrics that actually matter today.

5. The brands who are killing it in today’s world are brands that can get a broad ad to convert.

6. Nobody is talking about inflation anymore. It seems like that ship has sailed.

7. Despite the recession or slowdown or whatever it was that happened in Q2, brands that are hurting are brands that were hurting before the slowdown happened.

8. Email is still 25-35% of total revenue for almost everyone I’ve talked to.

9. When I mention the Meta/Klaviyo 7-day tracking problem, most people are not “problem-aware”, almost no one is “solution-aware”.

10. Everybody wants to know if this will be a “normal” BFCM season.

11. Everybody is much more cautious about literally everything than just 90 days ago.

12. Almost all DTC founders wish they had started a SaaS instead.

#ecomm #DTC
