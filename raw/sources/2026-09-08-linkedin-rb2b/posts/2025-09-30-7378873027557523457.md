Whenever I say “RB2B is at $6.3m ARR in 18mo w/ 3 FTE’s”, everybody asks “WHAT ABOUT CONTRACTORS AND CONSULTANTS?" It's a fair question. Here's every contractor we use and exactly what they do:

BACKSTORY: 

Before we launched RB2B, I watched Ryan Babenzien build Jolie - a shower head company - to $50m in revenue with 3 FTE.

They built an 8-figure annual profit business in a physical product space with a tiny but incredibly talented and productive team.

They kept core competencies in house and outsourced the rest.

I thought to myself, “this HAS to be possible in SaaS.”

Fast forward to today…

We are growing at 5.9%/mo off a $6.3m ARR base w/ 3 FTE’s, and our core competencies of building, customer interaction, and actually writing Linkedin content are kept in house.

But what about everything else?

We have an army of incredible contractors delivering us best-in-class performance across ads, email, and SEO.

Here’s the full breakdown of who’s who and who’s doing what:

—
RB2B FULL TIME EMPLOYEES: 

ADAM (me): runs AI sales clone, posts on LI, 12 customer calls/wk, marketing site updates

Robb (Head of AI): AI doc architecture, Fin (AI support), resolves escalated support tickets, UI mockups w/ Loveable

TATE (CTO): Builds w/ Jules AI and Claude validates

—
CONTRACTORS:

Kyle Williams: LI outreach/”here’s your guide” messaging

TAKEAWAY

Ryan Babenzien proved 3 FTE was possible with physical products at $50m.

We’re proving it’s possible in SaaS at $6.3m ARR.

It’s becoming easier with every passing day.

It’s 2025.

$10m ARR is the new FU Money of SaaS.

Stop building teams. 

Start building machines.
