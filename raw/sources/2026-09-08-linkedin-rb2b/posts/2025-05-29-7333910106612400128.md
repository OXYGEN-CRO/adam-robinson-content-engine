Me: The RB2B website is VERY ugly. But I want to make it even uglier.

Eli: Ooooookkaaaaayyyyyy… Tell me more…

Me: I want this to feel like nails on a chalkboard. The literal anti-YC, anti-VC, anti-6Sense site.

Eli (smiling): I gotcha. I think we can deliver that. Do you want to do the standard agency process, or do you just want us to come up with something?

Me: Surprise me.

——————

You may be asking yourself why I set out to have the ugliest website in B2B. 

The answer boils down to the most important law of marketing: 

BE AS DIFFERENT AS POSSIBLE.

In every way you can. 

When Dave Gerhardt introduced me to Eli and said I should let NoBoringDesign rebrand our site, I thought my $75 Webflow template riddled with typos was “nails on chalkboard” enough. 

But Eli proved me wrong. 

And today I am THRILLED to announce… 

In collaboration with No Boring Design… 

THE NEW RB2B!!!

Not only is our branding confusingly 1990s, we: 

- Added Demandbase’s Global, Company-Level ID
- We’ve built deep Hubspot and Salesforce Integrations
- We’re on a path to solve the filtering, routing, and what now problems

AND…

We’re turning on ads, email, SEO, and probably influencer marketing again. 

Of which I will be providing up-to-the-minute “build-in-public” insights about. 

SO… 

1. I hope you hate the new branding, so much so that you talk about it
2. I hope you are willing to try RB2B again (if not today, very soon) 
3. I hope you talk to Eli if you are considering a rebrand

He does “attractive” work also. Not just this. 

Click through my “visit my website” link and check out the site … 

Let me know what we missed in QA. I’m sure tons of stuff is broken.
