In 10 years, I bootstrapped $0-1M ARR twice, and just 14 weeks in we’re at $800k ARR with RB2B. Here's the highly controversial secret weapon I used to go $0-1 (and why it might NOT be right for you):

SHITPOSTING

Here’s a breakdown of what I did, why I did it, and whether you should do it to: 


Shitposting Campaign #1: 

The GetEmails (now Retention.com) Facebook Ads Campaign of 2020

- I made 1min talking videos answering the sales questions w/ Helen (now wife)
- They were amusing, we used props, we rotated backgrounds
- A month in I realized that I was agitating a vocal minority of privacy people
- They were calling me a scumbag for reasons X, Y, and Z 
- I made ads specifically about X, Y, and Z, and their heads exploded
- Facebook loves this more than anything, the ads crushed
- People still say they recognize me from those ads today!!!

Results: 

$0-1m ARR in 26 weeks. 6 FTE.


Shitposting Campaign #2: 

The 6Sense Saga of 2024

- I was upset at a slimy tactic 6Sense used to get us to renew early
- I put up an inflammatory (and hilarious!) post about how they are afraid of me
- By some act of God, they sent me a Cease and Desist
- I posted the Cease and Desist and re-wrote the shit post 
- It was the single greatest PR campaign for any 8 week old startup ever
- Everybody on LinkedIn hates 6Sense, so it lit a fire I NEVER expected

Results:

$0-1m ARR in ??? weeks. 5 FTE.

(I can’t tell right now but if I had to guess I’d say it will be 17 weeks).

TAKEAWAY 

Should you shitpost? 

On one hand, you might look at this and say, “social media loves it, so DEFINITELY”. 

You would be correct about social media loving it. 

The problem is you run the risk of ruining your reputation… 

So if you do, it BETTER be worth it. 

Separately, I’ve found that it’s also very bad for my mental health. 

When I’m shitposting, I’m living in a world of negativity that I do not thrive in.

My wife says I turn into the worst version of myself.

I’d say don’t shitpost unless you are: 

- At a VERY early stage
- There is an INCREDIBLE awareness opportunity

1. How to shitpost
