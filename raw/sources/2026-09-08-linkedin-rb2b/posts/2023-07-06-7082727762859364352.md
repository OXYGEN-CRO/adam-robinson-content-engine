## Exact post text

Last week I walked into the woods of Colorado with just a backpack. No cellphone. No GPS. No plan except to avoid seeing another human being for 7 days. It was incredibly therapeutic. Here’s 4 reasons why I think every founder needs to try “social starvation”:

1. It gives you time to think deeply about who you really want to be.

- Who do you want to be 10 years from now? 
- When you are 80 and look back on your life, what do you want to see?
- What do you want your children to believe?

2. You are able to think clearly about priorities and boundaries you want to set for yourself.

- Am I doing too much travel for work? Is it really worth what it’s costing me at home?
- Can I pull off a one-month personal trip with my family each year for the next five years?
- Can I spend Wednesday mornings with my daughter and make up for the time at some other point in the week?

3. I’ve done this twice, and both times I’ve felt an overwhelming sense of gratitude for everything I have.

- My wonderful wife, beautiful daughter, and fiercely loyal 6lb chiweenie
- My incredible team, many of whom I’ve had the pleasure of working with 5 years or longer
- My wonderful community here in Austin, and the ways we get to spend our free time here

4. You can really reflect on business, the recent past, and the road ahead.

- So much has changed since two years ago when I did this.
- I’m imagining what my world will look like in two years when I do this again.
- I came up with a crystal-clear vision for what I think we should accomplish during the back half of the year.

When I was sitting there contemplating everything, the phrase “what got you here won’t get you there” kept running through my mind, over and over again. 

I realized that the future of our company is a product that we have in beta right now, that not only helps people with their email, but also helps people with their ads, by extending the life of the cookie. 

We're nearing $20M ARR.

Our goal is to build a unicorn.

What got us here won’t get us there.
