## Exact post text

This weekend I was speaking to the CEO of a $100M apparel brand who hit a wall in Q2. His CAC was performing 15-20% worse, he revised his 2023 forecast down $20,000,000. 

But 11 days ago he made ONE CHANGE that turned everything around and now he’s going to beat his original forecast. 

BACKGROUND

He’s all Meta and Google. 

He’s been growing at 100% per year, and over time, repeat customers have become more and more of his business. 

Things were fine when they reached 40% of his ad spend. 

As they got to 50% and 60%, things started slowing, and the ads performed worse. 

He decided to cease showing ads to repeat customers, FULL STOP. 

The result? 

Every day in August has been an all-time record, he reversed his $20,000,000 downward revision for 2023, and he’s beside himself excited about 2024.

TAKEAWAY

Sometimes a simple change can have a massive impact. 

Never stop throwing spaghetti against the wall.
