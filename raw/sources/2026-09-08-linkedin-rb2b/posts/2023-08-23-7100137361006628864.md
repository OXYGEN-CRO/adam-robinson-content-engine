## Exact post text

How I took my startup from $0 to our first $1 million in ARR:

We tried a bunch of channels to see what worked (most didn't).

Here's the story of the one strategy that did...

It started when I saw people posting short videos on Instagram.

It felt like a new trendy, funny way of communicating.

This was back in late 2019.

So I thought, “How can I apply this to my business?”

For the next 6 months, my wife Helen and I posted one 60-second selfie video ad to Facebook every week.

We wore costumes, we acted outrageously, and focused on humor

And we got attention.

We ran these short videos as ads and got our first several customers

Unfortunately, the customers sourced from these ads had a 20% monthly churn.

Pretty bad, but the ads were paying for themselves.

So we refined our messaging and the product to lower that churn.

Looking back, the videos were ridiculous.

Miles behind what our marketing ended up being

But they worked.

We got to $1 million in ARR on those ads alone.

Here's the lesson: 

Just get started.

Put your message out there. 

Try to cut through the noise and catch some attention.

Be silly, try things other's wont, and innovate.

You can refine it later, but put V1 out there and see how the market reacts.

Don't overcomplicate it.

You'll never "be ready".

Just go for it.
