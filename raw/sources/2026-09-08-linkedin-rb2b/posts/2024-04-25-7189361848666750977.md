## Exact post text

Last week I moderated a roundtable with Devin Reed - the mastermind behind Gong’s game-changing LinkedIn strategy - about growing your personal brand. Here are his 8 secrets to LinkedIn success:

1. Be inappropriate.

LinkedIn is the “safest” social network out there. People behave like they would at work. If you can be bold, offensive, or even the slightest bit inappropriate, your content will stand out. For example, at Gong they put out content saying to swear in the subject lines of emails.

2. Start small, very niche.

I’m the CEO of a bootstrapped SaaS. My content about struggling to grow my bootstrapped SaaS will resonate more (and lead to a deeper connection) with founders struggling to grow their bootstrapped SaaS rather than if I were to write about entrepreneurship broadly.

3. Post every day until you find content/market fit.

Content-market fit is when you know exactly what your readers want and you have a growing audience with increasing engagement. This took me 12 months to achieve. I’ve heard similar timelines from other people. Once you find content-market fit, you can scale back.

4. Recycle and repost your old stuff.

Devin downloads all of his Linkedin content once every six months, goes through his posts that killed, and either copy-pastes them and schedules for later, or if he thinks he can write it better now, he improves it.

5. Build growth loops.

Devin uses his LinkedIn for discovery, and drives readers to his Newsletter. He can then use his Newsletter to drive traffic back to LinkedIn posts that he wants to see more engagement.

6. Small posts can have a BIG effect.

Broad, viral posts may get lots of likes, and grow your follower count. But often the posts that lead to the deepest connection and real business are niche posts about a specific topic that not very many people are interested in. Devin said that Dave Gerhardt reached out to have him on his podcast from a post he would have otherwise deemed totally unsuccessful.

7. The algorithm isn’t THAT consistent.

But it is SORT OF consistent. Meaning, on average a great post will do better than a bad post, but day-to-day, you never know. Just because a post didn’t get the attention you thought it would, doesn’t mean that it won’t if you modify it slightly and repost 8 weeks later.

8. Hardly ANYONE will see your post.

Even your huge posts are seen by a tiny audience relative to the size of LinkedIn. Peter Conforti did an analysis of my audience and 85% of my engagement is unique post-to-post. Don’t be afraid to repost, and don’t be afraid to put your best content out over email. It WON’T hit the same people.

TAKEAWAY:

Crushing LinkedIn in HARD.

It takes long-game dedication that most people can’t handle.

But if you commit, it unlocks a marketing channel unavailable to most people.

Not only that - if you do it right, it’s the single most efficient trust building machine on the planet.

And trust = brand.

In 2024, brand is built one post at a time.
