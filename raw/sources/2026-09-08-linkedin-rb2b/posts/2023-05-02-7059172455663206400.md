## Exact post text

This time last year, I was playing it ‘safe’ with my company and our growth.

GetEmails was stuck at $8M ARR, I was burnt out — and I didn’t think it had the potential to grow exponentially as a company like Jasper.ai did (who I was sharing an office with).

But after a couple of conversations with really smart people, I realized I had a money-printing machine that was sitting dormant.

Santosh Sharan came on after having built multiple colossal data companies and now, we’re at $20M, on a path to $50M

Within 2 months, we were selling all annual deals at a high velocity…

✅ We had a more robust product suite…
✅ We only did demos for Shopify Plus clients…
✅ We rebranded to Retention.com…
✅ We’re building out a BDR org…
✅ We’re building out an agency partnership org…
✅ We’re building out an influencer/affiliate channel…
✅ We’re building out an events channel…

Need I go on?

The lesson here:

“What got you here, won’t get you there.”

One of my favorite quotes — and a saying that every entrepreneur needs to have etched into their brain.

PS - I’ll be sharing exactly how we take Retention.com from 20M ARR to 50M ARR right here on LinkedIn. Follow along if that sounds interesting to you.
