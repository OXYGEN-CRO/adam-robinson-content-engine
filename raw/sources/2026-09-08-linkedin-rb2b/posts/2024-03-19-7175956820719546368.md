## Exact post text

Two weeks ago we (accidentally) launched R!B2B. We got 2,122 free signups and 1,083 script installs, but only 14 paying customers. Am I worried? Nope. In fact, here's a 20-page guide explaining EXACTLY how to never pay us:

To set the stage: 

- We launched our Person-Level Website Visitor Identity tool March 1
- It’s for Slack, but we are trying to charge $495/mo to push data elsewhere 
- 19 days in we have a 0.7% conversion rate to paid, and it’s getting worse
- One (of many) reasons is you can parse the Slack block w/ Zapier and send it wherever for FREE!

As you can imagine, when I learned this for the first time, my reaction was... 

“Well, shit!”

We had spent weeks building integrations to Instantly, Apollo, and Smartlead, thinking that many of our “little guy” customers would use Slack like a dating app and pay $495 to push a contact that looked good into a sequence in one of these enablement apps. 

It turns out, zero people have wanted to pay $495/mo for that use case. 

*Sigh*

My emotional state went from disillusionment to despair as I realized that the main thing we were hoping to charge for… filtering and a push to Hubspot or Salesforce CRM… could be done with a workaround using Zapier. 

I'm realizing that the fundamental problem is that people are getting an UNBELIEVABLE amount of value from our free tool, and very little INCREMENTAL value from paid. 

Especially because you can do what you need to do with Zapier.

So, what do we do???

- Do we gate the Slack app like everybody is telling me to do? 50% resolution free, 100% paid?

- Do we bag the forever free offer entirely and do a 14-day free trial? 

- Do we try to somehow scramble up the Slack blocks and make it so Zapier can’t parse the LinkedIn URL and send it elsewhere?

- Do we stop going for market share and focus on a mid-market sales-led motion (like I planned originally)? 

None of these sound like great options. 

Especially given the insane positive feedback we’re getting from our free users. 

Instead, I decided to make a 20 page step-by-step google doc explaining EXACTLY how you can avoid paying us FOREVER using Zapier.

Why?

1. I included reasons why you would actually pay us in the doc
2. I want as many people to book demos with R!B2B as possible
3. I want the challenge of adding value to our paid plan BEYOND the integrations, rather than pulling back value from the free plan

The best companies have something so good people can’t stop themselves from talking about it. 

I’m trying to do everything I can to speed that process up. 

Enjoy the guide!
