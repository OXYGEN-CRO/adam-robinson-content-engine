## Exact post text

Over the past two years, I’ve spoken to 300+ startup founders and DM’d with 1000's more. Most feel stuck, strapped, and stressed. Why? Because they're all making the same startup-killing mistake. Here's the #1 reason founders fail:

They have not done (or do not know how to do) true Customer Discovery. 

This usually plays out one of two ways. 

The FIRST CAMP of Founders raise seed capital for their brilliant idea.

Then lock themselves in a room for months (or years) and build their vision. 

They emerge to find that no one wants or needs what they’ve built. 

Not even the people who said they cared initially.

The SECOND CAMP is even more heartbreaking. 

They’ve read all the YC blog and memorized Four Steps to the Epiphany.

They KNOW they need to be doing discovery. 

They are doing multiple customer interviews per day.

But alas, they launch, and… No one cares, again. 

Not even the people who said they cared initially. 

What happened to these poor, unsuspecting Founders? 

They fell victim to the fact that most people - even during discovery - can’t help but be nice to a hungry Founder who is working his ass off building something. 

They’ll tell you your idea is great. 

They’ll say they have the problem you’re solving. 

The’ll tell you they want the solution your presenting. 

They may even tell you they’ll pay for it when it comes out. 

But here’s the thing… When you go back to them, they won’t buy. 

Why? 

Because you ran your discovery in the wrong way. 

Here's how you run discovery the RIGHT way:

1. You talk prospects about their DAY, not your product
2. You analyze their WORKFLOWS, but don’t mention your solutions
3. You understand what MOTIVATES them, and how they measure success

The idea that you are building a product to solve a problem they have may not even enter the conversation until the last few minutes. 

When it does, here are a couple pro tips: 

- Try to talk far less than a normal conversation.
- Watch for facial microexpressions. People can’t fake those. 
- Try to get them to pay for something you don’t have. GREAT indicator.

I’ve gone $0-$1M 3x times, each time in half the time with half the resources. 

My discovery got twice as good each time. 

The most successful founders I know ALSO ran the best discovery processes… 

Whether they realized they were doing it or not. 

Don’t let a flawed discovery process keep you from the startup you deserve.

Massive success is out there … Waiting for you to come grab it.

Unicorns are born one discovery call at a time.
