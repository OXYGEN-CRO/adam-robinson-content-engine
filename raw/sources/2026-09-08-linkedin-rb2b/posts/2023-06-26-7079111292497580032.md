I’ve scaled my company to $20M ARR.

Here’s a simple piece of advice I have for first-time founders (I wish I understood this when I started my first company).

Ads are expensive. Product development is expensive.

So do not spend a single dollar before you have SOME indication that someone will actually buy your product.

Get an indication that your intuition is correct before you light your money on fire.

Crazy how many founders ignore this step.
