I’ve learned a ton scaling this company from $0 → $20M ARR.

Here’s the 1 piece of advice I have to offer for first-time founders:

Do not spend a single dollar before you have SOME indication that someone will actually buy your product.

Ads are expensive. Product development is expensive. Scaling a business is expensive.

Get an indication that your intuition is correct before you light your money on fire.

Don’t ignore this step.
