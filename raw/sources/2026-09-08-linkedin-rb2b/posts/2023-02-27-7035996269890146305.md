## Exact post text

Every serious entrepreneur needs to study this person.

I’ve recently become a fan of MrBeast — and I’ve got 4 lessons for you that I’ve learned from him that you should take with you and implement in everything you do, starting right now:

1) Success is not random or accidental 

When talking about virality, everyone always puts the blame on the algorithm. “The algorithm made you famous”, etc.

Virality is not an accident. Success is not an accident.

MrBeast is proof that you can go viral again and again and again — if you have the right formula.

He’s spent over a decade developing an SOP for exactly how to get hundreds of millions of views and get the world to talk about you.

2) Choose your friends wisely

If you’re playing a big game and you don’t have friends that don’t point out your weaknesses, you’re never going to be as big as you’d like.

MrBeast had a posse of ragtag YouTubers who would review each others’ content and give brutally honest feedback about what could be better.

You need to have friends like that who will call you out on when you’re not at the top of your game, whatever that game is.

I share an office with the Jasper.ai guys for a reason. We all give each other feedback and help each other grow.

Neither one of our companies would be this big if we didn’t get involved in each others’ stuff.

3) Your content sucks. Sorry.

If you watch one of MrBeast’s videos, you immediately should feel humbled.

It’s like watching LeBron play basketball. There isn’t a person in the world who can make content the way he makes content — the way he engages an audience for as long as he does.

It makes you want to be better at both short and long form video.

4) There is great joy in the pursuit of excellence

I listened to him on Joe Rogan, and he goes on for hours about how much he loves the idea of being the best.

He commits every hour he has and every dollar he earns into making better and better videos for his audience. It’s all he cares about.

He doesn’t want to be a billionaire like some of us do.

He doesn’t care about a 50,000 square foot mansion and a 300 foot yacht.

If he wanted to be worth $5 billion, he could make two phone calls, sell his empire, and he’d have everything he wants.

But that’s not his pursuit.

His goal is to be the absolute best in the game of content, and I believe he’s already there.

That’s all I’ve got for you today.
