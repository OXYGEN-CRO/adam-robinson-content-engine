## Exact post text

My heart almost stopped when I saw that Instantly ($30m+ ARR) created a Person-Level Visitor ID tool that undercut RB2B pricing by 66%! I felt a HUGE sense of relief when I figured out what was REALLY going on: 

To set the stage:

If you are me, and you see Instantly - a BEAST of a company - created a person-level ID tool, you would be slightly worried.

If you then heard that they only had 2 plans:
             
500 credits = $97
5000 credits = $397

And RB2B’s pricing is: 

200 credits = $119
1000 credits = $399
5000 credits = $699

You would have a heart attack!

I felt a massive sense of relief when I realized what was actually going on:

____


1. RB2B is only charging for complete records with a Linkedin profile, and your verified email rate is much higher

- Instantly is charging for partially complete records
- RB2B is charging for complete records with Linkedin URL’s

My view is that the user experience of getting passed a name and a phone # with no LinkedIn profile is a bad experience.

But I may be wrong. People are out there saying our coverage being lower - even though our quality is higher - is hurting us.

[SEE IMAGE FOR AN EXAMPLE]

That means: 

- Our match rates look lower (and they are, but barely)
- Our plans look more expensive (and they may be, but barely)
- Instantly is resolving WAY fewer business emails than we are
- But you are only spending money on records that are complete


2. DATA FOR OCT 14-18 from Retention.com’s Website:

Instantly:
- Profiles Identified: 313
- Profiles w/ LI: 105
- Profiles w/ Biz Email: 94
- ICP Leads: 10
- ICP w/ LI profiles & emails: 5

RB2B:
- Profiles Identified: 89
- Profiles w/ LI: 89
- Profiles w/ Biz Email: 69
- ICP Leads: 7
- ICP Leads w/ profiles & emails: 7

Since we are resolving full profiles, and don’t yet have company ID yet (coming soon!), our ICP leads are lower, but profiles with LI & biz email are higher.

Which was our goal from the start.


3. The ICP overlap between Instantly and RB2B is ZERO

I believe that there is ONE THING you should be evaluating with visitor ID tools: 

“Who are the ICP visitors that I’m getting from this vendor?”

When we compared the two, there was ZERO overlap of ICP visitors.

If two vendors give you a completely different set of ICP visitors, and you can make EITHER tool work, you should BUY BOTH.

Why? Because each vendor does ID differently.


TAKEAWAY

At first glance, Instantly’s pricing looks like it was designed to crush RB2B.

If you dig deeper, you’ll notice the extra credits you get in their plans buy you incomplete records without LI profiles.

What’s even MORE important?

There LITERALLY ZERO ICP overlap!

What does that mean?

If you can make EITHER the Instantly signal OR the RB2B signal work…

You should buy them both!

I have deep respect for Instantly.

This is by no means a sh*tpost.

I want everybody to have transparency about what they're buying.

And if you buy Instantly’s ID product, and you can make it work…

…Use us too!
