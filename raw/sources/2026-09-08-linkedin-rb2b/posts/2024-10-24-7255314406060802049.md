## Exact post text

3 weeks ago I posted that RB2B’s churn hit 17%/mo at $2.9m ARR. Since then we’ve lowered churn to 10.9% and grown to $3.5m ARR (+20%). Here’s what we learned, what we did, and what we plan to do:


WHAT WE LEARNED

1. Our product is unusable for our ICP (growth stage SaaS w/ sales teams)
 
If you're tiny and get 3 leads a day, it’s great. If you’re an Enterprise with a growth team and know exactly what to do with our feed, RB2B is great.

For growth stage startups with sales teams, filtering and routing is currently impossible without Clay. We are in the process of fixing this in several ways.

2. Our product exposes our customers’ traffic problems

Some people don’t realize how low a % of ICP visitors hit homepages in general. When our product highlights it, the product gets blamed, and in some cases, willingness to pay goes down ("Why should I pay for junk leads?").

3. We have broken LinkedIn links

We are in the middle of a huge database update. This problem will resolve itself in the coming quarter. We've had one public accuracy test done with Clearbit - we were far more accurate - and are having 11 more done now.

4. These aren’t actually leads

I’ve come to the realization that a site visitor isn’t a lead, it’s a signal.

We shouldn’t be selling demos, we should be selling alerts (and how to action them) to sellers that help them move prospects down the funnel.

Booking a demo is only one use case and it’s lower value than ongoing signals to salespeople from accounts they are monitoring and prospecting.


WHAT WE'VE DONE

- Added Hot Leads
- Added URL restrictions
- Changed weekly stats email to be more informative
- Added Zapier (usage is 4x what it was 2 weeks ago)
- Added “the most successful RB2B accounts do these 3 things” email


WHAT WE ARE GOING TO DO

1. Tell Hubspot CRM users EXACTLY how to filter and route in HS

We are going to create a feature that ONLY sends contacts at accounts that are already in Hubspot TO Hubspot, grab the account owner, then send it BACK to Slack with the rep tagged. We believe this will make our product usable for growth stage SaaS on HS.

2. We’re messing around with other delivery methods

We think reps might want this signal delivered to their Gmail (or Outlook) inboxes. If this works, it will eliminate the friction of marketers having to set up a HS workflow.

3. We think the “what now” problem is really a prioritization problem

A rep who carries a quota and/or manages a team has so much going on that they don’t want to take 5 mins to come up with what to write. We think we can use AI to tell the rep what they should say and why they should say it - and cut that to 15 secs.


TAKEAWAY

10.9% churn is absolutely god-awful.

While it’s better than 17%, we need to chop it in half.

My hope is if we keep listening and trying to eliminate churn reasons.

And we can come up with a product that growth stage sales teams LOVE.

THAT might get us to $50m ARR.

And I’d take $50m ARR.

So I’ll keep building.
