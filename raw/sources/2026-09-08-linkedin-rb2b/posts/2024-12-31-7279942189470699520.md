## Exact post text

Retention.com closed 2024 at $25m ARR (+15.2%) and we’re at $12m run-rate profit. RB2B went $0-$4m in 10mo. Yet I sit here anxious and annoyed about this seemingly glorious outcome. Why?

We’re STUCK again.

Here’s the good, the bad, and the ugly of 2024....

THE GOOD:

- R! landed its first “true enterprise” deal with a MAJOR brand
- We closed 40 mid-market deals (over 250 employees) since July 1
- We launched RB2B March 1 and ended at $4m ARR with a tiny team
- We adopted EOS, are more focused and more efficient

THE BAD:

- Churn slows us down on both sides of the business
- We do not yet know our ICP for RB2B
- We have 37 copycats at RB2B
- We shrunk 3mo in a row in Q124

THE UGLY:

- We were wrong about how most people should use RB2B
- We did a large layoff in June at R! (reduced headcount 40%)

2024 was a rebuilding year.

Given where we are right now, 2025 will be too.

===

WHY WE ARE STUCK?

We have two business units – Retention and RB2B.

Retention.com:

We’ve been stuck since late 2023 because despite winning the “large Shopify store” market for what we do, we learned:

1. The number of stores we could make successful was smaller than we thought
2. Competitors show up faster than we thought they would
3. Despite fighting churn, there are still 8 reasons you’d turn this off

So, stores drop, but we have a great product, a great brand, an amazing in-person selling motion, and we’re slowly moving upmarket.

Add all that up and we chop around the same spot, ~$21m ARR.

RB2B:

RB2B was a rocket ship until about 5 weeks ago.

$0-$4m ARR in 9mo, with 30-50% monthly growth.

But all of a sudden we hit a wall.

In fact, we’re going backwards.

What the F happened?

1. We sold site visitors as leads, and promised demos (WRONG)
2. We thought the use case was emailing through smartlead/instantly (WRONG)
3. Our free slack app works against us because web traffic is so noisy
4. Credible competitors with other features bolted this on
5. People got stuck at the “what now” moment, we didn’t provide a solution

Meanwhile, our $119/mo plan is 20% churn per month, and some of our larger customers are and saying “we ran this for 6mo and didn’t close any deals”.

That sounds pretty bleak, and in many ways, it is.

And it’s why we’re moving backwards.

Add those things up and guess what you get…

Stuck. With a capital “S”.

===

If you would have told me on Jan 1 that we would end the year +$25m ARR with $12m run-rate profit and RB2B would go $0-$4m ARR in 40 weeks, would I have been happy?

No.

I would have been ecstatic!

Because Dec 31 last year I was stuck, with a shrinking business.

Somehow we had an out-of-body experience with RB2B.

At the same time, we “grew up” as a company and improved our profitability.

Yet as I sit here at my desk on Dec 31, 2025, I find myself just as stuck as Dec 31, 2024.

And just as annoyed.

Despite now having a business that is my moonshot goal from 5 years ago.

So, what did I learn?
