## Exact post text

I had a very interesting conversation with Erik Huberman, the founder of Hawke Media, a $150M e-commerce marketing behemoth. 

If you have visibility into thousands of businesses ranging in size from $1M a year to $1B a year, you’re going to have some extremely valuable data that no one else has.

I was curious enough to ask him about some of the best tools that every e-commerce brand needs to be utilizing right now, and how they should be doing it.

These are the 3 that he absolutely insisted on:

1. Postscript

It may come as a shock to you, but I’ve noticed that 20-30% of brands I speak to are still not sold on SMS.

They think it’s expensive, invasive, and they want to be respectful to their customer by not annoying them with text messages.

SMS is opt-in.

If they give you their number, it’s fair game.

If they don’t want to hear from you, they’ll unsubscribe.

So many brands leave money on the table by not doing SMS because they’re afraid to annoy people.

You won’t. It’s industry standard now.

If you write fun copy that makes people laugh, that’s even better.

At LEAST set up an Abandoned Cart flow. Bare minimum.

2. Hawke AI

Yes, Hawke as in Hawke Media. An incredible agency play is to take what you’ve learned through working with clients, and package that into some sort of info tool to lift all ships.

Hawke AI is a collection of data across 8000+ brands, which allows marketers to look at this data and identify weak points for your own brand.

You can analyze how you stack up against everyone else in terms of performance across channels, where you’re overspending, etc.

You can’t rely on just your own data to make decisions for the future of your brand.

Having access to other brands’ data is what’ll help you grow sustainably.

3. Retention.com

Listen, I know what this looks like. But I did not pay Eric to say this. He said this of his own accord. And I’m just paraphrasing it.

He said this:

“Retention.com is just capturing so much more data and so much more audience, you just need to do it. There’s no question here. You’re trying to build a business? You should check these boxes. Retention.com is one of those boxes.”

In terms of ROI alone, this makes sense for virtually any DTC brand over $5M in annual revenue.

Like I said…not to toot my own horn…but check this out if you trust the good word of Eric Huberman…the founder of a $150M agency…

Also. I’m curious. What tools would you add to the list?
