## Exact post text

I try very hard to be as anti-guru as I can. My opinions reflect that, almost universally.

I do not talk about anything related to “get rich quick”, I don’t talk about feelings all that much, and I don’t talk about things that are not tried-and-true ways of growing and operating a unicorn.

However…

I’ve actually recently gotten into manifesting. But I did it backwards.

Instead of trying to outsource my effort to some Deus Ex-Machina for problems that I have in business or in life, I started manifesting before I could even admit that I was manifesting.

Christie Ellington, the superstar who’s interviewing me for Billion Dollar Challenge, actually told me that I manifest.

When I really thought about it, I realized that that’s probably true.

I have manifested some insane outcomes.

Apparently, I speak about a future world that seems impossible to imagine as if it’s already happened.

For example:

“Retention.com is going to have a billion-dollar valuation by year-end.”

This sounds insane. But I know it’ll be true. And it comes through in the way that I say it.

Not only have I been able to convince myself of this fact, but I’ve been able to convince my team of geniuses that this is true as well.

So it’s not just me.

The manifestation hooks onto other people as well.

Here’s what I’m manifesting:

1. $50M ARR by the end of the year. Only 250% growth from where we are now. Really not that crazy.
2. A small Q3 investment round from Shopify. Triple Whale just did it. We can too.
3. A 9-figure secondary round at a unicorn valuation in 2024.

Easy.

Tomorrow I’ll run you throw exactly HOW I ‘manifest’ these outcomes. So follow me here on LinkedIn if that sounds interesting to you.
