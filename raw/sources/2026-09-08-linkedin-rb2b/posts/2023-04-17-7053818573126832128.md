## Exact post text

Back when I worked for Lehman Brothers, I believed that the only reason why people worked was to make as much money as possible.

Since then, I’ve had a change of heart.

I’ve seen how much pride and satisfaction our team is getting from working for this company. Employee satisfaction is just as important, if not more, than making huge profits.

How do you improve it?

Simple. Here are 5 ways to 10x employee satisfaction in your company:

✅ Make sure that your mission and vision is clear so that new recruits have a full understanding of what it will be like to work for you and your company.
✅ Put your employees first. If your employees feel that they’re valued and appreciated, they will take care of your customers.
✅ Optimize for velocity and reduce friction.
✅ Goal Setting. Make huge goals and show your employees why they’re achievable.
✅ The best leaders harness positive energy and transfer it to their leadership teams so that it trickles down to their teams, the customers, and partners.

Save these for when you're building out your team.
