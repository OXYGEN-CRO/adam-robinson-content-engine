## Exact post text

Last week I spoke to the CRO of a $50M ARR SaaS who just KILLED a six-figure ABM campaign because after 8 months they only booked 3 meetings. Here's what went wrong and why (broken down by the #'s):

BACKGROUND:

The ABM campaign ran to 1,600 target accounts from January to August across digital ads and email marketing. 

Here's the numbers breakdown:

- 1,632 target companies
- 13,000 contacts
- 231 people engaged with the ads (1.7%)
- 66 visited the website (0.05%)
- 3 demos booked

Here's the vendors they used: 

- Terminus was used for ads
- Hubspot was used for email
- Salesforce was used for reporting

Here’s how my conversation with the CRO went: 

CRO: Adam, it was a very horrible outcome.

Me: If you could do it over again, what would you change?

CRO: Well, this campaign started before I arrived, and we didn’t have a tight definition of our ICP, relevant content, and personas.

Me: Sure, that'll lead to abysmal results in any campaign. What else did you think was the problem?

CRO: Well, we sell to 500+ person companies, and we only had company-level website visitor resolution. We knew that 66 companies hit the site from the campaign, which is low, but honestly I was fine with it… But our BDRs had to try to figure out which of 20+ people at the company it might have been! It was a huge undertaking that yielded very few results.

Me: What if I could tell you the exact people at those companies who were on your website? 

CRO: My BDRs would be all over it. We might still be running that campaign!

TAKEAWAY:

ABM is expensive and hard.
 
What makes it even harder is that once you actually drive the right person back to the site, vendors like 6Sense, DemandBase, and Terminus only tell you the company they work for and the geolocation.

If you’re selling to enterprise, this information is not very helpful.

That's why we need a new “Identity Layer” as the last step in the loop.

Meaning...

An *exact match* to the individual that hit your site, rather than only the company name.

99% of SaaS revenue leaders aren’t aware this is even an option. 

But it is!

And it's going to change the trajectory of ABM in 2024.

P.S.

DM me if you want to know how to add person-level identity as the last step of your ABM campaign.
