## Exact post text

Earlier this year, I was in Aspen with a friend who had been building a fintech startup for 7 years as the COO/CFO.

They sold for $275M 🤯

And she now has a cushy job where her salary is 3x what it was before…

She has a three-year deal where she's rolling equity…

And she made enough money on the sale to retire.

I was expecting to show up and have her glowing because cruising and being cash rich is what we all strive for. The crazy part?

I saw the exact opposite.

She was so bored, burned out, and in a post-liquidity even malaise that I hear about more and more. What are the lessons here?

I’m not sure I have all the answers. But it does confirm the feeling I’ve had that if I were to sell Retention.com today for $1B … I’d deeply miss the thrill of building this company.
