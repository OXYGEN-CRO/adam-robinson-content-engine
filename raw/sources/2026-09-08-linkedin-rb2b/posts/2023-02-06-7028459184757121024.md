## Exact post text

More founders should have a bootstrapper mentality.

I just finished going through a rapid hiring period. It cost us $1.54M to get that done.

I didn’t hire another person until we added $1.57M to our ARR so we could pay for that last expense.

That’s just how I am.

If you get a wire for $10M into your business checking account as a newer startup founder, you will 100% splurge on things that you do not need.

You hire pointless employees…

Sign a crazy office lease…

And take delivery of a big, marble statue of yourself in the foyer 🤣

Unnecessary.

When it’s your own money, you treat it differently.
