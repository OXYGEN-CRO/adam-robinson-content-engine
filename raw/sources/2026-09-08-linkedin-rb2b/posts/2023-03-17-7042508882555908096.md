## Exact post text

When writing copy — it’s very easy to come off as desperate.

Instead: write like you’re writing to someone you’ve known for years.

It’ll put you in the mind frame of being comfortable with the way you write, which signals status and makes people respect you and trust you.

For example, I ‘could’ say:

“Hey there,
I’m Adam, the founder, and CEO of Robly!
Thanks for sending your first email with us. Myself and my team are happy to have you on board with us!
You are now a member of our family!
Don’t hesitate to ask me any questions and we’ll get back to you within 24 hours.
Yours truly,
Adam Robinson”

This is a “take me out of your system, I’m switching to Sendlane as we speak” email.

It’s trying too hard.

And it’s SO obvious that the purpose of this message is to be a little bit ‘too nice.’

It just feels like I’m about to ask for your credit card information. Yuck.

Write like you’re writing to a peer, and the person will feel like you’re a peer.

It’s a self-fulfilling prophecy.

Don’t be needy.
Don’t say too much.
And of course, have a little bit of fun with it.

That’s how you write high-converting copy.
