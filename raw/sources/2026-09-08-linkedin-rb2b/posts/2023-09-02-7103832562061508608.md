## Exact post text

It’s 2023 and we’re *still* setting up young salespeople to fail.

99% of companies assume their BDR teams create demand (they're wrong).

This idea is leading to TERRIBLE outcomes for both companies and sellers: 👇

It looks like this...

- The “Predictable Revenue“ model  (or what I call the Old Way) requires us to create a revenue goal based upon an arbitrary growth rate

- We then look at the BDR team’s productivity and fail to realize 90% of the demand they captured would have found its way to you through another channel (Yes, it’s really 90%)

- We then take the number of reps divided by the number of demos they booked per month and assumed that if we hire 4x more reps we'll have 400% more demos

- We then grow our BDR team by 4x with young reps, don’t train them how to sell, and teach them a playbook that literally does not work anymore

- We then layer on managers to help with the new headcount, add expensive tech, the whole nine yards

- Then nothing appears to be happen… but that’s okay, you just need to give it 6 more months (maybe more!) to actually start working

- Then 6-12-18 months goes by and EVERY WEEK the manager of the 400% bigger BDR team is asked by the executive team why they're missing their targets by 75% 

- Finally, either the manager quits or is let go, the BDR team shrinks to the size it used to be or smaller, pipeline remains unchanged, and you feel guilt/shame for having put everyone through it all 

Trust me, I know.

I just went through this at Retention.com

Here's the takeaway:

The Predictable Revenue model is broken.

If you're going to work for a company that still runs that playbook, be careful.

It fails to acknowledge:

- Cold outreach doesn’t work in 2023 like it use to
- BDRs in 2023 are capturing (not creating) demand 
- Growing teams won’t actually grow demand

And it puts salespeople and their managers in an impossible position.

Our young sellers deserve better than that.

Train them right.

Flood them with demos. 

Be content-led.

Capture demand through traffic to the website (both identified and anonymous) 

Use partnerships, influencers, and other ecosystem plays. 

And let sellers sell. 

Stop trying to replicate the Salesforce playbook.

It's not 2010 anymore.

Your salespeople (and your bottom line) will thank you for it.
