## Exact post text

Gratitude can be hard to find as a startup founder. You’re always pushing. You’re always aiming at the next milestone.

But it is SO important — and I try to only surround myself with grateful people.

You want people to advocate for you so that when a recommendation is needed, your name and your business is at the top of their list.

Here are 5 easy ways to cultivate a gratitude mindset…

✅ Make time to stop and reflect on the good things in your life.
✅ Journaling. If you haven’t tried it, I highly recommend that you do because it works!
✅ No complaining allowed. It’s not always easy to do, but if you catch yourself doing, just stop.
✅ Show how grateful you are by sending thank you notes to those that make a positive impact in your life.
✅ Be thankful for your health and the things we take for granted. It’s easy to forget how lucky we are

Anything you’d add?
