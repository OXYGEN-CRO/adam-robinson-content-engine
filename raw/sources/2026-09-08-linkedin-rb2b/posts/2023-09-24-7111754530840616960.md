## Exact post text

In 2020, my startup went from $0-$1M ARR with a controversial growth hacking strategy. Here's the highest ROI Facebook ad we ran and the story behind the social campaign that saved the company:

BACKSTORY:

In early 2020, Covid had just hit.

The world had gone into lockdown.

My SaaS was in its infancy and we had a handful of large event clients cancel contracts overnight.

50% of our revenue disappeared.

I didn’t know if we were going to survive.

Then I had a crazy idea.

I noticed more people taking selfie videos and posting them on Instagram.

So I thought... What if I could make a weekly 60 second selfie video every week with my wife that was:

- Amusing
- Had a different background
- Answered the top 3 sales questions

We did a couple of these “It’s Adam and Helen again, week [X] of lockdown” with masks around our necks, in a different part of our house.

They did okay, but with this video, I had a breakthrough.

Facebook LOVES controversy.

I knew I needed to lean into it however I could.

Fortunately for me, there are some very controversial aspects about our core product.

We identify anonymous web traffic and can resolve them to email addresses, even if someone doesn’t fill out a form.

There is a very loud minority of people who think this practice is horrible.

Not only that, I figured out that they will call you a SCUMBAG, a CRIMINAL and a THIEF in the comments.

Facebook LOVES that even more!

So, what did I do?

I leaned into it.

We created ads that I KNEW they would hate.

When the ads went live people started attacking me and I fired insults right back at them…

…from the BRAND account!

This made their heads explode.

My idea was that we didn’t have a brand to destroy, so who cares.

The pissed off people would write another novel length comment back, I’d fire back at them, and meanwhile, this type of inflammatory dialogue is Facebook’s favorite thing ever, and the ad would absolutely crush.

What happened next?

I created new ads based on the inflammatory comments from the last post.

The campaign took on a life of its own.

People WERE anticipating the ads every week.

And new customers and leads rolled in.

This whole process ended up lasting less than a year.

The ads eventually just stopped working.

But the company was saved.

And we hit $1M ARR shortly after.

–

Disclaimer: I would NEVER do anything like this at our current stage.

We actually do have a brand to destroy and an image to uphold.

I just want you to know how hard it is to go from $0-$1M.

And what you have to be prepared to do to get there.

$1-$10M is easier.

$10-$20M is easier than that.

But $0-$1M???

That’s the hardest thing there is.

Get creative or die.
