## Exact post text

Against the advice of counsel, I'm posting Retention.com's Q2 P&L.

Here's my analysis on the current state of our business: 👇 

Q1 was peak euphoria, mid-Q2 was peak disillusionment. 

It seems like most (or all) of the vendors selling into our ecosystem had a hard time this quarter.

Pipeline growth basically stopped, and to make the problem worse, we switched prospecting strategies from a "spray-and-pray" to a personalized approach... 

Meaning we went from "stopped" to "really stopped". 

We ended up fixing the problem and finished with a very strong June. Just for context, we do a 12mo deal w/ 60 day opt-out. Here are 12mo deals initiated, by month, in ARR:

Jan: $1.75m
Feb: $2.3m
Mar: $3.0m
Apr: $2.0m
May: $1.68m
Jun: $2.2m

Let's get into the BIG PICTURE details...

What we did well:

- Successfully switched our sales culture to a team of "hunters"
- Started paying attention to spending (we're back to running a tight ship)
- Have less than 10% of our current revenue in month-to-month deals
- Experimented with a new product that expands TAM
- Grew our engineering team from one to 6
- Nailed down our partnership motion 
- Made A TON of RevOps progress. 
- Brought our flagship product (Reclaim) into 100% Klaviyo AUP compliance - without sacrificing customer ROI 

What we could have done better:

- We should have moved slower. Knowing what I now know, and knowing a macro slowdown was looming, I would have. 
- We ended up overselling a chunk of deals in Q1, which has led to contraction at the 60-day period. It was a headwind for growth in Q2. We see the problem as solvable, and if we can nail it, we believe we will be back to 10% growth months.
- I could have been a better forecaster. Because of all of the above, I was off by a mile. I'm off my $50m end-of-year ARR target. We are aiming for $25-30m, highly profitable, and believe that is firmly rooted in reality.

What we are focusing on the rest of the year:

- Spending less and making more every month.
- Focusing on the customer lifecycle to minimize contraction
- RevOps/Reporting. Lots more wood to chop! 
- Bringing the new product to market.

BONUS INSIGHT:

We believe there is a large B2B opportunity within our product suite and will start experimenting this quarter. If you want to know what visitors are on your pricing page and want to be a beta tester, DM me.

Keep following along to watch us build a unicorn in public.
