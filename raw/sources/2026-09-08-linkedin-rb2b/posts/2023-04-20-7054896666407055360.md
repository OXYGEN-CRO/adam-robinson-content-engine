## Exact post text

Five years ago, I was stuck. We just couldn’t get past $3M ARR.

An opportunity came up that would help me get the leads that I was chasing forever.

This guy had been let go of another company and was putting together a program that his former company had created.

He was under a non-compete but we thought we were fine to move forward because of how non-competes worked in California. A few months later, the former company sued us and we quickly learned that everything was NOT fine…

Here are 4 critical lessons I learned from that experience:

✅ If you don’t want to get sued, respect non-compete agreements.
✅ In America, anyone can sue you for anything. Why? Because it’s America, baby!
✅ When you’re new to the startup business world, it can feel a bit like a David vs Goliath scenario. Be very careful when poking the bear, especially if that bear is a Goliath.
✅ If you ever do get sued, get out of the situation ASAP. Even if it means settling for more than you’d like. It’s not worth the mental pain and anguish.

Hope me sharing my experience helps you avoid a situation like that.
