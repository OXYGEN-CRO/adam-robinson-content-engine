## Exact post text

In the last 48 hours, our AI sales rep has handled 80 prospects, delivered 19 demos, sent 6 people SOC2 docs, and gave custom pricing to 2 enterprise accounts. Here’s how our first AI sales motion works:

Last week we downsized RB2B ($5.5m ARR) from 5 to 3 FTE.

One of the roles we eliminated was an Account Executive.

Why? Because our “sales-assisted PLG” turned out to just be PLG.

Meanwhile, a year ago we made an early bet on Fin. 

I wanted RB2B to be a huge free offer, but I was terrified of the support burden.

Fin wasn’t great a year ago. It was answering things incorrectly, humans had to get involved over 75% of the time. 

Over time, Robb has willed it to resolving over 70% of tickets correctly, with no human intervention, declared by the user! 

That is bananas!

———

All Robb and I have been talking about is how we can create a system that is as effective as Fin inside the app, but answers the main six questions our prospects ask:

1. Can I get a demo?
2. How does pricing work?
3. How does your tech work?
4. Are you SOC2, can I get docs?
5. What about GDPR/CCPA?
6. Do you integrate with __________?

I saw Lemkin’s post about his Delphi clone a couple months ago. I signed up thinking I’d use it for my community, loaded it up w/ my LinkedIn and YouTube content, and did nothing with it. 

Robb and I decided to drop it on RB2B’s website on Wednesday to see how it handled inbound sales inquiries. 

And boy were we surprised at how it did … 

Our AI has spoken to over 80 prospects in 48 hours. It’s given 19 demos, sent our SOC2 docs to 6 people, and explained our pricing to 15 people.

Here’s the crazy thing… 

It’s only answered one thing incorrectly (and it was an easy fix).

———

Here’s why I think this is going to lead to us to $10m ARR in with 3 FTEs:

This morning I had an email from a prospect who blew through 1000 contacts in 4 days and needed custom pricing for a larger plan.

I was on calls all morning, but after I got off, I checked chats people have been having with my clone.

This guy had asked the EXACT SAME question to the AI, and guess what it said.

1. It correctly calculated the plan size he would need
2. It told him he would need Robb to set it up for him
3. It gave him Robb’s email so that he could reach out and move forward

The prospect’s response to the AI?

“Thank you … That is so helpful. Early feedback on this AI is it’s so helpful!!!”

Want to see my clone yourself?

I’ll leave a link to it in the comments. 

WELCOME TO THE FUTURE!!!!!
