## Exact post text

We’ve scaled Retention.com at an insane velocity.

And as that happens, my schedule has gotten more and more insane — and managing my calendar has become a high-value skill for me (without it I’d go crazy).

Today I want to walk you through how I keep my calendar in check, without an Executive Assistant.

Here are 3 quick lessons I learned...

✅ Use Calendly. I have separate accounts for separate purposes and it’s a game-changer.

✅ Every day has a purpose. Dedicate a day each week for specific purposes like content creation and meetings.

✅ Schedule your time to be at home. If it’s not on the schedule and it’s important, how will it ever get done?

Anything else you’ve found to work well for you?
