## Exact post text

In 2016, I was stuck at $3M ARR with my Email Newsletter App for 36 months. Here's the best piece of startup advice I got during that period (and why it ultimately got me unstuck):

“Try to solve a problem in email that hasn’t been solved yet.” 

This sounds obvious in hindsight.

But I couldn't see it.

When I was stuck, I was: 

- Building features my competitors had to catch up
- Trying to do growth marketing w/o product-market fit
- Frustrated that nothing I tried would work

Once I solved an unsolved problem, I was:

- Selling something new that my prospects hadn’t heard of
- Had inbound demand, so didn’t need growth marketing
- Excited to get out of bed because I was having a blast

Try to solve a problem that hasn’t been solved in your vertical. 

Don't just try to do what everyone else is doing better.

Do something different.

It’s the quickest way to get unstuck.
