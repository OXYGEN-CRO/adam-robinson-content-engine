## Exact post text

Here are the 5 biggest problems I see that stop Shopify brands from scaling faster:

1) Lacking strong product-market fit (the big one)
2) Low stock levels and long lead times in hero products
3) Failure to look at Media Efficiency Ratio for ideal ad spend
4) Copywriting that doesn’t speak the language of the Ideal Customer Profile
5) Waning ad performance due to inability to identify returning website visitors

There are over 4,000,000 Shopify stores.

Less than 1% will ever make it past 7 figures in revenue. 

The ones that do, have solved these 5 problems.
