## Exact post text

Two weeks ago I went to a CEO dinner at SaaSOpen where the cumulative revenue of the 9 CEOs was over $1B ARR. Here were my 7 most interesting takeaways:

(In attendance: Chili Piper, Apollo.io, Clay, Justworks, HootSuite, the ZoomInfo of China, Pavilion, and Ebsta)

1. Without making it a rule, for 4 hours, only one person spoke at a time, at a table of 12 people. The level of intense focus and attention was incredible.

2. The CEO’s that had hundreds of millions of ARR and thousands of employees set so much better of an example than I do. Just the way they speak. They are playing the game at such a higher level than I am. You can tell they are used to having thousands of eyes on them at all times. It was admirable, that level of poise. Yes, I have thousands of people watching my every move I make too, but it's different...

3. We talked about the nature of reality and the way people interpret it for like 30 minutes. I never thought that would have been a topic of discussion for a bunch of SaaS people.

4. Nobody could decide if we were more or less politically divided than the past, and there was a 50/50 split between people who thought tech was responsible for it vs. didn’t contribute at all. 

5. The Justworks CEO had a cool way of getting buy in from employees. They articulate a super hi-res vision of the future, where we are today, the gaps that needed to be closed, and WHY it was so important to close them. Every executive and manager could recite it word for word! That was so motivating. I want to be able to articulate that clear of a vision to our people.

6. I walked around the room thanking everybody, feeling so humbled to be able to participate in such a fabulous conversation, and surprisingly they said my zone of genius (which I see as these weird social media pranks) was equally enthralling.

7. Sam Jacobs said everybody he’s speaking to had a much better '24 than they were expecting … and were optimistic about 2025. I was surprised by this because I think it’s actually HARDER for my audience, which is 50% $0-1m ARR.

TAKEAWAY:

Why was this dinner so cool for me? 

I’ve never sat at a table where every single attendee has been ahead of me. 

I’d love to re create that somehow, more often. 

Thank you Alina for putting this all together.
