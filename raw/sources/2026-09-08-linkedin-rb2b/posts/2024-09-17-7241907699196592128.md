## Exact post text

Startup execs think that the most important things in business can be measured. They’re wrong. This video (below) is a great example:

Long form, deep storytelling, beautifully shot, and very expensive...

Making a financial argument for why this is worth it is impossible.

But if you read through the comments on some of our past videos, you can tell people are emotionally moved in a way that they just aren't by our classic "talking heads" videos.

In your business, there are numbers that you can look at that show you that it’s working. 

But the more powerful things are things you experience.

I believe that we’re entering a new world - where software will become much easier to build, and therefore completely commoditized. 

In that world, what moats are left? 

- If you have network effects, great, most of us don’t
- Brand (more and more I believe it will be personal brand)
- Community 

Join me, Chris Walker and Andy Mewborn today as we talk about what makes a good moat… 

While we hang out on a boat!!!

(see what I did there??)

As Andy so wisely put it… 

“The game is no longer hiring a 10x developer to build features faster. It’s hiring a 10x marketer to help you figure out how to push out your personal brand.”
