## Exact post text

I’ve had 18,402,926 views on my Linkedin posts over the past 365 days, and I plan to double that in 2024. Here’s my 5-step content plan for next year (and how it’s changed from 2023):

BACKGROUND: 

1. I have a $22m ARR bootstrapped SaaS, selling into ecommerce
2. We are launching a person-level identity product to the B2B SaaS space
3. I am now spending 90% of my time building (and creating content for) the new product

Doubling impressions from such a high number is going to be hard. 

And it’s going to require being DIFFERENT.

We have to give people something new. 

Something they’ve never seen before. 

So what are we going to do? 

Here’s the five-step Retention.com content playbook for 2024:

1. Daily high-quality posts, coming from 3 different execs at our company

- Santosh, Diana, and I will all post regularly to expand the attack surface
- We will continue to use our favorite templates/formats (that crush) 
- Our hook and “takeaway” game will get even better 

2. Build-in-Public, taken to the EXTREME 

- This year we gave monthly updates w/ MRR and shared quarterly financials
- In 2024, we want to do DAILY updates with a new reality show called "$0-$10m" which documents every step and details of us building and selling our new B2B product
- We want it to be an immersive, reality-show type experience
- We are going to host it all in a community space, where we’ll be constantly publishing GTM/outbound experiments we’re doing and sharing what’s working and not

3. Bi-Weekly “AMA”-type Virtual Events w/ me & Santosh

- We want to give more time and direct access to our audience
- This is also a great source of content (ask Linkedin OG Chris Walker)

4. Get on as many B2B sales/marketing podcasts as possible

- Demand is created when you get in front of new audiences
- NOT by BDRs, they capture demand created elsewhere
- I’m going to take these video recordings and the AMA and make them our podcast, as well as distribute weekly on LinkedIn as 1-5 minute video clips (with text) optimized for in-feed consumption

5. OVER-use Video 

- I think video is more powerful than anyone realizes
- I want to do talking heads, shorts, reality show episodes
- Video accelerates the automated trust-building machine
- Text only is great for reach, but connection is built with your face!

TAKEAWAY:

Creating great content is HARD, especially on LinkedIn.

It takes time, energy, and persistence.

But every CEO (especially in B2B) should be doing it.

In fact, your executive and front line team should be doing it too!

Don't just reshare company blogs and boring ghost written content.

Invest the time to learn how it works.

Create a unique voice and develop a POV that's different.

Then study the platform, measure your results, and adjust...

That's the only way to harness the power of Linkedin.

And in 2024, organic content will be king!
