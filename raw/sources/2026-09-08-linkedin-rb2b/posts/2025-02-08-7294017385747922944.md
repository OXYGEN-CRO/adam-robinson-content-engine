## Exact post text

Early-stage startup founders are bad at retaining employees. Here’s what you need to give to retain any employee, at any company, in any situation: 

Interpersonal Capital: 

- Are they working with people they respect and admire?
- Are they working for leaders whose status will be higher in the future?
- Is their contribution respected by the team, do they feel heard/seen?

Intellectual Capital: 

- Are they working on things that are interesting and challenging?
- Do they see a path towards more new and dynamic things?

Financial Capital:

- Are they being compensated fairly vs. peers inside and outside?
- If performance and equity is involved, is that appropriate/fair?
- (I have observed that “fairness” is more important than the number)

TL;DR

1. Interpersonal
2. Intellectual
3. Financial

Always solve for these three types of capital. 

You’ll never lose an employee again.
