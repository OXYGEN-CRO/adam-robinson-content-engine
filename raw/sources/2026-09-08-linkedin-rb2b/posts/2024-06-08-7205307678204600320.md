## Exact post text

Just 24 hours after changing RB2B's entire pricing model (and adding $150k ARR in a day!) we are changing it AGAIN. Here’s how we screwed up, and how we plan to fix it:

BACKGROUND:

- We “accidentally” launched our person-level identity product Mar 1
- We gave the leads and a push to Slack away for free
- We charged $495 for integrations, but you can parse the Slack block
- Our free-to-paid conversion rate was only 1.3%

That pricing was broken because: 

- Smaller co’s couldn’t pay $495
- Higher traffic sites needed an ICP filter
- Nobody knew what to do with the leads

So, two days ago we pushed out pricing that looked like this on the site: 

Credits: 

200: $119/mo
300: $179
500: $299
700: $419
1000: $599
1000+: Talk to us

Then our “enterprise” pricing was: 

2,000: $1,139 
3,000: $1,164
5,000: $2,466
10,000: $4,140

What happened when we pushed it out? 

PROBLEM #1: 

People who were over 1,000 credits per month were scared of pricing they would see once they talked to a rep. 

I know because a few people messaged me their concerns.

PROBLEM #2: 

We found ourselves in the middle of a bunch of conversations of 2-5k contacts of people that were like...

“What happened to the $495/mo unlimited?”

 I get that.

They felt bait-and-switched.

PROBLEM #3: 

Leadfeeder is part of the (inferior) company-level identity market, and they publish pricing. 

If I look at their pricing, the price-per-lead drops DRAMATICALLY at 1,000 leads/mo. 

400: $358
700: $499
1,000: $549
2,000: $649
3,000: $699
…etc

What’s a possible explanation for this? 

I’ve noticed that the lower a customers’ traffic volume is, the higher % of ICP visitors they have.

Why? 

I think it’s because marketing campaigns that drive traffic to your site raise overall traffic but lower traffic quality. 

Under 1,000 leads/mo is probably before people run campaigns. 

So smaller traffic companies can actually AFFORD to pay MORE PER LEAD than larger co’s. 

Because the quality of their traffic is higher. 

WHATS OUR SOLUTION?

We lowered the high end of our pricing, and undercut Leadfeeder. 

So, better product AND lower price.

This is one of my favorite value frameworks: 

“Great taste. Less filling.”

Our new pricing: 

Contacts: Was / Is: 

2,000: $1,139    /   $599
3,000: $1,164    /   $649
5,000: $2,466    /   $799
10,000: $4,140  /   $1,149

EVERY mid-market SaaS will be paying us $1,149 or under, monthly. 

With a 17% discount if you want to pay annually. 

TAKEAWAY:

Santosh and I declared that we would optimize RB2B for: 

1. User Growth 
2. Low time-to-value
3. Eliminating friction

Our “Day 1” pricing change was the opposite of #1 and #3. 

I think we’ve fixed the problem with our new pricing. 

And I think we’re giving Mid-Market SaaS what they want. 

Nobody wants long-term, slow-to-negotiate, over-priced contracts in 2024. 

They want it now, want it for free, and if you prove value, you earn your dollar.

And at RB2B, we want to earn every dollar we collect.
