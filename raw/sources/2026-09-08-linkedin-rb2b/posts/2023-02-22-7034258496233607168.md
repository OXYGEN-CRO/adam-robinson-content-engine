## Exact post text

Word-of-mouth is like a cheat code for your startup. It can have customers knocking at your door, wanting to pay $10K per month on annual contracts.

But how do you generate it? Here are 3 simple strategies:

1) Create a WIN-WIN-WIN offer

The person referring wins. The new customers wins. You win.

2) Be ultra-generous with refunds

I believe in karma when it comes to this stuff.

If someone’s asking for a refund, just give it to them quickly. It will come back to you down the line.

3) Systematically give out gifts to people who help you

If someone refers us a deal, I’ll send them a pair of AirPod Max’s.

A $530 gift. Again, I believe it will come back around to us.

The TLDR here is to make it stupidly attractive for people to refer you customers.

Is there anything that you’ve done that has helped accelerate word of mouth? Let me know in the comments or DMs.
