## Exact post text

Since January, I’ve spoken with over 50 startup founders between $1-3m ARR. They all ask the same 3 questions: 

1. What would I do if I were them? 
2. Should they try to raise money to get unstuck?
3. How was I able to bootstrap $23m ARR in 4 years? 

I end up saying the same thing over and over again … 

Something along the lines of: 

- Read 4 Steps to the Epiphany
- Find REAL buyers as you are building
- Bootstrap as long as possible, fend off the VC beast
- If you can bootstrap $10m, the world is at your fingertips

It got me thinking… 

Should I create a course? 

I’m asking you, LinkedIn community.

If I made one (or all) of these courses, would anyone pay $500-$1000 for them? 

The courses: 

1. $0-$1m Secrets
2. $1-$10m Secrets
3. Linkedin Secrets

P.S.

QUESTION FOR FOUNDERS:

If you WOULDN’T buy, could you tell me in the comments why not? What DO YOU want to learn?
