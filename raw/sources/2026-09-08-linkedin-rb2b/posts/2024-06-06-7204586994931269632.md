## Exact post text

Last week on Jason Bay’s webinar Mark Kosoglow called BS on my idea of Inbound-Led Outbound. He said, “Adam, do you REALLY think anything you are doing is new?” It was a fair question. Here's my response:

What do I think?

- Each individual thing I am doing is not novel in itself
- The combination of all of it together is not only unique, it’s tough to replicate

Here’s the breakdown of my Inbound-Led Outbound plan, point-by-point, and how it’s new and different:

1. Be the #1 Founder Brand on LinkedIn.

Mark said, “Everybody is saying I need to do what Adam’s doing, so it’s going to stop working like anything else.” 

Frankly, I don't think “everybody” can do what I do. 

Here’s why:

- Few have the patience to learn the craft. It took 12 months of zero results
- Few are comfortable sharing what I share. They have investors, etc
- Few have the capacity to dedicate their life to it. They're CEOs of scaling co’s.
- Few can spend the money on the team and production with ZERO connection to business results.

Founder brand is not new. 

I believe the way that I am approaching it is.

2. Have a crazy freemium offer that drives insane word-of-mouth.

My obsession with Mailchimp is ultimately responsible for our insane pricing. 

Having a free offer is nothing new. 

I believe that the REASON I’m doing it…

Designing a business model that THRIVES when outbound response rates literally go to zero… 

ESPECIALLY in the product category that we’re in…

Makes this unique and different.

3. Get a guest slot on EVERY podcast in the B2B space.

The idea of a Podcast Tour is also not new.

The issue is that no one wants you until you have a large audience!

What’s new is that all of a sudden I can actually get ON all the podcasts.

4. Drive visitors to the site. If they don’t sign up, personalize automated outreach.

Retargeting is not new. 

Person-Level Website Visitor Identity with RB2B IS new.

Running visitors through Clay to determine ICP and send a message written by chatGPT IS new (and it converts at 11%!).

TAKEAWAY:

Mark is right.

There is nothing new about any individual thing that I’m doing. 

I know to some, it just looks like marketing.

But I believe that the combination of it all… 

And the WAY we are doing it… 

Everything together IS new and different.

Everything together is Inbound-Led Outbound.
