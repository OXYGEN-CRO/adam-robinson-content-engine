## Exact post text

99% of people think a great Linkedin post results in 50,000 views and 300 likes. They’re wrong. Success on LinkedIn means your competition uses screenshots of your posts in their cold email campaigns: 👇

A few weeks ago I had a viral post about 6sense and Demandbase.

It led to 50+ booked demos for our beta.

Awesome, right?

But then the next day I was forwarded some *surprising* emails.

Friends were getting cold emails from a competitor using a screenshot of my LinkedIn post and Retention.com as the subject line.

At first, honestly, I was annoyed…

"This is MY audience!"

"This is MY content!"

"This is MY face!"

Of course, I was wrong.

As more people forwarded me the email, I felt validated and encouraged:

- This is positioning us as the market leader (even though our product isn't out)
- If I email these same folks, the odds are the response rate will be 10x higher
- It’s elevating my status as a thought leader in the space

Finally, I had a moment of clarity.

This is EXACTLY what I've been saying BDR teams should be doing!

This is the “new way” of doing outbound.

This is signal-based selling.

This is inbound-led outbound.

This is the playbook.

My takeaway:

There are downsides to building an audience in advance of launching a product.

But the trust you build and community you foster far outweighs any negative.

And I have to give them credit...

It was a clever and well written cold email.

A solid play to add to the the inbound-led outbound playbook.

Touché, Wormly.
