## Exact post text

My company Retention.com hit $20M ARR in under 3 years with no VC funding.

But it wasn’t always like that. My last company, Robly, was stuck in the mud for 3 years…

We competed with Mailchimp — an incredible company that was near-impossible to compete with, and I was trying to do things they weren’t doing

I thought there was an underserved portion of the market above Mailchimp and we could build software to fill that hole

So we spent a year and over $1M trying to create a new platform (nearly identical to what we already had) to serve these people

Not only did no one care…

A business coach spent a year trying to tell me that building and running two almost identical products that served different market segments was a terrible idea

A year and over $1M later we abandoned the product and the idea. Epic fail.

Why am I sharing?

Most of the entrepreneurs you look up to as “crushing it” now have had their fair share of failures

You just don’t see a lot of that in public

So if you’re ‘in the mud’ right now. Keep going. It gets better.

Let me know if you have any questions for me in the comments — I’ll answer what I can 👇
