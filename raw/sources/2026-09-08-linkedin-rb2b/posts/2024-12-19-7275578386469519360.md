## Exact post text

I f*cking hate trade shows. Why?

Here’s my life at trade shows in 2019:

- Buy a ticket for large industry show “X” for me and Diana
- Have zero pre-arranged meetings or any kind of plan
- Sit at lunch tables attempting to peddle our 8 week old startup, no one cares
- Look at other vendors who have killer booths in both envy and awe
- Feel stressed and anxious because I’m afraid of talking to people
- Drink way too much bad coffee, eat horrible food, sleep terribly
- Leave deflated, telling myself that if I made 1 connection it’s worth it

I still have PTSD.

Here’s my life at trade shows now:

- Lobby show owner in advance for a keynote, hopefully land one
- Figure out if I want to meet any of the other speakers in advance
- Post that I’m speaking, arrange a bunch of meetups in advance
- Show up two hours before my keynote, stand there
- Have inbound convos with people who know me from my LI stuff
- Connect with the 10-20 people who go to all of these who are now friends
- Try not to drink too much coffee (but fail, because I have a keynote and need energy)
- Give keynote and get 100x more exposure in 45min than I did in all of 2019

TAKEAWAY

Even though I hate them, they are a necessary evil.

The value of in person interaction is irreplaceable.

I acknowledge that my experience at shows today is unique … 

But I’ve been there, with no plan, no resources, and no chance of success.

You are going to have to learn how to navigate the hell that is tradeshows.

Why?

You HAVE to get in front of people and tell them your story.

You CAN have a plan, even if you don’t have resources, or a big LinkedIn following. 

Figure out who you want to meet. Meet them. 

Tell them what you’re doing. 
Show them a diagram. 
Ask them if they’d pay for it.

What they say back to you (and how they say it) will tell you a lot about the journey that lies ahead.
