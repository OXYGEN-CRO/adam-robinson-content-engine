## Exact post text

As a founder, you need to be ruthless with how you analyze your business.

At $12M ARR nothing was really that “wrong” with Retention.com.

We were profitable. We only had 6, highly efficient employees at the time. We were growing decently.

We had some roadblocks with our pricing strategy. 

Our plans were confusing for most of our customers. Pricing was organized in a number of different monthly tiers based on traffic, contacts, etc.

This led to a low conversion rate and a lot of churn.

Despite this, we were doing great.

The thing is… if I ignored the roadblocks with our pricing and sales cycle, we’d never get to where we’re at today.

Bottlenecks in your business are insidious — and will fly under the radar if you don’t actively look for them.

Never settle. Never get complacent.
