## Exact post text

In the past 90 days I've run a massive experiment on LinkedIn. It resulted in 6,499,623 views and doubled my audience from 25K to 50K followers. Here’s why and how we're approaching ROI:

First, to set the stage…

On July 1st, Retention.com decided to modify and expand our core product.

From selling website identity only to Shopify stores to a tool optimized for B2B SaaS companies.

However, before we developed a single line of code, I wanted to see if I could attract an engaged audience around the problems we solve:

- Cold outreach has become brutal
- Reply rates are ½ of what they were 2 years ago
- Teams need consistent and reliable intent signals

So, I decided to do a 180 on my Linkedin strategy.

From content just for Shopify marketers to content for people in B2B sales.

I've been dumbfounded by the results. 

Not only the increase in views, followers and website traffic, but the genuine engagement from our target market, the connection requests, the DMs.

I've even gotten Santosh and Diana to start posting with similar results.

This Linkedin experiment has the potential to reshape our business. 

But here’s the 100% truth about the ROI...

I have absolutely no idea what it is yet.

And that’s OK.

🎥 As I get into on today's episode of BILLION DOLLAR CHALLENGE, it’s the CEOs job to constantly experiment. And sometimes in order to figure out what’s next, you need to trust your gut. You need to do things you can’t track, things that don’t scale, things that might not make sense to everyone else.

So today I’m committing to tripling down on Linkedin.

Yes, I can’t track ROI yet, but the results could be massive.

That's what experimentation is all about.

P.S.
