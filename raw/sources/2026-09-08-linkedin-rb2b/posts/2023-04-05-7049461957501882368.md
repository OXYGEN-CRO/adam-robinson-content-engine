## Exact post text

Santosh Sharan is helping me build a billion-dollar company.

And there’s one saying he loves to share…

“What got us here, won’t get us there."

Our journey to $12M ARR grew entirely from cold email and one salesperson.

But that strategy wouldn’t have been enough to keep the growth going.

When Santosh joined our team, he saw our potential and knew what changes were necessary to get us to $50M ARR—which he believes we can accomplish within 15 months.

Here’s 8 of the changes we’re making to get us THERE:

✅ Selling all annual deals
✅ More robust product suite
✅ Only giving live demos to large Shopify stores
✅ We rebranded
✅ Building a BDR organization
✅ Building an agency partnership organization
✅ Building an influencer/affiliate channel
✅ Building an event channel

We’re becoming more confident every day that we’re going to hit that $50M ARR goal by year-end. 🎯

But remember, what got us here, won’t get us there!

We’re already working on what will get us from $50M to $100M and beyond.

Always be thinking ahead.
