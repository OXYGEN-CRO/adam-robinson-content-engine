## Exact post text

‘Slow and steady’ business growth is a bit overrated.

Instead, I prefer to look at one metric (the most important business metric out there in my opinion):

VELOCITY.

Let me explain a bit more…

✅ Grow with velocity.

Just grow faster. As long as you’re not setting yourself up for burnout, grow your business as fast as you can. 🏃

✅ Velocity trumps revenue.

You’re better off charging a lower price at first because you can always increase your prices.

✅ Remove friction anywhere and everywhere that you can.

Friction is sticky and by allowing it to stay around, you’ll end up being stuck instead of moving forward.

Would love to hear your thoughts on this one. I’ve laid out mine in this video. LMK.
