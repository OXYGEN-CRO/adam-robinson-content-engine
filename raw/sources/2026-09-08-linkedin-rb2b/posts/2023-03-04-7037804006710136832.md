## Exact post text

Bootstrapping vs. VC

A debate that continues to rage on in my comments that isn’t really a debate at all…

Look. I’m not saying VC-backed companies can’t be successful. I’m just saying the safety net provides a false sense of security. 

Bootstrapping puts the emphasis on spending less and generating more, while a pile of money to sit on usually leads to a lack of urgency (and a lack of profit). 

Retention.com went from 0 to profitable in 12 weeks. We had no choice.

Yes. I’ve been around the block more than once but bootstrapping with focus on velocity made this company explode.

I wouldn’t change a thing.

PS - follow along if you want more insights from bootstrapping this thing to a $1B valuation ✅
