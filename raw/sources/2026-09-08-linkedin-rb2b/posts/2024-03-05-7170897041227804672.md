## Exact post text

In 7 days, we generated 431 sign ups for R!B2B through fully automated outbound using our own Person-Level Website Visitor Identity tool and Clay. Here's the playbook (and step-by-step instructions): 

To set the stage: 

- We are launching our Person-Level Website Visitor Identity product 
- When I learned about Clay the combined value of the tools was obvious
- We set up our own Clay table for automated retargeting of ICP site visitors

Here’s our 6-step playbook for driving high-intent conversions through fully automated outreach: 

1. Install R!B2B pixel, connect to Clay via a webhook integration. 

The R!B2B pixel install takes under 5min. In Clay, you have to create a new table and import data from a webhook. You take the webhook URL and paste it into the Clay integration field in R!B2B. This is easier than it sounds. 

2. Once the LinkedIn profiles are flowing from R!B2B, enrich them in Clay. 

We pull in the entire LI personal profile AND company profile from Clay to compliment what we sent over, use Harmonic to indicate last raise and stage (since we target SaaS), add traffic through the SEM rush/store leads waterfall.

3. Guilt trip the AI into figuring out which leads are in your ICP with Clay.

Rather than just saying “tell me if these are SaaS companies," we tell it our job is on the line, take your time figuring out the correct answer. This weirdly yields better results.

4. Guilt trip the AI into figuring out who the prospects sell to with Clay.

Same strategy here. “Use the LinkedIn page and website domain to figure out who the company sells to. Take your time, be very careful, my job is on the line, you MUST have high confidence. And if you can’t figure it out, return the word "purple’.”

5. Create AI generated email copy in Clay

“Would you like to know how many of your [SITE TRAFFIC] website visitors who are [TYPES PEOPLE YOU SELL TO] and were poking around your site but didn’t book a demo? Would you like to see their LinkedIn profiles pushed to Slack or Teams in real time? Write back YES if so.”

6. Connect with our cold emailing infrastructure to send automated emails.

Taylor is using a combination of smartlead and 30 domains with 60 outlook accounts for our cold email campaign. We are taking the ICP visitors and sending them emails that Clay’s AI generates from the same infrastructure.

TAKEAWAY:

Clay is an incredibly powerful tool, once you know how to use it. 

Especially when you combine it with the first-party signal coming from R!B2B.

The only problem is that the learning curve for Clay is steep.
