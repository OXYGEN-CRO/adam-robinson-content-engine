## Exact post text

So, you want to start a SaaS?

Here’s some advice from my previous 8-figure exit:

My screw-it, all-in approach wasted a lot of time and money in my previous venture.

The company, Robly, did okay.

As I said — we sold to a PE firm for 8 figures. But looking back, I left A LOT of money on the table.

All because I lacked the skills and experience. I was making it up on the fly.

And sure. Sometimes it’s better to throw yourself into the fire — but I think a smarter approach would have been to work for someone else, first.

Become a 10x employee at another coming. Learn key skills. Build a rock-solid reputation.

When you take this approach, you’re pretty much accumulating the skills + experience + network you need to grow a real company… on someone else’s dime (solid deal).

So, if you want to become a SaaS entrepreneur:

Learn how the machine works → then go build it. 

Do you agree?
