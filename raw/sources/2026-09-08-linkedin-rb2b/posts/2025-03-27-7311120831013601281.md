## Exact post text

I sold my first startup for $10m cash. And immediately got scammed out of $1m in banker fees. If you're a founder thinking about selling your company, here's a step-by-step process for closing multi-million deals (w/ $0 in banker fees): 

BACKGROUND

- I had Aye Moah (CEO of Boomerang) on UnF*ck My Startup LIVE
- She bought GQueues for millions, but unlike me …
- She did the deal directly with the Founder, no banker, minimized legal expense
- Early stage SaaS buyers and sellers need to READ THIS POST 👇

Here’s Mo’s 8 playbook to close multi-million deals w/ ZERO banker fees: 

1. Don’t use a banker!

- Contrary to popular belief, if you have the buyer (or seller), you don’t need a banker.
- Lawyers can advise you and create all documentation to get a deal done

2. Hammer out main deal points w/ Founder on the other side.

- This is key for minimizing your LEGAL fees
- Don’t spend billable hours on points that don’t matter

3. Operate from a position of trust. 

- It’s VERY helpful to have a relationship with the counterparty years before
- This was Mo’s situation, my situation when I sold my co, and Jesse Stein’s situation on all 3 of his acquisitions
- You can tell lawyers to ignore inconsequential things… like a $9 payment that occurs after a cutoff time/date that could be wordsmithed infinitely to take care of edge cases

4. Be aligned with values and product philosophy.

- Cultural fit is the most important factor for making post-transaction integration successful
- Make sure both sides care about the “why”, both of the business, and product decisions

5. Find out what MARKET is.

- Tap into your network to figure out transactions that have CLOSED for deals like yours
- Ideas about what market might be (without closes) are not relevant
- You can get a business valuation if necessary

6. BUYERS: Figure out what you can afford to LOSE.

- “If this goes to ZERO, what can I afford to pay and still survive”?

7. Figure out deal structure. 

- Asset vs stock deal, cash vs. equity payout, holdbacks, earnouts, etc.
- Lawyers can help with what is possible, Founders can figure out if it’s appropriate

8. Sell to the RIGHT buyer. 

- These are our babies. You want to sell to the right person
- The acquirer of my business still had ALL employees after the 3 year non-compete

TAKEAWAY:

There are a few lessons here if you’re buying or selling an early stage SaaS.

1. Don’t pay bankers millions of dollars unless they are finding your buyer
2. You can minimize legal fees by hammering out major points w/ Founder
3. You should get to know potential buyers of your startup TODAY

Selling a startup is the ultimate reward for this journey we’re on. 

When you sell (or buy), keep as much money as you can. 

Don’t make the same mistake I did. 

You’ll regret it.

P.S.
