## Exact post text

Retention.com posted a PAINFUL down 1.4% for January, only our 4th down month in 51 months of existence (Jan ’21, Jan ’22 and Apr ’22). Notice a pattern? Here's why January sucks and what it says about our business:

The nature of our product lends itself to be bought BEFORE the holidays by brands looking for a boost.

Unfortunately the opposite is also true… it gets cancelled after the holidays.

Why didn’t January last year suck?

In late 2022 we honed in on ICP and grew our staff.

We sponsored everything in the ecosystem.

Long story short, we created a sh*t storm.

As a result, we had a MASSIVE January in 2023 (+10%).

The unfortunate reality of that 10% was we were overselling.

This lead to the most painful period of my career.

Our VP sales quitting, shrinking our sales org by 2/3, and lowering my topline  growth targets from $50m ARR (+350%) to $22m ARR (+60%).

What’s in store for '24?

I overestimated the effect on GRR that honing in on our ICP would have.

I thought our churn would go from awful to acceptable.

While churn improved a ton, it's still not great.

From my 50+ conversations with investors, I’ve come to accept that we're simply a high-churn product category (intent data) selling to a high-churn buyer persona (SMB ecom).

The fundamental problem is that in MarTech, being a system of record is the base-case for low churn.

Klaviyo and Hubspot are systems of record.

There’s only one reason you quit Klaviyo – to switch to Sendlane.

Sadly, there are 8 different reasons you stop buying us.

Accepting this and its implications on future growth was the worst part of 2023.

Yes, we can make it better.

Yes, we have the entire org focused on things within our control.

Please don’t DM me telling me you can help.

We know what we need to do, I promise.

As an aside, competitors will say this is an indication of the quality of the product. The truth is they're either lying about their own situation, overstating the revenue they drive for you (I can show you how), or are too early to see the problem.

Enough of the sob story...

Now for the wins.

We’re bootstrapped, generated $500k in profit in January, and the feedback on our new B2B tool is like nothing I’ve ever seen.

PROUD OF:

- We hired Eddie as our VP Rev Ops, it’s life changing
- We are executing a focused upmarket effort
- We sped up time-to-value and grew audience size
- We have 100+ beta users for our B2B product
- The free forever Slack app is dope, we’re booking a demo/day w/ it
- We hired Pete to build out chat bots to run the B2B sales/CS org

NOT PROUD OF:

- See above about churn

BIGGEST OPPS:

- Focus on key accounts on the Ecom biz
- Soft launch Feb 15 on B2B

TAKEAWAY:

Writing updates like this one SUCKS.

But I’m committed to doing it.

Why?

Because we need to tell the real story of what it’s like out there.

I’m sharing all of this so that you know you’re not alone.

2023 and early 2024 sucked for all of us.

Keep your head up, and keep building.
