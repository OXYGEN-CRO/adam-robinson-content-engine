## Exact post text

99% of startups think they need to maximize predictable revenue. They're wrong. The next crop of $1B unicorn startups will solve for sales velocity above all else. Here's 9 reasons why velocity will define the new world:

1. Revenue was never actually predictable

Everybody underestimated their revenue in 2021-2022 and overestimated their revenue in 2023... all while making staffing decisions that were backed into from year-end growth targets.

2. Prospects do their own research

The best GTM motions touch the prospects early and often in the research process with timely and relevant content that helps them on their buying journey. Making it easy for prospects who aren’t ready to buy to access and consume your content to wins deals down the road.

3. Outbound channels are maxed out

Automation overwhelm has led to response rates being cut in half over the last 24 months. Inboxes are ruined, LinkedIn is worse, and AI tools will increase problem 100x. Most sales teams are reacting by increasing activity!

4. The world is set up for a new GTM model

As cold outreach becomes more difficult, the model of the future will be one that warms prospects up through founder brand, innovative marketing strategies, UGC-generating machines, small events, indirect channels like partner and affiliate, and captures demand VERY low in the funnel. Close rates are 2-3x, sales cycles are 60% less and sales teams will be smaller.

5. “Influencers” will become more powerful

As the era of AI content unfolds, the best, most authentic content coming from the most trusted voices will become even more powerful. Social continues to become the primary method to create awareness, even for software platforms. 

6. High-Velocity environments attract the best talent

The best people want to be on teams that prioritize moving FAST. The antithesis of this is a bloated GTM org that values head count above all.

7. You can be profitable (at the expense of being predictable)

Running a lean team that captures demand at the bottom of the funnel (and most importantly resists the temptation to layer on outbound reps who will simply capture the demand that you are creating elsewhere) will increase focus on capital-efficiency, reduce system-wide dependence on external funding and create a less volatile ecosystem in the long-term. 

8. Massive user acquisition sets you up for global domination

Santosh always says that 100,000 users is the equivalent of a $1B/yr marketing budget. I have yet to successfully execute this pay, but we’re going to try to with our B2B person-level identity product. 

TAKEAWAY:

It’s almost 2024.

The Predictable Revenue model is dead.

The next great startups will sacrifice predictability for velocity (and profitability).

Not only is it what our buyers want from us, but...  

It will create healthier companies with happier employees.

Everything is setting up for this to be the next great go-to-market motion…

I'm calling it “Inbound-Led Outbound”.
