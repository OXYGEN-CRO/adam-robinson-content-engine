## Exact post text

I've sold a company for $10M+ and built my new company to $1.6M MRR.

Here are the 7 biggest lessons I've learned about business building:

1. Bootstrapping creates discipline 

You're forced to find product/market fit before you scale

You build a much stronger foundation when you bootstrap

2. Always have a few experiments running that can be 1000x-ers.

Companies stop growing because the founders settle 

But great CEOs are always looking for the next S-Curve - their next product that will take the business to the next level.

3. Decide to really go for it

When building my new startup, I decided in my mind that we were going to GO FOR IT

This may sound silly, but this change in mindset is probably the most important thing

It changes how you look at investments related to brand, events, & visibility

4. Create content

Document what you're doing and learning

If you're transparent and thoughtful, incredible people will follow along and even join you in the journey

5. Attend events

I attended every relevant DTC/ecom marketing even I possibly could

Every event or conference gave me at least one connection that's been worth 7-figures to my company

6. Spend aggressively, but reallocate quickly

I'm aggressive with spending money on growth

When something looks like it might work, I'm not afraid to pour $50k into it

But as soon as the data says it's not working, cut it quickly and reallocate to something else

7. Keep making your core product better.

In the long run, this is the #1 most important thing

Having a great product is a cheat code that makes every other part of running the business easy
