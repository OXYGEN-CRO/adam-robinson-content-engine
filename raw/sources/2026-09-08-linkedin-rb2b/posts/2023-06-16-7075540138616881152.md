## Exact post text

Last week, I took Thursday and Friday off without telling anyone. That might seem crazy for someone who loudly committed to building a billion dollar company, but here’s the truth…

This is why I do it. 

Right here (see the cute pic).

For Emma, my beautiful daughter, and Helen, my loving wife.

Without them, none of this matters.

Recently, someone asked me, “Why don’t you retire?”

“Why are you so focused on building a BILLION DOLLAR company?.”

“Why don’t you build a little lifestyle business or travel the world posting pics to Instagram?”.

“You have the money,” they said.

But that’s not the point. 

That’s not what life’s about.

Life is about the pursuit of self transformation. The greatest driver of satisfaction is becoming more than you were yesterday.

Building a billion dollar company is a MASSIVE goal.

Some might say it’s insane.

But regardless of the outcome, the journey will require everything I have, and at the end of it, I will be satisfied that I chose a path that required so much of me.

That’s the precedent I want to set for my daughter.

The bigger the adventure and the bigger the struggle, the more you can grow and transform. And ultimately, you will have derived more from the brief time you have on this planet.

My game is building startups, and ten years in I’m trying to hit it as BIG as possible. 

But I will never forget, the most important part of achieving anything great, is sharing life with those you love most.
