## Exact post text

Out of all Ronak Shah’s accomplishments (including having built a DTC brand to $40M in revenue in 3 years), the thing I was most impressed with was his insane network.

After chatting with him for an hour, I gathered 5 traits that he excels in — and if you’re trying to build a powerful network, you need to do these too:

1. Work. In. Public.

It’s the title of one of my YouTube series for a reason.

Working in public is a selfless way to teach people about what you’re working on, so that others can use it to help themselves.

Tweets, blogs, LinkedIn posts, or YouTube videos will document what you’re doing while you’re doing it.

It inspires, and it teaches.

People need to know that you’re working on something in order for them to pay attention and be interested in you. Which leads us to #2…

2. Post regularly.

If you’re gonna work in public, be consistent about it.

Ron posts multiple times a day about the things that are working for Obvi, and he only tweets things that he believes will teach people something.

Even just 3 posts a day (which can be repurposed across every platform) is sufficient to leave an impression.

3. Dedicate time to jamming freely with people.

This is one thing that I have a specific block on my calendar for.

I absolutely love talking to entrepreneurs who own businesses similar to mine so we can swap stories and tactics on growth.

Always be open to connect and speak freely about things you’re both passionate about.

4. Send opportunities to others (for free).

When you get to a certain level in an industry, you have people asking you for recommendations on agencies, freelancers, experts in XYZ field, etc.

Just because you can ask for a commission/referral fee doesn’t mean that you should.

Having people owe you a favor down the road is much more powerful than a few hundred dollars that you’d get for an intro email.

5. Collect connectors.

I’ve met people who aren’t rich, famous, or particularly talented at something, but they’re one of the most valuable people to have around because of who they know.

Connectors are people that you can go to and they have a guy or gal for everything.

Get in touch with 2-3 people who have a black book worth billions, and make sure you provide them with as much value as you possibly can.

The saying is cliche, but it’s true — your network is your net worth. And I hope these 5 tips help you build yours. Share this with a founder who might need it.
