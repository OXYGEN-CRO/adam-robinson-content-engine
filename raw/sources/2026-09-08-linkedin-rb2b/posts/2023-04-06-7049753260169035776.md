## Exact post text

Back when I had a moment to think and breathe (I currently do not), I used to journal a lot.

It really does help to see your thoughts on paper.

If you’re ever feeling anxious, it’ll make you realize that you’re probably just overthinking.

If you’re feeling really happy, you can write down *why* you feel happy so you can recreate that feeling every day.

Write down everything that you’re grateful for, and it’ll give you a little dopamine rush when you need it.

Do you journal?
