## Exact post text

Me at 25 years old:

- Wall Street Trader
- Single and actively dating anyone and everyone 
- Living in a tiny box in downtown Manhattan
- 5:15am alarm to get to the trading floor at 5:45am
- Never took more than a 3-day weekend for vacation
- Obsessed with making money and living the fast life
- Major partying habit, drank every night, weekend warrior
- Out of shape, under-slept

Met at 42 years old:

- 2nd time entrepreneur
- Happily married with a 10mo old daughter 
- Living in Austin, TX in a charming house
- Haven’t set an alarm in 10 years 
- Taking 2 one month trips with the family this year 
- Obsessed with the journey, happy with whatever outcome 
- Completely sober for 3 years
- Work out 6 days per week

What changed?

I learned a few things about myself...

Building a family and a business is what drives me. 

Great partnerships matter (at work and at home).  

I get way more out of life as a sober person. 

Raising a child is magical.

And so is not setting a 5am alarm.
