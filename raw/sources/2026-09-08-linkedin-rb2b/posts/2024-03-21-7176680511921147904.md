## Exact post text

In 2023, I spent $10,785 on PR Newswire to push press releases out about scaling Retention.com. It was a mistake. Here’s the unfortunate truth about bootstrapped startups and PR:

It doesn’t work!

The traditional top-of-funnel PR machine is expensive, inefficient, un-engaging, and for a startup, it’s very, very hard to see ROI. 

BACKGROUND:

Last year we pushed a handful of articles to PR newswire.

We didn’t have much to say, so we announced things like: 

- big hires
- a rebrand
- a docuseries

But these articles were just SOO boring. 

It was a perfect example of a big trap startup founders fall into:

Doing things that you THINK you need to be doing but you’re not sure why.

Most of the time it’s driven by watching what others are doing and copying them - without understanding why they are doing it (or if it's even working).

I have 3 key issues with strategies like this:

1. Pushing something out that you know sucks over a channel that you know won’t do anything for you because you feel like you have to is very “old way”

2. It’s a very NOT-human way to (attempt to) build brand 

3. You aren’t fostering any type of two-way engagement or community building 

I think traditional PR as a brand building strategy is completely dead, and the world is set up for startups to build FAR MORE trust in a much faster, capital efficient way. 

How? 

By becoming an “influencer” and crushing the organic social media game. 

Here’s why organic social absolutely crushes PR: 

- People are on social media all day every day (more than any one publication)
- People are on social media to connect with other people
- You can form ACTUAL RELATIONSHIPS through creating great content
- You can widen the attack surface by building up several internal influencers
- You build HIGHLY VALUABLE community through dialogue in the comments
- You have a GREAT feedback loop - you can see what hits and iterate on it

The future of B2B sales is Human-to-Human. 

Traditional “push” PR efforts like PR Newswire and paying $2k to be a Forbes contributor are losing ground to the far more engaging world of storytelling on organic social media. 

Your prospects want a relationship with YOU.

Not your company.

Not your 22-year-old SDR.

You can build that relationship by making yourself an “influencer”, drawing people in with your content, and engaging with them in the comments section. 

It’s a push vs pull model for PR and brand building. 

In 2024, pull wins
