## Exact post text

I posted our entire detailed financial history on LinkedIn 1 week ago — and it’s kind of mind blowing that I’m sharing this much of our financials publicly.

But here’s another in-depth recap of some of the crazy numbers Retention.com did in January:

→ $171,950 worth of new MRR (see below)
→ 101 annual deals closed won, $20k ACV
→ 8 deals closed lost
→ 9 out of 71 haven't rolled (we actually think the denominator will be much higher and the cancellation rate lower) - it's a reporting issue, I will update you
→ Our current sales cycle = 7 days

It wasn’t all positive, though…

On a negative note, we did see $83k of contraction from large monthly customers after the holiday season

Leaving us with a net of $88k in MRR growth.

I do want to clarify one note on the Baremetrics graph:

None of our revenue that is in onboarding hits Baremetrics. There is currently $120k MRR in onboarding. If you want to handicap that by our "roll" rate and get an accurate picture of MRR, add 103k to the 1.2 million. (120k * 86% = 103k).

We're at 15.6m ARR right now.

This was inconsistent with the last financials I showed because Baremetrics was even less accurate than it is today — I was guessing at that point.

We are working on a Tableau instance that will give us much cleaner revenue and churn reporting.

Going into February, I believe our single biggest opportunity to keep this momentum going is dialing in our BDR team.

Why?

Simple. Our partner channels — agency and affiliate — has unbelievable pent-up demand for our new key hires to hit the ground running. It’s going to be fun.

And one last thing:

I’m looking to crowd source what’s next for Retention.com

Do you have any good ideas for us? Would love to hear them in the comments or in my DMs.
