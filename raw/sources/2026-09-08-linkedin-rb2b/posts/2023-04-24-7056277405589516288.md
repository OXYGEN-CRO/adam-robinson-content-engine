We recently attended ShopTalk — where we had a booth. Those things are pricy.

In total, we spent just about $250k. Here’s the breakdown:

 • $81k for the space and the meeting
 • $70k for the booth
 • 13 people total (executives, partnerships team, etc) cost us $75k
 • A handful of other smaller events at $5-10k each

Only 8 days after, we’d already closed $41k in ARR.

Wait… what?

This may not sound great, but considering our normal sales cycle, it was. It’s only 8 days later, and those are only leads from the booth.

Of our 116 scans, we plan to convert 30-40% of them.

That’ll put us at a 4-5X ROI on our initial cost.

All of the leads from other things are still trickling in as we speak.
