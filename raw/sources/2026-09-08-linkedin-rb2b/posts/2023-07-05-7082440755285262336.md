## Exact post text

I’ve spent the last 10 years in email marketing and I often find myself giving the same advice, over and over again.

Here are 7 dead-simple ways to make more $$$ from email marketing:

1) Only have ONE purpose for the email

2) Only have ONE thing someone can click (no distractions)

3) Make what is clickable much, much larger. Like 5-10x larger. Trust me

4) Use a large, aggressive CTA on that button

5) Send higher discounts to unengaged segment and lower discounts to engaged segment

6) Resend the email to your non-openers 24hrs later

7) And if the first 6 strategies didn’t work, run through the checklist again

Use these tips to get ahead of your competition.

What would you add to the list? 

Let me know 👇
