## Exact post text

In 44 months, I grew my startup from $0-$20m ARR. Here are the 8 biggest mistakes I made along the way (and the revenue levels where I made them):

1. We sold to anyone who showed up at our doorstep

Revenue Level: 0-$12M ARR

Not focusing on an ICP and running a self-serve app was the story of our first 2.5 years. I didn’t realize how wrong this was until later. Now I know we would have gone *farther, faster* if we sold only to big Shopify stores.

2. We wasted 6 months and $300k working towards a sale that didn’t happen

Revenue Level: $5-$8M ARR

We attempted to sell the entire business in late 2021. Such a waste of time and money. Thankfully we didn’t sell. We’re much more valuable now.

3. I got major FOMO
	
Revenue Level: $14-$17m ARR

I felt like if we didn’t go as fast as humanly possible we were going to miss the opportunity. This led to panicked hiring, a horrible corporate spending culture, and a bunch of wasted time and money trying to “be everywhere”.

4. I thought our sales and onboarding was easier and less complex than it was

Revenue Level: $12-$18m ARR

We thought we could have inexperienced AEs closing 5 deals per day with a 7 day sales cycle and rush people through onboarding. As you can imagine, that wasn't true! We needed alignment on expectations and what success looks like.

5. I set a FY23 revenue target that was 50% too high
	
Revenue Level: $13-$18m ARR

My idea of “We have to get to $50m” led to over-hiring before we truly had a repeatable sales model. Empty AE calendars led to turning everyone to hunters, which didn't work (because most demand is created elsewhere in 2023). Then our VP sales resigned and we shrunk from 15 to 4 reps.

6. I assumed other vendors’ playbooks would work for us

Revenue Level: $12-$18m ARR

Because Klaviyo and Yotpo had a large agency partnership sales motion, I thought we needed one too. We have a great opportunity in the partnerships space, but it doesn’t look like either of their motions. We had to figure out what works for us.

7. We created a $5k/mo unlimited pricing tier
	
Revenue Level: $12-$19m ARR

This just didn’t work to capture mid-mkt market share. Pricing is relatively less important to those companies. What it did create was existing customers contracting down to 5k, capped our upside, made overage impossible to capture, and upgrading non-existent.

8. We oversold people and didn’t realize it for 5 months

Revenue Level: $12-16m ARR

This was my fault for not seeing it coming. The consequences were horrible. It created a euphoria about the opportunity that led to over-hiring and overspending, then on the back-end led to a massive wave of dissatisfaction, contraction, and opt-outs at 60 days. We're doing the opposite now.

TAKEAWAY:

Growing a startup is hard.

I hope I don’t make these mistakes again. 

What I do know is that I’ll continue to make mistakes...

Just new and different ones!

Here's the trick:

Make sure you’re doing 9 things right for every 8 you do wrong.
