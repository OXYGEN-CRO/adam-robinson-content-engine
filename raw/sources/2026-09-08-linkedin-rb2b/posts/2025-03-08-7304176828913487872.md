## Exact post text

I’ve written 100+ viral Linkedin posts. That means over 1,000 likes or 100,000 views. Everyone thinks it’s because I sh*tpost about 6Sense or brag about how much money we make. They’re wrong. Here’s how you can unlock LinkedIn virality for your ICP: 

You MUST find your “Prolific Zone”.

What’s the story behind the Prolific Zone? 

I can explain it in three easy steps. 

Step 1: Don’t be mainstream.

Mainstream, down-the-middle content is just noise, ESPECIALLY coming from a small voice. You’ll never break through.

Step 2: Don’t be crazy. 

If you are TOO far out to the right or left of any issue, you won’t be taken seriously. Don’t sound crazy.

Step 3: Find your PROLIFIC ZONE.

Just right or left of middle, but not TOO far out, lies your prolific zone. It’s not crazy, but it just far enough out there where one side LOVES it and the other side HATES it… meaning it’s POLARIZING.

The beautiful thing about social media is that people on both sides engage with prolific content.

Not only do they let YOU know how they feel, they let EACH OTHER know how they feel. 

The best prolific zones are true, authentic to you, and out there enough to bring in both sides to the discussion… 

And they ultimately become your content pillars.

Here were a few of my prolific zones of 2024:  

1. The Predictable Revenue Model is dead 
2. Bootstrapping $10m ARR is the new unicorn 
3. I spend 60% of my time (as CEO) producing content

For 2025… I have some ideas, but TBD.

TAKEAWAY

If you learn one thing from this post, it is to NEVER post anything mainstream. 

Don’t waste your time. It ain’t even worth it. 

And a second thing - you’re going to have haters if you do it the right way. 

Here’s my advice if you’re trying to become "the next Adam Robinson":

(Yes, I'm talking in the 3rd person, but I get this exact question a lot)

IF you’re going to try to play this social game, you have to try to be the best. 

Because if you don’t try to be the best, you won’t even be good. 

In order to be the best, you must find your Prolific Zone. 

P.S.

Who of you out there has found their Prolific Zone?

LMK what it is in the comments.

We’re all looking for inspo.
