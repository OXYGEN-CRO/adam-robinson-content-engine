## Exact post text

I expect to get 4-5X ROI out of our $250K investment into ShopTalk a few weeks ago.

But the biggest driver of that success was NOT the main conference — it was all of the private, more intimate events that took place that same week. We held…

 • A private dinner with Yotpo.
 • A happy hour with We Make Websites where we sponsored a cabana with them.
 • We were the unofficial sponsor of the Accenture Stables.
 • We co-sponsored Racetrack Day with Parker.
 • We did a mini-event with Triple Whale 🐳.

The standout event was Racetrack Day.

People were begging to be on the waitlist for that. So fun.

Same with the We Make Websites cabana.

There were more people in the cabana with us than on the show floor.

It was crazy. But why put so much focus on this?

Simple. For a company at our stage, we want to be literally everywhere. We need to be active in our community, building real relationships.

We need to flood the market and have our name everywhere that the eyeballs of our target customers are going.

So, the lesson?

If you’re planning for a trade show, make sure you put just as much effort (if not more) into the smaller, private events during that same week.

You might be surprised by the ROI.
