## Exact post text

Thrilled to announce my co-founder Diana Ross (who has gone $0-10M ARR as the ONLY rep 2x) has now joined RB2B full time to build our full GTM motion. 

If you’ve been following the journey, we went $0-$4m ARR in 40 weeks, but we’ve hit a wall for four reasons, all related to spiking churn: 

1. Our product is unusable for our ICP (fixing this now)
2. We have tons of competitors who have different (confusing) angles
3. We have been selling leads and demos, which is wrong/doesn’t work
4. We are not telling customers EXACTLY what to do and how to use it

Here’s our LIVE SaaS metrics: https://lnkd.in/gTh6_8_S

At GetEmails, we hit a wall at month 9 and didn’t break through until month 18.

Our churn was actually far worse (which is hard to believe).

If you ask Diana what major change happened in month 18, she’d say: 

“We stopped letting the market decide how to use our product, took complete control over what to do and how to implement, and coached on evaluation.”

What was our revenue at GetEmails (now Retention.com) over that period?  

Month 9: 200k MRR
Month 18: 230k MRR
Month 27: 620k MRR

This is why I’m excited to bring Diana on board. 

We think we know exactly what our problem is, and how to solve it. 

Will we be stuck for 9 months again? 

Will we break out sooner? 

Only time will tell… 

But you get to watch the whole thing unfold, right here, on LinkedIn. 

And no matter what happens… 

We’re going to just keep building.
