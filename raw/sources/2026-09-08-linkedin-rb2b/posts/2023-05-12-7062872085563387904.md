## Exact post text

If everyone isn’t acting with integrity at all times, that’s a red flag. 

Integrity as a core value in your company is an absolute MUST-HAVE. From the people who are on your team to the partners and clients that you team up with.

There’s nothing worse for a company that is operating with velocity to be wasting time on contract negotiations or new hires.

So how do you foster integrity as a core value in your company? 
✅ Lead by example.
✅ Talk about integrity and keep it top of mind in your culture.
✅ Fire fast. If there’s any doubt, there’s no doubt. If someone doesn’t have IT, move on.

Any other necessary core values?
