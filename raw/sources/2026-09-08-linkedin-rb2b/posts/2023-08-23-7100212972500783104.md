How you see my life: founder, $20M run rate, beautiful family.

How I see my life:

- Constantly struggling between choices with MAJOR downsides

- Feeling like I have no time, yet more to-dos keeps showing up

- Knowing I shouldn't be “doing” much as CEO, but “doing” everything 

- Dreading travel, because it’s exhausting, and I’m just going to have to do the stuff I didn’t do during the week over the weekend

- Constantly being told that we need to slow down, have roll-out plans, keep everyone informed of everything going on… but feeling like we can’t afford to actually do that

The life of a founder is hard.

No matter what your run rate is.

Take joy in the little things.
