## Exact post text

Transparency matters.

Today’s video is a little different…

Instead of showing you what we’re building, this video highlights how we handle user data, how we operate from the inside out, and exactly who we are at Retention.

What We Are Not Doing:

#1 We are not taking a customer’s web traffic data and giving it or selling it to another customer.
#2 We are not taking a customer’s email address and giving it or selling it to another customer.
#3 We are not giving any third-party identities to customers through any type of lookalike audiences.

What We Are Doing:

#1 We are only enriching first-party identities of website visitors.
#2 We are adding additional contextual insights to this first-party data.
#3 We are being incredibly judicious with categorizing an email as deliverable.

The most important thing for our brand? Maintaining the highest possible standard of email deliverability. 

So what exactly are we doing to deliver for clients?

Check out the video below for an inside look at the details behind Retention’s promises to our clients and the consent factors we take into account.
