## Exact post text

I bootstrapped RB2B from $0-1m in 16 weeks. I'm going to get it to $10m ARR in the next 9 months (with only 6 employees). Here’s my 7-step playbook to make RB2B an 8-figure product with a tiny team:

1. LinkedIn Thought Leadership Ads with Alex Fine🌲 and Ali Yildirim🌲

- LinkedIn has been the entire go-to-market so far, we want to maximize
- I believe TL ads are a superpower if you can create great organic content
- We’re trying to double our weekly organic LinkedIn impressions this year

2. RB2B Creator Program 

- B2C understands the power of systematically harnessing UGC/organic social
- Bruno at Clay has built a program that Pete hopes to successfully emulate
- Notion was the first to absolutely crush at this

3. Competitor, Integrations, Alternatives and Product Review SEO/Landing Pages

- Clay works with Nick, so we’re excited about emulating them there, too! 
- We’re taking the “use us too” angle - there’s 0 overlap between us and others
- Great content will come out of showing how we retarget visitors to these landing pages

4. Perfect the Inbound-Led Outbound Workshop

- Christopher Merrill made Santosh and I realize we don’t understand what a “good show” is
- We will nail promo, prep, run-of-show, and follow-up, and put on a better show for more ppl

5. Add a Two-Touch Sales Motion

- Ilija started July 1, focusing on larger customers who didn’t convert, annual upsells

6. Influencer Content Creation

- We want content about how sales reps would use RB2B w/ other AMB tools
- How to do the research from pageview history, personalize outreach, etc

7. Cold Email “The New Way” 

- Can’t forget Taylor Haren, the silent killer

TAKEAWAY:

Startups are hard. 

$0-$1M is the hardest. 

Each of the 3 times I’ve done it the playbook has looked totally different.

Why? 

Because the world looked different. 

Not only that… 

I have been different. 

Each time I have the collective experience and wisdom from the times before. 

That experience doesn’t help me avoid mistakes or make bad decisions … 

It DOES help me evaluate opportunities worth pursuing and pivot quickly. 

Why am saying all this?

Because there's never been a better time to start and scale a SaaS.

Each day that goes by there is more demand for software, and more tools to make launching products easier.

If you’re struggling... 

Just know that every successful entrepreneur before you struggled too.

Most for a VERY long time.

You just have to keep going. 

Continue to accumulate the experience that will allow you to achieve escape velocity in the future. 

Keep building.
