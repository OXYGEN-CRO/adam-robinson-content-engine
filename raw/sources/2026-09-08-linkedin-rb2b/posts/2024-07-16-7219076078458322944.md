## Exact post text

Last week Mark Kosoglow BELITTLED me for having not reached $25m ARR (bootstrapped, as Founder/CEO), stating that he added $25m per quarter at Outreach ($500m raised, as VP sales). Here’s four reasons why Mark should start a SaaS from $0 before running his mouth: 


1. Being a Founder/CEO is the hardest job there is.

I deeply respect Mark’s skill as a builder of sales teams, and he’s reached “big boy” ARR numbers that I can only dream about. 

Having said that, turning an idea into a viable business - being both the product and the go-to-market - is an entirely different game.

Just ask Anthony Kennada who has talked openly about the steep learning curve going from unicorn CMO to CEO. 


2. Going from $0-1m (as a Founder/CEO) is the most difficult job of all. 

As the old SaaS adage goes: 

0-1        =  Impossible
1-10      =  Improbable 
10-100  =  Inevitable

Ask anyone who has done it (as the Founder/CEO), they’ll agree with you.

And I’ve gone $0-1m 3x times.

Most recently in 16 weeks with RB2B.


3. Bootstrapping $10m SaaS revenue is so difficult no one ever does it. 

Mark said he went from $1-10 in 27mo and only spent $1.5m.

Then he asked me what I spent.

Are you ready to hear something SHOCKING? 

We went $1-10m in 27 months at GetEmails (now Retention.com)…

…and we PAID $5M IN DIVIDENDS to our founders getting there. 

If you’re in SaaS, you’re shaking your head and asking yourself, “how is that possible?"

I will refer you to the RB2B public Baremetrics dashboard. 

It is happening again, right before your eyes.


4. We WILL get to those “big boy” ARR numbers someday.

Here’s our closing ARR numbers, by year: 

Nov ‘19 Launch
2020: $2m
2021: $5m
2022: $13m
2023: $22m
Current: $23m 

You may not like how the line flattened out. 

I don’t either. 

We got to $22m in Q3 of last year, then a lot changed.

We shrank in Q4 and Q1 of this year.

I wrote all about it on LinkedIn. 

It was terrible, and writing about it was even worse.

(I’d love to see Outreach’s revenue growth between Q323 and now… My guess is they probably shrank too.)

Santosh and I are confident that our B2B products will get us to 9-figure ARR. 

And unlike Outreach, we won’t take a single VC dollar to get there.


TAKEAWAY:

Mark was responsible for building one of the most prolific sales orgs in SaaS.

But he doesn’t know: 

1. What it’s like to be a Founder/CEO
2. How hard it is to bootstrap $23m
3. What it feels like to go $0-1m

Mark claimed they added more ARR in a quarter than I’ve ever generated. 

But then I looked on Crunchbase and saw Outreach raised around $500m. 

I heard at their peak Outreach had $250m ARR. 

I’m left with one HUGE question about Mark… 

Is he just a MASTER at selling 2 dollar bills for a dollar???
