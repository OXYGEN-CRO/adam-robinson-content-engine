## Exact post text

In June, we downsized from 5 to 3 FTE (40%), replaced 2 FTEs with a $99/mo AI clone, and grew 4.9% to $5.5m ARR. AI agents are now handling 90+% of sales AND support at RB2B. Here’s the good, the bad, and the ugly from June:

THE GOOD:

- Our monthly churn rate is around 7%. That sounds horrific to some of you, but it’s down from 15% at Thanksgiving
- Our Fin customer support agent is resolving 66% of all chat requests with no human interaction, up from 49% in May
- Our “Adam Clone” handled 1,700 inbound requests over the past two weeks - demos, SOC2 docs, pricing, product tours, GDPR, etc
- We’ve been reading and improving the responses - it’s getting good!
- We’ve solved a ton of product issues that have led to better experience

THE BAD:

- I haven’t talked to customers in 15mo because I was too busy building my brand and chasing dopamine hits from LinkedIn
- I became everything I used to hate about “influencers”, I lost touch and our product suffered badly
- To rectify this, I created a sequence that gets me on calls every Wednesday with 12 tier 1 customers
- I’m going through all resolved support and sales AI chats to make sure our AIs gave the best possible response… which gets me closer to customers
- I’ve kicked off a churn research project with the help of Jonathon S.
- While the product has improved, we need a transformation, which is coming
- I fear our 7% churn rate isn’t “real”… it’s seasonality and will go back up to 10%

THE UGLY: 

- Downsizing is terrible, if anybody is looking for AE or fractional growth, DM me
- It’s somewhat jarring how effective the AI is in handling inbound sales, in a “what does this mean for all of us” kind of way

TAKEAWAY:

Q3 and Q4 will be transformational for us. 

1. AI agents are now successfully handling 90%+ of our sales and support

2. Soon AI agents will be solving the core filtering, routing, and outreach issues inside our product

3. I’m in direct contact with dozens and indirect contact with 100s of customers every week

4. We’re picking cold email, ads, and SEO back up in Q3 and Q4

I’ve said it before and I’ll say it again…

$10m ARR is the FU money of SaaS. 

If Tate, Robb, and I can just maintain this 5% growth rate for the next 12mo, we’ll hit it by July 15 of next year.

Now I admit: 

- That’s not as exciting as selling for $80m after 6mo (Base 44)
- It’s not as exciting as adding $1m ARR per month (Fyxer/HeyReach)
- It’s not as exciting as being worth $9b (Cursor)

But here’s the hard truth:

While other founders are raising Series A to hit $10m ARR with 60+ employees, we’re doing it with three humans and a bunch of AI agents.

In front of you, with wild transparency, right here on LinkedIn.

Most of you reading this are building SaaS the wrong way in 2025.

You’re over-hiring, over-complicating, over-raising.

Prove me wrong.

P.S.

Want to see the UNCENSORED internal revenue dashboards of a $5M ARR SaaS? Comment “metrics” and I’ll send you access to our full metrics dashboard.
