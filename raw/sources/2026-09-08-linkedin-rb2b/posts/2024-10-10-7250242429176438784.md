I’m proud to say we closed Sep at $24m ARR (+2.2%) with a team of just 40 FTE! Here’s the good, the bad, and the ugly that went down in September - and why I think our growth is about to SPEED UP:

Retention.com:

RB2B:

- We're going to experiment with value stacking coaching, courses, and RB2B
- I recorded four courses in a studio before my paternity leave, which I’m violating right now writing this
- MRR keeps growing - 7mo in we’re at $260k
- Diana is meeting with Ilija every day to optimize our sales model

_______________


A month ago, I STILL felt like we were navigating rough waters. 

Somehow today - just 30 days later - I’ve never been more excited about what’s ahead.

I’ve been at this for over a decade, have a profitable, mid-20’s ARR business …

And my outlook on the future is STILL changing by the month.

This is one of the biggest reasons I champion bootstrapping. 

Capital adds risk to an already INCREDIBLY uncertain situation.

Profitability, on the other hand, helps you weather ANY storm.
