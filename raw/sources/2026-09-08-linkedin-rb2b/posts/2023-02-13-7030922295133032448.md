I want to share how I’m feeling today. More and more, I believe that anything is possible.

Just by observing exceptional people around me, I realized that so much more was possible for me and my journey.

Dave Rogenmoser 🚀 made me realize I could create a unicorn in just 1 year.

Santosh Sharan made me realize that by changing a few small things I could play so much bigger.

Klaviyo made me realize that you can create a monopoly even when a monopoly already exists (Mailchimp).

Triple Whale 🐳 made me realize how quickly I could build an online presence from scratch and create community at an early stage.

Allan Levy made me realize we could create GetEmails.

Tate Blahnik, over and over again, has made me realize that we can build anything we want to, faster than anyone thought possible.

Diana Ross and Keleigh Spinner made me realize that the right person in the right role can do the job of 10.

Anne Vickman made me realize that some people don't have it in them to do less than a great job.

Tony Hsai made me realize that I could create companies that people would love working for.

Anthony Robinson and Josh Abramson made me realize I could be an entrepreneur.

Helen Sharp made me realize how important having an amazing, supportive partner is.

Emma Robinson made me realize how meaningful being a father is.

My parents made me realize that I could do anything I set my mind to.

The thing I’m most excited about is that more and more incredible people are coming into my life at a faster and faster pace.

It’s compounding.

And I can’t wait to see what I think is possible in 1 year, 5 years, 10 years.
