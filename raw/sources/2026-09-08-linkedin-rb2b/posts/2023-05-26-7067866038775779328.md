## Exact post text

Retention.com wasn’t always what it is today.

I was the Founder/CEO of a failing company for years before we were on the road to a billion.

I worked on Robly. I was competing with giants. I couldn’t keep up…

We were barely profitable, and I was lucky to be able to get rid of it.

I then began working on GetEmails (our former name). It was doing better than Robly, but it still felt stagnant.

It was an absolute grind to get it to where I wanted it to be, and I wanted to get rid of this company, too.

I just wanted to sell it for whatever I could get, have an early retirement, and work on passion projects without caring if they made money or not.

That was only until I began hanging out with Dave Rogenmoser, founder of Jasper.ai.

You’ll remember that we had a long conversation about the future of his company, how they were on a path to going public, and how they were insanely profitable.

It lit a fire in me that actually changed my life.

I knew that if I had one good chance at a moonshot, it was with Retention

I was determined to hit a $1B.

And among all of the things that I changed to get us from $10M ARR to $20M ARR in less than a year…

This sales deck was one of the most important parts.

Everything changed.

The product suite, the offer structure, the price, the guarantee, etc.

All of these were crucial to getting us over the hump.

We went from a stagnant, painful business – to a rocket ship.

And I’m showing you everything as we scale this thing all the way to $1B in my new series, “Billion Dollar Challenge.”

Episode 1 is out now. I hold nothing back. I’ll drop the episode in the comments for you to watch.
