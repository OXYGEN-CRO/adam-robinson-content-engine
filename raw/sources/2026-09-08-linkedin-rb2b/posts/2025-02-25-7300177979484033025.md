## Exact post text

On November 22nd, I declared RB2B dead. I cut $100k/mo in marketing spend (cold email, ads, UGC, SEO). Then in January we grew 10% to $4.5m ARR. Here are the 5 changes we made that led to our biggest ARR gains yet:

1. Cut all marketing spend - over $100k/mo

I went from thinking we had PMF in July to thinking we absolutely did NOT in November. When you fall out of PMF, there is only one thing to do - get as small as humanly possible, and find a new PMF.

2. Brought in Diana Ross to help with sales, onboarding, and enablement

Diana has been a part of two journeys to $10m ARR as the only rep (once with me), and her contribution was far beyond just selling. We have a hypothesis that this and other signals can be filtered by accounts in the CRM, they can trigger workflows in the CRM, and sales and marketing teams would rather action these signals from there than build in another platform.

We realized the workflows we sold last year were either put a human in front of a noisy Slack channel, or run leads through Clay as an ICP filter and shoot them out the SPAM cannon.

Slack is hard to action. Clay --> Instantly/Smartlead works GREAT if you’ve got a ton of traffic and a large TAM. But... ABM? Forget about it.

3. We stopped selling “leads” and “demos” as success

I got this REALLY wrong. Traffic is not leads. Demos are not the only measure of success of knowing a named account is on a high intent page. Orienting people towards that in the beginning led to disappointment.

4. We rebuilt our Hubspot integration

We realized in this new world, we needed to integrate w/ Hubspot CRM (not Marketing), we needed to add more fields, and most importantly, we needed to improve our documentation to show people how to alert reps, add to lead scores, trigger automations, all from site visitors, all in Hubspot.

Do you have a sales team that would like to know when a named account is on a high intent page and trigger HS workflows? DM me.

5. We built a Salesforce integration

Similar to #4, we believe that teams who use Salesforce CRM want to have their CRM filter the signal and reps alerted when a named account is on a high-intent page. And that signal can trigger workflows in the CRM.

Not just the first time… Every time the account comes back.

DM me if you want to route the signal into your CRM and alert your reps of a named account on a high intent page… and/or trigger automated outreach.

TAKEAWAY:

If I look back at what we did in Dec and Jan, I still don’t understand why we grew so much.

Maybe it was just that churn took a breather.

But if that’s not the case…

And the Startup Gods allow us to be correct about our CRM hypothesis, that…

1. Marketers want their reps to know when a named account is on a high-intent page
2. They don’t want to have to adopt another platform to build out workflows you can build in the CRM

...we might actually be able to get this thing moving again!

Here’s where I turn to you LinkedIn:

Is that hypothesis crazy, or not?
