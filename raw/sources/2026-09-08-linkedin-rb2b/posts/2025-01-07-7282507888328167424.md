## Exact post text

In the past 24 months, I’ve spoken to hundreds of $0-1M ARR startup founders and 99% of them are STUCK. Here are the 5 biggest mistakes they're making that prevent them from finding Product-Market Fit:

1. They can’t clearly articulate what they do. 

This seems pretty basic, but under 1% of founders I speak to have nailed this. Why is it so important? A clear one-liner is what you use to get people to talk to you, and conversations (customer discovery) is what gets you from $0-$1.

2. They can’t clearly articulate who they’re for. 

“Small businesses” isn’t good enough. If you can’t tell me the age, gender, geography, income level, habits, and places these people hang out (both digital and IRL), you will get nowhere. 

3. They can’t articulate why someone would pick them instead of someone else.

Most founders are either not different enough or they are building something that if it works will get copied in a nanosecond (I’m guilty here too). Few seek difficult-to-build opportunities or businesses with true network effects.

4. They are trying to do growth hacking way too early.

This is the most common problem I see. “Adam, can I talk to your cold email guy?” or “Can you connect me with your paid ads agency?” from people with less than $1m ARR. You're not there yet.

5. They’re trying to do too many things for too many people. 

Unless your name is Parker Conrad, niching down is the only way to get something from $0-$1m. Be like Nathan Barry and create an Email Newsletter App for Food Bloggers to start. Then slowly expand wider and wider.

TAKEAWAY:

Product-market fit is very difficult to achieve. 

Most startup founders will never get there. 

But here’s the crazy thing… 

The reason most founders won’t get there isn’t because: 

- They aren’t smart enough
- They don’t have a good enough team
- They don’t know enough about sales and marketing

Most founders won’t get to $1m ARR because they aren’t doing the horribly manual customer discovery process that going $0-$1m requires. 

I want to help.

We built a new private community for founders around these very simple ideas. 

We’re helping founders save months (if not years) on their journey to PMF by helping them refine their messaging and enter a true customer discovery loop.
