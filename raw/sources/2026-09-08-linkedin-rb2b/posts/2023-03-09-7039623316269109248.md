## Exact post text

My roommates in Manhattan started Vimeo out of our apartment…

I was always very captivated by their lives and I wanted to be an entrepreneur in tech, but at the time I didn’t even really know what that meant.

After the financial crisis (which by the way ‘The Big Short’ was literally a movie about the job I had) I took off on this long and winding journey to do what my roommates were doing…

The first stage of it was building this small email service provider that basically attracted Konstant Kontact customers — a really hard market with a customer base that was hard to build for. 

I spent YEARS trying to figure out what feature would make an email marketing and marketing automation application different from Mailchimp and Klaviyo. 

Then, during that process, I heard there was a possibility that you could, with a pixel, grab a deliverable email address from an anonymous website visitor. Which was a wild idea.

We added it to our ESP as a feature.

And something weird happened.

People would use the product, say they loved the product, but then put it in their own email marketing app without switching over.

It became clear we needed to make it its own company: GetEmails

Three years later, we rebranded to Retention.com.

We’re focused on DTC eCommerce, but we do a lot more than that email thing I just described. 

The majority of the value we create for brands is helping set up more triggered email flows than Klaviyo and then using our identity tools to send way more of those triggered emails out:

- Welcome series
- Cart abandonment
- Product abandonment

Anyways, what’s my point?

Your career won’t be a straight line, it might not even be moving forward all the time, so don’t get discouraged if it doesn’t go the way you thought it would.

I worked on Wall Street when the economy blew up in my face and then spent a decade over different email marketing companies chasing this elusive competitive advantage, all to no avail…

Until I finally landed on Retention.com… and now we’re on our way to $1B.

Keep going 💯
