## Exact post text

We scaled to our SaaS to $12M ARR in 2 years with a team of 6 highly-efficient people.

But, we ran into a big problem.

The main roadblock was that my co-founder Diana Ross was in charge of all things related to sales.

She is insanely talented and was able to close at a high % 

But she was really the only person who had any real information on how to make decisions that everything had to go through her.

Everything.

This slowed us down a ton.

The solution?

Hire more A+ players. Build systems to empower them to make decisions and move with speed. And get out of their way.

This also freed Diana up to work on other, higher-leverage tasks in the business.

The lesson? Delegate. Delegate. And delegate some more.

PS - follow me Adam Robinson for more insights as we scale Retention.com to $50M ARR.
