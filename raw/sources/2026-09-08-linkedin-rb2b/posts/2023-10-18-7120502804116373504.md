## Exact post text

Here's 6 reason why SDR response rates have plummeted in the last 12 months (and what you can do about it):

1. Inboxes are ruined

We have all responded to lower conversion rates by adding domains and inboxes (and sending far more volume). Any attempt you have made at low-volume personalization is drowned out by everyone else’s high-volume spamming.

2. LinkedIn is even worse

Between automation tools, outbound services attempting to execute “push” plays, and InMail, every prospecting LinkedIn message looks identical to the last.

3. Phones only work for certain TAMs

More people are working from home than ever. Carrier blocks on cell phones and certain TAMs that just don’t answer at all make cold calling 100x harder than 15 years ago.

4. Money isn’t free anymore

Every day that goes by where you can get 5%+ on your cash in a checking account makes the entire world increasingly question the need to purchase anything, software included.

5. Fundraising has effectively ground to a halt

Klaviyo IPO’d and there have been a handful of SaaS deals, but growth capital into companies scaling out teams and buying more software is nothing like it was 24 months ago.

6. The (now busted) Salesforce playbook hasn’t changed in over 10 years

100,000 SaaS companies are still trying to build an assembly line lead generation process when it’s not what the buyer wants and #1-5 render it completely useless.

So...

What can you do about it?

🎥 On today's episode of our docuseries BILLION DOLLAR CHALLENGE, I get into why we've leaned so heavily into LinkedIn content creation, the new tech we're quietly rolling out for B2B sales teams and the positive changes taking place in our core Shopify business.

Because here's the truth:

Response rates aren’t bouncing back anytime soon.

We need to build a new B2B sales model.

This is what I think it looks like:

- Tiny SDR teams with messages triggered on signal like social engagement, job change, website visits, tech deployment/removal, progression in partners’ CRMs, etc

- Partner and ecosystem plays drive indirect high-intent leads to your sales team

- Demand created over months (if not years) through founder-led and edutainment content that cuts through 

- High-Intent demand captured through an ultra-lean, super-experienced sales team

- Conversion rates > 60%, sales cycles cut by ⅔ 

- Your sales team only spends time talking to people who want to buy

The classic Salesforce playbook doesn't work anymore.

It's bad for business and it's bad for salespeople.

We need to design sales teams in a new way.

That's what we're doing at Retention.com

And I'm going to document every step.

Have any questions? Agree? Disagree?

AMA in the comments 👇
