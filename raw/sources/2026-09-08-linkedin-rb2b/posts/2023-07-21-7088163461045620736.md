## Exact post text

2016 Facebook Ads vs 2023 Meta Ads:

2016 Facebook Ads:
- Upload a static creative
- Run it for 2, 3, even 4 weeks
- High quality event data from the Facebook pixel
- Super low CPMs
- 40X ROAS, money rolling in, you feel like a genius

2023 Meta Ads:
- Creative has to be full-funnel
- Meta ONLY receives high quality events for purchasers
- Match rates are 10% or lower for PageView, Add to Cart, or Initiate Checkout
- CPM’s are through the roof
- You're ripping your hair out trying to squeak out a 2x ROAS, wondering why you chose this profession

What made brands rich in 2016...

Will stunt your growth in 2023.

Evolve or die.
