## Exact post text

WE ARE IN THE GREEN AGAIN - closed June ‘24 up 2.1% as a combined company. Retention.com at $21.8m ARR (+0.1%) and RB2B at $1.15m ARR (+123%). Here’s the good, bad, and ugly from June: 

THE GOOD: 

- We made new combined company ARR highs!!! GO TEAM!!!
- RB2B seems to be lightning in a bottle. We are all so grateful
- Our pricing struggle appears to be resolved
- RDATA (Santosh’s data sales biz) contributed $128k of closed revenue
- We continue to make great progress with our EOS rollout 
- We had a great executive offsite and have clear priorities
- We’re starting with our SOC2 audit process this quarter

THE BAD: 

- Santosh and I aren’t executing our weekly event very smoothly
- We need to be better at setting priorities for the entire org (not just execs)
- We’re still seeing churns on the Retention side that we feel like we could avoid
- We have SOOO much more to do on the growth side of RB2B
- We need to clean up a dead link problem in our database
- We are behind where we thought we’d be with feature development for RB2B

THE UGLY: 

- I will be on Clark Barron’s podcast - who called me out VERY publicly about the privacy implications of RB2B - some time in July, which I’m sure will get ugly 

TAKEAWAY

In April of this year we were down the 4th month in a row. 

And April was the BIGGEST down month (-2.3%).

We shrank from $22.1m to $21.2m (-$900k ARR).

The hardest part was that I had NO IDEA when the slide would end.  

Not only that - I had no idea if or when RB2B would start to ramp to give us some wind at our backs.

60 days later: 

1. Retention has added $500k ARR, 
2. RB2B has added $1.1m, and 
3. As a business we’re back at our highs again

What’s the lesson here? 

It’s always the darkest before dawn. 

You CAN get unstuck. 

Just keep throwing spaghetti against the wall. 

You DO have what it takes to create a bigger SaaS. 

You just have to keep building.
