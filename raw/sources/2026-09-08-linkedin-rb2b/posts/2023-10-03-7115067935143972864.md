## Exact post text

After a VERY hard Q2, things are feeling better at Retention.com. We grew ~9% this quarter (slower than I would have liked) and had big operational wins that are setting us up for a strong 2024. Here’s what I’m proud of (and not so proud of) that happened in Q3:

Proud of:

- We crossed the $20m ARR marker and we’re back to profitability (thank God)
- Santosh joined us full time on Sep 1. Big win!
- We identified a major problem in Q2 (overselling) that was leading to terrible downstream consequences (60-day contraction/opt-out, bad customer experience, etc). We fully reversed this problem through underselling, managing expectations, and creating alignment on what success looks like. 
- We made incredible progress on our B2B product. We should start charging our first customers in the next few weeks. Our resolution rate is fantastic (~40% of US visitors to a LinkedIn URL). If you want to know more, DM me! 📪
- We slightly increased our growth rate while downsizing our sales force
- We made a major tech improvement to one of our core products, Reclaim, that boosted performance by 15%, cut sends by 65%, and made the product 100% first-party opt-in.
- Our product and engineering teams are functioning extremely well, led by Anne Vickman and Michael Cottenden.
- We onboarded 4 engineers who are already making meaningful contributions to our product, added a product marketer and senior product designer, redesigned and launched our customer-facing dashboard, completed integrations with Attentive, Yotpo SMS Bump, and Listrak, and pushed around 60 other product improvements. GO TEAM!   

Not so proud of:

- We still don’t know some key business stats, but we have done our first round of tagging of active accounts for our analytics dashboard, so it’s not far away.
- An operational issue caused us to miss around *274 demos* over the last few months of people that were qualified when they filled out the form, and we routed them incorrectly. This is bad for our revenue and for the brand.
- We gave up on the $50m ARR by the end-of-year target that I was yelling about and held onto for WAY too long. It led to a lot of bad decisions and wasted money. We should end the year at $22-23m ARR, generating $500k/mo free cash, growing around 60% YoY. I’m not complaining, just not proud of that whole exercise.

Biggest Opportunities:

- I’m so excited about launching our B2B product I can barely sleep 
- We’re launching a free Shopify app that’s a “lite” version of our core product
- I think we can do some pretty amazing DemandGen for the B2B product using LinkedIn. My B2B posts have been doing well, Santosh opened up with a big one, Diana starts this week (go follow her)
- We’re re-working our entire outbound strategy for the DTC side
- We know exactly what we need to do for the next 12-18 months

Q2 was brutal. We bottomed out in Q3. 

I can’t wait for what’s to come in Q4 and 2024.

Scaling a startup isn’t easy. 

Doing it in public makes it even harder.
