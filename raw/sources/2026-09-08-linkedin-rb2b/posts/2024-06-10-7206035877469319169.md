## Exact post text

In 13 weeks, we grew RB2B from $0-$806k ARR with 4 people and zero funding. But we have a *broken* PLG model with a 1.3% conversion rate. Next month, we’re rolling out a two-touch sales motion that may (or may not) work.

Here’s a breakdown of the “Old Way (PLG)” vs “New Way (Sales-Assisted PLG)” GTM: 

OLD WAY (broken PLG) - March 1 - June 30 2024:

Model: 

- Free/unlimited push to Slack
- People will pay for integrations 
- Pricing is $495/mo no matter who you are
- I will wake up every day to hundreds of $495 upgrade button clicks

Problems:

- Too much value in FREE, not enough in PAID
- $495 too expensive for little guys 
- Not enough features (yet) for big guys (need ICP filtering & Hubspot)
- You can parse the Slack block with Zapier and send the leads anywhere

NEW WAY (Sales-Assisted PLG) - July 1 2024 - ???

- 90% of users will always be free, but cap free plan at 200-250 LI profiles/mo
- Tiered pricing starting at $99/mo, volume-based up to $750/mo
- Enterprise deals for $100m SaaS and above, super high traffic
- Of the 10% we try to charge, find out if they have a sales org when they join
- If they know how to book demos already, get them on phone
- If they can’t book demos, put them in the “toy funnel” (don’t try to convert) 
- For high value, set up ICP filter, have their best rep do 1-to-1 outreach
- Find out what a demo is worth during the initial setup call
- Our rep stays on top of customers’ rep until demo is booked
- When demo is booked, close monthly, make a compelling annual offer

Problems: 

- Don’t know yet, but there will be many I am sure!

TAKEAWAY:

I’ve never done a PLG, but I HAVE created a high-velocity inbound sales model. 

It was called GetEmails (now Retention.com), and I grew it to $12m ARR in 2.5 years with 6 people. 

I have a very strong feeling that with how RB2B is going that it will be bigger, faster. 

But here's my biggest challenge...

I’m not a salesperson, so I have no idea if this PITCH DECK I made is any good.

LinkedIn community - any feedback on the sales deck?

Rip it apart!

I need your feedback :)
