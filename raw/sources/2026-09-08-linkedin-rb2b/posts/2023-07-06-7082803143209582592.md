## Exact post text

I’ve been having lots of conversations with email marketers lately.

And there’s one, super common (expensive) mistake I keep seeing them make. What is it?

Trying to get the customer to do more than ONE thing at a time. This is bad.

Why?

Remember: a confused customer doesn’t buy!

In each email:

ONE call-to-action.

And make that CTA big, obvious, and hard to miss.

Keep it simple, and watch more revenue roll in. Try it out this week.
