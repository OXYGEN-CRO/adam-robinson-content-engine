## Exact post text

Against the advice of many, I will again share our financials.

Q1 ‘23 was a transformational quarter for Retention.com, and I’m proud to say I’m part of a team that has accomplished so much in such a short time (see the full numbers below).

We put all of the foundational blocks in place to scale in the coming quarters, hit $17M ARR, and are still on track to hit our $50m ARR goal by the end of 2023.

With respect to our financial statements, this is the tightest cash generation quarter we will ever have. Why? 2 points:

1) We layered on a massive amount of employee expense, who are now coming close to fully ramped.

2) We also started spending a ton on events, dinners, podcast and newsletter sponsorships, and my Founder Brand content.

Our expenses should increase linearly from here, while our revenues increase exponentially.

Our current ARR is $17.3m in Baremetics (+26.8% q-o-q), but we have 3m ARR in onboarding that doesn’t hit Baremetrics until they are onboarded, so we’re really a little north of 20m ARR.

Q123 Sales Metrics:

New Logo Deals Closed - 410

💰 Q1 Total New Revenue - $644,840 MRR / $7,738,080 ARR
💰 January - $172,550 MRR / $2,070,600 ARR
💰 February - $209,215 MRR / $2,510,580
💰 March - $260,075 MRR / $3,120,900

What we did well:

→ We crushed sales (see metrics above)
→ We hired (and got up to speed) incredible VPs/Directors in every department
→ We figured out our BDR motion (took us until mid-Feb)
→ Our Agency Partnerships program is in place and growing rapidly
→ Our Influencer/Affiliate channel is firing on all cylinders
→ We CRUSHED Shoptalk, and did well at the Whalies, GrowLA, and eTail West
→ We made incredible progress on all things content marketing thanks to Julia Bouterakos
→ Our annual contracts are VERY sticky - 85% of brands roll through the 60-day opt-out window into the 12mo contract

Where we can improve:

→ RevOps/Reporting … We are building out our systems for the first time
→ We made significant hires this quarter but had to let go of some. We are still learning how to recruit candidates that best fit our culture
→ Our monthly deals are churning …2/3rd of our churn is currently coming from monthly deals. We told non-ICP customers to leave, but have to convert ICP customers to our annual plans. This should continue to decline as the year progresses

The 3 biggest opportunities for Q2:

→ Lead generation - improving and scaling out BDR, Agency Partnerships, Influencer/Affiliate, and Events motions aggressively
→ We’re completely rebranding, launching a new website on May 1
→ We’re building an engineering team in Canada

That’s everything. Follow me if you want to watch us build a unicorn in public.

I’ll show you literally everything we do (clearly, not kidding).
