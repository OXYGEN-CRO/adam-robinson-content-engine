## Exact post text

I share an office with a billionaire — and it changed my life.

Here’s the fascinating story:

When I started GetEmails, I was burned out from my prior startup, and the plan the whole time was to sell after 18 months

GetEmails turned out to grow way faster than we had hoped, the market was hot, so we were able to get a smoking offer for it at the end of last year

I was at the 1-yard line of getting this off my hands, and I had someone coming in the next day to sign an Asset Purchase Agreement

The buyer bailed. At the last minute. Ugh.

This sudden loss of a life-changing amount of money threw me into an emotional tailspin for 24 hours

I had completely internalized the identity of a semi-retired dude who was going to spend time learning about crypto and doing the minimum possible to hit earn-out targets

I went into the office to talk to Dave Rogenmoser (CEO of JasperAI), I told him what happened, and he went on a rant for almost an hour that I wish I’d recorded

It would’ve been an audio recording worth a billion dollars

He told me about every single thing they were doing, and how they were aiming for the moon

He told me they hired Shane Orlick, the former Chief Revenue Officer of WalkMe, how big Shane thought, and how he attracted some of the best talent in the world to work with Jasper

He told me how they were now on the IPO track, and gunning for that within a handful of years

He told me they were on an absolute rocketship, and he told me I was thinking WAY too small

I realized my product was equally as good as theirs was

My feedback from customers and my numbers (kind of) reflected that

The difference?

They were spending over $1M+ a month on sales & marketing, at $50M ARR

I was spending $30K a month on sales & marketing (all of that was sales), at $8M ARR

Lightbulb goes off

Where would I be if I was in a position to spend $1M on salespeople (and/or marketing) for my operation?

I’d be at LEAST at $50M ARR

I decided that I had no choice but to think as big as Dave and the Jasper team were. I was playing way too small

Now, because of that conversation, I’ve done everything I can to be in the same place as Jasper is this year, by this time next year

There’s no way we’re not at $50M ARR by this time next year

If you’re curious where Jasper is now…

They just raised $125M at a $1.5B valuation in 18 months — after contemplating quitting the game and starting a restaurant 18 months prior

If you asked me in January 2021 if I believed that was possible for them, I would’ve cried laughing

It just wasn’t in the cards. I literally would have said “impossible”

But they proved to me and everyone else that it’s possible, and I’ve never been more motivated in my entire life

Hopefully this is a signal to you, too, that a failing business does not spell “The End”

Business and entrepreneurship are unpredictable. Every situation is different. But we can all think bigger, and execute on a much larger vision

Jasper proved it.
