## Exact post text

Word-of-mouth is like a cheat code for your business.

It can have customers knocking at your door, wanting to pay $10K per month on annual contracts (speaking from experience here).

But how do you generate it? Here are 3 simple strategies:

1) Create a WIN-WIN-WIN offer

The person referring wins. The new customers win. You win.

2) Be ultra-generous with refunds

I believe in ‘karma’ when it comes to this stuff. If someone’s asking for a refund, just give it to them quickly. It will come back to you down the line.

3) Systematically give out gifts to people who help you

If someone refers us a deal, I’ll send them a pair of AirPod Max’s. A $530 gift.

Again, I believe it will come back around to us.

Is there anything that you’ve done that has helped accelerate word of mouth? Let me know in the comments or DMs.
