## Exact post text

Over the past 9 months, RB2B's churn dropped from 'holy sh*t we're dying' (15%/mo) to 'still bleeding but breathing' (7.2%/mo). That's a 50% improvement that literally saved our company. Here are the 5 biggest mistakes I made in 2024... and how I fixed them in 2025:

1. I was delusional about product-market fit.

When we launched 18mo ago, person-level website ID was meant to close the loop on ABM, be a new source of meaningful pipeline, and completely transform GTM. We now know it’s not any of that, but it’s still by far the best source of intent, so people keep buying it.

2. I was optimizing the product for my ego, not customer happiness.

I resisted fixing certain things because I thought they would expose other problems that would ultimately lead to churn, in favor of bigger things I thought were transformational (like deeper Hubspot and Salesforce integrations). 

Turns out the integrations weren’t transformational, and fixing the little things has made people happier. Whooops.

3. I stopped talking to customers. Now, every Wednesday from 1-4pm I get my ASS handed to me by customers.

I do a rapid fire, back-to-back 15min customer calls for 3 hours every Wednesday afternoon. This has been revelatory. I know what people hate about RB2B, I know what we need to build. I also review EVERY Delphi chat that comes in, and get a ton of Intercom tickets.  This informs user pain and product dev.

4. We stopped trying to extract - and started trying to deliver - maximum value. 

We aren’t there yet in terms of pricing, but we now think the average $149/mo user is getting so much that they would keep the product even if there wasn’t hard-dollar ROI. 

5. I stopped LARPing as Russell Brunson/Gary Vee

Last year I was on a podcast tour, making courses, trying to create a youtube channel, doing one-to-many sales webinars, and making a docu-series … which was taking 60% of my time at least. Now I spend 1 morning a week writing LinkedIn posts, 1hr on “UnF*ck My Startup”, and the rest of my time goes to RB2B product (and a few one-on-ones and exec meeting for Retention.com).

TAKEAWAY

You can judge my churn all you want. 

Here’s the reality: 

5% churn = $15m ARR w/ 3 FTE’s.

I’m 90 days away from figuring out how to get there.

Stay tuned...
