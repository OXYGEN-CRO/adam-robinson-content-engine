## Exact post text

There’s a 99% chance you’re playing too small as a founder.

Let me share a quote with you that changed my trajectory as an entrepreneur:

"It's as easy to do something big as it is to do something small, so reach for a fantasy worthy of your pursuit, with rewards commensurate for your effort".

This is from Steve Schwartzman’s book, What It Takes — and I have found that statement to be true in our journey of scaling Retention.com to $20M ARR (and onward to $50M).

Something to think about as you plan your next venture… are you playing to small?
