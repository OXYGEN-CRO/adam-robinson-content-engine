## Exact post text

In 19 days, our entire $5.7M ARR company disappears for a week. AI agents will run everything - sales, support, development. Zero human intervention. This has never been done before at our scale.

THE CHALLENGE + JULY UPDATE:

We’ve been training our AI agents to handle a week with ZERO human intervention. 

We’ve been reading EVERY SINGLE RESPONSE our AI agents give, correcting to the best possible answer, trying to identify “lose-their-mind” type situations, coming up with solutions… rinse and repeat.

Robb Clarke and I have been doing this over and over again all month, and we think we’re in a pretty good spot.

———

What’s an example of a “lose-their-mind” type situation? 

RB2B sends a magic link to our users for login. 

Once or twice per week, somebody doesn’t get the email. 

They tend to get very worked up about it. I don’t blame them. 

The issue?

Sendgrid runs a tight “bounce protection” process, and the user’s email is usually on our “bounce protection” list within our sendgrid account.

We’ve been able to solve every magic link email problem after looking at sendgrid and either: 

1. Taking the email off bounce protection and telling them to resend the login email

2. Or identifying that it was an actual hard bounce, and the email was typo’d in the first place

We were freaked out because we KNEW this would happen a couple times while we were gone, and it’s not something that can wait a week. 

So - Robb started thinking about he could build an AI agent to solve the problem. 

———

24 hours later: 

1. Fin sends the email address to n8n

2. n8n checks Sendgrid’s API to see if the users email is on our “bounce protection” list 

3. If so, it removes the email from bounce protection, emails the user to try again

4. If not, it checks to see if it’s a hard bounce, if it is, emails the user that it’s a typo

This AI agent has worked 100% of the time the last 14 days. 

It’s agents like these that are going to save our lives when we disappear! 

———

July was a crazy month in the “signal/outreach” space we’re in.

- One competitor shut down
- Another doing the exact same thing raised $40m at $260m
- Another competitor (VC backed with 65 FTE) did a mass layoff

All that grabs headlines … but we’re slowly but steadily showing founders what’s possible. 

While competitors raise $40M and do mass layoffs, we’re proving you can hit $10M ARR with 3 people…

AND that you can disappear for a week and let your whole $5.7m ARR business run itself with AI.

Most founders will call this reckless.

The smart ones will ask: “How can I do this too?” 

Follow along - on September 3 you’ll find out whose right.
