## Exact post text

Last week I had coffee with a founder who's been stuck at $2.5-$3m ARR for 9 months, is burning $400k/mo, closing a TON of new business, but can’t get ahead of churn. He asked me what I would do, and was surprised by what I said: 

Founder: What would you do if you were me? 

Me: Before I tell you - what do YOU think you should do? 

Founder: I think we should run our SMB business as lean as possible, and focus on the Salesforce ecosystem.

Me: Why do you like the Salesforce ecosystem? 

Founder: Because NOBODY has anything like what we sell, unlike the SMB ecosystem, where there are several competing offers. The deals are larger - we have a few we’ve been working on - but they take forever to close.

Me: How do you get in front of those prospects? 

Founder: Our investors give us a couple introductions per month, which is cheap, and we exhibit at shows, which is expensive.

Me: Why is the SMB segment churning?

Founder: They don’t see the results they are looking for during the first several months, or they are just too small for us to move the needle. 

Me: I feel you there.

Founder: So what would you do if you were me?

_________

The advice I gave him was very similar to the path we chose a couple months ago at Retention.com

If you can’t stay in front of churn at $2.5-$3m ARR: 

- You have a product/market fit problem in SMB
- You need to prove out the upmarket segment
- You should definitely not be burning $400k/mo

I told this Founder that the only path forward was to acknowledge that there is a product-market fit problem, and until it was solved, $3m ARR was his ceiling.

When there is a product market-fit problem, there is only one way to handle it. 

The brutal reality is that you must get as small as possible. 

You need to completely wipe out this burn, because this model is unsustainable.

You need to do the things YOU KNOW you need to do to find PMF:

1. Try to talk to every churned customer yourself.
2. Slowly improve the product to eliminate churn reasons.
3. Learn about personas and who actually is and isn’t ICP
4. Keep pushing forward with the upmarket experiment 

When you find PMF, burning $400k/mo at $3m ARR COULD make sense. 

I mean, I hate it, but I can see how others would argue it’s the right move.  

Before then, it most certainly does NOT.

A critical mistake early stage founders make is trying to do ANYTHING to scale - and I mean ANYTHING - before they find PMF. 

Save yourself the agony. 

Before PMF, do things that don’t scale. 

PS.

I wrote #1-#4 as a reminder to myself. 
I’m guilty of the same thing at RB2B.
