## Exact post text

It’s easy to look at ‘successful’ startup founders and think they’ve got it all figured out.

My company Retention.com hit $14M ARR in 2 years — but I had my fair share of failures before this. A few painful examples…

→ I blew through $1M in a year trying to (unsuccessfully) build a Mailchimp competitor
→ I once had to lay off 25 people at once because I miscalculated an opportunity
→ I almost fumbled an 8-figure acquisition on the last day because we accidentally deleted the code for our software
→ I was on the verge of selling Retention.com and missing out on this wild ride we’re on now (this is still scary to think about)

Oh yeah… and I almost got sued one time. That was fun 😆

And I’m sure there are more, but I’ll spare you the time. My point?

On social media, you see the highlights FAR more than you see the failures.

Remember that before you get too hard on yourself. Nobody’s got it all figured out.

Keep pushing ❤️
