## Exact post text

We’ve audited over 1,500 Klaviyo accounts in the past 36 months.

What we learned is that there are only 5 Klaviyo flows that really matter for large Shopify stores:

1) Welcome Series
2) Product (or Browse) Abandonment
3) Category Abandonment
4) Cart Abandonment
5) Checkout Abandonment

Getting these flows right is key to optimizing your Klaviyo account.

Why?

- They catch people at every part of the middle of your funnel
- They trigger Email and SMS messages that are timely, relevant and personalized
- It’s easy to see total flow revenue, and see what’s working and what’s not
- Engagement and profitability are much higher than your campaign/newsletter sends
- You can use identity solutions to amplify the power of these flows

If you’d like to see an in-depth breakdown of each flow, tips on creative for each, which ones to send SMS to and which you’d better not, and how they all work together, we put together a guide to walk you through the entire system.
