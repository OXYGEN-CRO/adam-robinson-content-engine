## Exact post text

Between 2015 and 2018, Drift raised $107M and built one of the best brands in B2B SaaS. In 2021, they found an acquirer at a $1B valuation. Now, I'm trying to do the same with R!B2B with $0 raised. Here's my brand playbook:

The “Drift Way”: 

- Build the affinity around the company itself
- A steady stream of books, podcasts, blog posts, and shareable PDFs that made you feel like you really needed it and gave you EXTREME FOMO
- They had a lightning bolt on their website and they had a lightning emoji everywhere (you couldn't escape it)
- Have a sexy, cutting-edge website that changes almost weekly
- Have a super-expensive physical presence - lots of people, offices, etc
- Pay for it all with over $100m of VC $$$

The “R!B2B Way”: 

- Build affinity around individual social profiles of individual thought leadership (not the company)
- Create a type of trust in top execs that can only be achieved human-to-human
- Build the business and offering to compliment and amplify this megaphone
- Use influencer and UGC marketing to build a different type of trust
- Have the top executives be the face of the brand, make their faces synonymous with the product category
- Do not worry about what the website looks like (use a template, get it up)
- Do not hire people, use chatbots instead, don’t have offices
- Pay for it with free cash from another business unit

TAKEAWAY:

I was intoxicated by the brand David Cancel & Dave Gerhardt created at Drift.

It attracted the best employees, customers, investors, and acquirers.

However, it required a ton of capital, both human and financial. 

We’re trying to use LinkedIn to create the same brand (without the capital).

I’m watching it happen in real-time.

We booked 300+ calls with ICP execs, driven thousands of waitlist signups, and over 1,500 free signups for our tool without spending $1 on ads.

How?

People know and trust Santosh Sharan and I because of our content.

I think the next generation of unicorns gets built like we’re building R!B2B. 

Light, fast, hyper-capital efficient.

But here's the thing...

It all starts with building a brand.

The one true moat left in SaaS.

You can't hire a fancy PR firm or marketing agency to do it for you. 

You (as founders and execs) have to invest the time to build it yourself.

That's the “new way”.
