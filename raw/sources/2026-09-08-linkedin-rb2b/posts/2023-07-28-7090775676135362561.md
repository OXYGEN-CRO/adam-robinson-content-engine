## Exact post text

I meet with 100’s of executives and aspiring founders every year. They often ask for advice on how to bootstrap their own startup. Here’s what I tell them:

1. Keep throwing spaghetti against the wall:

- You need to find an “S” curve… a 1,000x
- The only way to find it is to run a bunch of wild experiments
- 9/10 of them will be total failures

2. Maintain your financial freedom.

- The only way to run crazy experiments is if you have free cash
- Investors would never let you behave in the way necessary to create an “s” curve
- If your growth slows down, that can’t lead to killing the crazy experiments

3. Try to be the 800lb gorilla in your space.

- Winner-take-all economics exist in tech 
- Compete in a way where you are impossible to compete with
- Try to compete for market share rather than growing revenues

4. Be obsessed with delivering value to your Ideal Customer Profile.

- If it doesn’t drive immediate revenue for merchants, don’t build it 
- Focus relentlessly on minimizing time-to-value
- Figure out how to show incremental revenue as clearly as possible

5. Stay lean, but when the time is right, go as fast as possible. 

- We bootstrapped $14m ARR with six people
- We figured out that large Shopify stores were our ICP
- We grew to a team of 50 in 60 days

TL;DR

1. Keep throwing spaghetti against the wall. 
2. Maintain your financial freedom.
3. Try to be the 800lb gorilla in your space.
4. Be obsessed with delivering value to your Ideal Customer Profile.
5. Stay lean, but when the time is right, go as fast as possible.
