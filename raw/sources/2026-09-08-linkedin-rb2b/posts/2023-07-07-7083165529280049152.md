## Exact post text

How to bootstrap your startup from $0 to $20M ARR:
 
1. Create a truly magical product in a fringe market

2. Create a hilarious and inflammatory Facebook ad campaign to get to $3M ARR

3. Let anyone use it for the first couple of years - because your idea of who the ICP is in the beginning will probably be wrong

4. Run WAY too lean for WAY too long (we hit $13M ARR with 6 people)

5. When you hone in on an ICP, bring on a killer operator, learn how to sell annual deals, then start scaling as hard and as fast as possible
 
This may not work for everyone.
 
But it worked for us.
