## Exact post text

I built a SaaS to $14M ARR with 6 employees and no VC funding

Here are 10 lessons I wish I'd known before starting:

1) You’ve got to sell something magical. Read “Purple Cow” by Seth Godin

2) Read the Y Combinator blog and understand the signs of product-market fit

3) Don’t hire a single non-cofounding salesperson before you have product-market fit

4) Focus on a new and exciting market rather than an old and mature one

5) If you’re running a SaaS company, orienting yourself towards growth instead of ‘lifestyle’ will pay off big time in the future (took me a while to realize this)

6) The difference between a GREAT person in their role and an AVERAGE person in their role is much greater than the difference in their compensation between the two

7) You need to think bigger. There’s NO upward bound on your potential business growth if the product-market fit is strong enough

8) You can run a both high growth AND highly profitable SaaS company — you just need the product-market fit to be strong enough (seeing a theme?)

9) You don’t need an expensive office to sell a company

10) Fire fast. If there’s any doubt about a person… well, there’s no doubt about that person
