## Exact post text

Last week 6sense threatened to sue me because I bought their product to compare it to mine. So I was DUMBFOUNDED when I saw that 6Sense created an RB2B account on April 5 to do the same thing! 

Here are 6 reasons why 6Sense shouldn’t sue the little guys for something they’re ALSO doing: 

1. It’s hypocritical. 

Suing someone for something where you’re doing the exact same thing is ridiculous. Should I sue you too, 6Sense? I’ve never heard of anything like this. 

2. It’s not nice. 

You may think this sounds strange coming from me, but it’s genuinely unkind to attack a little guy for something that you are also doing.

3. It’s not good for 6Sense’s brand. 

Suing the little guy is already bad enough for your brand. Suing the little guy for something you’re doing back at him is appalling. 

4. 6Sense’s brand was already deteriorating (before this).

Look at the comments on any post I’ve written about anything to do with 6Sense and ABM. Almost nobody makes money from the tool, despite the FOMO-driven sales motion that pulled everyone in. 

5. It shows the market how afraid 6Sense is of RB2B. 

It’s obvious why this is happening. 6Sense is trying to squash this new threat to their business however possible. Our Person-Level Website Visitor Identity tool is so much better than their company-level solution that they just want to stop it however they can.

6. It’s not good for 6Sense employee morale. 

How would you like the whole world knowing the company you work for is running around suing bootstrapped entrepreneurs for a practice that you are ALSO engaged in?!?!?

TAKEAWAY:

Dealing with new competitors is hard. 

We went from zero competitors to 5 with Retention.com. It’s brutal. 

6Sense - I completely understand your desire to eliminate RB2B in its early days by suing us into oblivion.

I also understand why you want to silence my voice, because every time I mention your company, hundreds of people chime in to say how it doesn’t create value as advertised.

However…

I CAN’T BELIEVE that the only thing that has ANY weight in your Cease and Desist … 

… Using their tech to compare it to ours …

Is something 6Sense LITERALLY DID TO US 17 DAYS PRIOR.

I don’t even know what I should do about this.

LinkedIn community - any ideas?
