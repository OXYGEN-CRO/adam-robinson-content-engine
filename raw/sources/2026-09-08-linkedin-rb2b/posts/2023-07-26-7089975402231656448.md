## Exact post text

10 common mistakes startups make that slow down growth:

1. You rip off another vendor’s GTM playbook, it works for a second, then stops
2. Your pricing doesn’t have a path to upgrade with increasing value delivered
3. You under-price your product thinking it’s helping grab share (and it’s not)
4. You try to force individual contributors into management roles
5. You don’t understand your TAM and it dries up quickly on you
6. You don’t focus on reducing churn and NRR until it’s too late
7. You scale before you have a fully repeatable sales model
8. You don’t understand your unit economics by channel
9. You under-invest in rev-ops until it’s too late
10. You hire too many salespeople too quickly 

I made every single one of these mistakes in 2023.

Don't be like me!
