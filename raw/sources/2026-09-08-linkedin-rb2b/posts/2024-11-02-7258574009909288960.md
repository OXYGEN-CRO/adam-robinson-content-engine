## Exact post text

We closed October at $24.9m ARR (+3.8%) with Retention.com at $21.1m (+1.2%) and RB2B at $3.8m ARR (+20%). Here’s the breakdown of our BEST MONTH since 2022: 

GOOD STUFF: 

- We generated $872,835 profit (and are bootstrapped)
- Made progress on churn at RB2B (still horrible, more to come)
- Churn seasonally low at Retention (we have fixed stuff too)
- Came up with a new KILLER RB2B offer (and new selling motion)
- Have some incredible upmarket POCs for Retention
- Kyle and team pulled of an INCREDIBLE Retox Retreat in LA
- We’re launching “RB2B Traffic Insights” next week
- I am the Austin CEO liaison for Pavilion (CEOs watch your DMs)
- We are becoming better managers thanks to Jonathan Shapiro
- Staff unchanged - we are doing all this with just 40 rockstars!

BAD STUFF: 

- RB2B churn is bad, more competitors every day… How will we win? 
- We think a large, happy Retention(.)com customer will churn Mon or Tues 
- Managers have struggled with being too entrepreneurial for our size
- This really means we are just learning how to clearly articulate what we want
- Which we’re learning, but we are still early in the game

I’m “Pleased But Not Satisfied” (great book) with how things are going.

At the same time, I’m a little afraid of how Q1 will be… 

There always seems to be a bunch of churn in the ecomm business. 

But for the first time in a while, there are three very clear growth levers we are pulling. 

It was such a different situation than April of last year:

- RB2B wasn’t contributing
- We had no upmarket motion
- We were shrinking at 2.5%/mo (4mo in a row)

2024 has been an incredible turnaround year for us. 

I’m very grateful for every member of the Retention(.)com team … 

This team has done a KILLER job navigating the rough waters of 2023-2024.

It hasn’t been easy for SaaS who sell to ecommerce stores. 

But I’m proud to say I’m part of this elite group. 

Even when everything looked and felt like sh*t earlier this year… 

We just kept building.
