I had not one, but TWO epic fails last year and one of them was with GetOpeners.

With GetOpeners, we were helping brands bring their dead emails back to life by getting their email open rates to 50% from a measly 2%.

We had 100k in monthly revenue at one point and 6 months later all that revenue had churned off and was GONE. 🌬️

So what happened? I’ll tell you.

Here's 4 lessons I learned from that experience:

✅ Apple basically made our product obsolete with phantom open signals. It was bad luck and even worse timing for that to happen.

✅ Brands want to make MONEY. If it’s not driving ROI, we’re not doing it.

✅ Recurring revenue is an absolute must-have and we didn’t have it.

✅ We had ZERO product process, which is something that we are working on every single day now.
