## Exact post text

Last month I was talking to the President of a $2 Billion tech unicorn and what he told me about their biggest growth challenge was shocking.

He said that on their way from $6M to $100M+ ARR what slowed them down wasn't lead flow, marketing, product or churn but employee satisfaction.

He said employees weren't connecting with the company's values and vision.

They weren't deriving *real* meaning from their work. Just money.

And because of that, they tended to not stick around very long.

So what did the company do?

They created a "pull vs push" recruiting mechanism.

They stopped using headhunters.

They stopped using internal recruiters.

They stopped investing in LinkedIn job postings.

And they went ALL IN on creating a brand people wanted to work for.

Within a year, they had stopped trying to convince people to work for them and had countless people knocking down their door, ready to opt in to their mission.

Pull vs push.

That's what it takes to build a unicorn
