## Exact post text

The last few months have been ROUGH for Retention.com

Here are the dirty details - broken down by MRR:

February: $1,333,331 (10.2% growth)
March: $1,441,924 (8.1% growth)
April: $1,509,890 (4.7% growth)
May: $1,566,145 (3.7% growth)

That is not the direction we want to be headed.

Or the news I want to be sharing.

But here’s the thing…

Building in public means sharing the good, the bad and the ugly.

And we’re 100% committed to transparency.

But May was emotionally grueling.

And confusing…

THE ANALYSIS:

In early 2023, we experienced a surge of demand and insane sales metrics with new and untrained employees that led us to BELIEVE we should keep growing headcount as fast as possible and go for the land grab.

But once we got fully staffed up, everything just stopped…

And it’s NOT totally clear why.

We think we can fix a lot of the problem and get moving again (maybe slower, but still moving) with sharper focus on doing things that matter and more careful spending.

However, some of it seems to be market-related.

Anecdotally, I have been speaking with revenue leaders from other SaaS vendors who sell into the Shopify ecosystem and they had terrible months in April and May as well.

The interesting thing is that we're not hearing that the merchants are hurting.

The hope is that they are just waiting to see if there is a huge dip before the holiday season, and if there isn't, they start gearing up again.

Our product is very Black Friday friendly. And I bet we see a tailwind.

THE TAKEAWAY:

Will we hit $4m MRR by year-end?!?!?!

It's not impossible. But after these past few months, it's become a truly epic challenge.

As I have reminded myself, when your team has bootstrapped $1.55M MRR and a surprisingly soft month is growing that MRR by 3.7%, I have nothing to complain about.

This would have been my dream only months ago.

$2.5m MRR would be 100% YoY growth for us.

Will we get there?

I think so.

But the BHAG goal is $4m and that’s still what we’re aiming for.

PS.

New around here?

Follow me to watch us build a unicorn in public.

There will be ups and downs, but I’ll be sharing every step along the way.

Updates daily!

#shopify #ecommerce #saas
