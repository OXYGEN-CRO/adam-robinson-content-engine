## Exact post text

I bootstrapped my startup to $12M ARR with only 6 people. Now I’m launching a brand new product and plan to get it to $10M ARR by the end of 2024 with no more than 5 people. 

Here's my 7-step playbook for launching 8-figure products with tiny teams: 

Step 1: Beta test a valuable, differentiated product

- We are beta testing an individual-level website identity product for B2B
- All of the other products in the space are IP > company name
- We will deliver LinkedIn URL, email, phone, and page view history
- We are doing everything manually, delivering files daily to beta testers

Step 2: Create an inbound sales engine

- We will use two main channels: cold email and founder brand
- We are validating demand before we even start building the actual product
- Cold email is a team in the Philippines - low conversion, high volume
- Founder Brand is LinkedIn content (text posts and add a video element)
- I amplify everything by “working in public” and sharing financials as we go

Step 3: Build the product

- Once we have decided the exact feature set we want, we’ll start building
- Well build as light of a version as possible as quickly as we can
- Tate, our CTO, is the master at this lightning-speed building
- Build/improve sales channels BEFORE the product is done

Step 4: Hire one SUPER HUNGRY AE

- The idea is the sale is a 15min close, this AE does 8 demos per day
- The goal is to have this person completely flooded with activity
- This person likely needs a sales assistant to help with follow-up and admin

Step 5: Hire one onboarding/CS rep

- Onboarding is simple - it will be mostly self-serve
- With our current company, we got to 12m ARR with just Clint (rockstar)
- The job is answering questions, making sure value is understood
- Need the kind of person that literally CAN’T leave things undone

Step 6: Figure out how we’re going to create self-serve customer support documentation

- Anne is the best in the game at this, but she is now our VP product
- This is a critically important piece of this that I don’t have a solution for yet

Step 7: Make everything go faster with a few marketing campaigns. 

- We’ll team up with sales influencers and SaaS influencers and drum up a buzz
- I’ll want to go on a podcast tour, and get key people on mine
- It will be interesting to see if there is a strong affiliate channel for this product

From here, we just keep trying to find ways to go even faster. 

I think we can get to $10M ARR by the end of 2024. 

That is 1,667 people paying us $17/day (or $500/mo). 

My first SaaS had 5,000 customers in 36mo.

We onboarded 1,500 customers over the last 12mo at a much higher price to a much more price-sensitive DTC Shopify store audience at Retention.com. 

I’m feeling really good about the playbook. 

Remember:

You don't need a BIG team to make BIG money.

Stay lean and thrive.

P.S. 

As we push to $10m ARR, I'll report it all on Linkedin. The good, the bad and the ugly. Follow along for the ride.
