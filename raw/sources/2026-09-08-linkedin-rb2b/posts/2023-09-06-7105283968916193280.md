## Exact post text

This will not be a popular thing to say on LinkedIn, but my bootstrapped $20M ARR SaaS just shrunk our sales team from 15 to 4 reps and growth SPED UP (!).

The days of massive sales teams for early stage startups are numbered. The next crop of tech unicorns will have BDR orgs that look VERY DIFFERENT than Salesforce. Here's the breakdown: 👇

The Old “Predictable Revenue” Push Way:

- You choose a revenue goal at the beginning of the year that is based on a “predictable” growth rate

- You see what current reps were producing the year prior and hire X more of them based on assumption of linear productivity

- You see “proof” early on that assumptions were accurate from the first round of hires. Exciting!

- You decide that “it’s working” so you should “really go for it”

- You accelerate the pace of hiring reps and layer on managers and tech

- You don't provide proper training because how could we when you have to move this fast!

- The whole thing breaks when you hit a (very mild) macro slowdown and buyers stop mindlessly buying anything and everything

- As the BDR team stops producing, you make your AEs prospect because their calendars are 20% of what they need to be

- Of course, your AEs aren’t booking demos either because they too are using the same busted outbound playbook

-  You are looking at the whole massive org and how much time talented (and expensive!) reps are sitting in front of screens putting so much in and getting little back

- You give all of your best leads to your top 3 sellers, which makes the other 12 miserable (can’t blame them), and has severe negative cultural consequences

- You’re sitting there, trapped, wondering how the f*ck you ended up here


The New “Ultra-Lean, High Velocity” Pull Way

- Very small team of reps (we have 4 and are at $20m ARR), literally flooded with demand of high-intent, low-in-the-funnel prospects who close at above 60% in 25% of the time

- Demand is created over months if not years through content that cuts through

- High-intent, low-in-the-funnel demand is captured through indirect, high-velocity external channels (partners, small events, influencer/affiliate), fully-automated, high-volume, low conversion email (AKA spray-and-pray)

- Your 1 BDR is focused on missed demos, closed lost, referrals, inbound from founder brand and podcast, changed jobs, and high-value intent data… Your org NEVER touches a cold lead

- Your buyers have a relationship with YOU (the Founder) through your content, and haven’t been qualified/sold by someone half their age with 1/100 of the knowledge and experience

- You actually close MORE ARR and grow FASTER because your reps are senior, can handle more, been around a long time, and make fewer mistakes

- Your company culture improves dramatically because hyper-efficient companies are fun to work at and you don’t have a single person who isn’t crushing it

It's a Push model vs a Pull a model.

And in 2024, Pull wins.
