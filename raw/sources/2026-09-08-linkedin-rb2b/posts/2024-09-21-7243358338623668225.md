## Exact post text

Just 6 months after launch, RB2B hit $2.9m ARR but our churn rate is 15.6%/mo and it’s getting WORSE! Here are the 4 biggest mistakes I made so far and how we plan to fix them:

1. I didn’t realize how big the “WHAT NOW” problem would be

There's an emotional euphoria when people see their site visitors flowing into Slack. However, there is an (almost) universal lack of understanding about what to do next. We’ve provided how-to content, but our customers are putting the burden of coming up with a playbook on themselves… So they either don’t know it’s there, or it’s not good enough.

POTENTIAL SOLUTION:

Make content that answers the question “what - EXACTLY - is another business like me doing that is working?” persona-by-persona, and figure out how to get it into onboarding.

2. We don’t REALLY understand why people are churning.

The hypothesis is people are getting stuck in the “what now” stage, not getting value out of the leads, not getting ROI, dissatisfied with data quality, or going to a competitor.

POTENTIAL SOLUTIONS:

We have implemented Churnkey, but I want to hear it from the horses’ mouth. After I get back from paternity leave, I’m going to speak with customers >$300/mo as they leave and will post about it.

The Clearbit team also said Reveal had a nasty churn issue and that getting other teams within the company using other products was the thing that helped the most. So, we’re on it. Building two more solutions.

3. We need more than just one feature

I alluded to this in the previous section. I was convinced that a point solution with a clear value proposition and a monster free offer had the best shot at growing in 2024. While that may be true, it’s also an unbelievably high churn scenario.

Providing more sources of value for different uses of the tech and different people at the org should have been a more obvious thing to me.

I also believe we can provide info products/coaching for our $0-$1m ARR community that will not only be value-add to the RB2B deployment...

But that can help founders get unstuck and get to the next rung in a scalable, efficient way.

4. I didn’t realize how enthusiastic people monitoring their traffic would be.

We’re not sure whether or not it’s because of who we’re selling to - SMB and VSMB - or because of the nature of our product.

But people LOVE seeing visitors come into Slack, seemingly even more than the people who are creating business outcomes with the data.

Appreciating this, the emotional journey of it, the customer journey built around the emotional journey, and products built to help people monitor traffic better are where we need to go. 

TAKEAWAY:

Simply put, at 15% churn we aren’t going to grow ARR much more.

If we can cut churn to 8%/mo, I believe we’ll get to $10m ARR in 12mo.

If we can’t, we’ll get stuck at $5m.

So Linkedin… This post is a cry for help.

I'd LOVE to know for all you churned RB2B users...

1. Why did you actually churn? 
2. What could we have done to save you?
