## Exact post text

99% of early-stage SaaS founders think they need VC capital to get to the next level. They’re wrong. What they ACTUALLY need is better product. Here’s my 7-part breakdown of what founders get wrong when they raise money:

1. They don’t realize word-of-mouth is the ONLY thing that matters

If your product isn’t growing on its own through word-of-mouth, you’re already dead, you just don’t know it yet. If it is, there are a million ways to speed it up. Sure, VC money may help with that, but it can't create what doesn't exist.

2. They don’t know what real product-market fit feels like

Trying to explain what PMF feels like to someone who hasn’t felt it is like trying to explain to a fish what it’s like to walk on the land. One day of true PMF is better than 100,000 words describing it. I know because I’ve now spent years on both sides.

3. They lie to themselves about their crappy products

So much of our identity and ego are tied to our amazing products and businesses that it’s *very* difficult to realize when you DON’T have PMF. You see founders who have been able to raise and raise again without true PMF, which makes everything look bigger, but now as the tide goes out, we’re seeing a lot of naked people.

4. They hire too many people before they have a repeatable sales model

I made this mistake several times - most recently in early 2023. Fortunately for me we had a ton of positive cashflow, but if we didn’t, this all-too-common urge would have led me to go out and raise money, only to burn through it all and end up back at the same place.

5. They get FOMO because their startup competitors are raising big rounds

This is the second dumbest mistake we all make. We don’t realize that STARTUPS DON’T KILL STARTUPS, they kill themselves. In almost all cases startups would have been better off keeping their head down and focusing on product than raising rounds because their “competition” did.

6. They think it will help them hire and close bigger deals

This is another LIE investors tell you to get you to transact. Do you know what will help you close bigger deals? A better product and a better sales process. What will help you hire better people? The reputation of having incredible PMF and great execution.

7. They want to feel rich

This is the DUMBEST mistake we all make that investors use against us. Even if we take zero or little secondary, and give investors preference with all sorts of controls, a big valuation makes us FEEL good about our beautiful business, even though it means nothing.

TAKEAWAY:

Institutional capital isn’t necessary to level-up a SaaS.

A great product will catapult you higher than you can imagine.

The problem is that most founders have no idea what a great product looks like

Cheap, abundant capital has kept mediocre SaaS alive for far too long.
