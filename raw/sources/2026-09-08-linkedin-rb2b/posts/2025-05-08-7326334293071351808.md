## Exact post text

3 ways I’m NOT using AI at RB2B (and 5 ways I am):


1. I’m NOT using AI to write LinkedIn posts

Call me old fashioned, but I think if you’re trying to create THE BEST art, the best art is valuable because of the perspective of the artist. I still write it all.

2. I’m NOT using AI as a SPAM cannon

Despite the promise of the category, I haven’t heard of a single person that has been satisfied with an engagement with an outbound AI SDR.

3. I’m NOT using AI to do sales

I’m second guessing this one after we had Kevin "KD" Dorsey on Unf*ck My Startup yesterday. He said the only reason AI hasn’t taken over SDR is because it’s illegal, and you have to say you’re an AI. But he said inbound is 100% there.

——————

I AM using AI to:

1. Handle 8,500 support requests/month

Robb Clarke has done an incredible job at molding Intercom’s Fin into something that works very well. Not perfect, but very well. Only 3% of our support requests involve a human.

2. Filter, tier, and route our PLG signups

Brendan Short created a Clay table that sorts our 1,500+ signups per month by traffic, industry, company size, sales team size, CRM used, location and routes them accordingly.

3. Create scorecards for AE’s and CS reps to evaluate calls

This is a new one for us, and what I believe is the most impactful but hardest to measure. Did the rep hit all of the areas of a discovery call? How was their tone in a CS call? What objections did they handle poorly?

4. Come up with an excuse to reach out to prospects

My brother Anthony showed me a research AI he created with Make using perplexity deep research that is SUPER expensive (~25c per domain) but can manufacture a relevant reason to reach out for any large retailer. The output is incredible and his team loves it. We ran our list through it. Our team loves it too.

5. Be my business coach

After Sam Parr told me he has made a custom GPT coach for every key pillar of his life and he’s fed them TONS of context and engages with them many times per day. I made a business coach. I asked it to ask me every question about my business. And I agree w/ Sam - LLMs as any kind of coach, especially once given a bunch of context, are amazing.

TAKEAWAY

AI is incredible, and getting better every day.

But I don’t think the best application is replacing people doing the same “old way” tasks that people did to sell “old way” products, services, and software.

We’re trying to jam it in that box right now because it’s how we all make money.

And in many cases, it’s doing those things pretty well.

But, I’m most intrigued by the “always on coach” application.

Our basic humanity - the lizard brain, our heuristics - is our worst enemy.

ChatGPT - especially with context - allows you to rise above it, with little effort.

All of our lives are the decisions we make.

Those decisions compound.

What would life be like if every decision you made over decades was 5% better?

There’s only one way to POSSIBLY find out…

Ask ChatGPT.
