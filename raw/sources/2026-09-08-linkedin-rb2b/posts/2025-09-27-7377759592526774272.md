## Exact post text

I’ve bootstrapped $0 to $1M ARR three times. If I had to start over with no money and no personal brand, here’s how I’d hit $1M ARR in under 12 months:

1. Hunt whales in their natural habitat.

Alina Vandenberghe told us on UnF*ck My Startup that she went to sales meetups and showed the people in the room who looked like they knew everybody a diagram of what she wanted to build (now $40m ARR Chilipiper). She asked if they’d buy, if not, what else would they need. Then she built it.

2. Turn your journey into content.

The indirect benefit of an audience rooting for you comes in the form of “pull” (rather than push) recruiting, a customer base who are also fans, and improved content creation skills as time goes on. B2B companies are transitioning to a Kardashian era. You may think it's cringe, but it is the future.

3. I’d test different offers with cold email. 

My good friend Nick Weldon loves to say 5x5’s product was not great, but their offer was incredible. High response rates to cold email - even if you don’t have the product yet - can help you nail your packaging and messaging.

4. Obsess over word-of-mouth like your life depends on it (because it does).

If you have word-of-mouth, everything you do will work. If you do not have word-of-mouth, nothing you do will work. How do you get word-of-mouth? Product-market fit. Deliver more value, quicker, for less money, with less friction, until you have a wave of inbound. 

5. Ignore every “growth hack” until step 4 is bulletproof.

This is where I see founders waste the most time and energy. “Growth hacking” works for companies with product-market fit ONLY BECAUSE they have product-market fit. Save yourself the heartache. Stop focusing on button colors and 30 part email flows. Focus on giving more, better, faster.

6. Don’t hire ANYONE.

Most software businesses are capable of getting to $1m ARR with the founders alone, ESPECIALLY with AI. RB2B is currently at $6.3m ARR - we only have 3 FTE’s. You absolutely can do this too.

TAKEAWAY

I’ve gone $0-$1m ARR 3 times.

Each time twice as fast with half the resources. 

It’s not because I got smarter… 

It’s because I learned what NOT to waste time on. 

The fastest path to $1m ARR isn’t a secret framework.

It’s being 1% better - at the RIGHT things - every single day.

Massive success is inside of you. 

You just need to find it.

Keep building.
