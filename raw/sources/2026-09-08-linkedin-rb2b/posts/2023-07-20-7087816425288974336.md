## Exact post text

From George Clooney’s tequila to Kim Kardashian’s $4B apparel company, celebrity consumer brands are everywhere. Here are 3 marketing lessons you can steal from these A-listers:

👉 Lesson 1: The name behind the product matters MORE than the product itself.

We all know that influencer marketing is taking over.

But this new trend is influencer marketing taken to the EXTREME: 

Instead of endorsing the brand, the influencer owns a piece of it (or all of it)


👉 Lesson 2: If your name’s on the sign, your reputation is on the line. 

Can you position yourself as the ‘celebrity’ or ‘expert’ in your niche?

If so, you can expect customers to have a lot more trust in your brand.

But that means your name is on the business - for better or worse.

A while ago, Mr. Beast made headlines with his Beast Burger brand.

It was a smash hit at first, but he recently shut it down.

Why? 

Because they outsourced fulfillment to ghost kitchens operated by others, and the quality was inconsistent.

He’s since pivoted to Feastables, a product he CAN fully control.


👉 Lesson 3: The thing you’re most well-known for isn’t necessarily the best way to monetize.

George Clooney was the highest-paid male actor of 2018 without even appearing in a film.

He made $239 million, nearly twice the next-highest paid actor (The Rock at $124m) 

How?

Because that $239 million was his proceeds from selling his tequila brand.

Clooney certainly made a fortune as an actor, but he stepped into a new level of earning power by taking on an opportunity with even higher leverage.
