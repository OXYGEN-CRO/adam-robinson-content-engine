## Exact post text

It was a HARD April at Retention.com. We basically got stopped dead in our tracks from April 1 - April 14.

FIND OUT WHAT HAPPENED:

❌ The Bad:

 • We did a big sales contest at the end of the quarter. Anyone who was even thinking about closing or booking a demo got pulled into March from April.
 • We had raised prices in a strange way - we doubled the prices of all of our plans, which caused people who would have been on $2,500/mo to pick $1,500, instead of the "new" $5,000 plan
 • Our Average Contract Value drifted down to $12k from $20k two weeks before!

✅ The Good:

 • We managed to snap right back the last two weeks of April in terms of sales and pipeline generation and closed a soft month with 6.1% ARR growth
 • Our ACV is back to $18k as of the last two weeks
 • We have unbelievable "brand heat" at the events we are attending
 • We dropped our rebrand. The site is still a bit buggy and I want to get more case studies from some of our customers with stronger brands, but I love the look.
 • We are making major rev ops progress ... We're starting to have visibility into who our customers actually are and how the product is working for them, by ICP

Biggest Opportunities for May:

 • We have finally switched to a highly personalized, high-penetration BDR strategy, and we should see this start to work in our favor in the coming quarters
 • I've been having great conversations with different people at Klaviyo, showing them how successful our customers are with our products, and how well everything delivers. More productive discussions to come!
 • We are working on TONS of collateral for our agency partners - case studies, standard operating procedures, new agency-specific onboarding methods that are extremely conservative but produce amazing results

Will we hit $50m ARR by year-end?!?!?!

It's not impossible. But after this month, it's become a truly epic challenge.

But, as I have reminded myself, when your team has bootstrapped $18.35M ARR and a surprisingly soft month is growing that ARR by 6.1%, I have nothing to complain about.

This would have been my dream only months ago.

$30m ARR is 100% annual growth, 40m ARR is 166% annual growth, and I think there is still a shot we get to $50m.

PS - Follow me to watch us build a unicorn in public. Updates daily!
