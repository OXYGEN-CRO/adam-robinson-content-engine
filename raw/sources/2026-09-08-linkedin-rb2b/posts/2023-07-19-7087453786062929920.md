## Exact post text

I asked the President of the fastest-growing Shopify store on the planet (with a $150M run-rate) what the biggest problem with DTC email marketing was. His answer is packed with insane insight. 

I spoke with Ben Yahalom, the President of True Classic last week.

Here's what he said: 

“I don’t think brands realize how much of a *SIGNAL GAP* there is between Meta and Klaviyo. Apple has been clamping down on the ability to track people. First it was iOS14, which crushed the ability for Meta to track people across websites. Then, as of a couple months ago, Apple made it so that Meta and Klaviyo could only track people for seven days MAX. 

The problem is that there are a bunch of things happening on the Shopify store (like cart abandonments) that due to Apple’s changes, aren’t making it to Meta and Klaviyo. The easiest thing to do to improve performance in Email Marketing is to focus on doing whatever you can to close the *SIGNAL GAP* between Shopify and Klaviyo.”

So important he said it twice...

The SIGNAL GAP.

That's the biggest problem facing DTC email marketing.

So...

How do you solve the signal gap? 

1. Build a 1st party cookie pool through server-side tracking
2. Facilitate cross-device identification

The fastest growing Shopify store on the planet is focused on closing the SIGNAL GAP.

Are you?
