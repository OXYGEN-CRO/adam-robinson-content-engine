## Exact post text

Hey CEOs: if you disappeared into the woods for 7 days, would your company run smoothly without you? This is the ultimate test of your delegation skills. 
 
I tried it. Here's what I learned: 

–

The CEO is the Chief Emergency Officer.

Anytime there's a fire, the default is for the employees to bring it to the CEO to solve

You need to switch this default option.

Instead of coming to you for a solution, empower your employees to solve huge problems on their own 

–

CEO involvement kills scale.

If the CEO has to be involved in every decision, the company will never grow past the CEO's personal capacity.

Delegate as much decision-making power as possible. 

–

The CEO's job is NOT to run the company.

The CEO's main job is recruiting A+ talent.

I worked my ass off to recruit great people.

Now I can sit back and allow them to work their magic. 

– 

My company does $20 million in annual revenue

The larger we get, the less important I should become 

When we had 5 employees, I was 20% of the team

But when we have 1,000 employees, I'll be just 0.1% 

– 

A lot of these are counter-intuitive, but your job as CEO should be easier as you scale

The playbook you created is playing itself out.

You *should* be less involved in the day-to-day.

Think big picture and trust the process.
