## Exact post text

Life-changing money slipped through my fingers in February of 2022 as the deal to sell my company fell through. 

I was on the verge of selling Retention.com — and the buyer walked. I was devastated. Turns out it was one of the best things that could have happened to me.

Since then: We’re sitting at $15M ARR and on our way to $50M ARR (and a $1B valuation). So, what changed?

My mindset. 

I started thinking WAY bigger. 

I was referred to Shane Orlick (President of JasperAi) who urged me to keep growing as quickly as possible.

See, he and the Jasper team had gone through a similar predicament.

After spending 2 years building a product that just didn’t work… they were contemplating selling and opening a restaurant. 

Clearly that didn’t happy. Instead, they threw spaghetti against the wall until Jasper ‘stuck’ and they exploded to a $1.5 billion valuation.

It’s pretty funny… I didn’t know I wanted a billion-dollar company until I saw my peers do it.

That’s called Mimetic Desire.

The lesson here? 2 things:

1) Think bigger. Like, a lot bigger.

2) Surround yourself with people who force you to do #1

You’ll be surprised what you’re capable of.

PS - follow along if you want to see inside info on exactly how I’m scaling my SaaS to $50M ARR in 2023.
