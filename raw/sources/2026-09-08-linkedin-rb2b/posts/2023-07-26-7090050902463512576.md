## Exact post text

I got my nuts kicked in during 1H23, but still somehow managed to grow Retention.com (profitably) from $15,000,000 to $20,000,000 ARR.

Here are the 6 biggest startup lessons I learned trying to go as fast as possible… and running into a brick wall:


1. No matter your revenue numbers, run a tight ship or the cash will dry up. 

Coming into 2023, I was desperate to get the brand EVERYWHERE. I sponsored newsletters, events and podcasts with no clear ROI. We hired as many people as we could as fast as we could (with zero regard for titles and comp packages). And I watched us go from generating $700k free cash to almost $0 for six straight months. That was all so dumb. I hate not making money. We fixed it this quarter. 


2. It’s far better to have a company with less employees and flood them with activity than to have a ton of people with a bunch of spare capacity (even if it means sacrificing some top-line growth).


3. Other people's playbooks are inspiration rather than prescription. 

I made the mistake of assuming that since the large Shopify apps like Klaviyo and Yotpo were able to make the BDR model work, that we would be able to do the same. WRONG. Every business is different. What worked for them isn’t necessarily going to work for us.


4. Organic social media is an effective method for generating awareness, but you have to be intentional about it. 

Last October I started posting on Linkedin every single day. The GOOD NEWS is we generated a ton of attention. The BAD NEWS is most of those people had no idea what we do. Now, I'm working on building the exact audience we want and creating content specifically for our buyers, rather than chasing followers.


5. To remain bootstrapped, we need to have higher ROI standards. 

We can’t spend the same amount of cash with the same output as companies who have raised $50-100M. We have to be more creative with how we do events, sponsorships, sales, literally everything.


6. Never forget who you are. 

I want to run a business that I look at and say “that’s an awesome f*cking business”. So, what does that look like? High revenue per employee. Insane monthly cash flow numbers. A culture that is fast-paced and addicted to activity. And healthy growth...between 50-100% per year. In Q1, we were the opposite of all this. But, we’re getting back to who we are. By the end of the year, we'll check each one of those boxes.
