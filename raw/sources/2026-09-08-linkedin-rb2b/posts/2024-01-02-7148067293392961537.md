## Exact post text

2023 was a PAINFUL year for Retention.com. We made 3 HUGE mistakes - overselling, overspending and overhiring. And yet we ended 2023 growing ARR 59% YoY and generated $1.35m free cash in Q4 alone.

Not only that… We’re about to launch a B2B product that seems like it’s a BIGGER opportunity than the ecomm business.

Here’s what I’m proud of (and not so proud of) that happened in 2023:

Proud of: 

- We assembled a team of absolute rock stars. 
- We ended the year with >25% EBITDA margins after being break-even for the first 9 months of the year
- We went from single digit employee count and zero processes to (almost) operating like a “normal” 60 person company
- We built a trusted brand in the Shopify ecosystem
- We built an amazing product and engineering team
- Our CS team became a well oiled machin
- We figured out who our TRUE ideal customer profile is
- I grew my Linkedin following from 10k to 60k and built an amazing community
- We are in beta with a B2B person-level ID product that I've been using since  November and now think is mind-blowing

Not so proud of: 

- I was delusional about our growth prospects. I thought we were going to hit $50m ARR in January (look at the slope of the line in Jan/Feb), but I was wrong by over ½. 
- We were overselling and didn’t realize it for the first 5 months of the year
- Our VP sales quit, which led us to downsize our sales org. It was the correct decision, but terrible
- We had 10 “key hires” (VP and director level) start on Feb 1, only 4 remain. BUT, our employee attrition was only 2.1% in total in Q4, so we have ended in a place of stability.
- I created a horrible spending culture that put us in a very uncomfortable cash situation. We're now acting like bootstrappers again, which I love
- We onboarded a BUNCH of customers earl in the year who we now realize weren’t going to have success, which led to churn and brand damage

Biggest Opportunities: 

- Santosh and I are spending all of our time on our B2B product. Our enthusiasm, along with our beta testers, continues to grow
- We decided to offer the b2b person-level identity product 100% FREE with unlimited use (you read that correctly). If you need to push data elsewhere, you pay in a PLG motion
- We're going to create the most immersive build-in-public that has ever been created, documenting the launch of the B2B product, warts and all.
- We will improve our DTC product to serve mid-market use cases, which will hopefully raise ACV & lower churn

There's much more, but that's all I could fit in a single post!

Bottom line:

Startups are hard. 

Building in public is harder. 

2023 felt like it was both the worst and best year of my career.

I’m so grateful for my team and to be in this situation...

A rock-solid, profitable, scaling core business, and a totally new, unknown project where we’re playing for global domination. 

What could possibly go wrong??? 

Hah!

Stay tuned. 

I’ll be reporting it all, the W’s and the L’s, right here on LinkedIn.
