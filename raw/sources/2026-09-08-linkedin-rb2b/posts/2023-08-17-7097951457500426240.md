## Exact post text

This guy is going to do $160,000,000 this year and at the beginning of COVID his business was just 3 sewing machines and an Etsy store.  

Meet Curtis Matsko, CEO of Portland Leather Goods.

How was he able to pull off such insane growth (so quickly)? 

If you have to ask, clearly you've never met Curtis

Here’s why:

1. This guy is truly one-of-a-kind:

- An unmatched energy and entertaining presence. 
- An unabashed creative who believes he can do anything
- A dreamer who knows he can play BIGGER than he’s playing today.

2. He has a KILLER team

- I’ve been sooo impressed with the 10 team members I have met
- They presented at Blue Whale Group and I was like “no wonder”
- Read “Amp It Up” by Frank Slootman - they are living that book

3. He delivers an incredible product at a ridiculous price

- These leather bags are super high-quality and only $85-$150
- Unlike most DTC, they will look better in 12mo than today
- It’s lower margin, but “that’s why they go viral” he says

4. He understands that above all else they are a marketing company

- They are first-order profitable and have a great media buyer
- They are near is Meta now, but they'll figure the rest out when the time comes
- They leverage Community (people go crazy for their bags)

5. This is all a game to him.

- It’s easy to take big risks when you don’t perceive any downside
- We should all try to lower the startup stakes to a “game” like Curtis 
- I can tell he’s having fun playing (most of the time)

One-of-a-kind companies have one-of-a-kind leaders. 

Curtis Matsko is one of those leaders.

Have any questions for him? Ask below 👇

If we get lucky maybe he'll stop by the comments and spill his secrets.
