## Exact post text

The other week I got some blowback from my 6sense post. A few of the sales influencers we lined up for a RB2B marketing campaign dropped out. Here's my question for LinkedIn:

If you got messages like this (see image) EVERY DAY, wouldn't you feel obligated to say something??

This is just ONE seller at a big tech company:

“My company bought 6Sense thinking it was the greatest thing since sliced bread. We’re getting less than 10% of numbers correct and my entire leadership has been sacked. I only wish they’d been able to see the tool for what it is - snake oil. Thank you for calling out these rats.”

But I get DMs like this one… LITERALLY… every day.

From VPs of Marketing, CMOs, CEOs and AEs.

From the whole GTM community.

If you need more examples, just read through the comments on any of my 6Sense posts.

There are so many people who feel they were sold expensive tech that doesn't work.

People who feel they were taken for a ride.

People who lost their jobs.

Some said my last 6Sense post was thirsty.

Other's have said it's time to move on.

But of the 1000+ comments I've gotten.

NOBODY said I was wrong.

So my question for you is this...

Should I move on?

If you got sent stuff like this, would you move on?

If in private you were bombarded by 6Sense hate…

But in public all you saw was 6Sense envy, with their glowing G2 reviews and Gartner rankings.

Or would you dig a little deeper to figure out why there's such a disconnect?

I’d love to know…

What would you do if you were me?
