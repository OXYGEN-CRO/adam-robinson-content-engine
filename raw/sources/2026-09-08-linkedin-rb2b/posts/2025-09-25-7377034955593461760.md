POP QUIZ FOR FOUNDERS: If you could start over and rebuild a 2027 AI-native version of your business, how different would it be? Getting the answer right is life or death for your startup👇

a) I would do nothing different - everything would be the same
b) Product/business would be marginally better, but bones remain 
c) It would be completely unrecognizable - everything would be different, exponentially more efficient, better, smaller

If you are being honest with yourself, you probably answered C. 

And guess what… 

If you have a decent business, some young founders are out there doing exactly that… 

Building an AI-native, 100x more efficient version of YOU.

The takeaway? 

If you don’t build the AI-native version of your business, someone else will.

And they will bury you. Full stop.

Don’t let that happen.

There’s still time.
