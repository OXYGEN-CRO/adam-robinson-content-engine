## Exact post text

1,850 paying customers, 13 months, $5m ARR, 5 employees. How did we get so far so fast at RB2B? Here's our 6-step startup playbook (and my message to founders who feel STUCK):

1. We created MASSIVE awareness.

Santosh and I spent the six months prior to launch building an audience of buyers. And doing discovery with over 300 of them to validate that what we had was worthwhile.

2. We threw a terrible MVP out there and got feedback.

We set people up with a pixel and emailed a spreadsheet to people and tried to charge them $495/mo. It didn’t work. Some said “I’d pay $199.” Others said “meh”. We kept throwing spaghetti against the wall. 

3. We found a magic moment - the Slack App.

Tate built a beta that pushed site visitors into Slack, with a headshot. I started showing people at dinners and parties on my phone, and saying “this person is actually ON our website right now”. I had never experienced this type of positive reaction to anything I had built in the past. I knew we were on to something.

4. We made that magic moment FREE.

I believe in the immense power of free, ESPECIALLY if you have a magic moment that you can make free. We screwed up on many things related to pricing along the way, but I think we got this one correct. I do not think we would be anywhere near where we are if Slack wasn’t free.

5. We listened to customer problems. But didn’t actually fix much.

If you’ve been following from home, you probably know that we have a churn problem. You may even be thinking “they really haven’t built much over the past year”. Customers gave us tons of feedback on the product and user experience, but I viewed the solutions they were asking for as the first steps to causing other problems. We have much better solutions now (on their way) so I’m glad I waited.

6. We stopped all marketing (besides me posting on LinkedIn). 

I thought the product and experience was so bad that we were just burning leads. I killed every channel but posting - which I’m able to do because we don’t have investors. We are starting to ramp other channels back up. We slowed down, to speed up.

TAKEAWAY

For anybody but an AI coding company, $0-$5m ARR in 13mo with 5 people is exceptional. 

Especially because it’s not just something we’re SAYING we’re doing… 

We actually DID it.

What’s the flip side? 

We’re stuck until we provide an experience that is different than today.

I’ve been stuck 6 times in my career. 

It feels just as bad at $25m ARR as it did when I was stuck at $3m. 

But here’s the good news… 

1. We have gotten unstuck 5 of the 6 times
2. I know how to get RB2B unstuck 

The anxiety of being stuck and the satisfaction that comes from getting unstuck is probably why I like the game so much in the first place. 

If you’re stuck, just know that you CAN get unstuck. 

I was right where many of you were 5 years ago, stuck at $3m for 3 years. 

And I got to $25m. Bootstrapped. Thanks to my lean, hungry team.

You can get there too.

Just keep building.
