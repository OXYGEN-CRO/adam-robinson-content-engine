## Exact post text

Michael Kurson (CEO of First Touch) is booking 5 demos/mo using RB2B.  

Here’s his 9 step playbook ANYBODY can use to turn site visitors into pipeline:

1. Install RB2B, connect with First Touch via webhook.

- First Touch is a platform for research, enrichment, and activation

2. Filter down to the top 10% of site visitors who are perfect ICP.

- Most web traffic is noise - focus on the best to not waste time

3. Enrich with deep company and contact-level research. 

- Michael does this through First Touch, many accomplish it through Clay
- What if RB2B could do this for you in Slack?!?!

4. Identify others in the buying group. 

- Michael uses First Touch to do this, we will do this for you soon

5. Multi-threaded connection requests on LinkedIn. 

- Michael has several people at his company reach out to several people at the account for a very strategic outreach operation
- He believes “if you are putting a site visitor into a sequence, this will not work”

6. Post content, hope for engagement

- Accepted connect requests will get served your content

7. If engagement comes, reach out around that engagement on multiple channels.

- Michael uses First Touch to help stack the second signal on the site visit

8. If engagement does NOT happen, perform deeper company research to find a trigger. 

- Perplexity deep research can come up with a reason for you to reach out by searching the web and relating it back to your domain in any scenario

9. Start omnichannel outreach based on the deep research. 

- Rinse and repeat.

TAKEAWAY

As it turns out, it’s still quite difficult to turn a site visitor into pipeline. 

But here’s the thing… 

It’s still WAY easier than prospecting a cold lead.

There’s no doubt that the web signal is the most valuable signal there is… 

But in the age of AI, to make a signal work, you need to have a process.

I ask Diana all the time - “why does everything have to be so hard?”

It’s 2025, in the age of AI. 

That’s just the way it is. 

And it’s only getting worse.
