## Exact post text

My startup has gone from 0 to $20M ARR in ~2.5 years, completely bootstrapped and profitable.

What I did is repeatable. Here's how:

I ran several businesses before starting Retention.com.

I sold one for a lot, but most of the others flopped

There was no 'secret formula'

Instead, I think of business building like this:

There are a few areas you absolutely need to nail when starting a business.

They are:

1) Great product
2) Understanding your customer
3) Repeatable sales process

Everyone knows this.

But they're much more complicated than people think.

While building my first few businesses, I was sharpening my skills.

Knowing your customer takes a lot of nuanced knowledge.

It sounds simple, but it takes years to master the in's and out's of your target audience

New founders always look for the hacks and shortcuts.

But experienced founders know that they have to just start with an educated guess, and then whittle down to the 'right' answer.

It takes months, if not years, to nail down your product. Let alone selling it.

How can you repeat what I've done?

By starting with your best guess, and then adjusting after you learn what your customers want.

My greatest successes took thousands of iterations before I nailed it.

Your first guess will be wrong.

But your 1,000th educated guess might be genius.
