## Exact post text

In 3 years, Retention.com went from an unknown startup struggling to close our first deal to a $20M ARR company serving the world’s top Shopify stores.

Here's our 5-step playbook for bootstrapping your startup:

1. Decide in your mind that you're going to GO FOR IT

- This may sound silly, but this change in mindset is hugely important
- It changes how you look at investments related to brand, events and visibility
- It needs to be the right time, but if it is, there’s nothing like being all-in

2. Start creating “Founder Brand” content on Linkedin and Twitter

- This raised the visibility of our brand more than anything else we have done. 
- I have been crazy about building in public, sharing financials, and using video.
- Be 100% transparent, share wins and losses. Don't hide anything.

3. Buddy up with all of the influencers in your space and start doing events

- Small, cool private events did well for us
- We found people with networks of brands and partnered with them
- DTC Surf Club in Austin, Post Malone booth in Toronto, Pizza Tour in NYC

4. Get on stage at a bunch of different venues (big, small, doesn't matter)

- GROW LA/NYC were my favorites … Get on stage with your best customers
- We sponsored tons of other events, but none have been as good as Grow
- Shout out to Greg Ashton for putting on a great event

5. Hire an incredible partnerships team

- In our business, the ecosystem is super important
- We have been able to get in front of every major agency
- It's a long-term game, but the potential reach is far & wide

This is what worked for us.

Will it work for you?

Maybe!

There's no one-size-fits-all approach.

You have to constantly experiment.

Test and see what gets you traction.

Then double down.

That's the key.

You got this!
