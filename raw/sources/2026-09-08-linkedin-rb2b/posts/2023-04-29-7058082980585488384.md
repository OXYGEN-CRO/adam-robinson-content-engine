## Exact post text

Human emotion is the rocket fuel for our growth and you can’t measure it…

It plays into what I call “Brand Heat.”

In short, it’s just straight-up excitement around your company.

The excitement of someone telling ten people that they’re 50X ROI is something you’ll never be able to measure. 

Once you start measuring sales numbers, it will make you raise prices to the point where people are no longer excited and talking to other people about it.

Allow space to consider the unmeasurable emotion around your brand and its pricing.
