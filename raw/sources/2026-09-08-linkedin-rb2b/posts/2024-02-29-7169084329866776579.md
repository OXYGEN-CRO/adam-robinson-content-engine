## Exact post text

In the past 90 days, using LinkedIn we generated 2,500+ signups and booked 328 meetings with VP and C-level marketers for our new Person-Level ID tool. Here’s our Inbound-Led Outbound playbook:

1. Create the BEST content. Full stop

You need killer headlines. Each line has to be so good it makes you want to read the next. You have to wrap it up with something coy and create a dopamine hit. Not only that, you have to have unique takes that provide a POV. It helps if you provide a level of transparency and authenticity no one else will. 

It took me a year to crack it and it’s STILL a ton of work. But, these posts drive insane engagement. They are automated trust-building machines, and when we put a call to action in them, it drives demand.

2. “DM me if you’re interested in…“

My LinkedIn consultants also do inbox management, because Santosh and I get 100s of inbounds a month. Depending on what we’re trying to accomplish, they either set calls or put them on a waitlist. 

We have far more demand than we can handle. Right now Santosh is taking 5 calls per day and Pete is taking 8. We aren’t selling, we are using these for discovery and relationship building, so we can find product market fit.

3. Spend WAY too much time in the comments

The comments section of LinkedIn posts is where B2B companies should build their initial community. You form real relationships with people. This is how your brand gets built, one comment at a time. Once your community is vibrant enough, you can take it off-platform. Until then, spend more time in the comments.

4. Share successes (and failures) by working in public

We’re all in this grind together. Everybody wants to know what everybody else is doing, what’s working and what’s NOT working. If you have traction and you share things about your business that others wouldn’t, ESPECIALLY your failures, you will build an audience.

5. Get other people posting

I had been the only person at my company posting until Labor Day. Something clicked right around then, and we wanted to get Santosh involved to try to improve the attack surface. I’m now posting 3 times per week, Santosh is up to 2, and Pete is going to start posting soon.

6. Connect with your site visitors over LinkedIn and Email

This is shamelessly self-promotional, but we’re booking several demos per day with the strategy. Using our Person-Level Website Visitor Identity tool, we connect with ICP visitors on LinkedIn, send them a thoughtful email, send a LI message +24h, send a 2nd email +24h.

7. Use Video

I don’t think we understand how powerful a talking head video is. The actual face, mannerisms, etc. I know that if people feel like they know me and trust me, they will try my product… And video is the quickest way to get there.

TAKEAWAY:

I’m dumbfounded by the power of our Inbound-Led Outbound model.

I’m trying to figure out ways to go even deeper.

We’re seeing a massive shift from push to pull models for SaaS GTM.

Nothing pulls quite like LinkedIn.
