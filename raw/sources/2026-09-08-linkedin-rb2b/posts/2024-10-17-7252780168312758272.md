## Exact post text

Building a LinkedIn founder brand is probably the best business move I've ever made. So naturally, a few months back, I thought... "How can we go BIGGER??"

BACKGROUND

If you were to ask me what RB2B is killing our competitors at, it's not features.

Yes, it's a hot product, but people are copying it left and right.

But what they can't copy is the content, the brand, the EDUTAINMENT. 

And the clearer we can tell our story...

And the better we get at telling it...

And the more people we tell it to...

That power only increases.

The question was, how do we widen the aperture? 

So, I reached out to Dave Gerhardt.

He wrote the book on Founder Brand.

And he suggested expanding to Youtube. 

So, down the Youtube rabbit hole I went...

I spent hours researching and watching videos.

I wanted to see what the BEST looks like.

I looked at Dan Martell, TK Kader, Rob Walling...

And I had 8 back to back meetings with YouTube people. 

But in the end, there wasn't a single person who thought we could easily replicate the success of Linkedin. 

And it wasn't clear to me what was ACTUALLY going to come from YouTube. 

Sure, we could build a YouTube audience.

We could get more people to watch more of my videos.

But would it be the same funnel of qualified people buying RB2B from Linkedin? 

That wasn't clear at all.

My goal is to build (and help you build) companies with a tiny team. 

If you're doing that, you don't always have the luxury of trying MORE.

You have to focus on a core set of things and do them in a wildly efficient way.

That means learning to say NO.

Not just to others, but to yourself.

Keep things simple.

Don't lose focus.
