## Exact post text

CEO Friend: We just crossed $4m ARR bootstrapped, but are break-even, stalling out, and have virtually no cash in the bank. I’m so stressed I can’t sleep. I’m thinking about raising $5-$7m in VC to invest in growth. What do you think about that idea?

Me: I hate that idea.

CEO: Why?

Me: If stress reduction is what you’re after, raising VC for growth will make your stress level even higher.

CEO: What? Why?

Me: There are three buckets you can fill with money - either from investors, or customers. 

Bucket 1: Pay yourself/Your team
Bucket 2: Build up a cash buffer
Bucket 3: Invest in growth

Filling the first two buckets will MEANINGFULLY reduce your stress levels. 

But the 3rd bucket won’t … 

ESPECIALLY if you add an outside partner, and you’re using THEIR money. 

You will be far, far more stressed, because from that day forward, if your business EVER stalls - like it stalled just now - the odds are that it won’t be worth more than the capital that went in. Which means you’re unlikely to see a dollar.

CEO: Hmm. I never thought of it that way. What are my options?

Me: If you were coming to me saying you’re stressed and you were going to take $5-$7m of secondary - investor capital straight in your pocket - that would be a different story.

If you’ve never made money before that would likely eliminate some stress … 

But if you did $10-$15m that would probably eliminate a LOT of stress.

CEO: Yeah … But I can’t really do secondary right now. We’re just not there yet. What then? 

Me: Growth stalling out is a sign of falling out of product-market fit. It happens all the time, to me included. 

When it happens, the only thing you can do is get as small as possible and find a new product-market fit. 

You may have to take a step or two back, but you’ll figure it out, get unstuck, and start moving forward again. 

CEO: Hmm - I didn’t even realize that was an option. I’m going to get smaller, build a war chest, and pay myself and our team w/ customer money. 

Thanks for the advice… I feel my stress level dropping already!
