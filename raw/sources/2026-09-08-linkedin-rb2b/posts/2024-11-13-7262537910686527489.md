## Exact post text

Yesterday the CEO of GMass said that RB2B was “The WORST SaaS he’s ever used”. Here’s my response to this EPIC shitpost:

Backstory: 

- I have (incorrectly) sold this tool as a source of leads and demos until now
- That is not what it is, nor is it a source of emails to blast… It is a signal
- That being said, implying accuracy is 0% is factually incorrect
- No one who does anything like we do has better accuracy
- But shitposting ME gets you visibility

Here’s 4 Reasons why this guy doesn’t know how to use RB2B:


1. This isn’t for a site that gets 1000 signups (mostly overseas) in a day

The value of resolving a website with a US-based publisher network - as was highlighted by several people in the comments - is low-volume prospecting of US prospects. If 80% of your users are spammers from overseas, we’re not going to match them.


2. Here’s a famous unicorn who has actually tested the data’s results:

- 33,000 pilot size
- 1,800 ICP visitors - REALLY scrubbed the data hard
- Sent to 1,800 ICP visitors (NOT 33,000)
- 10x higher response rate than any outbound channel

So… They upsized to a 700,000 contact annual deal, closed yesterday.

This is how you use RB2B.


3. Retention.com gets value daily (that would be impossible w/ 0% accuracy):

In the last 24 hours, we’ve had the CEO, Chief Data Officer, and SVP of Legal of a company that acquired one of our top partners hit our site - because R! would be a great follow-on acquisition.

Other sightings from yesterday:

- Founder of huge client we lost last month
- Senior lifecycle manager at large ecomm agency
- Senior global campaign manager of massive consumer app (dream prospect)
- VP ops of large publisher (new TAM for us, just closed a huge deal here)
- Head of product innovation at a current customer
- Large growth equity PE firm
- Owner of small Shopify store worth talking to

This was out of a total of 53 profiles.

If what the GMass CEO said is true, yesterday couldn’t have happened.


4. MadKudu found we matched 65% of their form fills (the person, not email)

Check out Sam Levan’s post about us vs. Clearbit: https://lnkd.in/ggkRncgU

Our coverage was lower (because we only do US for now).

But we got the person correct for 84% of their form fills. 


TAKEAWAY

This is a really annoying post. 

But here’s the thing.

We need to change the narrative around this tech.

- It’s not to create a list of leads to spam 
- It’s to filter out signal from the needle in the haystack
- It’s to nudge people with the right message at the right time

I agree if you look at RB2B how he looked at it, it looks bad.

But our big customers are filtering down to ICP and engaging appropriately. 

We have a lot to build, but if we’re able to: 

- Filter the signal 
- Route it appropriately, and  
- Tell the rep exactly what to do 

RB2B has the potential to be a KILLER product.

But we’re a long way from that reality. 

So, in the meantime, Gmass CEO… 

Keep shitposting.

You know I like it.
