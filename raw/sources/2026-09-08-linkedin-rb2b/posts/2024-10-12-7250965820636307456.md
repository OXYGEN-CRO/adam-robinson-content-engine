## Exact post text

You don’t need 100,000 followers to be WILDLY successful on LinkedIn. Here are my 3 favorite stories of people who had their mind blown by the power of LinkedIn:

1. Curtis Matsko, CEO of Portland Leather Goods

Curtis is a good friend and loyal customer of Retention.com.

/// Curtis's LinkedIn process ///

Once per week, he opens his laptop, hits record, and creates 7 videos in a 30min sitting with his coworker, Mariana (who LinkedIn believes is his wife … but is not).

Zero planning. They just talk about what they are working on and thinking about. His assistant then posts the videos daily.

/// The Results ///

His last 3 major hires have come INBOUND from people who have watched his Linkedin videos. They showed up and said, "I love what you’re doing, I know EXACTLY what you’re all about, and I want to get involved. I’m all-in!"


2. Megan Bowen, CEO of Refine Labs

Megan is business partners with one of LinkedIn OG’s - Chris Walker - but set about building her following very differently.

She surgically connected to every single member of her ICP with a consistent connection request campaign over years. Her content then warms them up, and gives her the ability to open conversations with them.

/// Megan’s LinkedIn Process ///
 
2020-2021: 
- 5X / week LI posts 
- Co hosted DGL with Chris
- 5-10 connection requests / day

2022-2023: 
- 2-3X / week LI posts
- Took a step back from podcast focused on running biz
- Continued 5-10 connections requests 3x / week on average

2024: 
- Became CEO, bumped activity back up to 3-5X / week
- Got back into podcasting for Stacking Growth
- 5-15 new connection requests / week

This is the playbook Alec Paul advocates, and would run with someone if they were just starting out.

The true power of LinkedIn is getting connected to your ICP, then having them see your content for a few months. As Alec once told me, “You literally become a celebrity in their eyes by the time you ask them if they want to chat.”

/// The Results ///

Demo book rates through the roof!


3. Robb Clarke, Head of Technical Ops at RB2B

Robb runs our entire support effort at RB2B. I encouraged him to make himself a LinkedIn creator when we launched. He committed to posting 3x per week.

/// The Results ///

With only 1,800 followers, Robb has been featured on multiple pieces of high-reach content, webinars with brands like Intercom, and was flown to attend the Pioneer conference in London!

TAKEAWAY:

LinkedIn is the most powerful networking vehicle on the planet.

Even if your goal isn't to generate leads, you can quickly and efficiently create content that will benefit you.

If you’re CEO, every person who: 

- Wants to work for you 
- Wants to invest in your company
- Wants to partner with your company

Will check out your LinkedIn profile.

Why not turn that profile into a trust-building machine?

Curtis, Megan, and Robb sure have…

They all understand that trust = brand.

And in 2024, brand is built one post at a time.
