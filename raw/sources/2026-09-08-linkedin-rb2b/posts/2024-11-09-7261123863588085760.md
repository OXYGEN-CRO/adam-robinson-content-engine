## Exact post text

Most SaaS founders *think* they need to build a product that saves time or makes money, then a big Go-To-Market machine once they find Product-Market Fit. But in 2024, the best founders know they need to build 3 things:

- An addictive product that is engineered to create dopamine hits
- Embedded virality that can lead to SUSTAINED exponential growth
- Trust-building machines (like community and founder brand content)

It’s easy when you simplify it like this.

In 2024-2025, these will be the only true moats.

Master the art of bottoms-up.

Because true disruption always comes from below.
