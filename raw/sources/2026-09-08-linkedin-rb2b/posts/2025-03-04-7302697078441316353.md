## Exact post text

February was ROUGH, we're down 1.1% at $25m ARR. Retention.com dropped 3.2%, but RB2B grew 8.7% to $4.8m ARR. I was scared Q1 would be bad, but we have a MAJOR problem I didn't see coming:

We’re in transition right now. 

Again. 

It's the hardest part of the year if you sell to ecommerce and retail.

So what the heck is going on at Retention.com (-3.2% m-o-m)???

- We have to move upmarket to reduce churn (where our product works better)
- We have case studies vs. incumbents that are SO good they don’t make sense
- We have convos going with retailers we wouldn't have dreamed of 12mo ago

BUT… 

- Our Shopify audience buys in 1 month max
- The enterprise market buys in 9-12 months (once they are in-market at all)
- All our focus is on building pipeline of those larger, stickier, slower deals

If you add all of that together in Q1, what do you get? 

- Seasonally high churn (we’ve now shrunk in q1 4/5 years)
- Bucket is not getting refilled with high-velocity smaller deals
- We are shrinking and it’s difficult to forecast when it’s going to stop

fack.

——————

I had a very strange emotional experience this month. 

We spent 4 days in Mexico with our entire team. 

It was one of those super-nice resorts between Cancun and Playa.

15% business, 85% hanging out. 

We hung out on the beach, had a sunset catamaran afternoon, big amazing dinners on grassy lawns by the ocean … 

All 30 who made it were so happy to be there and happy to be part of the team.

It was a dream come true. 

But that wasn’t what I was most excited about. 

What I was most excited about was morning 2 when Diana presented for an hour on the Enterprise deal we closed with Bloomreach in December. 

How complex our implementation was, how much value we provided vs. the brand’s existing solution, and how big of a win it was for us, the brand, AND Bloomreach.

That whole afternoon - and for the next week - I was on Cloud 9. 

“We’re going to pull this thing off … We’re actually going to be able to move upmarket,” I kept telling myself in my head. 

Reality hit me like a ton of bricks in an uber on the way home as I was talking to Tate (CTO). 

Tate: “Did you see the baremetrics this week? Down 3.5%.”

[My heart sped up. I felt dizzy and a bit sick to my stomach]

Me: “What?” 

Tate: “All of that enterprise stuff sounds so amazing. It seems like a foregone conclusion … But are they going to be able to do it fast enough?”

Me: [speechless, looking at my feet]

Sigh.

——————

So here I sit, once again, in a year of total transition.

But this time is so different than the others. 

- I’ve never been so optimistic about our future
- I’ve never been so excited about our teams and leadership, but
- I have never been so confused about the short term and this pickle we’re in

Euphoria, then despair. Back to back.

Welcome to my world as a startup founder.

There’s only one thing to do. 

Keep building.
