## Exact post text

Last November we bought 6sense for $18,000 so we could compare it to Clearbit and our Person-Level Identity tool. Last week our rep told us renewal will cost $55,000!  Here are my 6 theories why they are 3X-ing our pricing and don't want us using their tool:

1. 6Sense knows their data is terrible

Let’s be honest with ourselves. 

Company/Account level site visitor identity isn’t nearly as valuable as Person-Level ID, and the more we compare results of our data vs theirs, the higher likelihood we are to have a case study reporting how many more demos we booked using our tech (which is free) than theirs. The good news for 6Sense is that our sales team thinks it’s so bad they won't use it!

2. 6Sense is worried what we’ll find when we compare it to Clearbit and our product. 

We actually DID this, and the results were very interesting. 

There was single-digit overlap between the three - Clearbit, 6Sense, and RB2B. 
What this tells me is that if you can make one of them work, you should use them all. If you can’t make any of them work, you should only use the free one.

3. 6Sense is actually worried about the VIRAL expansion of our free tool.

We don’t have tech we could sell to a mid-market 6Sense customer yet, but we will. In the meantime, we’ve had 4,780 people sign up with an 80% self-serve script install rate. 

Are they seeing people churn off an $18-100k solution to use a free one? 

If that is actually happening, that would worry me too.

4. 6Sense knows that their 3rd party intent signal isn’t a buying signal. 

Content consumption doesn’t equal purchase intent. 

Genius marketing makes you believe that it does.

The market COULD BE starting to understand that most of the value of 6Sense is in different methods of retargeting website visitors, in which case, our Person-Level Website Visitor Identity tool is in direct competition with the most valuable use case at 6Sense. So, they are figuring out the nicest possible way to say “F OFF”.

5. 6Sense's pricing is just wrong, and they are scared of the future.

I heard that they will GUESS the contacts on your site for another $30k/year, on top of the tens of thousands that you’re already paying for an inferior product to what we’re giving away for free.

6. 6Sense wanted me to post about it.

If THIS was the goal, mission accomplished. 

You got the eyeballs of my LinkedIn audience!

TAKEAWAY:

6Sense doesn’t want us to use their product. 

I can understand why. 

It’s so bad that our sales team won’t even use it... 

Even though we paid $18,000 for it!

Surely they must be hearing the noise of what RB2B is up to, and the positive response we’re getting.

Here’s my biggest question...

Should we just pay the $55,000 to piss them off and make them ask... 

“Well, what do we do now?!?!?!"
