## Exact post text

Last year Retention.com went from $600k-$1.3M per month.

Here are the 5 biggest breakthroughs we had scaling a SaaS product for Shopify store owners to near $20M ARR:

1. We figured out our ICP.

- We realized big Shopify stores never churned
- We built our Reclaim product in Q3 and it absolutely crushed
- We stopped giving demos to anyone else

2. We started selling annual deals.

- My CRO Diana and I previously thought this was impossible
- We do a 12mo deal w/ a 60-day out
- We’ve sold over 600 of them since October of last year
- We now have $12M ARR that has rolled past the opt-out date

3. We brought on Santosh Sharan.

- He is a serial unicorn builder
- He is showing us how to architect exponentially
- He’s known as “the godfather of contact data”

4. We hired 50 people in Q4 of last year.

- We had 6 people in October.
- This was probably way too fast, but we thought we needed to land grab
- We had to ask for forgiveness … No onboarding, training, etc
- We are just now getting the organization efficient again

5. We invested in RevOps.

- We have been wrestling with Salesforce for 9mo
- Our reporting is getting better every week (still room to go)
- We are just now seeing signs of hope in RevOps


So to recap 2022:

1 - We figured out our ICP.
2 - We started selling annual deals.
3 - We brought on Santosh Sharan.
4 - We hired 50 people in Q4 of last year.
5 - We invested in RevOps.

TAKEAWAY:

What got us to 600k MRR didn’t get us to 1.3m MRR.

We think we can double once, maybe twice, with the building blocks we have, but it’s not going to get us to 9-figures of ARR, which is my personal goal for this business.

We’ve already got many experiments in the works…

Things that might get us there.

It's going to be an exciting 2023.

Stay tuned!
