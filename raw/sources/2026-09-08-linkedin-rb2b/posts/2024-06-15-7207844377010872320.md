## Exact post text

In 19 months, I grew my LinkedIn followers from 1,000 to 84,000. Every month I get millions of impressions, thousands of likes and comments (and it's all ICP). Here's a behind-the-scenes look at my Linkedin journey: 
  
When I started posting, I was lost, frustrated and conflicted.

I had no idea what to say or who to say it to.

There were 5 MAJOR PHASES of my LinkedIn journey.

Here's who I was working with and what I learned:
 
1) Tommy Clark, B2B SaaS CEO Ghostwriter Extraordinaire
 
October – Jan 2022
 
19 months ago I decided we needed to hone in on an ICP – large Shopify stores – and within that ICP we had zero competition, low penetration, and an awareness problem.
 
I read "Founder Brand" by Dave Gerhardt, but I was afraid to get started.
 
- I didn’t want to start posting against the best in to world
- I didn’t want to get on the Social Media treadmill
- I didn’t want to be famous (so I told myself)
 
I met Tommy, who convinced me that I should start on LinkedIn because the ecosystem was there(ish), and there was a creator/user imbalance still in favor of creators.
 
So, we started.
 
I remember coming home from an event and writing my first post.

It took 2 hours.

The results?

12 likes. 3 comments.
 
The next day, Tommy posted a screenshot of a tweet of someone I had never heard of before.
 
2,750 likes, 450 comments.
 
I sat back and observed for a while.
 
I had this feeling I heard other CEOs articulate after they start posting:
 
“I’m not sure what this is doing for me, but it’s doing SOMETHING”.
 
2) Tommy + Video

January – June 2023
 
I'm convinced that video creates a deeper connection than text alone.
 
Also, if you're willing to say and do things others are NOT, you will stand out.
 
I was willing to share financials, be transparent about our journey – ups and downs, and my company was at $12m ARR with 6 FTE when I started… All of which was unique.
  
I wanted to add video to my LinkedIn effort, but didn’t know how.
 
I was still conflicted about trying to create content for ecommerce store owners having not been an ecommerce store owner or service provider.
 
So, I started cranking out daily work-in-public videos that were “hook, story, three lessons, soft CTA” format, which Tommy attached a light text post to.
 
These got an average of 30 likes, a handful of comments, but I DID notice several people at Shoptalk saying they watched my videos every day and loved them.
 
Around nine months in, I still felt like something was wrong.
 
3) Alec Paul – Ecomm Content
 
July 2023 – August 2023
 
I saw Alec Paul on a webinar with Sam Jacobs.
 
I loved Sam’s content, and thought it came across more “thought leader”.
 
I wanted to deeply understand the LinkedIn machine, and what made great posts that got 1000's of likes.
 
Alec also had a lead gen motion which he implemented with his clients.
 
So, I started working with Alec.

And that's when things got INTERESTING....

**Ran out of room!
