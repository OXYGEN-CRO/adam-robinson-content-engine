## Exact post text

In late 2023, my CFO grilled me about my Founder Brand investment. She said I was wasting time, being an ego maniac, and lighting $20k/month on fire to film a docu-series about myself. And she was right.

My first year of content creation was going terribly.

My CFO questioned everything:

– None of my social accounts had any followers
– None of my posts were getting any engagement
– I was spending insane money making a docuseries
– We couldn’t track any of it back to a single demo

I hadn’t seen the line-by-line breakdown of the costs.

I hadn't see the lack of results laid out like this.

She said, "We’re telling people to cut costs and you’re spending $50k/m on THIS."

I almost gave in.

I almost said, “Fine, I’ll stop. I’ll focus on improving the product.”

Instead, I said, "Give me 12 months to figure this out."

Because I saw something...

The real effects were coming to life for the first time on LinkedIn and Twitter. 

I showed her the inbound I was getting from recruits, partners, investors.

But I was sure I was going to be at war with her on this. 

A constant struggle to get invoices approved, always brought up in meetings…

Then the next morning, I got a Slack message.

With a note: "Thanks for explaining all of that yesterday. I still think this is ridiculously expensive, but I can see the upside. You have my blessing."

18 months later, I was at 135,000 followers on LinkedIn, had started a BRAND NEW company and RB2B was at $5.5m ARR… 100% from the founder brand.

What's my point?

Your team isn't there to be yes-people. 

They're there to make you think harder, defend better, and execute smarter. 

When my CFO challenged me, she wasn't being difficult.

She was being a fiduciary. She was doing her job.

But it's YOUR JOB as a founder to have a vision and understand your best investments can often look like terrible decisions at first. They require you to see around corners that others can't.

That $20k/month "ego project" became the foundation of a $5.5M ARR business. The "waste of money" became our most profitable channel.

Trust your gut. Defend your vision. But most importantly?

Give it the runway it deserves to take off.
