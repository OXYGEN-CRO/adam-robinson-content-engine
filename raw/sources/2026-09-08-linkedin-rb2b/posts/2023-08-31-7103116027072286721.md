## Exact post text

After 5 months of getting smacked by the slowdown, crushed by churn and devastated by downsizing, I am *finally* confident Retention.com has turned the corner. Here’s what I’m proud of (and not so proud of) that happened in August: 

Proud of:

- We crossed a major milestone - $20m ARR in Baremetrics
- We’re back in the black! Out of the PAINFUL breakeven period
- We are building an increasingly deep understanding of the 60-day contraction problem that was the major headwind since April and cleaning up the core business
- We have made a handful of pricing changes that we believe will change pricing from a headwind to a tailwind
- We are in beta for a $77/mo PLG product aimed at smaller Shopify stores
- We have big early enthusiasm for our product for B2B sales teams (DM me)
- We have a lean and mean, super high velocity sales motion and reps who are drowning in demand and the size feels great
- I have figured out how to write some viral Linkedin posts that could REALLY help with our B2B demand generation

Not so proud of:

- I was hoping to report our monthly growth rate was increasing again (it didn’t)
- We got smacked by a huge legacy publisher contraction and non-core churn that put us back at the 3-ish% total topline growth rates we have been seeing
- We don’t have the analytics yet to look at the core annual subscription rev which we know looks great from Salesforce but can’t see yet
- I HATE the messaging on our website, which is totally my fault
- Our RevOps isn’t yet at the state where we need it to be, despite pushing hard
- Despite massive progress on the customer journey, it is clear that we are under-resourced post-sale, which is leading to some sub-optimal customer experiences

Biggest Opportunities: 

- Santosh is starting full-time today! He’s going to help us improve operational rigor, own the PLG motion on the DTC side, and help with many strategic aspects of the B2B biz
- We hired Charlie to help us with our product marketing, which has been a massive gap
- By the end of September we will be able to look at analytics by revenue type
- We should be getting valuable customer feedback on the B2B product and PLG DTC product that should help us with targeting and messaging
- I will be further honing my Linkedin skills to create maximum awareness through founder brand in the new B2B universe we will be targeting
- We should start generating $350k+ free cash as the months go on (phew!)

August wasn’t as good as I wanted it to be. 

I truly hoped to report a higher growth rate from a higher total revenue number. 

While I can’t report that, I do believe that we’ve turned the corner. 

We’re back on track to be the lean, mean, very profitable and rapidly scaling company that I have always sought to create.

Sometimes it’s the darkest before dawn.

Hang in there, keep building.
