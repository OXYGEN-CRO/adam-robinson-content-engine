“Do you know what happened on April 23?” I asked him.

“No… what happened?” 

“On April 23, Apple made it so that Meta and Klaviyo can’t track a user longer than 7 days."

His jaw dropped.

He couldn't believe it.
 
–

BACKGROUND:

In layman terms, here's what happened on April 23rd:

There is a set of pipes that connects your Shopify site to Meta and Klaviyo. 
 
Those pipes used to last for many months. 

Now, Apple breaks those pipes after 7 DAYS. 

Meaning, historically if a visitor clicked on a Meta ad, browsed around, and came back to the site a month later, Meta could serve them a retargeting ad. 

That worked beautifully, and the ability to track that returning visitor’s behavior was a big part of the magic behind Meta's infamous targeting machine.

Now, if someone clicks on a Meta ad and comes back just 8 days later, Meta doesn’t know who they are, and can’t show them an ad.

Back to the executive...
