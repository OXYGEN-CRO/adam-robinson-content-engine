## Exact post text

I’ve been getting hate on LinkedIn because people think I’m saying outbound sales is dead. So let me be clear... Outbound sales is NOT dead! But the old “Predictable Revenue” model used by most SaaS companies is. 

Here are 6 reasons why we need a new outbound playbook for 2024:

1. Inboxes are completely destroyed

My co-founder texted me this morning and asked how I can possibly manage all of the prospecting emails that we get blasted with every day. The answer? I can't. 99.9% get deleted or ignored. 

2. LinkedIn inboxes might be even worse

Despite LinkedIn’s attempts to control the spam on the platform, my inbox here is just as bad if not worse than email. I had 4,200 incoming connection requests in the last 3 weeks and 70% of the new messages I get are pitches.

3. The number of SDRs has grown exponentially 

Predictable Revenue was a GREAT book (I read it many times) and it birthed an SDR industry on the back of the idea that pipeline generation can be accomplished through assembly-line tactics, and more bodies = more rev. This was true. It's no longer the case.

4. Automation tools and $12 domains made everything worse

People have replaced the ideas of resonance and relevance (quality) with VOLUME (quantity) as their primary goal of an outreach program, which combined w/ #3 has led to #1.

5. AI tools are sweeping the leg

Every day more and “better” messages are being created by large language models, getting stuck into #4 by #3, leading to #1

6. Teams got scaled too big because you can measure their output

A booked demo can be measured, so if you want more of them, you add more bodies. My argument is that you can capture the same amount of demos with 20% of the bodies.

TAKEAWAY:

In 2023, buyers aren’t responding to cold outreach like they used to. 

I know a dozen founders who have scaled back their BDR teams and have seen no change in pipeline generation (me included).

Why? 

Because in the Predictable Revenue days, demand was created by SDRs, because the channels available to them weren’t ruined.

In today’s world, SDR’s are (mostly) an expensive and inefficient way of capturing existing demand.

The new playbook for building SaaS revenue machines in 2024 involves creating content, driving indirect leads through ecosystem and partners, and running a super-lean in-house sales team whose outreach is driven by inbound and high-value intent data.

Outbound is alive and well...

But SDR's prospecting cold leads IS dead.
