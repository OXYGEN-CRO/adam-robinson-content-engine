## Exact post text

We finished November at $24.7m ARR (-0.5%) with Retention.com at $20.7m ARR (-1.5%) and RB2B at $4m ARR (+7%). Why did we shrink this month? Here comes the SHOCKER: 

CHURN

The main story is that RB2B finally hit a wall with an 11% monthly churn rate. 

We still grew 7%, but it flatlined the last two weeks.

Am I annoyed? 

Tremendously. 

This happened way sooner than I thought it would… 

And it means we’re stuck (again).

Am I discouraged? 

Not at all. 

Why not? 

1. We are solving the filtering/routing/what now problems RIGHT NOW with our product

2. We are improving the data quality and coverage next quarter

3. Diana Ross is joining us to build out a proper sales and onboarding motion (go follow her, she's going to document the entire thing on LinkedIn)

4. Revenue churn of our $119 plan is 19%/mo, anything above $1000/mo is 3%/mo

...#4 kind of tells you everything you need to know.
 
Nine months into the Retention.com journey our churn was far worse than this.

I know we can chop it in half… which is still bad, but enough to get us to $15-20M ARR. 

And we’ll do it with under 15 people.

What else am I excited about? 

Despite shrinking 1.8%, there are some very exciting things happening at Retention.com:

1. We closed our first $50k/mo 2yr deal (which hasn’t hit our numbers yet)

2. We landed 17 mid-market deals since July 1 (defined as over 250 employees)

3. We did our first true enterprise POC, and it was so successful the client was worried about how to present it internally

4. We’re getting traction with TikTok enrichment, which we’re finding is a real pain point 

We’ve also been focusing on operational efficiency and ALMOST hit one of my epic moonshot goals…

We generated $979,043 (+12%) profit, which is within spitting distance of $1m/mo.

TAKEAWAY

It sucks to post about down months.

I’m two years into this and it still seems like I’m the only one who does.

Here’s the problem… 

Monthly ARR in high churn product categories is a backward-looking indicator.

And while we were soft this month, we were up 3.8% last month.

Also in our world people care more about your top line than the fact that you made epic efficiency improvements and are running a super lean, profitable company.

But I’m committed to building in public. 

Why? 

Because this is what I would have wanted to see from someone 5 years ago.

I wanted to see when companies I admired ran into walls… 

To understand why, and how they planned to get moving again. 

So yeah, we’re stuck… again… 

But you know we’re going to try to get unstuck.

And you’ll get to see if it works!

Because we’re going to put our heads down…

And keep building.
