Yesterday I claimed that I was ‘manifesting’ some absurd outcomes:

1. $50M ARR by the end of the year. Only 250% growth from where we are now. Really not that crazy.
2. A small Q3 investment round from Shopify. Triple Whale just did it. We can too.
3. A 9-figure secondary round at a unicorn valuation in 2024. 

But how? These are the 4 strategies I have for you to steal:

1. BELIEVE

It’s in all caps on purpose.

You absolutely *need to* believe that this is possible deep in your soul.

Most people mistake believing for thinking.

Thinking something might happen is different from believing it will.

Think about something as true as what color shirt you’re wearing. You believe you’re wearing a white shirt. It’s obvious. You’re literally looking at it.

That’s the type of certainty you need to have.

2. Map out people

You shouldn’t try to make stuff happen solo.

Even though it’s possible, it can happen faster if you have other people involved.

I already know which people I need to invite into my manifestation craze to get investments from Shopify and raise a 9-figure Series A next year.

I also know which team members will be most crucial to hitting $50M ARR.

I’m talking to investors via email and DMs, getting on calls with them, and selling hard.

Starting now. A year in advance.

The world is all about who you know.

3. Bulletproof stories

No matter what the numbers look like, you need to create a story for what’s going to happen, why, and how.

The quantifiable difference between a good story and a great story is a billion dollars.

You need to know how to tell a story not to fool people, but to properly demonstrate YOUR belief, so that other people can believe, too.

Life is people believing stories.

That is the secret to manifestation.

The stories I told my team about how to shoot up to $50M ARR is because I showed them (with spreadsheets) that if we start putting basic effort into one single channel, we’ll have colossal growth.

Same for Shopify.

Same for investors.

Nobody can argue with these stories because the growth and the metrics are so astounding.

We have the proof to show that these stories are legitimate, but it’s on you (the manifestor) to tell those stories.

4. Persistence

Do. Not. Give. Up.

If you accept this belief as your reality, there is no giving up.

It’s all about how far you’re willing to push to make reality happen.

That’s it.

Start manifesting.

Best of luck.

Oh. And if you want to watch me bring these manifestations to life — I’ll be revealing every step of the journey right here, in public, on my LinkedIn profile. Follow along if you want.
