## Exact post text

9 things that will kill you as a first time startup founder:

1. You can’t convince a real CTO to work for you (you don’t even know how to find one)

2. You don’t pair up with the right sales and marketing co-founder

3. You build way too much product as your MVP. This is almost everybody

4. You raise too much money too early at too high of a valuation

5. You try to do too many things because everything seems so exciting

6. You copy the industry leaders’ feature set and attempt to compete on price

7. You don’t realize that you should spend as little money as humanly possible

8. You try to solve a less painful problem for a lot of people, rather than a very, very painful problem for a much smaller group of people

9. You don’t realize that an "overnight success" will take at least 10 years

What would you add to the list?
