## Exact post text

At Retention.com, we're trying to put everyone on our team on a trajectory so that their NEXT job is even better.

In today’s world, no role is permanent. Especially in tech or eCommerce. Nobody is going to do the same thing for 40 years like they used to.

If you were to build a sports team, you want the best athletes, but you also need them to fit in your company’s culture. When they don’t, chalk it up as a loss and move on.

So, what are the lessons I’ve learned from hiring?

✅ Hire A-Players, not family members. You want to build a team that can crush the competition and that doesn’t

✅ Be the stepping stone that helps them on their way to the top.

✅ Be the light in your market that attracts the best people. Like a moth to a flame, or a mosquito to your light.

✅ Let go of people who aren’t working out quickly. As I always say, “If there’s any doubt, there’s NO doubt.”

What would you add?
