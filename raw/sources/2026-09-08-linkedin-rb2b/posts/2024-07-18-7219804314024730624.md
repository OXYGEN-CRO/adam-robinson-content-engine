## Exact post text

Last quarter Dave Gerhardt told me to let Eli Rubel re-do our RB2B website and branding. The current version is a $79 webflow template called “SaaS”. When I spoke to Eli, I told him I only care about one thing:

Give me ugly, shocking branding that at least 80% of people in SaaS will hate.

Why?

Here are the 5 reasons I want RB2B to have hideous branding that makes peoples’ skin crawl:

1. I hate the sterile, inauthentic typical Silicon Valley SaaS branding.

Take a look at 6Sense’s website and you’ll know exactly what I’m talking about. The soft color palette, the politically correct stock photos, the stupid robot, the endless buzzwords … It all makes me want to vomit.


2. I believe that pattern interrupt is the best marketing strategy.

Nails on a chalkboard will make people stop and pay attention.

And we are in an attention war, full stop.


3. Two years later, people start copying you.

We rebranded Retention.com in late 2022. I loved it. It was weird, aggressive, strange colors, it didn’t look like any SaaS site I had ever seen. We did a poll when we were picking brands and it was dead last of the four options. That was a signal.

I was not surprised to see that a wannabe competitor OpenSend copied us nearly identically 18mo later (seriously, go look at it).


4. I remember how weird Mailchimp’s 2018 rebrand was, and how it made me feel.

Does anyone else remember looking at that yellow website and just thinking “what am I looking at?”

This was after everyone and their mother in SaaS copied their prior branding.

They are legends.

Then Intuit bought them, and made them look like 6Sense :/


5. Russell Brunson is my hero.

I first saw the Clickfunnels site in 2017 and had no idea what I was looking at.

It seemed like a kindergartner got ahold of a website builder and slapped something together in 1 hour

I couldn’t figure out how he found people that looked like the actors in the explainer video. They looked so unapologetically “normal” … I had never seen anything like it.

But they were actors (I think).

I dismissed it as garbage, just like everybody else in B2B.

But as I dug deeper, I realized that he was in the process of growing the fastest growing bootstrapped SaaS ever.

Still, everyone wrote them off, nobody has tried to do what he has done since…

Mostly because of who he is and who he was selling to.

____

TAKEAWAY

Eli loves the fact that I want branding that is different from anything that has ever existed.

Something that will make people FEEL something…

Instead of this drab, bland, sterile, inauthentic crap that everyone else makes.

Designers LOVE working on projects like that.

He was like...

“We could do the standard agency onboarding… or you could let us wing it”.

I chose the latter.

Last week we had our first call where he revealed the branding...

I absolutely LOVED it.

Below is my reaction as we went through what they put together. 👇

Want to see what’s behind the curtain???

Stay tuned…
