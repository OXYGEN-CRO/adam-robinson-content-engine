Will 6sense have to do a massive Twitter-style layoff by 2026? Here’s my take on why 6Sense is a “Fake Unicorn” and MY detailed 23/24 P&L (so you know where I’m coming from):

To set the stage: 

Last month 6Sense put out a press release about crossing $200m ARR.

They said they grew revenue +40% last year, and I hear they are shooting for +30% this year.

What the article DIDN’T say is that: 

1. 6Sense has raised a total of $526m to create that $200m ARR 
2. The last $100m was Venture Debt in June of 2023 - the legalized equivalent of loan sharking
3. I hear that they are going to BURN $25m this quarter

And as a kicker, the plan is to IPO next year.

As you can tell from looking at MY P&L, none of this makes sense to me. 

Unless I am doing the math wrong, 6Sense’s “Rule of 40” is currently -10.

I believe that 6Sense is in a very dire situation.

Here’s the THREE THINGS 6Sense can do to survive, and what will happen if this plan fails.

___

BACKSTORY:

In the fall, I was an outsider to B2B. 

I started asking people about the landscape of ABM tech. 

The first thing I heard was: 

“6Sense created a ‘Dark Funnel’ narrative that hypnotized the CMO community. They all bought the tech. The dirty little secret is the tech just doesn’t do what they say it does. Hardly anyone makes money.” 

That sounds strange to hear for the first time.

But when you say it to someone who has been in B2B for a while, you watch as heads nod.

Another weird thing is the fact that their G2, Glassdoor, and Gartner presence is 95% positive, but when I post ANYTHING about 6Sense it elicits a 95% negative response.

Here is what you will NEVER hear when you ask around:

“6Sense is a great product.” 

___

So 6Sense is burning uncontrollably and needs to pull a rabbit out of their hat to survive.

What would I do if I were in this position?


Step 1: 

I would fly around to industry events and CMO meetups to deliver the following message: 

“We figured it out. We’re crushing it this year with OUTBOUND.  And it’s ALL BECAUSE OF OUR OWN TECH!!!!”

The community of CMOs eats the message up. 

Finally there is a solution to the SaaS Pipeline Crisis.


Step 2: 

As the community turns into pipeline, I would oversell an unattainable dream.


Step 3: 

I would send all customers early renewal notices stating that the price of 6Sense is about to increase by 3x. 

If the recipient loses their mind (like yours truly), I would offer flat extensions. 

If they did not, I would get the knives out and butcher whoever I could.

____

6Sense may be able to hit their 2024 goals by: 

- Spreading the narrative that they figured Outbound out (and it’s their tech)
- Overselling EVEN MORE than in prior years, and 
- Raising prices on current customers with a ferocity never seen before

Even if they do, where does that put their “Rule of 40”? 

Somewhere between 0 and 10?

Is THAT an IPO profile in 2025? 

But what if, and here’s the REAL kicker…
