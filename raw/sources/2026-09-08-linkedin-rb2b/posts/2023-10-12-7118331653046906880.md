## Exact post text

Last week I spoke privately with a BDR working at a $6B unicorn startup and what he told me about life as a seller was shocking. Here's how our conversation went:

BDR: By Friday every week I feel like my soul has been destroyed.

Me: Why? 

BDR: The repetition is completely unbearable. 

Me: Geez. What do you mean? What did you actually do today? 

BDR: I looked at the report of my accounts in Salesforce. Found the ones who have opened my emails (I have 60 of them) and sent them Amazon gift cards because it’s Prime Day. 

Me: Why on earth would you have US-based reps doing that? How the other BDRs feel?

BDR: Let me hit a couple on Slack. 

(a few minutes pass… )

BDR: Okay - I got 3 responses:

“it feels like there's so much pressure on us that trickles down from the top because if we’re not successful everybody suffers.” 

 “the constant changes make it feel like you're behind, no matter what type of preparation you do.” 

 “We don’t get recognized as much for all the work we do. It’s a constant struggle mentally, physically, emotionally … I sometimes can’t keep up.”

Me: That's rough. What’s your guys ACV? 

BDR: $25k and $50k annual plans. 

Me: So your managers are telling you to book 3 demos per week, and you’re booking one. 

BDR: Yep. Me and basically the whole team. Over half of us, at least.

Me: And every week they ask you what you are going to do to hit your target next week… to which you respond…

BDR: MORE ACTIVITY!!! 

Me: Right. 

BDR: The worst part is that they are threatening to take away our personalized flows in SalesLoft. If they do, I’m quitting. They just want more, more, more. And it's not working. 

TAKEAWAY:

We're witnessing a mega-trend. 

It’s been happening for a while, but 2023 accelerated it.

It's no longer possible to ignore.  

BDRs teams are failing.

Why?

Because:

- Your buyers don’t want to talk to salespeople
- Inboxes are ruined, so personalization doesn’t work, which...
- Leads to EVEN MORE SPAMMING using the same busted playbook

Look, you need outbound sales.

I get comments all the time saying I'm "anti-outbound".

Wrong. I've built two startups on the back of outbound.

But we need to do it smarter now.

Using fewer people, using signals and automation and data to cast a massive net that your buyers will swim into when they’re ready.

It’s 2023. 

Telling BDRs they just need "More activity" isn't going to cut it.

We need a new GTM playbook. 

The old Salesforce model isn't just bad for business.

It's bad for our reps and their well-being.
