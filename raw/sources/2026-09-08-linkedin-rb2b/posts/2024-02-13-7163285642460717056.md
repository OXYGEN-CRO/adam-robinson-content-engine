## Exact post text

In 2023, if you followed the classic B2B sales playbook (like I did), it either broke you or left you licking your wounds. That's why the next B2B tech unicorns will be built like DTC companies. Here's a $50M example:

Enter: Ryan Babenzien and his company Jolie.

In 18 months, he bootstrapped his DTC beauty brand from $0-$50M.

And with a team of only 3 people!

His main product?

A shower head.

Here's his playbook and how I think B2B companies will steal it:

1. The Jolie Water Report

DTC Play:

While Ryan was building the product, he created a lead magnet called The Jolie Water Report, which allowed users to see what chemicals were in their local water supply. It made the market problem aware (i.e. "oh sh*t our water is full of toxins!") and generated THOUSANDS of leads.

B2B Equivalent:

Build a massive, thirsty audience, pre-launch. I’m doing it by writing on LinkedIn about the problem we're going to solve MONTHS before the product comes to market.

2. The Massive UGC Machine

DTC Play:

In 2023, Jolie generated 10k pieces of UGC, which was one of the secrets of their explosive growth. They hand-picked medium-sized influencers, sent them a shower head, and said if they liked it, review it. 

B2B Equivalent: 

I’m currently trying to accomplish something similar, using a combination of influencer outreach and incentives in our PLG motion. This is a TOP priority of mine, because I believe the world is set up in a way to absorb this UGC, much better than any other type of marketing you could do.

3. Non-Traditional Advertising

DTC Play:

Ryan does things like wrap trucks with things like “your water is dirtier than this truck”, and puts stickers on the subway that say “your shower water is dirtier than the handle you are about to touch”.

B2B Equivalent:

I’m trying to advertise our company in a way that no one else is, mainly using video. We’re creating two episodic shows this year, along with three other video content lines, including me publishing weekly team calls as we grow our startup from 3 customers and $1,250 MRR to ??? at year-end.

4. Tiny, highly-capable Internal Team

DTC Play:

This is my favorite. This team outperformed every other DTC brand with three people and zero funding. 

B2B Equivalent:

My goal is to keep our core team to five, using “consultants” from our DTC operation to execute on things that are not full-time roles. A key requirement is to make support 100% handled by chatbots. Not just a first line of defense. 

TAKEAWAY:

For the future of B2B go-to-market, look to the top-performing DTC companies. 

The $1B enterprises of tomorrow will have lightweight, fast GTM motions with tiny, highly-efficient teams.

In 2024, the idea that more salespeople equals more revenue is dead.

We can no longer afford to run that outdated playbook.

It's time to get creative.
