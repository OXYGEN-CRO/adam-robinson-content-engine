## Exact post text

Entrepreneurship is art.

It’s a creative pursuit.

But where does creativity come from? And where does it NOT come from?

Legendary music producer, Rick Rubin, wrote a book, titled The Creative Act: A Way of Being, which teaches readers that creativity and being an artist isn’t about your specific output, it’s about your relationship to the world.

While this isn’t a book about business, it’s the best book I’ve ever read on how I approach my craft as an entrepreneur.

In business, there are intangible factors that have a major impact on the success of your company.

For instance…

How does someone FEEL when they use your product or service?

Does your brand provide so much value that people just can’t help but talk about it?

Until I read this book, I never really knew how to communicate these unmeasurable forces in business.

These forces are very much a part of the entrepreneurial journey—which is something that my advisor, Santosh Sharan, and I, both agree on.

After reading the book, Santosh felt like we could write a book that focuses on everything Rick Rubin is saying about art, as it pertains to the world of business and entrepreneurship.
