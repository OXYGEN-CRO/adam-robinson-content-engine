## Exact post text

This is by far the worst part of building in public. Last week we decided to downsize the RB2B team from 5 to 3 FTE, so I can focus more on the product and get closer to the customer. Here’s what happened:

1. We have major product problems

This is strange to say at $5.4m ARR in 15mo. We thought we were on to something with CRM filtering in Q1, but that didn’t really work. I am on a mission to fix the three major issues - filtering, routing, what now.

2. Our product has proven to be entirely PLG

A year ago it looked like we’d have an enterprise motion selling $50k+ deals, but that has mostly fizzled due to competitor undercutting and larger volume customers wanting more functionality. Our bread and butter is the little guy, and it’s truly PLG, rather than sales-assisted PLG.

3. I felt distracted and far from the customer.

I initiated a GTM motion that included courses, community, and software attempting to replicate the Russell Brunson playbook. It didn’t work for me like it did for him. The only thing I want to focus on until $10m is 3 LinkedIn posts per week, product and customers, and UnF*ck My Startup (weekly show).

4. I want to get in the weeds with Robb and build insanely lean w/ AI

Stuart Chaney showed us how Devin is writing 80% of his code, updating his support docs with feature changes, and enabling Fin to answer complex billing questions that used to take a rep an hour.

5. We can’t get huge, but we CAN be insanely profitable.

Our churn rate hovers between 8.5-10%/mo. If we can improve the product and get it down to 7-8%, we can get to $10-$12m ARR, and I think we can do that with our team of me, Tate, and Robb. We’re about to add an AI layer which will eat into our margins a bit, but we should be able to run at 40-50% total margin.

———

So what are the changes we made?

1. Diana Ross is going back to focusing 100% of her time on Retention.com

2. Pete Crowley is still going to co-host our show and is going to take on fractional clients interested in RB2B-type growth

3. Ilija Milutinovic is looking for his next big role

———

IF YOU ARE LOOKING FOR AN AE:

Ilija is a top 1% AE. He was a great fit, smart, hardworking, built rapport, listened to the customer, and always went above and beyond. We would have moved him to R! but he’s in Europe and our team travels in North America several times a month. DM me if you are looking for an AE that can smash it.

IF YOU ARE LOOKING FOR FRACTIONAL GROWTH HELP:

Pete ran the cold email, linkedin ads, SEO, and webinar effort last year that took us from $0-$4m ARR in 40 weeks. He’s AI-first, and fluent with the modern tech stack. He's still my co-host at UnF*ck My Startup and I will continue to love working with him. DM me if you want to talk to him.

———

Letting go of people you love is the hardest part of this job.

But as Ilija said when I said that to him last week…

“No Adam, it’s the hardest part of MY job.”

Correct.

DM me about Ilija or Pete.

You will not be disappointed.
