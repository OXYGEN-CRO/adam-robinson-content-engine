## Exact post text

I’ve read 100's of business books. But not a single one articulated the way I approach my craft as an entrepreneur like the non-business book I just read....

Rick Rubin’s "The Creative Act: A Way of Being."

It’s about how you harness a flow of energy to manifest greatness without focusing on the outcome. 

Meaning, the journey IS the reward.

This is exactly how we operate at Retention.com. 

Everyone working here can feel it.

They wake up everyday with a purpose. 

And a funny thing happens when you start operating in this way... 

You attract people that work in the same way. 

People start to treat their “job” like it’s their craft. 

Like they're an artist, who is obsessed with producing that perfect song. 

And those people can do the job of 10x people.

I posted about this on Linkedin yesterday and the comment section was INTENSE.

We know this way of working won’t be for everybody. 

But I believe this is the only way to run a business today.

We go deeper into WHY in this week’s episode of the Billion Dollar Challenge. 

Watch it, then tell me if it changed your mind on the 10x mentality.

P.S. 

Missed episodes 1-4? 

Catch up on the full series via the link in the comments!
