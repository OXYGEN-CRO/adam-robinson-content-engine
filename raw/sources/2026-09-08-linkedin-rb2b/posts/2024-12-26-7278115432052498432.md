## Exact post text

In 2024, I grew RB2B from $0-$4M in 40 weeks. Here are the 7 biggest mistakes I made along the way (and the ARR ranges I made them):

1. I created a $0 and $495 plan, got 3,000 signups, and 13 paying customers

Revenue Level: $0-$75k

I had the genius idea to give infinite leads and the Slack app away for free, but charge for other integrations. People parsed the Slack app, sent the data wherever they wanted, and laughed at me. We should have done credit-based pricing from the start.

2. I thought running the leads through Clay would work for everybody

Revenue Level: $0-$1.5m ARR

Clay has been an incredible partner and when you have enough traffic and the right agency partner, it really works. I didn’t realize that all of that needed to be true for people to be successful.

3. I sold web visitors as leads, and “success” as booked demos.

Revenue Level: $0-$3m ARR

I didn’t realize that a site visitor wasn’t actually a lead, it was a signal, and that a signal can be very useful even if it doesn’t lead to a booked demo (what if the visitor ALREADY had a demo?). Web visit + other signals and criteria make a visitor worth contacting.

4. I thought people would know what to do with the data.

Revenue Level: $0-$2m ARR

I was surprised when “what now” came back as churn reason #1. We've finally solved this problem and made these signals actionable.

5. I didn’t invest in filtering and routing through the CRM until three weeks ago

Revenue Level: $0-$4m ARR

We tried to build an ICP filter, but couldn’t nail it. Clay does a decent job, but most people don’t want to add Clay just to use this. Running the signals through the CRM for filtering and routing is a relatively easy solution that should have occurred to me sooner, and did not. Thank you Jeremy Horowitz'. Our new Hubspot integration fixes this.

6. I had no idea how many people would copy us.

Revenue Level: $2-$2m ARR

ZoomInfo now jumped on the bandwagon, as copycat number 37. Shame on me for not realizing that any SaaS that works will immediately be copied by 1,000 people in 1 week.

7. I had no idea how bad churn would be.

Revenue Level: $0-$4m ARR

I thought that we would be able to make B2B companies SUPER successful because of how well we do for our larger ecommerce companies. But, we sell a $149/mo plan that churns at 18%/mo. I did not see that coming. Maybe I should have?

TAKEAWAY:

Startups are hard.

RB2B was really hard, too.

I made some big mistakes…

Not the least of which was assuming a site visitor makes a lead.

I hope I don’t make these mistakes again.

What I do know is that I’ll continue to make mistakes.

Just new and different ones!

That's part of the game.

We keep building.
