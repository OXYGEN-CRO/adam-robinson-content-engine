## Exact post text

Since November, we've used LinkedIn to drive 600 calls, and I’ve answered 5,000 comments. When I told Chris Walker this was informing our entire pricing and GTM strategy he said, “You’re MISSING OUT on the most powerful channel by far."

The Weekly Live Event.

In Chris’ words, the fast feedback loop is EVERYTHING in the early days. 

I agree with that, which was what I was gloating about in the first place. 

The combination of the MASSIVE free offer we have, PLUS the feedback I get when I post - mostly about being wrong - about our GTM with RB2B is SOOO helpful. 

What do I mean? 

I had the initial hypothesis that in order to attempt to create a low churn business, we would:

1. Give the leads away for free, along with
2. A push to Slack

And charge for a TINY percentage of high-value users who wanted a Hubspot and SFDC integration $495/mo, and everybody else would enjoy the product for free.

As we all now know, I was very wrong about this. 

You can parse the Slack block with no code using Zapier and send the leads wherever you want.

I also heard “there was too much value in free, not enough in paid”, and realized a MASSIVE amount of our signups just couldn’t jump to $495/mo. 

So, we decided an appropriate strategy was sell credits, with 80% of our signups getting the product for free forever.

As one LinkedIn community member commented on one of my posts: 

“Well the good news is that you got a year’s worth of information in 6 weeks.” 

So, back to Chris...

He said his Weekly Live Event is an even BETTER feedback loop, from MORE people, FASTER than LinkedIn.

Chris gets 50-200 people to show up every week.

Then another 10,000 listen to it on the pod.

He starts off with a 10-30min monologue, then opens it up for questions.

Why is the live event so good for Chris’ business?

1. He gets REALLY fast feedback
2. He can figure out what people are struggling with
3. He can work on his messaging to the market

But my absolute FAVORITE part of Chris’ whole event motion…

Which I have seen NOBODY copy to date… 

Is that LITERALLY 100% of his LinkedIn posts come from the event.

Here is his process: 

1. Record live event
2. Team clips out short form video by topic
3. Wakes up in the morning, looks through the clips, picks one, writes post

After hearing this, Chris has inspired me to have one of my own.

The first event will be this coming Tuesday, May 7 at 3pm EST.

The event will be called “The Inbound-Led Outbound Workshop”.

Topic #1 will be: What is Inbound-Led Outbound? 

I’m going to share all my secrets of how I bootstrapped Retention.com to $22m ARR in 4 years, and how I’m getting RB2B off the ground in an explosive fashion!

You can ask me and Santosh anything you can think of.

Register for the event here: https://lnkd.in/gkSJSGeu

Look forward to seeing you there!
