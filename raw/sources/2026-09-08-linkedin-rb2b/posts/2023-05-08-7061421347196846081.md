## Exact post text

Getting the right employees on my team is my most important job as a founder. It is the main determinant of whether we will hit $50M this year…

So, how do I hire A-players?

I’m building ridiculous energy around our brand to attract the best and brightest people on the planet. People who want to work in this environment.

Here are my 3 biggest takeaways:

✅ Consultants can make excellent hires — Many of the best people on our team started out in consulting roles, which helped to determine if they were truly fit for the job.

✅ Building a team of A-players is crucial to scaling — They require little direction and can move the business forward with ease while maintaining a positive attitude.

✅ Working in public is a powerful recruitment tool — Showcasing your brand's mission, vision, and values on a podcast or via social media can create excitement and build familiarity with potential recruits, even those who are not actively looking for a job.
