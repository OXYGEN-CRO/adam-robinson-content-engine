## Exact post text

I *really* thought we were going to turn the corner in July, but it was another hard month at Retention.com. Here are the dirty details: 👇

The Highlights: 

- Client revenue was up 15% and email sends down 50% system-wide
- Our flagship product is now 100% Klaviyo AUP compliant 
- We’ll be in beta on a totally new product (for a totally new TAM) in 14 days
- We are firmly and permanently on the right side of profitability

The Lowlights: 

- We got SMACKED with a large amount of 60-day opt-outs to start the month 
- As a result, we have had to shift massive focus to our CS org
- We are focusing on setting customer expectations & alignment on evaluating success
- Our VP sales resigned, which led to a much leaner team

The Opportunities : 

- We are set up to have a SaaS that grew 70-90% YoY with a 20% total margin
- We are starting a beta test a mass-market product that solves a bigger problem
- Our sales team will be a super-lean, super-efficient, ultra-fast-paced organization
- We can design a sales and CS experience designed for the complex nature of our product

TAKEAWAY:

We keep getting hit with annoying problems, but...

They are making us much stronger. 

These last 4 months were harder than I could have ever imagined.

But my optimism for the next 24-36 is HIGH and increasing by the day.

Stay-tuned for what comes next.

P.S. 

What data would you like to see from our bootstrapped SaaS? I'm committed to building a unicorn in public. Share in the comments and I'll try to incorporate into the next update.
