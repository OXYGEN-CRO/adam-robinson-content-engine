## Exact post text

DM: Can I be a fanboy? 

Me: Huh??? 

=============

On June 6, shortly after the 6Sense saga started, I got a DM from an enterprise rep asking if there was any RB2B swag he could buy. 

“Adam,

Been following your content, like the whole internet has. 

Absolute masterclass.

One part education, two parts entertainment, and a dash of sales. 

I’m sold on whatever you’re selling because it’s like a movement. 

The SaaS co I work at is like that and developers were literally lining up at our conference to get POSTERS of our company! 

Who puts posters of a SaaS company on their wall?!?!? 

Now… Can you please make some swag for RB2B?”

================

Until now, we have had no swag. 

BUT - Eli Rubel and the NoBoringDesign squad have whipped up a REALLLLLYYYY cool brand for us, which should hit some time this quarter.

There are TONS of opportunities for cool pixelated RB2B swag items in black and fluorescent green that we can get to our RB2B Insiders to rock around town.

That brings up THE major question as far as I can tell… 

The same question Postal is trying to answer with their State of Swag report (link in comments).

The question: 

WHAT SWAG DO PEOPLE WANT FROM B2B COMPANIES???

I personally want to know what RB2B swag our fans would be interested in. 

1. Do you like t-shirts?
2. Do you like hoodies? 
3. Do you like mugs?
4. Do you like backpacks? 
5. Would you like potted plants? 

All of that seems a little tired to me, TBH. 

I want to get into the swag game, but I just DON’T know what to create. 

RB2B fans… WHAT SWAG WOULD YOU LIKE US TO MAKE???

Let me know in the comments and if we make it I'll send you some!
