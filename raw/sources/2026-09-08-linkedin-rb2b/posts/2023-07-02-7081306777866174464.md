In startup land, some events you don't see coming...

Last month at Retention.com, we faced two BIG ONES at once:

1. An industry-wide slowdown that throttled our month over month growth.

2. An unforeseen tax bill that came in the middle of our most expensive quarter ever.

As someone who is committed to bootstrapping, Q2 was a heartbreaker.

Especially after the insane growth trajectory we saw in Q1.

It was a nice reminder, failure is inevitable.

It's how you react to that failure that determines whether you succeed.
