## Exact post text

I completely butchered 2024 and nearly killed RB2B.
How? I didn't talk to customers for over 15 months.
I was too busy trying to become an "influencer".

I became everything I hated about founders building a personal brand.

I completely lost touch with the customer, and our product suffered.

Our MONTHLY churn rate hit 15% last Thanksgiving… and I celebrated that as great content 🤦

How badly did I mess up?

- 15% monthly churn = 99% of customers gone within 12mos (we could be at $10m ARR!)
- I was spending 4+ hours daily on content vs 0 minutes with customers
- I had 47 podcast appearances scheduled but couldn't name our top 3 customer pain points
- I spent Q3 + Q4 creating courses/community instead of going deep with our 1,500 paying customers on how to be better

———

My come-to-Jesus moment was in June at our quarterly planning in Toronto.

I was asked to give a resource audit on RB2B.

I realized that MOST of what I was spending my time on was working AGAINST the only thing that would get us to $10m ARR.

I wasn’t talking to customers.

I was posting hot takes and going on podcasts…

Then trying to get intel from team members who were talking with customers.

Here’s the problem with that strategy:

If you’re not PART of the conversations, hearing the frustration in people’s voices, observing the micro-expressions on faces as they say what they say…

- You’re out of touch
- You miss a HUGE amount of meaning
- You can’t make the call on what to build or fix next

———

That night in Toronto I was sick to my stomach.

The way I was spending my time and running my org was wrong.

I couldn’t sleep - regret, anxiety, shame overwhelmed me.

I was making such a basic error for such a long time, unable to see it.

Our product was suffering, and our churn was so bad that it would be borderline IMPOSSIBLE to grow any more.

I knew I had to make BIG CHANGES:

1. We shrunk the team and removed the layer between me and the customers
2. Robb and I created an onboarding flow that asks tier 1 users on the 21st day if they’d like to have a 15min call (see template below)
3. I spend 30m/day reviewing support tickets with Robb (and improving Finn’s responses)

THE RESULT:

- My Wednesday afternoons are packed with video calls with REAL tier 1 customers explaining REAL pain that I want to fix
- I’m reviewing 100s of support tickets every week with Robb

But the biggest difference?

I now have an EMOTIONAL connection with the pain are customers are experiencing when they use our product.

Which is the driving the total product transformation we’re undergoing.

TAKEAWAY

If you're a SaaS Founders reading this, don't make the same mistake I did.

Don't optimize for vanity metrics (followers, likes, podcast appearances).

Focus on the only metric that matters: customer retention.

Jason Lemkin has said this before…

Your LinkedIn engagement is inversely correlated with your product quality.

Stop posting. Start talking to customers.

Your business depends on it.
