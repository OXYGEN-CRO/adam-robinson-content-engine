## Exact post text

I hear this twice a week, “We had RB2B installed - and LOVED it - but my CEO got scared of GDPR and removed the script.” Here’s the hard truth about GDPR and RB2B that everybody gets wrong:

This is a strange objection. 

The objection is factually invalid… 

There is no GDPR concern with RB2B.

Once Santosh Sharan or I speak to a concerned exec and they internalize that RB2B is a US-only tech, 100% move forward. 

But if we don't speak to them, they rely on misinformation in marketing chat rooms and what their friends are doing.

This sometimes ends up in script removal.

To reiterate, RB2B is: 

- A US-Only Technology, that 
- Fires on US soil only, and 
- Only has US data subjects in the database

Once CEOs understand those key facts, four more questions follow: 

1. Aren’t you collecting EU IP addresses to know when to fire your script? 

No. We are reading the EU IP addresses - but not collecting and storing them.

- IP addresses are not PII
- The intent of collecting this information is to EXCLUDE EU data subjects from marketing activity, rather than to target them

2. What about an EU citizen traveling to or living in the USA? 

- EU citizens traveling or living abroad are NOT protected by GDPR when they are in foreign countries. This is a common misunderstanding.
- We do not have EU citizens in our database.

3. What about someone in Europe on a VPN?

- GDPR adherence is in many ways “best efforts”.
- If someone tricks you into violating GDPR through a VPN, it’s not a violation.

4. What if I’m a multinational or have offices in the EU?

- While there are some edge cases where GDPR may TECHNICALLY apply if you do not have offices in the US, but ask your lawyer or CISO the actual RISK of EU enforcing GDPR on a US data subject on US soil running a US technology. It is zero. They do not seek to regulate US tech on US soil with US data subjects.

TAKEAWAY:

GDPR is scary. 

It gets even scarier when you have bad information. 

I just want everyone to understand that RB2B DOESN’T violate GDPR. 

If you don’t use RB2B, it should be for some other reason, like: 

- You are ethically opposed to Site Visitor Resolution
- You couldn’t actually book demos with the data
- You love 6Sense (and therefore hate me)

People in chat rooms don’t understand compliance. 

Don’t let them cost you money. 

P.S. 

I am not a lawyer and this does not constitute legal advice. Please seek counsel before taking any action related to compliance.
