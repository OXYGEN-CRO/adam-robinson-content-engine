Over the next 12 months, I will spend over $1,000,000 on Linkedin ads promoting OTHER PEOPLE’S LeadGen Agencies. Here’s our 2024 agency playbook and why I’m ready to part with 7 figures of cash:

To set the stage: 

- There is a massive opportunity for RB2B to partner with LeadGen agencies 
- We can provide value through retention, recurring revenue, AND new biz
- We’re trying to figure out exactly what the motion is

Here is our 7-step playbook for how a LeadGen Agency grows with RB2B: 

1. Pitch Inbound-Led Outbound as a bolt-on LeadGen service.

- If you sell a retainer, offer it for free for 30 days
- If you sell a price per lead or per demo, tell them you’ll charge the same price

2. Set the client up with the free pixel, integrate with Slack.

- Start delivering the client dopamine hits as they see the leads flow through
- Those dopamine hits get associated with you 
- Cost = $0

3. Set up a channel for “positive responses” to your outbound in slack

- Source #2 for dopamine hits that get associated with you
- These and #1 serve as a steady reminder of you’re doing for them
- Cost = $0

4. Set up Inbound-Led Outbound with tools like Clay and Smartlead

- This is the option if you want to automate everything (or use VA’s) 
- The key is to connect w/ ICP leads as quickly as possible
- Then add them to email sequence
- If phone calls work for you, call the ICP leads too

5. After 30 days of proven value, close the customer on this bolt-on

- If you are being paid per lead or demo, this will mean more leads or demos at the same price
- If you charge a retainer, this will mean a higher retainer
- If your customer gets under 200 leads/mo, never pay us, if over 200 leads/mo, our new pricing starts at $99/mo  
- You can either pass this along to the customer, or just pay us - you’ll be billing them far more for the leads, demos, or retainer that you’re charging them

6. Create a video case study of the successful workflow and post it on LinkedIn. 

- Nothing will make you look smarter and more desirable than sharing wins
- We can show you how to make the video and what to write over text

7. Get RB2B to promote the post to prospects with MY money. 

- I want to put MY dollars behind YOUR agency  - 7 figures in 12 months!
- Just tag your agency, your customer, and RB2B 
- We'll take care of the rest!

TAKEAWAY:

We believe that LeadGen agencies will be an unlock for us. 

Why do I think this initiative will be successful? 

Because we aren’t asking for $1 for the tech until the agency has proven out their ability to make money with it. 

Everybody wants more recurring revenue, better retention, and more customers. 

I know I do. 

If you're a LeadGen Agency owner...

DM me if you want to join the waiting list for our agency program.
