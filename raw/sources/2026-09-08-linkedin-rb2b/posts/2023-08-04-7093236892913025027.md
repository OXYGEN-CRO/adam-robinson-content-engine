## Exact post text

Yesterday I was talking to the CEO of a $150M/yr Shopify business and he told me 3 things about pricing Shopify apps that kinda blew my mind:

1. There is something magic about $59/mo.

- It’s not $9, but it’s not $99 either
- Triple Whale made a huge splash with the $59 price point
- Klaviyo and Gorgias push people to $60 on their pricing pages

2. Free is harder to execute than paid.

- You need 100% touchless onboarding
- It creates an enormous amount of unqualified signups/support
- But free products that win are actually BETTER than their paid competitors

3. There are only around 6,000 businesses who can buy 4-digit monthly SaaS.

- These stores are the best - they move fast and pay for revenue growth
- The average size of a Shopify Plus brand is smaller than you think
- You can learn a lot from Charm io revenue or SimilarWeb traffic

TL;DR

Lesson 1: $59/mo is magic
Lesson 2: Free is harder than paid
Lesson 3: Shopify Plus doesn’t equal enterprise

Agree? Disagree? 

Share your thoughts on Shopify app pricing below 👇
