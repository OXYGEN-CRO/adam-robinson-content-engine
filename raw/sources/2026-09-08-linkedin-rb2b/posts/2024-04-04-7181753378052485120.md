## Exact post text

I bootstrapped my startup to $12M ARR with only 6 people. Now I’m launching a brand new product called RB2B and plan to get it to $6M ARR by the end of 2024 with no more than 5 people. 

Here's my 6-step playbook for launching 7-figure products with tiny teams:

Step 1: Test a valuable, differentiated product

- We are testing an individual-level website identity product for B2B
- All of the other products in the space are IP > company name
- We will deliver LinkedIn URL and business email to a Slack channel, for free
- We have our early UX and building out integrations

Step 2: Create an inbound sales engine

- We will use two main channels: cold email and founder brand
- We are validating demand before we even start building the actual product
- Taylor is crushing it with our Inbound-Led Outbound Playbook
- Founder Brand is LinkedIn content (text posts and add a video element)
- I amplify everything by “working in public” and sharing financials as we go

Step 3: Develop the product

- With feedback from early users, we are developing the right feature set
- We’ll build as light of a version as possible as quickly as we can
- Tate, our CTO, is the master at this lightning-speed building
- Build/improve sales channels BEFORE the product is done

Step 4: Create a viral (and free) core offer

- I believe that word-of-mouth is the most important thing in a startup
- We’re trying to make our core offer so absurd that people HAVE to talk about it
- We’re going to attempt to nail down distribution before monetization this time
- I am obsessed with watching the rise of Mailchimp from the sidelines in my former life

Step 5: Automate 97% of customer support

- Onboarding is simple - it will be mostly self-serve
- With our current company, we got to $12m ARR with just Clint (rockstar)
- With RB2B, we hired Robb and Pete to automate it 
- So far we’ve been able to handle 93% of CS request with chatbots and support docs

Step 6: Make everything go faster with a few marketing campaigns.

- We’ll team up with sales, marketing, and SaaS influencers and drum up a buzz
- I’ll want to go on a podcast tour, and give keynotes where I can
- It will be interesting to see if there is a strong affiliate/reseller channel for this product

From here, we just keep trying to find ways to go even faster.

I think we can get to $6M ARR by the end of 2024.

That is 1,010 people paying us $495/month.

My first SaaS had 5,000 customers in 36 months.

We onboarded 1,500 customers over the last 12mo at a much higher price to a much more price-sensitive DTC Shopify store audience at Retention.com.

I’m feeling really good about the playbook.

Remember:

You don't need a BIG team to make BIG money.

Stay lean and thrive.
