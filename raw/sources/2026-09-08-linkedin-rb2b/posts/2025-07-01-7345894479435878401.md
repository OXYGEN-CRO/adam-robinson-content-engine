## Exact post text

My vision for RB2B is to create a $15-$20m ARR 3-man startup (at $5.5m now). Here are 5 things we ABSOLUTELY CAN’T do if we want to get there: 

1. We CAN’T do enterprise deals. 

We learned over the past year that the juice isn’t worth the squeeze - we can’t charge enough with our current product suite to make all the calls worthwhile. 

2. We CAN'T build anything even remotely complex.

There is a convergence happening where Koala, UnifyGTM, Common Room, Warmly, and Amplemarket all have platforms that are powerful (but similar) but complex enough to require sales which leads to a price tag to support a sales force. We have to stay as simple as possible, priced for zero friction.

3. We CAN'T scale revenue with people.

Robb Clarke was an early first mover with Fin by Intercom, and now it’s resolving 70% of opened chats successfully. We just dropped my clone on the outside of the RB2B site, and it’s handling inbound sales inquiry incredibly well. 

4. We CAN'T get on the phone with our prospects.

This one is difficult, because there are people who just want to talk to someone. But we’re a $200/mo PLG product. We need to get better at building systems to answer all questions over email and chat with a combination of text and video.

5. We CAN'T get ambitious. 

This is the hardest part. I get massive FOMO from Fyxer’s posts about $1-$10m ARR in 6mo, HeyReach going $5-$6m in 1mo, and all the AI companies going to the moon. If we improve the product and get more traction (which we will), I can’t get excited and decide it’s time to go for a unicorn again.

TAKEAWAY

$10m ARR is the FU money of SaaS. 

If you get there with 3 people, that’s FU times 10.

The problem is that it’s almost impossible to be disciplined enough to get there. 

Why?

If you have enough traction to get to $10m ARR w/ 3 ppl:  

1. You will have what’s necessary to go way bigger
2. Investors and other founders will all be telling you to go bigger
3. You’ll be reading about are people who have what you have that went bigger

The world is set up to make us bootstrapper feel small. 

But here’s the thing…

The world is ALSO set up to create 1000x more 3-man $10m ARR startups than ever before. 

I want RB2B to be one of those startups. 

But I know it’s going to be brutal… All the FOMO … 

And the world won’t stop making us bootstrappers feel so small.
