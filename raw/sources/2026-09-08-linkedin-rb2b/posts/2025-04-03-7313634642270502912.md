## Exact post text

THANK YOU Q1 for finally ending. Every year Retention.com slides in Q1 (we sell to ecomm). And RB2B did NOT bail us out. Here's what went down behind the scenes in March 2025:

We get crushed every year in Q1. 

This is now the 6th Q1 since we started, in 5 of them we’ve contracted.

Stores stay ramped through the holidays, then cut everything possible in Jan.

I would love to see how other people who sell martech in the ecomm ecosystem fare during Q1… 

But alas, nobody is dumb enough to sit here and write about it like I do.

I was hoping this year that RB2B would step in and make up for some of the Retention.com slide … 

But that didn’t happen. 

Sadly, RB2B is experiencing some growing pains itself.

It turns out once you get around $5m ARR with a PLG that is 10mo LTV, it’s hard to keep growing.

But don’t feel THAT bad for us … 

While we slid 2.9% in ARR since Jan 1 (… we slid 3.2% in Q124), we generated more than $3m in free cash, which is getting close to a 50% total profit margin.

The other positive note is that every year it feels like the world is ending in Q1… 

And consistently, every year we’ve pulled a rabbit out of our hat and grown 15-20% or more.

What am I excited about? 

1. I’m very excited about our strategic initiatives and partnership opportunities at Retention

2. I’m equally excited about our vision for what RB2B could be in 12 months time

3. I’m VERY pumped about some data accuracy improvements at both co’s

If we can pull off half of what we’re going to try to pull off, it will unlock our next leg of growth. 

If we can nail all of it … 

Look out! 

———

I’m also very excited to announce that after belittling all of LinkedIn for running bloated teams, we’re hiring 10 roles (growing our FTE by 25%)!

If you’re interested in joining us, and want to know more about these roles: 

- Sales Manager
- Technical Product Manager
- B2B Technical Support Specialist
- 3 Account Executives
- 2 Technical Account Managers/Solutions Analysts
- 2 Ruby on Rails Developers

Email a resume and cover letter to [ recruiting @ retention (dot) com ]

———

Startups are hard. 

Building in public - once you’re at scaling stage - is even harder.

You create competitors, copycats, and enemies that take swipes at you just because of how many people are watching. 

But I’m going to keep doing it - even as it continues to hurt more and more. 

Why? 

Because this is the content I wanted 5 years ago before I started Retention.

I wanted the truth from someone who was three steps ahead. 

I will continue to deliver that truth.

A 100% unfiltered inside look.

All I ask from you is...

Keep building!
