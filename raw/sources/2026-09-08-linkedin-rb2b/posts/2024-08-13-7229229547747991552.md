## Exact post text

Here are the top 7 reasons why you’re AFRAID to start posting on LinkedIn: 

1. You can’t be authentic.

You’re worried about what your investors/employees/prospects will think if you share what’s actually happening inside your company - which, let’s be honest, is not always good.

2. You can’t play the long game.

You know that 60 days in if you aren’t getting 1,000 likes per post you’re going to lose focus and squirrel to something else, so you already know it won’t work.

3. You’re afraid of the people who will hate you.

If you create content the right way - as the most unapologetically authentic version of yourself - you will get people who hate you. You may not be okay with that. But you have to be.

4. You’re in denial about outbound. 

You think that the reason you can’t build pipeline is because you have the wrong leaders or reps or the wrong tech or strategy, and you have yet to internalize that organic social is the only way to grow.

5. You’re afraid of how hard it is to be great.

This is legitimate, because it is hard to be great at literally anything in life. What makes it hard to internalize with social is the best gets SOOOO much more visibility than the 2nd best. And 3rd. Etc.

6. You’re afraid of the time commitment. 

You look at posts that I make about spending 25+ hours on Founder Brand content and podcast guest slots and think that you don’t have the time. You may be right - but soon, someone you are competing with WILL make the time.

7. You feel like you’re too far behind. 

The best time to plant a tree was 20 years ago. The second best time is today. I was behind when I started 18 months ago - I never posted on LinkedIn before - and now I’m ahead. You could be too.

TAKEAWAY:

It’s okay to be afraid of posting on LinkedIn.

But here’s my question … 

As outbound continues to be ruined by:

- Automation/Increased volumes
- Spam Filters
- Recession

How long are you going to let your fear of LinkedIn destroy your business? 

The clock is ticking.
