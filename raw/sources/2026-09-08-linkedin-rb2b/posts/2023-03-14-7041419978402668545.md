## Exact post text

It’s not every day that you hear about a guy in his 50s who consistently goes viral on TikTok and has a massive audience of people who actually want to hear from him.

I recently just did a podcast with Moises Sanchez, one of the most interesting people I’ve ever spoken to…

He came to America from Mexico illegally as a 12-year-old, has been to prison, and now in his 50s, owns a $50M a year roofing company.

And I’m not the only one who thinks he’s extremely interesting.

There are half a million people on TikTok who think that this guy is awesome.

I got to ask him about 3 of his best tips for going viral on TikTok (or any social platform)

Tip #1: Make 100 videos a day.

It sounds crazy, I know.

But we’re talking about brute forcing your way to virality. THIS is force.

These aren’t complicated videos. Each video is 15 seconds long, and you break it up into 5 blocks.

20 videos in each block, 5 times a day. It takes hours, but it’s worth it if you’re trying to grow.

Moises’ videos are all lifestyle content. If you have a cool lifestyle, all you need to do is showcase your surroundings.

If your videos are about excel tips, do a bunch of tiny tips at once. And then every day, post the same tips, but repositioned.

Tip #2: Stick to a micro-niche.

This is super important when you’re just starting out.

TikTok wants to see you reaching a higher percentage of a particular audience, not 0.00001% of the entire platform.

Moises’ micro-niche ended up being his ability to show off a closet full of designer clothes and his supercars in the driveway as a hispanic man in his 50s.

This will help your account.

Tip #3: Shock people.

We all know how social media works at this point.

The more shocking you can be, the better.

This is a growth-hacky way to go viral, but views are views. Followers are followers.

Shocking people is the easy part. Your job is to capitalize on the eyeballs once they’re on you.

You do this with pattern breakers – if someone’s expecting one thing and they receive another, that’s how you hold people’s attention.

Or, if you just so happen to be an unusual person with unusual hobbies or belongings (like Moises), then shocking people will be easy.

It’s a matter of creating enough content to see what hits, and then going even harder so you can keep those people in love with you.

I highly recommend that you check out this episode of the podcast so you can hear this guy’s story. It’s a great one.

I’ll drop it in the comments below.
