## Exact post text

Affiliates have absolutely crushed for Retention.com in the last few months as we’ve scaled to $20M in ARR.

It’s a great way to complement your other sales and marketing tactics — and I wanted to give you an inside look at how we run our program (steal it):

I will mention that this can work for everyone…

But it works even BETTER if you have a great business that people want to brag about (as do most GTM strategies).

We have that at Retention.com

So… we can pull up a simple spreadsheet and show our potential affiliates:

→ What type of revenue we drive for a company like BlendJet
→ How they’re getting a 100X ROI

And it gives our affiliates the true conviction they need to sell this stuff with confidence

Oh, and it also helps that they get 20% of the deal when we close (we make sure our people get paid).

So here’s the 3-part checklist of why our affiliate channel is working so well:

1) Product-market fit

We know we have product-market fit because every single person who hears about our product is blown away.

It’s unlike anything I’ve ever experienced before.

Product-market fit means it’s very easy to display what type of value our customers will get when they use us.

2) Above-market commissions

Again, we make sure our people get paid. And this includes affiliates.

We pay our affiliates very generously because we know how valuable they are to our company at this point.

Early companies will pay much higher commissions, but as they grow, those have to go down because shareholders will probably tell you to.

We don’t really have that problem — we’re bootstrapped 😉

3) Have multiple tiers in your program

In the Shopify Plus community, it seems like people are very well connected.

Finding people who will be an affiliate for your product is great…

But finding people who will introduce you to other people who will affiliate for your product is even better.

The well-connected person who helps find more affiliates for you should be compensated as well.

20% to the bottom tier, 5% to the second tier.

4) Show them the money

Show them what you’re going to do for your customers, and show them how they’ll be compensated.

I absolutely love breaking down the money math for our affiliates, because they see dollar signs for themselves and for their brand-owning friends.

Nobody wants to refer business to a company that sucks.

Make it look like a no-brainer for them to send you intro emails.

5) Send gifts

We just started doing this, but it’s been huge for us.

We send AirPod Max’s to anyone who sends us a deal and didn’t expect commissions.

It doesn’t have to be a pair of $600 headphones. It can be anything, really.

Just don’t do the money tree. Or the brick. That’s played out, lol.

Making someone feel special anytime they do something good for you is a good way to build goodwill.

I hope these 5 tips make you (and me) a lot of money.

PS — For more advice, follow Adam Robinson to see how I scale Retention.com from $20M to $50M ARR.
