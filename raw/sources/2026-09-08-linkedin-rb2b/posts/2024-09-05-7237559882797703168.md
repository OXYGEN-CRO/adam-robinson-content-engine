## Exact post text

We closed August ‘24 at $23.4m ARR (+.6%). Retention.com at $20.8m ARR (-2.2%), RB2B at $2.6m ARR (+34%). Here’s the good, the bad, and the ugly from what went down in August: 

THE GOOD: 

- Continued operational improvement across the company w/ EOS rollout 
- Many encouraging upmarket conversations at R! - we have an angle!
- Made a great showing at eTail east, had an awesome team lead offsite in TO
- RB2B top-of-funnel, activation, and free-to-paid is excellent (metrics here)
- Robb keeps improving the automated support system for more hours saved
- Pete’s UGC effort is working great - over 200 RB2B brand mentions last week 
- We generated $688k in total profit for August

THE BAD: 

- RB2B churn - every day is a day closer to us being stuck (story of my life)
- The underlying market dynamics of the Shopify ecosystem we target with R! continue to be very challenging, but we are fighting our way through it

THE UGLY: 

- We downsized the R! team - which I posted about three weeks ago - to focus on what IS working, and to find a new PMF, which is always difficult culturally
- I haven’t looked, but I’m sure led to me and our company getting blasted on Glassdoor

TAKEAWAY:

Last week I heard an entrepreneur say, “survive until ‘25”.

I’m sure that resonates with many of us… it certainly resonated with me.

This is the most difficult environment I’ve experienced as a founder.

Sadly, because of WHY it’s so difficult, I don’t see it improving soon.

Does anyone think: 

1. Spray and pray outbound is coming back
2. Money will be free so people will buy lots of SaaS
3. The economy is set to rip-roar again next year

I am 0/3 on the above.

It's hard to continue writing these posts as we navigate the rough waters.

But, I will keep doing it, as transparently and honestly as I can.

Hopefully, there are people out there that it’s helping… 

If for no other reason than to know you are not alone.

With a few exceptions, it's hard for most of us out there right now. 

It will turn at some point… It’s just not clear when.

We just have to keep building.
