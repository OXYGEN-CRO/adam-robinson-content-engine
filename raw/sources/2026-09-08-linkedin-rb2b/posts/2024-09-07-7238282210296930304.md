## Exact post text

RB2B’s 6 month birthday was September 1st. We hit $2.6m ARR in 184 days (WOW), but have 14% monthly churn (OUCH!). Here’s a detailed breakdown of the metrics, my 3 biggest takeaways, and what comes next:

180 DAY METRICS:

REVENUE

$2.63m ARR
809 paying customers
$271/mo avg subscription price

ACTIVATION

14,338 company signups
81% of users have received at least 1 contact
10%+ free-to-paid conversion
800+ Hubspot integration connections

CHURN 

14.1% User Churn (more like 10%, agency canceled 80 accounts, came back under 1)
15.5% Revenue Churn (see above)


MY 3 BIGGEST TAKEAWAYS:

1. Top-of-funnel coming from LinkedIn has been incredible

I’ve never done this before, so it’s unclear how deep the well is. Santosh HAS done it before, and insists the well is very deep and we have a long way to go before it dries out. I also think we are getting even better on the content side, which I think is our ace-in-the-hole here.

2. Our activation metrics are great

From conversations I’ve had with others, most people struggle for a 30% script install, while we’re above 80%. I think the quality of the traffic coming from LinkedIn is responsible. Free-to-paid is also great. It’s not entirely zero-touch, since we hired Ilija, but we are still trying to figure out the motion, so it’s close.

3. Churn is the VERY obvious problem

Jason M. Lemkin would say this isn’t even a SaaS. I can’t say that I’m totally surprised - we are selling leads to SMB and VSMB in a hard environment. Santosh again is unsurprised by this 9 month LTV out of the gates. We have started down the long road of adding products and features to increase stickiness. 

Our first attempt is a traffic insights product that tells you ICP visitors by page. The alpha version a couple weeks ago was a miss. Working on 2nd now.

Curious as to what the product people on Linkedin think. Your feedback helped a ton with our first six weeks pricing dilemma (when we had 3000 signups and only 13 paying customers).

I talked to people on the original Clearbit Reveal team and churn was always a nightmare - for the same reasons we’re seeing it, and we are selling to on average much smaller companies - and the only way they reduced it was users from different departments using different products within their product suite. 

TO SUM IT UP:

It’s really awesome to see $2.6m ARR in 6 months. 

That’s where my first startup was after 4 years.

However, I’ve been at this long enough to know this churn number will start haunting us pretty soon.

I feel like we know what we need to do. 

This product may not be low enough churn to ever be a massive company … 

But I do think we can meaningfully reduce churn by creating great solutions for other departments. 

WHERE WILL RB2B BE IN 12 MONTHS?

If you tell me where our churn rate will be, I’ll tell you our ARR. 

I know one thing for sure… 

We know EXACTLY what we need to focus on. 

Better onboarding. 

More “wow” products. 

Lower churn.

We’ll just keep building.
