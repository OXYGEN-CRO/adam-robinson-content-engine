## Exact post text

After 7 conversations with top e-comm CMOs, it's clear companies are struggling with rising Customer Acquisition Costs. There are really only 3 options on what to do about it (with 1 being easier than the others)...

1. Somehow retool your product/brand so that you can serve a broad, untargeted ad that converts. (HARD)

2. Hit retail harder and faster than you ever would have before. (ALSO HARD)

3. Use a combination of all of the best identity technology available to run your own private customer graph to maximize signal to the ad platforms. (SURPRISINGLY EASY)

Number 3 seems like the no brainer.

Ad platforms need the ability to track users over time. 

So brands must be feeding events back into them with identifiers attached. 

Meta’s equation for determining the winner of an auction is: 

Cash Bid x Estimated Action x Ad Quality = Total Value

If you tell Meta who is looking at products and abandoning carts, that will help your Estimated Action score because Meta will know who are the most likely to convert. 

Result? 

- Same cash bid 
- Higher total value
- More auctions won with the same cash
- Lower CAC
- Higher growth with the same capital

Don’t sleep on the importance of Onsite Visitor Identity. 

It’s becoming a more important pillar in the DTC tech stack every day.
