## Exact post text

99% of startups are oblivious to the *HUGELY NEGATIVE* impact the Predictable Revenue playbook has on their sales culture:

Massive BDR teams at early-stage startups breed a state of collective misery that’s bad for execs, management and young salespeople. 

Here’s the breakdown of an all-too-common scenario:

1. Hire an army of young BDR’s

2. Tell them to build relationships with executive prospects twice their age

3. Tell them they need to be creative enough to stand out in RUINED inboxes

4. Tell them their job is highly-personalized outreach (but give them software and AI tools to help them SPAM)

5. Define success as a booked demo

6. Push them to “go for the kill” too early 

7. Set their life (and self-worth) up to where they could go weeks without a win

8. Watch as 100,000 SaaS companies spamming the same prospects leads to lower response rates

9. Further increase “productivity” through higher activity goals

10. C-suite breathes down managers’ neck, who is breathing down reps neck, as targets are missed week after week

11. BDRs (and manager) recognize the absurdity of the game they cannot possibly win, become disenchanted, lose trust in exec team and quit or leave sales entirely

Some may read this and think “well that’s just what being a BDR is”.

Wrong.

There is an alternative to this entire machine.

And it’s better for your sales culture BY FAR...

I’ve been saying the same thing for a while now.

And I'll keep saying it, haters be damned:
 
- Demand isn’t created by the BDR team, it’s captured

- With signals, website visits, automation, indirect lead flow through partnerships, you can capture 90% of the demand with 10% of the reps that your marketing activities are creating

- Buyers do all of their research on their own, and show up when they are ready to buy, so if you cut headcount (like myself and many others have), you won’t see your pipeline change

And that's just on the BDR side!

What about AEs?

Most series A/B startups could run with 50% less reps.

Instead of running the now-busted Salesforce playbook...

Feed a smaller pool of reps all the demand.

This leads to a fast-paced and fun culture, where everybody has the opportunity to crush it.

Yes, you will sacrifice “predictability”, but you will replace it with something more important... 

PROFITABILITY.

By only giving high-intent leads to your AEs, you set your sales machine up to serve your buyers, rather than you and your investors.

More activity + fewer reps = better sales culture.

It's time for a change.

Let's sell better.
