RB2B gets 1,500 signups every 30 days on 30k visits. 99% is from my Founder Brand. Now I’m making a big bet on SEO and AI Search to see how we can 10x that traffic. Here’s a breakdown of our current stats and the plan: 

Background:

1. We killed all paid channels in December of last year
2. It was because I felt we fell out of product-market fit
3. I’m now feeling better about our product direction
4. We’re layering on cold email, ads, SEO, maybe ugc

Current stats: 

Domain Reputation: 56 (I bought RB2B domain in late 2023)
Backlinks: 1,700
Referral Domains: 472

The plan - month 1: 

1. 7 landing pages for keywords we’re raking for as articles

Leadfeeder alternative (350 searches)
Instantly alternative (250 searches)
Lead forensics alternative (150 searches)
Warmly alternative (30 searches) 
Leadinfo alternative (30 searches)
Snitcher alternative (10 searches)
Sales tools (5200 searches)

2. Link Building

Building 3 40+ DA backlinks to RB2B of sites for the keyword “RB2B”

What I expect:

Do I expect my traffic to 10x in month 1? 

Not at all. 

SEO is a slow roll. We’re playing the long game.

Nobody really knows what the future holds, given the explosive growth in LLMs. 

BUT… 

Google still has 373x the searches of ChatGPT, according to Rand Fishken.

AND… 

LLMs scrape the internet, so we probably all need to be creating brand-building meaningful content that people are searching for regardless. 

We’re also trying to capture more demand at the bottom of the funnel with the landing page strategy… 

So that will at least be in the consideration set for internal exec chats. 

Will this be a long and winding road? 

You bet.

100%.
