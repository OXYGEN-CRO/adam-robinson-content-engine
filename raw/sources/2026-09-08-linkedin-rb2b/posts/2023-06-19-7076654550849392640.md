## Exact post text

The first two weeks of April stopped us dead in our tracks.

What happened?

If you missed the update post I made a few weeks ago, I’ll catch you up:

We did a sales contest that pulled some deals from March into April…

And at the same time, we found out our Average Contract Value had decreased from $20K to $12K.

Big problem.

Turns out, we were selling to smaller merchants than we thought we were prospecting to.

As my colleague Santosh put it, “We started ferociously building the second floor of our house, then we looked down and noticed we had not yet finished part of the foundation.”

So what do you do when you get knocked off your feet?

✅ If you find yourself in a hole, stop digging (and regroup).
✅ Know where you’re at.
✅ Understand that safety beats growth.

Today I’m breaking down how these principles helped us bounce back to end up actually growing by 7.6% ARR in April.

Hope it’s helpful for you.
