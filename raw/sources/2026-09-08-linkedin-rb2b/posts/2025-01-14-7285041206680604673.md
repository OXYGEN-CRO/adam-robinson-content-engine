## Exact post text

I hit rock bottom Thanksgiving 2017. I was sitting in my brother’s living room watching a Digital Marketing 101 course on my laptop. 10 minutes in I started to panic... "There is NO REASON anyone would buy us over MailChimp."

TO SET THE STAGE:

- I was 4 years into building my startup Robly Email Marketing
- We had been stuck at $3M ARR for 2 years
- We got to $3m ARR sales-led, ran out of leads
- I thought I needed to learn about marketing
- I went to DigitalMarketer(.)com and started taking courses

So I sat there on the couch, listening to Ryan Deiss talk about marketing. 

A few minutes in a bright yellow card filled the screen.

At the top it said STATEMENT OF VALUE.

Ryan said, “If you cannot fill out this card for your business, do not do ANYTHING else.” 

He said that if you cannot articulate a clear statement of value for your business… who you serve, how you help them, and why your customers should choose you… Nothing else will work.

So like any bright-eyed founder in their 20’s, I set out to conquer the card. 

60 seconds later I felt a wave of panic in my body.

My face turned red, my heart raced, my breathing accelerated.

As I looked at this VERY simple fill-in-the-blank card… 

“[PRODUCT NAME] enables [CUSTOMER] to experience [OUTCOME]”

I realized I couldn't answer it.

There was nothing I could write down for Robly, my stuck $3m ARR startup.

There was not a single reason ANY group of people would choose us if they knew about Mailchimp. 

1. We weren’t any less expensive. In fact - Mailchimp would have been FREE.
2. We were WAY behind on features, and they were building faster than us. 
3. We had no integrations, and they had over 1,000 of them.

I sat there for an hour, thinking about every customer I had spoken to over the prior 3 years, comparing what we offered them to Mailchimp.

Then a wave of nausea came over me.

Nothing had been working for me because nothing was GOING to work for me.

I didn’t sleep much that night.

At around 2am, I had an epiphany.

One that many startup founders had before me.

“I need to do something COMPLETELY different than what I’m doing right now.”

There was no way we were going to catch up to Mailchimp in the email space.

I woke up with the naive optimism that only a Founder in their 20’s can have.

I was strangely at peace with the fact that what I was working on was dead.

I needed to make a product so differentiated I could fill out this simple card.

At the time, I had no idea how long it would take…

Nor did I realize that the answer would come almost two years later, when I launched GetEmails (now Retention.com).

TAKEAWAY

Go fill out that statement of value card for your business.

Be as brutally honest with yourself as possible.

If you do not have a compelling answer.

More ads and cold email aren't going to help.

P.S.

I started a private community to help $0-1M founders find PMF. Almost everyone is struggling with the “statement of value” problem. If you want to join us, DM me.
