## Exact post text

Hey. If you’re a startup founder, you should consider raising your prices.

Why?

When you raise your prices, two things happen:

1. You make your product appear more premium than more down-market options.
2. You attract better customers in general, who are less likely to complain, and more likely to actually do the work to get results with your product.

You’d think that it’s harder to sell something more expensive because you’re only looking at the dollar amount.

But if you do premium marketing, maintain premium pricing, and offer a premium product that focuses on real, tangible results…

You’ll quickly find that I’m right on this one.
