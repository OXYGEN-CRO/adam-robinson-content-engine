## Exact post text

Last week I had a 5 minute conversation with the Head of Ops at Clay that changed the trajectory of our startup. Here are the 3 questions I asked him, his answers, and why founders must seek OUTSIDE perspective:

BACKGROUND:

I'm currently in a state of confusion and emotional pain.

Two common feelings for founders.

What about?

Our free-to-paid trial conversion rate SUCKS.

We somehow managed to get 1,000 free signups our first week, but only 7 converted to paid.

This is my first time ever building a PLG motion.

So I have a ton of questions, all anxiety inducing:

- Are people not getting value from the tool?
- Is this because we don’t have key integrations?
- Is it because we lack features?
- Is this because we don’t have in-app messaging?
- Is it because I don’t have a lower pricing tier?
- Is this normal for your first week???

If you’ve never done PLG before, the above is enough to drive you nuts.

So I reached out to Varun Anand (Head of Ops at Clay) to bounce some of these questions off him. They've been on the PLG journey for years.

Here's what I asked him:

Question (1):

“I have a free plan, which pushes LinkedIn profiles of website visitors to Slack. Then I have a $495 plan, which is to push data everywhere else. Is that just WRONG?”

Varun said that he actually thought it was a decent idea, it would:

- Give us a chance to focus on our highest-value prospects
- Allow us to figure out what exactly they would pay us for
- Give us time to see if we could keep them happy once paying

He thought that once we figured out our “free-to-paid” motion, we could add a lower tier.

Question (2):

"I want to invest massively in a content-driven community related to signal-based selling. What are your thoughts about that?"

Clay has a vibrant community in Slack, but it’s because that’s where people go to get product support. He said that he thought I was already doing a great job at building community on LinkedIn and that I should focus there until we’re farther along.

Question (3):

I wanted to ask Varun about the Clay Creators effort they are making, because I was planning to do a bunch of “influencer” marketing very early.

Varun pointed out that if we’re picking up 1,000 free signups in a week without even trying, acquisition is NOT our problem. Our problem is free-to-paid conversion. Once we had that dialed in, speeding it up would be a problem, but until then... it’s not.

TAKEAWAY:

This call was 5 minutes but it was powerful enough to make me rethink everything.

I killed two massive initiatives so that my team can focus on what matters.

Focus on LinkedIn.

Focus on free-to-paid conversion.

Drop everything else.

Outside perspectives can be incredibly valuable.

You just have to be willing to listen.
