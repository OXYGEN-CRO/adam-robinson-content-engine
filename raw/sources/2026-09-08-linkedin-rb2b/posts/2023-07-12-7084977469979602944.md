## Exact post text

Last week a PISSED OFF customer about to churn sent me a Loom video that completely changed our business. 

Here’s what happened (and why it’s going to help us add $300K MRR in the next 3 months):

On Tuesday, I had just come off yet another go-to-market meeting of teams saying they were going to WILDLY undershoot their goals....

I couldn’t figure out what was going on with our business. 

We had a euphoric Q1. Kept ramping staff into it thinking it would go on forever. 

Then on April 1st it just stopped.

So when a customer sent a Loom video to our support team saying that WE SUCKED, our product was TOO EXPENSIVE, and he wanted to CANCEL… I was miserable.

The video showed him comparing the revenue of our emails flows with that of a pseudo-competitor.

And to be honest, the competitor was kicking our ass by a factor of 4-5X. 

When I looked at their website, they were doing something we had never heard about… “Server-Side Tracking”. 

I was devastated. What is that? How do they do it?

But then I had a thought that 1000s of startup founders have had before me:

"If they can do it, we can do it better."

So I went to my engineering team and we started figuring out what exactly this company was doing and why they were kicking our ass. 

Turns out, it wasn't that complicated.

What could have been a sh*t day, quickly morphed into one that changed the direction of our business.

And it all started with a super annoying Loom video from a customer that wanted to cancel.

What's the takeaway?

Every challenge is an opportunity in disguise. 

Always listen to your pissed off customers. 

They might just be the key to your next big breakthrough.
