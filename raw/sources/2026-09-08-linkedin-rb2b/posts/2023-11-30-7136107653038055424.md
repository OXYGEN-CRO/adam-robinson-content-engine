## Exact post text

After 7 straight months of FRUSTRATING 2% monthly growth, I am happy to report that we are posting a +4% topline month with +25% EBITDA margin ($500k cash generated). Here’s what I’m proud of (and not so proud of) that happened in November: 

Proud of: 

- Increased growth AND profitability. Incredible financial positioning for 2024.
- We pulled off our first successful medium-sized event in LA.
- We got over 50+ leads for the B2B product from a single LinkedIn post!
- We think we’ll have a B2B product by late next week. WHAT?!?!?!
- We started the process to onboard and support platforms beyond Shopify
- We added Abandoned Checkout flows to our product suite
- We released features to accelerate our SOC2 compliance process
- We built processes around supporting complex customer tech stacks
- Sharon is back from maternity leave and taking over leadership of CS
- Making progress to between CS and sales to very clearly define ICP, which is the single most important factor to reduce churn

Not So Proud of: 

- We’re struggling to get on the same page & operationalize it (i.e. OKRs)
- We don’t really have the ability to predict what’s going to happen in the future - I wouldn’t have said we were going to hit 4% this month … I wonder if anyone does these days.
- It was brought to my attention during a meeting with a recent hire that our Glassdoor presence is absolutely terrible (1.5 stars) because of the layoff we did when our VP sales resigned and the fact that we had 6 employees until Oct of last year. Not sure what to do about it.

Biggest Opportunities: 

- The B2B product. I can’t wait to get this thing off the ground and grow it. 
- The interest from Linkedin has been so crazy that Warmly (a competitor) has been cold emailing people who engaged with our posts...  and we don’t even have a product yet!!!
- We are creating a series of features for mid-market omnichannel retailers using CDPs that will expand our TAM and (hopefully) lead to larger, lower churn deals for us.
- Continuing to hone in on our ICP, not selling to people we know will not be successful (therefore lowering churn)

And the biggest opportunity...

50% growth rate and 50% run-rate EBITDA margin by the end of 2024. 

Anybody think that’s possible?

It seems crazy to say it. 

I’d settle for 25% and 25%.

But when I look at our current growth rate, the state of our Ecommerce business, and the response to these LinkedIn posts… 

I think we might just be able to do it. 

And that would be a MONSTER 2024 for Retention.com.

The good news is that you’ll be able to follow it all, the W’s and L’s, the misery and euphoria, right here on LinkedIn.

Stay tuned, and keep building.
