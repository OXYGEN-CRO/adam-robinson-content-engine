## Exact post text

I grew RB2B from $0-$4m ARR in 40 weeks. Then I killed cold email, ads, UGC, and SEO in December. We just hit $5.5m ARR with 3 FTEs. My goal is to hit $10m ARR by July 1, 2026. Here’s my 7-Step Marketing Playbook to get there:

1. Cold Email with Taylor Haren

- Hitting our entire TAM with 1 email (only) every other month
- 40% of our paying customers have been sent an email - it’s working

2. Thought Leadership Ads with Ali and Alex

- Content focused on 3 FTE + AI journey to $10m ARR
- Trying to exclude as much of existing customer and fan base as possible
- I want this spend to be as truly top of funnel (brand) as possible

3. Meta and YouTube Retargeting Ads with Ali and Alex

- I’m recording a bunch of selfie and at the desk videos
- Going to have testimonial and product videos as well
- YouTube first, repurposed for Meta

4. SEO with Sam Dunning

- Listicles, landing pages, and how to’s related to website visitor tracking and website visitor identification
- Updating existing content that is driving traffic, doing light link building

5. Influencer

- Once we update our product to fix the filtering, routing, and “what now” and I understand our ICP a little better I will prob want to jump into Influencer

6. Keep Growing UnF*ck My Startup (live weekly show)

- We’re getting 400-750 live attendees every week, 70% founders
- We’ve got some GREAT guests coming up - Kyle Coleman, Peep Laja, Simon Hoilberg, Will McTighe

7. Raise the Stakes on LinkedIn and Building in Public

- The world needs to see a 3 FTE (+AI) $10m ARR SaaS
- I will document every AI test, tool and failure along the way

TAKEAWAY

Last year I felt horrible about our product, so we killed all of our marketing. 

We think we’re about to solve the 3 major problems: 

- Filtering
- Routing
- “What Now”

If we actually solve the product problems, this marketing effort will catapult us to $10m ARR … 

With just 3 people! 

But if we miss on the product, it will all be wasted money and energy. 

What’s going to happen? 

- Will we hit $10m ARR by July 1 of next year?
- Will we get stuck again and miss?

Time will tell. 

Drop your predictions in the comments. 👇
