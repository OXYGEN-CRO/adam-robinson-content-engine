## Exact post text

I’ve scaled 3 sales organizations in 13 years. These are the 7 BIGGEST LIES I hear about scaling up sales teams at early stage startups:

1. Bigger teams mean faster growth

The opposite is true. The “Old Way” of building sales teams is a large internal org pushing on a string that appears to be “profitable-ish” but is really losing money. Why? Because it forces reps to use a prospecting playbook that doesn’t work in 2023 and is capturing (rather than creating) demand. 

2. You can scale reps linearly and maintain productivity

This never works. In 2023, prospecting activity doesn’t CREATE demand, it CAPTURES it. Ask anyone who has rapidly grown a BDR org this year, only to see pipeline numbers unchanged. Most of the time unmeasured (thus unscaled) activities like brand and podcast are actually creating demand.

3. It’s good to have your AE’s prospect

Wrong. What works is to have your AEs calendars so full of demos that they have to stay up until 9pm every night dealing with follow-up but they don’t mind because they are making SO MUCH money. 

4. You need to compete on expensive perks to attract top talent

Not true. Great reps don’t care about your kombucha machine or unlimited PTO. The best reps want to work for a highly-ambitious company in a fast-paced environment that has a reputation for flooding reps with GOOD leads they can close.

5. "Predictable Revenue" is the goal

This model is predicated on building a massively inefficient assembly line of reps who execute a playbook that 100,000 other SaaS companies are also running. It creates a massive amount of waste in the name of "predictability" that wont come.

6. Ecosystem plays are for the big boys

Part of the “new way” of building hyper-lean sales orgs is to create/capture demand through developing agency/partner/influencer type ecosystems the second you hit PMF and use them to funnel low-in-funnel, declared-intent leads into your small team of highly-trained, senior reps to close with incredible velocity.

7. Founder brand isn’t important for me and my space

DTC ecomm really isn’t on Linkedin and yet I spend the last nine months posting daily and creating video content. It worked. Now we're creating a B2B product and it’s working even better. Downside: It’s *very* hard work and takes tons of time. But, there’s nothing like it. It’s the future.

TAKEAWAY

Most folks think scaling sales teams is about creating Predictable Revenue. 

They think sales creates demand and the more muscle they put behind it the faster they will grow. 

Because of this they hire too fast, don't train enough, and force salespeople to live and die (mostly die) by a playbook that doesn’t work anymore. 

They have no idea that they can set up an ultra-high-velocity sales machine that creates low-in-the-funnel leads from months and years of content that cuts through and indirect channels. 

The Predictable Revenue, “Push” model is dead.

The next generation of sales teams will get built the “new way”.
