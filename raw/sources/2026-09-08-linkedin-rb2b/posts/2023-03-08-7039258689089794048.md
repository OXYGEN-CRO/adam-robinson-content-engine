## Exact post text

A lot of people ask me when I knew our product was destined to be a billion-dollar company…

Truth is, I always knew the potential was there but the real lightbulb moment was when even our beginner Account Executives were selling our product like crazy.

When we built out our sales team, we brought on some absolute superstars who have been crushing for years — and we also brought on some newer people who just had a ton of energy and were hungry for a new opportunity.

Our newest AEs were able to close $10k in MRR within their first week at the company, with barely any training.

If you know anything about software sales you know how insane that is.

My point?

A great product is easy to sell. You’ll know when you have ‘it.’
