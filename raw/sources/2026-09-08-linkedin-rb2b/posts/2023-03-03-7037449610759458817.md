## Exact post text

What’s drives you to keep building as a startup founder can make or break your ‘success.’

Let me explain.

I was listening to MrBeast on Joe Rogan, and he goes on for hours about how much he loves the idea of being the best.

He commits every hour he has and every dollar he earns into making better and better videos for his audience. It’s all he cares about.

He doesn’t want to be a billionaire like some of us do.

He doesn’t care about a 50,000 square foot mansion and a 300-foot yacht.

If he wanted to be worth $5 billion, he could make two phone calls, sell his empire, and he’d have everything he wants.

But that’s not his pursuit.

His goal is to be the absolute BEST in the game of content, and I believe he’s already there.

If you’re looking for the “meaning of life”, there are a number of different answers. But I think that one of the best approaches is simply the pursuit of excellence.

Get lost in your craft and do everything you can to improve at it. It’s one of the most fun games to play.

That’s why I do what I do, and I’ll continue doing it until I get there (I never will).

What drives you?
