## Exact post text

I have some bittersweet news. Yesterday, RB2B hit $4M ARR (just 40 weeks post-launch). Sounds great, right? Wrong. Churn has gotten so out of control that I’m officially declaring RB2Bv1 dead. Here’s what comes next:

Here’s what RB2Bv1 was: 

- Person-Level Website Visitor Identity
- Free push to Slack

Churn is higher than new business for four reasons: 

- People need to filter out the noise
- People need to route the signal to a rep
- Reps want to be told what action to take
- And there are now 3,247,892 copycats

Luckily, we’ve solved the first 2 problems, and the 3rd is coming soon.

How? 

We completely rebuilt our Hubspot integration 

We believe it solves the filtering and routing problem. 

Here’s the 5-Step RB2B >>> Hubspot Setup: 

1. Make sure your TAM is in Hubspot 
2. Send the RB2B Lead to Hubspot 
3. If the lead is an named account in Hubspot, HS tags a rep
4. Hubspot either alerts the rep in HS, who lightly tailors a message, or
5. Hubspot sends a slack message with the rep tagged, who gets alerted

There are a few key differentiators from our last HS integration: 

- Repeat signals (which for larger deals are very valuable)
- You don’t get charged $0.50 for a marketing contact
- We are passing LI URLs to the record and pageviews to timeline
- This allows filtering IN HS, and POWERFUL workflows to be built

We believe we’ve solved the filtering and routing problem for HS users, but we still have one more problem to go until we’re at RB2Bv2. 

The “what now” problem. 

Soon, RB2B will tell a rep exactly what to do when a signal comes in from a named account, based on context from the CRM and pageviews. 

“Connect on LinkedIn, and send the following message …” etc

While RB2Bv1 is completely dead, filtering, routing, and “what now” is what our customers are telling us they need. 

(in addition to solving the broken link problem, which should happen this week)

We’re also looking at adding reverse IP lookup for companies (which is worldwide), and adding several other identity vendors for higher coverage. 

That should give us a push to $10-$15m ARR … at which point we’ll probably stall out again and have to re-invent ourselves yet once again.

… RB2Bv1 is dead …

Longlive RB2Bv2!
