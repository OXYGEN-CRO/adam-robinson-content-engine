## Exact post text

99% of startups think they need to maximize predictable revenue. They're wrong. The next crop of $1B unicorn startups will solve for sales velocity above all else. Here's 9 reasons why velocity will define the new world:

1. Revenue was never actually predictable

Everybody underestimated their revenue in 2021-2022 and overestimated their revenue in 2023... 2024 seems to have either surprised people negatively or positively but certainly hasn’t been what most predicted.

2. Prospects do their own research

The best GTM motions touch the prospects early and often in the research process with timely and relevant content that helps them on their buying journey. Making it easy for prospects who aren’t ready to buy to access and consume your content to wins deals down the road.

3. Outbound channels are maxed out

Automation overwhelm has led to response rates being cut in half over the last 36 months. Inboxes are ruined, LinkedIn is worse, and AI tools will increase problem 100x.

4. The world is set up for a new GTM model

As cold outreach becomes more difficult, the model of the future will be one that warms prospects up through brand, innovative marketing strategies, UGC-generating machines, small events, indirect channels like partner and affiliate, and captures demand VERY low in the funnel. Close rates are 2-3x, sales cycles are 60% less and sales teams will be smaller.

5. “Influencers” will become more powerful

As the era of AI content unfolds, the best, most authentic content coming from the most trusted voices will become even more powerful. Social continues to become the primary method to create awareness, even for SaaS.

6. High-Velocity environments attract the best talent

The best people want to be on teams that prioritize moving FAST. The antithesis of this is a bloated GTM org that values head count above all.

7. You can be profitable (at the expense of predictability)

Running a lean team that captures demand at the bottom of the funnel (and resists the temptation to layer on outbound reps who will simply capture the demand that you are creating elsewhere) will increase focus on capital-efficiency, reduce system-wide dependence on external funding and create a less volatile ecosystem in the long-term.

8. Massive user acquisition sets you up for global domination

Santosh always says that 100,000 users is the equivalent of a $1B/yr marketing budget. I have yet to hit it, but we’re going to try.

TAKEAWAY:

It’s almost 2025.

The Predictable Revenue model is VERY dead.

The next great startups will sacrifice predictability for velocity (and profitability).

Not only is it what our buyers want, but...

It will create healthier companies with happier employees.

Everything is setting up for this to be the next great GTM motion…

How do I know?

Because this is exactly how we grew RB2B from $0 to $3M ARR in 6 months.
