Hey Linkedin, I need your help. My YouTube sucks. We have 905 subscribers, despite posting 222 videos. Here’s why YouTube is my next frontier and Dave Gerhardt's advice for expanding my Founder Brand:

My LinkedIn Founder Brand is the most impactful business move I've ever made. 

It’s the reason everybody in the ecosystem knows RB2B, Santosh and I.

LinkedIn is becoming a well-oiled machine for us. 

But it’s SOOOOO valuable. 

I’m trying to figure out how to go even harder. 

So I reached out to Dave Gerhardt, who wrote the book on Founder Brand.

Here was his advice:

______

Me: Wadup Dave. Is there a job title for “managing someone’s personal brand across channels?"

Gerhardt: I think you pick ONE channel, and start there. You already have the content you know resonates. A large audience you have built up. Lots of data on content that performs well. I think for you, that channel is YouTube and YouTube shorts.

Me: Dope. What do I do next? 

Gerhardt: Find someone who can help you with YouTube. Tell them you already have an audience and content ideas. You just need someone to help execute And then I’d post about it to your LinkedIn audience. See what inbound comes in. 

Me: I ❤️ u DG. 

______

That's where you come in Linkedin...

I’m looking for someone to help me grow my YouTube channel. 

I want us to be as good a YouTube as we are at LinkedIn.

I’m looking for someone who: 

- Knows YouTube and knows the algorithm
- Has a demonstrative track record that we can vet
- Can dictate programming/editing for my YouTube channel

I’m not looking for an agency. 

I either want to find a contractor, or possibly a FTE.

If you are excited about helping me grow on YouTube, apply here: https://lnkd.in/gSZ-9f97  

Tag anyone you think might help me crush YouTube!

Thanks Linkedin.
