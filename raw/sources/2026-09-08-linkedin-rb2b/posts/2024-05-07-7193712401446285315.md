## Exact post text

After four DOWN months in a row, I’m happy to report Retention.com ARR was up 1.6% in April. RB2B is up to $242,400 ARR (from $61k). Here’s what went well in April, what didn’t and why we’re not out of the woods quite yet: 

WHAT WENT WELL: 

Retention.com

- Up month (whoop! Go team!)
- I have successfully handed off day-to-day operations to Diana Ross
- We’re continuing the EOS build across all of our business lines
- We’re learning the meaning of “putting process in place” 
- We have a new deep understanding of the competitive space we find ourselves operating in (which is VERY different from 18 months ago)

RB2B: 

- One word: VIRALITY
- 5,043 companies signed up in 60 days, 78% have collected >1 contact
- We're at $242k ARR and don’t have any of the features we plan to charge for
- We pushed Hubspot integration yesterday, ICP filters and “credit system” next
- We think we’ve figured out a no-brainer offer for LeadGen agencies
- Support still greater than 90% non-human, cheers Robb Clarke

WHAT DIDN’T GO SO WELL: 

- We only generated $300k of cash in April. 
- I was expecting $500k. If April had 31 days, we would have generated $425k. 
- We make performance bonus payments quarterly, which weighed down April.
- Apparently we had a few chunky invoices, and it’s trade show season in DTC
- We have made several hires related to the Enterprise Data Sales business Santosh is building - positive for the long run, but negative for April’s cash
- Did I mention we're building an Enterprise Data business as a 3rd startup??? 
- Revenue growth of RB2B has been slower than expected as my initial PLG thesis was dead-wrong and we’ve had to figure out a new hypothesis

WHY WE’RE NOT OUT OF THE WOODS YET

Retention's market has become highly competitive.  

Determining a growth strategy in that environment is difficult on its own, but we’ve put it on the back burner to implement EOS so that no matter what happens we’ll run like a real company in the future. 

While all of that has been going on, we have been investing heavily in what Santosh has always said the larger opportunity was for us in the first place…

Taking our existing tech to the B2B space. 

I believe we are currently in the middle of our darkest moment. 

At Retention, we’re being forced to adjust to a new reality which is worse than we thought it would be a year ago. 

Meanwhile, we are investing in new businesses that aren’t going to meaningfully contribute to growth until later this year. 

But, if I close my eyes and imagine what this will all look like in 12-18 months:

1. EOS is fully rolled out, R! is running smoothly
2. We have come up with a credible growth strategy 
3. RB2B is humming, in the high single-digit ARR with 5 employees
4. Data Sales is contributing low single-digits  

There’s a chance we’re at 30m ARR with the crew we have right now, growing at a very healthy clip. 

That … for me … is the Promised Land.

Just keep building.
