## Exact post text

I’m thrilled to announce that RB2B crossed $5m ARR 13mo post launch, profitably, with 5 people, and even MORE thrilled to announce our partnership with Demandbase. Here are the 5 big reasons I’m so excited:

1. Our coverage is now 80% of total traffic!!!

This partnership will allow us to identify a whole new pocket of traffic we previously didn’t have access to. 40% person-level, 40% company-level.

2. We needed an industry-leading partner for Company-Level ID. 

In today’s market person-level ID isn’t enough. Company level is useful, and the overlap between the two is very low.

3. RB2B becomes a global product. 

People have been asking if we can ID European traffic for a LONG time. We still won’t do it at the person-level (GDPR), but we will do it for companies.

4. It democratizes access to Demandbase’s network.

Demandbase used to be for $250m ARR and above, now you can have unlimited access their company-level ID product with any paid RB2B plan, starting at $149/mo. 

5. People can finally rip out their existing provider and use us.

Both Demandbase and RB2B believe that true ABM can’t be a black box. You either need Demandbase’s full set of complex tools, or you should just be driving traffic and retargeting site visitors every way possible. There is no middle … either go up to them or down to us. 

TAKEAWAY

Demandbase and RB2B will be partners for a long time. 

I view this is foundational for our future, even though it doesn’t contribute to solving the three biggest pain points with our product: 

1. Filtering (get rid of the noise) 
2. Routing (get reps the signal)
3. What now (get reps great messaging) 

V1 solutions 1 and 2 have been solved with deep Salesforce and Hubspot integrations. 

We’re starting to understand how we might solve the “what now” as well. 

If we’re correct, the RB2B of 2026 will look nothing like the RB2B of today. 

Will we be correct, be able to solve these problems (and others), and actually create a sticky product? 

Or will our attempt at v2 of RB2B fail miserably? 

There’s only one way to find out. 

We have to keep building.
