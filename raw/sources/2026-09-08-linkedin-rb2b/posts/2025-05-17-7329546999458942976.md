## Exact post text

I just got roasted by a bunch of marketers on Wynter for my new RB2B website copy. I genuinely thought it could not be any clearer! “The whole website seems unclear.” “Most of the page repeats the same unimpressive points.” “I would suggest updating for clarity.” … OUCH!  

This was not an easy pill to swallow for someone who takes pride in being able to express marketing messages clearly and succinctly. 

We’re launching a new brand next week. 

After speaking at two of Peep Laja’s events and going to dinner with him many times, I decided I actually needed to give Wynter a try to give the brand launch its best shot.

Wynter is bananas…

It’s a marketplace where you can submit your brand and messaging and get unbiased feedback from your target market in under 48 hours.

I had never done anything like this in the past … but the results made me wish I had.

I dropped the new homepage in, thinking what I had was actually pretty good.

48 hours later I look at the feedback … It was like getting punched in the gut. 

Nobody thought it was great. 

Around half gave it a 3/5. 

A bunch of people had no idea what I was talking about. 

I downloaded all of the feedback into a spreadsheet, then put the following prompt into ChatGPT: 

“I am trying to improve the clarity of the website copy on rb2b.com. Here is a spreadsheet of feedback I got from marketers, who are our target market. The columns of the spreadsheet are survey questions asked. The rows of the spreadsheet are the person who answered, and their responses. Can you make suggestions on how I should change the copy based ONLY on what was said in the spreadsheet?”

And it gave me five areas of improvement which I completely agree with, actioned literally all of them, and everybody at RB2B thinks it made the copy MUCH clearer and more effective.

TAKEAWAY

Sometimes with brand and messaging it’s impossible to see the forest through the trees. 

(who knew that “deanonymization” and “person-level ID” are jargony?)

We should all seek feedback from people who aren’t being nice to us about website copy.

Peep created a great machine that can get you that feedback on demand.

Has anybody else been humbled by Wynter?

If you haven’t, you should. 

P.S.

Comment WYNTER if you want to see the transcript of my whole back and forth w/ ChatGPT about my Wynter feedback.
