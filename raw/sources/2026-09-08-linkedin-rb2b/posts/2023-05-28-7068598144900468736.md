## Exact post text

My company has gone from 0 → $20M ARR in ~2.5 years, profitably.

Here are 3 simple lessons I wish I knew before we started:

1) Velocity is king in startups. Whatever you’re doing, push to move faster.

2) The effort it takes to build something massive is pretty similar to the effort it takes to build a safe ‘lifestyle business.’ Go for it.

3) Staying bootstrapped for as long as possible gives you freedom to make choices that YOU want to make in the best interest of your business. I’d recommend it. 

Not an exhaustive list, but I think this will be helpful to you if you’re a founder or operator. Follow me for more insights as we scale to a $1B valuation.
