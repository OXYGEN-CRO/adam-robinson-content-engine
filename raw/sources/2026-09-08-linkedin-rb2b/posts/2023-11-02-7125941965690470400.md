## Exact post text

Every month I *think* we will show an increasing growth rate. But we end up stubbornly stuck at +2% MoM. It's very FRUSTRATING. But then my startup founder friends remind me of something important...

It’s 2023, man!

Most SaaS businesses are feeling a similar type of pain.

They’re just not dumb enough to post about it on Linkedin.

That's the tradeoff with building in public.

But I'm committed now.

100% transparency or bust.

So....

Here’s what went down at Retention.com in October 2023: 

What I’m Proud Of: 

- We crossed the $21m ARR mark in and generated around $325k cash flow
- We continued to make progress in tightening up the delicate dance between sales and CS to create a positive customer experience
- We released our new dashboard to give customers a better understanding of our impact on their business
- We dipped our toes into our new DTC media brand centered around micro-interviews with interesting people at in-person events 
- We’re almost done putting together our first 100-person ecomm event that sounds pretty amazing (We’re in LA 11/9! DM me to join)
- We got a version zero of our B2B product out and are currently sending a handful of B2B SaaS companies person-level website visitor leads
- I’ve had a ton of calls to understand the website visitor identity competitive landscape (thanks Brendan Short for the assist there)
- I recorded my first three episodes of our new B2B podcast called “Your Outbound Sucks”
- We’re nearing the end of a transition to SaaS financials, and are looking to hire in FP&A

Things I’m not so proud of: 

- At the end of the day, we’re SaaS, we sell into SMB… Churn can look better
- I thought we’d be farther along by now on the B2B product, but alas...
- We decided to postpone the Shopify app to prioritize engineering resources for B2B and SOC2 (can’t do everything all at once)
- I’m annoyed we can’t get our growth to 5%/mo

Our biggest opportunities:

- We’ll see how this 100ish person event in LA goes … If we can get the right people there, it could be a major unlock for us
- A continued drift upmarket will put wind in our sails
- The initial response from the B2B product is very positive … We just have to make it much more usable, and figure out who we’re selling it to

TAKEAWAY:

Things have been really hard since Q2. 

It doesn’t seem to be getting any easier. 

The good news is that we’re still a profitable, growing SaaS company … 

Even if it’s not growing as fast as I’d like it to.

Profitable will never go out of style.

P.S.

Have questions? What type of data do you want to see?

AMA in the comments 👇
