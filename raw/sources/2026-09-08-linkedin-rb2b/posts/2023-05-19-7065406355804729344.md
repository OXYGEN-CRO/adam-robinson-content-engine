## Exact post text

Being an entrepreneur is a scary pursuit — especially in the beginning.

I like to think of it as being a toddler that is dropped in the jungle to fend for themselves.

You’re alone, you’re in the dark, and you’re just trying to find your way and survive.

There’s no 2nd place trophy, no participation medals, no safety net, and everything that is around you wants to crush, destroy and devour you.

But you know what? I wouldn’t want it any other way.

I think it’s actually the most natural way to live. It’s primal, it’s a thrill-a-minute ride and what I love most… anything is possible. I can dream big and make those dreams my reality.

Who’s with me?
