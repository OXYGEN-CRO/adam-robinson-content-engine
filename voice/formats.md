# Formats

## Observed repertoire

These formats recur in the 200-post sample. They are not approved templates. A future post should have one primary pillar and funnel job, even where a source combines a lesson and commercial postscript.

| Format | Useful job | Structure / source | What it needs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dashboard with emotion | Build trust | Result→feeling→causes/hypotheses→next move. July 31 [P23]; Jan 17 [P142]. | Dated, scoped numbers; no invented screenshot. |
| Public experiment | Earn continued attention | Hypothesis→rules/stakes→constraints→watch it unfold. Apr 2 [P86]; Aug 25 [P10]. | Distinguish target from achieved outcome. |
| Founder/customer dialogue | Explain a tradeoff | Objection→response→new objection→implication. June 23 [P47]; Dec 5 [P167]. | Real evidence or clearly marked illustrative reconstruction. |
| Numbered lessons | Teach a method | Credibility/problem→points→takeaway. Sep 1 [P5]; Dec 4 [P168]. | Source-backed experience; accurate attribution. |
| Backstory/turning point | Help stuck founders recognize themselves | Before→failure→discovery→breakthrough→reader lesson. May 31 [P59]; Dec 14 [P161]. | Preserve date conflicts and public-use limits. |
| Before/after | Show a changed view or life | Parallel situations with honest limits. Apr 22 [P79]; Mar 19 [P94]. | No implied medical causation or synthetic biography. |
| One-line belief/joke | Introduce a worldview | One tension, little explanation. Apr 14 [P82]; Aug 22 [P12]; Mar 17 satire [P95]. | Enough context to distinguish joke from operating plan. |
| Industry take | Translate an event into a founder decision | Event→thesis→reasoning→implication. July 18 [P30]; May 13 [P70]. | Fresh verification before treating dated opinion as current fact. |
| Copy teardown | Teach clarity | Old/new example→reasons→rules. Feb 19 [P112]; Feb 27 [P107]. | Exact supplied copy and supportable results. |
| Guest playbook/invitation | Bring readers to deeper instruction | Guest outcome→audience pain→agenda→link. June 28 [P44]; Aug 31 [P6]. | Guest attribution and confirmed event details. |
| Resource/course | Convert interest into learning | Lesson→contents→access. Mar 5 [P103]; Nov 8 [P188]. | Real artifact, valid destination, authorized follow-up. |
| Team appreciation | Credit the operating reality | Gratitude→specific contribution→shared ambition. Dec 31 [P154]; June 16 [P51]. | Correct roles/names and source permissions. |

## Cross-platform adaptation

In the October 2, 2024 team interview, producer John Eley describes finding documentary stories through Adam's interviews and actual events, then filming, editing and adding supporting footage. Informal observations and live teaching require different production effort. Adam is absent from this episode; it is third-party process history, and the team's proposed video goals do not establish Adam's approved pillars. [Source: 17:10–19:58](../raw/sources/2026-09-08-youtube/videos/VhaxdYjgKyY/VhaxdYjgKyY.md); [[research/founder-content-history-review|full review and attribution]].

The October 29,2025 YouTube workflow begins with an ideas doc and LinkedIn posts, voice notes, presentations and courses. Samu prepares intro/outro/body bullets; Adam lightly edits, uses a teleprompter and records in Riverside; Samu edits. The then-current distribution was a weekly video plus newsletter/LinkedIn promotion. This establishes a process, not a permanent schedule. [P195]

Repeated ideas are part of the observed repertoire: AI business dialogue[P64]/[P199], seven growth plays[P119]/[P158], and the $10m freedom argument[P77]/[P153]. Repetition does not create independent proof or mandate near-duplicates in a future calendar. No visual identity is established by these posts; consult [[brand/BRAND]].
