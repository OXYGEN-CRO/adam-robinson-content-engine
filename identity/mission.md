# Mission

## Summary

**Proposed synthesis, not an approved mission statement:** help founders build profitable software businesses by sharing the decisions, mistakes and distribution methods behind his own companies. His tutorials repeatedly turn a personal operating experience into advice the viewer can use. [March 31, 00:00–00:45 and 05:35–06:21](../raw/sources/2026-09-08-youtube/videos/F-WwDP1d-pM/F-WwDP1d-pM.md).

## Who to help

The expanded archive makes the intent explicit. In his [November 11, 2023 post](../raw/sources/2026-09-08-linkedin-rb2b/posts/2023-11-11-7129220348616593409.md), Adam says he shares the journey for founders who are stuck and for a younger version of himself. In [January 2025](../raw/sources/2026-09-08-linkedin-rb2b/posts/2025-01-07-7282507888328167424.md), he turns that intent into a concrete teaching job: clearer messaging and a real customer-discovery loop for early founders. The community promoted in that post is a historical offer, not a currently available program established by this repo.

**Working mission for editorial decisions:** make the hard parts of building a software company more understandable and actionable for the founder experiencing them now. Pair recognition of the emotional problem with a specific decision or learning task. This wording is our synthesis of his stated intent.

The clearest audience in these tutorials is the founder building or growing a SaaS business, especially one working with limited people and capital. He also teaches founders to create useful LinkedIn content for their own audience. This audience overlaps with buyers of his software, but the educational subject matter is wider than website visitor identification. See [[audience/ideal-follower]].

## Change to create

- Make difficult operating experiences usable for other founders, including hiring mistakes and lost product-market fit.
- Help founders create trust before asking buyers to purchase.
- Encourage continued building and improvement while keeping teams and operating commitments manageable.

These are interpretations of his stated lessons, grounded in [March 24, 02:14–04:06 and 07:03–09:00](../raw/sources/2026-09-08-youtube/videos/aaBZ3f5TViQ/aaBZ3f5TViQ.md), [March 31, 05:35–06:21](../raw/sources/2026-09-08-youtube/videos/F-WwDP1d-pM/F-WwDP1d-pM.md), and [April 16, 00:50–02:40](../raw/sources/2026-09-08-youtube/videos/eUzqVJpZPP4/eUzqVJpZPP4.md).

## Current expression of the mission

September 1 advice retains the emphasis on differentiated products and marketing, delayed capital, careful hiring and founder freedom. The newer Claude workshop adds a concrete teaching commitment: guests must give away skills and teach in a way non-engineers can implement. This extends the existing founder education into practical GTM work; it does not establish that Adam personally built every demonstrated system. [September advice](../raw/sources/2026-09-08-linkedin/posts/2026-09-01-7500601621890879488.md), [June workshop brief](../raw/sources/2026-09-08-linkedin/posts/2026-06-28-7477048608886812672.md).

## Why this matters in his account

He argues that founders often hide failures while peers are struggling with the same problems. His own account of sharing the failed Retention.com expansion connects candor to trust and a new B2B audience. Treat this as his explanation of the outcome, not an independently isolated causal experiment. [March 24, 03:07–04:06](../raw/sources/2026-09-08-youtube/videos/aaBZ3f5TViQ/aaBZ3f5TViQ.md).
