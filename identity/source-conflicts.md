# Source conflicts and interpretation boundaries

## Summary

Recency determines which report is most relevant; it does not resolve differences automatically. Retain the original wording, date, company and metric definition. Distinguish true contradictions from different periods, denominators, plans and promotional framing. The working treatment below is an editorial decision, not independent financial verification.

Publication is not necessarily measurement date. The [November 7, 2025 course at 9:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VaJ6IK7YeNs&t=562) explicitly situates a section in 2024; its opening and closing use other RB2B stages. Treat the compilation's metrics as separate recording-relative accounts.

## Interpretation and attribution

Automatic-caption text is evidence of what the caption service returned, not a verbatim human-certified transcript. In [Xovf7vlPfOU at 3:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xovf7vlPfOU&t=219), the apparent Albacross reference conflicts with surrounding RB2B promotion. Raw text stays intact; the company map does not acquire an extra Adam venture.

A compelling headline can omit conditions. The seven-day AI challenge ends with [zero critical issues requiring human intervention at 11:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b7wERLHGkDo&t=669), a narrower outcome than proof of no human work. Its ordinary operating walkthrough includes [daily founder review at 4:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b7wERLHGkDo&t=253). Promised seven-month autonomy [P86] is not proof it happened. Customer testimonials in the [October product video at 4:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IS4rbNLdPOk&t=274) remain customer claims, not Adam-company revenue or independently tested ROI.

Advice can change or depend on stage. [November ABM guidance at 10:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YNaPeDu4xo8&t=640) keeps manual research but switches its earlier manual sending to sequencers. The course's [financing options at 1:11:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VaJ6IK7YeNs&t=4260) allow VC after profitable scale; criticism of premature fundraising is not an absolute ban. Its $10–15m annual distributions example is explicitly counterfactual, not personal income.

Personal-content boundaries are time-specific. [October LinkedIn guidance at 1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IlM44T-Pl_o&t=74) says he excludes personal life and hobbies; his April 2026 post discusses sobriety, family, Aspen and recreation [P79]. Observed later disclosure qualifies the earlier rule without authorizing fresh private-life claims.

**Pricing feedback and performed uncertainty:** the December 3, 2025 course (31:08–31:30) and February 20, 2026 video (10:19–10:54) credit commenters with helping change RB2B pricing. In the April 23, 2025 Tom Hunt conversation (46:23–47:27), Adam says an early pricing discussion supplied insight without, he thinks, changing the decision already intended; he also describes later invitations that made him sound more undecided than he was. These accounts differ in how much causal credit they give public feedback. They do not establish that every retelling refers to precisely the same pricing revision. Preserve the explicit admission and avoid a clean claim that the audience originated every decision. [[raw/sources/2026-09-08-youtube/videos/M0VvUsyJQ-M/M0VvUsyJQ-M|April source]]; [[raw/sources/2026-09-08-youtube/videos/bMm9bYTTeJs/bMm9bYTTeJs|December source]]; [[raw/sources/2026-09-08-youtube/videos/4Wyy1YiMWwo/4Wyy1YiMWwo|February source]].

**Competitive storytelling:** in that same April conversation (48:54–49:29), Adam calls his early claim that 6sense feared his three-week-old startup somewhat disingenuous and says it was not reality. Record this as his retrospective characterization of a particular narrative; neither the original fear claim nor a universal judgment about his disclosures is established. [[raw/sources/2026-09-08-youtube/videos/M0VvUsyJQ-M/M0VvUsyJQ-M|Direct passage]].

The video published February 22, 2025 contains a separate, explicit fiction boundary: when asked for a 6sense meeting link, Adam says it was a fake script he wrote and that Chris Walker talked him out of posting it. He discusses sharing it, but this passage establishes neither a real meeting nor that he publicly posted the script. Its exact text was not inspected. Keep the invented scenario distinct from a company transcript or documented competitive reaction. [[raw/sources/2026-09-08-youtube/videos/23rVr8tozS8/23rVr8tozS8|56:35–57:09]].

Adam's own [December 31, 2025 video at 8:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r47Q4Cfit5s&t=512) names three content categories, while the user's four requested pillars split GTM and SaaS building more explicitly. The wiki can use the user's four-part structure while preserving Adam's stated taxonomy as evidence.
