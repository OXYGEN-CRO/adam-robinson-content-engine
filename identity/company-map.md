# Company map

## Summary

Adam's current business context is a Retention.com identity-data portfolio containing several products. Robly is a previous company he sold; Robly ID was a feature, GetEmails became Retention.com, RB2B serves B2B visitor identification, and MoltSets is the newer agent/API contact-data offering. This is a map of the business relationships he describes, not a verified legal organization chart.
