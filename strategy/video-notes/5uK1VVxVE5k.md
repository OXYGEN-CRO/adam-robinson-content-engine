## Summary

The conversation focuses on generating early sales pipeline through compensated introductions from trusted industry contacts, then choosing an appropriate first sales hire. Scott explains partner selection, incentives, prospect matching, communication and founder-led validation. Adam connects the approach to Retention.com's affiliate experience, adds RB2B's use of visibility as a noncash incentive, and qualifies the strategy for smaller contracts and product-led businesses. The final third covers positioning an AI product around a narrow workflow, considering customers outside SaaS, and avoiding prestigious but stage-inappropriate sales hires. The episode does not establish a completed $0–$1M result from this playbook.
