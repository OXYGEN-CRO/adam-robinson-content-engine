## Summary

This webinar examines outbound for smaller SaaS companies competing with larger brands in 2024. Jason Bay teaches coordinated outreach across channels, valuable offers, customer-informed messaging, concise emails, executive participation and content-supported prospecting. The final Q&A covers paid demos, rescheduling boundaries, prerequisites for outbound, buyer education, AI and LinkedIn. Adam contributes a historical admission that Retention.com's 2023 BDR expansion failed, describes the influence of offer design on RB2B, and explains his belief that consistent LinkedIn content builds familiarity before outreach. The source supplies no Adam-company ARR, revenue, profit or growth figures.
