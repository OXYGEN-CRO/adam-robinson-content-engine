## Tensions and corrections

- The opening claims complete business automation and zero human intervention. The demonstrated systems cover pre-sales, onboarding, and support; routine operations include daily human review, customer calls, product development, and human escalation.
- The experiment conclusion reports zero critical issues requiring intervention. That narrower outcome does not independently verify the opening claim of zero intervention across the entire business.
- The title implies $6 million per year, while Adam explicitly reports ARR and later specifies $6.22 million. Neither figure establishes recognized revenue, profit, cash flow, or valuation.
- The 85% overall customer-interaction figure and 75% support-resolution figure have different stated scopes. They should remain separate; the later 'above 75%' may reflect rounding.
- The funnel uses questions, chat initiators, tickets, and people initiating tickets as different units around the same 1,000 figure. These are not interchangeable without reporting definitions.
- The 2,000 free sign-ups exceed the 1,000 clone-chat initiators. The transcript does not establish that all sign-ups pass through the clone or that the clone causes the reported conversion.
- The 250 new payers, 200 monthly cancellations, $230 average monthly payment, and 18-month duration do not constitute a complete reconciliation of $6.22 million ARR. Do not silently repair or extrapolate the snapshot into a historical revenue model.
- The 40 calls and approximately 15% of paying customers have an unclear period and denominator. They cannot safely establish total paying-customer count or calls per Wednesday.
- Adam calls his business the first SaaS above $5 million ARR to attempt this experiment at 00:00:07–00:00:13. No evidence supports that exclusivity claim.
- The reported autonomous improvement during the absence is not accompanied by an explanation of learning mechanisms or before-and-after evaluation.
- Caption spellings include 'RBTB,' 'Finn,' and a different-looking support email domain. These are transcription uncertainties, not verified names or contact details.
- No financing history or explicit bootstrap-versus-VC position is established. Small headcount and low apparent labor requirements should not be converted into a funding claim.
