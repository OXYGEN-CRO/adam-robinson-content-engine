## Summary

The episode examines high-volume cold email through Taylor's work for RB2B, Fixer, and other clients. Its substantive argument is conditional: broad outreach can work when the offer is relevant, the next step is easy, infrastructure supports delivery, and downstream conversion works. Discussion covers activation metrics, concise copy, relevant AI personalization, link-free initial messages, enterprise delivery, failed campaigns, audience fatigue, and sending limits. Adam contributes an emphasis on separating emotionally memorable complaints from performance evidence and reports that RB2B's longstanding copy still converts. Most numerical results and technical recommendations are Taylor's reports, not Adam's achievements or independently verified findings. The transcript contains no substantial bootstrapping-versus-VC discussion.
