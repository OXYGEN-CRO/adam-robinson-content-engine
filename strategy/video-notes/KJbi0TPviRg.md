## Summary

Anthony argues that category creation is justified when buyers lack an existing frame for the problem, but can otherwise impose unnecessary cost, delay, and investor friction. He describes Gainsight's community origins and AudiencePlus's evolving approach to building relationships with people before they are ready to buy. Adam questions his own category ambitions, explains why AI-generated content pushed him toward visible, transparent founder storytelling, and distinguishes targeted LinkedIn engagement from broad video reach. The final third covers product-roadmap boundaries, inexpensive audience development, Adam's difficult early camera practice, Hopin's unusual pandemic growth, channel selection, and AudiencePlus's change from 'owned media' to 'audience marketing.'
