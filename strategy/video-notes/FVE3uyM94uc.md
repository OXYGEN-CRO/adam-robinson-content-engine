## Tensions and corrections

- Adam's channel ownership and use of his name in a demo do not establish his participation. The host explicitly says Adam is absent from this episode and will return next week.
- The captions' $88,500 deal figure sits awkwardly beside Bethany's approximately $10,000 normal-deal comparison. Preserve it as uncertain rather than silently changing it to $8,500.
- Bethany presents an end goal of full automation but recommends manual validation initially and retaining Slack visibility and an intervention window potentially indefinitely.
- The segment initially calls the safeguard manual approval, but the demonstrated mechanism is a notification plus delay with an opportunity to opt out; affirmative approval is not shown.
- The HubSpot automation is a demonstration replica. Bethany discloses that her running implementation is in Clay.
- Pricing-page and repeated-visit triggers are optional recommendations. Bethany says Sendspark itself currently prioritizes customer-fit filters without those extra behavioral gates.
- The self-service routing flow is a stated intention, not an implemented or successful system.
- Bethany's suggestion to prioritize inbound assumes usable inbound demand. Tracktile's consultation exposes the separate need to create demand before visitor-conversion automation can help.
- Video is advocated as personal communication, while AI name replacement and dynamic backgrounds automate parts of the appearance of individual effort. Pete describes the result as looking researched; the transcript does not establish recipient perceptions or disclosure practices.
- Bethany's deliverability explanations, including replying into the inbox and using a matching domain, are operational beliefs without supplied experimental evidence.
- The older model is deliberately selected for demo reliability and cost despite the newer beta's better lip sync. Later wording about which lip-sync model was being shown is ambiguous.
- Tracktile's long cold calls and impressive demo reactions do not establish product-market fit. Jordan still reports weak demand generation and sometimes very long sales cycles.
- Bethany's first-day YouTube visibility claim and comments about AI damaging SEO are anecdotal, not validated forecasts for Tracktile.
- The LinkedIn 250-per-week statement switches between messages and connection requests; preserve this ambiguity.
- The closing announcement says the recording will go live tomorrow at 9:00 a.m. Central. Metadata records the public release on February 15, 2025 at 15:47:49 UTC; the planned time does not establish the exact recording date or actual publication minute.
- Company and personal names are inconsistently transcribed. Sendspark and Tracktile are inferred spellings; names in the threefold-lift customer example remain unresolved.
