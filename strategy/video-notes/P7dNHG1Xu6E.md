## Tensions and corrections

- The title says cold email is dead, but the spoken recommendation preserves email outreach and changes its audience, timing and context.
- The opening 10–30× conversion uplift differs from the later comparison of approximately 10% against 0.1%, which implies roughly 100×. Different cohorts could explain this, but none are identified.
- The market-wide contemporary benchmark of one in 500 or worse and Adam's personal benchmark of one in 1,000 have different stated scopes; they should not be merged into one rate.
- The visitor campaign is described both as above 10% and as one signup per 10 emails. Preserve the wording difference instead of choosing a more precise rate.
- Adam criticizes artificial personalization but later endorses light AI personalization. His distinction appears to favor relevant context and simple copy, rather than rejecting AI altogether.
- He names retargeting as part of the traffic-and-outreach system but provides no retargeting setup or results.
- The free-product promotion coexists with a statement that broader integrations require a paid RB2B plan. The source does not establish that the complete demonstrated workflow is free.
- The approach depends on relevant website traffic, identifiable visitors and ICP qualification; the transcript does not establish universal effectiveness for businesses without those conditions.
- No explicit personal change-of-mind story is given. The shift is presented as a response to changing outreach conditions.
