## Summary

Pete Crowley and Common Room's Florian Tatulia discuss converting anonymous website activity into useful sales context. Their central argument is to combine visitor identification with ICP fit, hiring, technology, social engagement and product activity, then use that context to prioritize relevant outreach. They cover account research, rapid routing, manual validation before automation, outreach to multiple stakeholders, experimental signal selection and the limits of identification coverage. The final Q&A addresses traffic quality, AI-assisted ideation, channel preferences and applicability to service businesses or audiences with little online activity. This is useful internal GTM research associated with Adam's company, but supplies no direct Adam testimony.
