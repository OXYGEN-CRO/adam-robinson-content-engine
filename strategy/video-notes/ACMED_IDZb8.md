## Tensions and corrections

- Sending volume is stated as 160,000 emails per month at 00:02:48–00:02:53 and 160,000 per day at 00:09:19–00:09:25. The source provides no reconciliation.
- The described 200 domains, two inboxes per domain, and two to 10 emails per inbox daily imply approximately 800–4,000 daily sends if applied together. This calculated range does not support 160,000 daily sends; the configuration may refer to a different operating period or contain caption/spoken errors.
- Revenue percentages use different periods and denominators: 42% of total revenue eight months earlier, 32% in the opening, and at least 20% of daily new revenue near the end. They should not be flattened into one channel contribution figure.
- Waitlist and pure cold revenue shares are given as 6% and 21%. No explicit visitor-retargeting revenue share is stated; assigning it the remaining 15 percentage points would require unsupported assumptions about periods, overlap, and attribution.
- The opening 12.7% campaign conversion and later 10.4% campaign conversion are distinct reported figures. The transcript does not establish whether these describe different campaigns, periods, or an inconsistency.
- The 26-times conversion claim is consistent with 10.4% divided by RB2B's own 0.4% cold conversion rate. It is not consistent with a 0.1% industry baseline if the malformed 'a,000' caption means 1,000. The intended comparator remains unclear.
- A 30% positive reply rate is repeatedly reported without a denominator. It must not be presented as 30% of all sent emails yielding positive replies or as 30% signup conversion.
- The channel label includes waitlist activation and recent website visitors. Adam explicitly recognizes the waitlist outreach as warm, and says even some cold contacts may have encountered his LinkedIn content.
- The AI-focused framing exceeds what the best-performing message example demonstrates: the featured visitor email is a brief contextual question, without visible page-specific or industry-specific personalization.
- Claims of reaching primary inboxes and identifying site visitors are promotional self-reports without delivery audits, identification coverage, or accuracy evidence.
- Automatic captions inconsistently render RB2B, waitlist, Clay, domain examples, and the industry benchmark. Company naming is resolved contextually to RB2B, while uncertain figures and proper names remain flagged.
- The final sentence cuts off at 00:10:50 while introducing a separate AI automation video. It does not provide evidence that Adam automated his entire SaaS.
