## Tensions and corrections

- Adam is absent throughout. None of Ryan's founder history, Pitch Lane performance claims or Pete's personal campaigns should be entered as Adam's self-reported achievements or beliefs.
- Pete's reference to Adam discussing outbound being dead does not establish Adam's exact position; the spoken position developed here belongs to Pete and Ryan and concerns changing tactics.
- The workflow is introduced as automated, but the simple version requires recurring CSV exports and imports. Full downstream delivery requires additional automation, and Pete says some profile routing is still in development.
- The humorous roast attracts engagement, but Pete questions its likely purchase intent. Ryan presents it as a relevant creative demonstration rather than a proven high-intent acquisition channel.
- Reusable video is presented as personal and relevant, but the demonstration changes prospect-specific visual context around a single recording. The sample script implies the sender reviewed the website; individual manual review is not established.
- Ryan's preference for natural, minimally polished recording coexists with Pete's advice to rehearse and improve eye contact, articulation and background. Both favor clarity over elaborate production.
- The 2× engagement claim, 30% meeting uplift, five customer meetings, nearly 100% viewing anecdote and early 2–3× sequence comparison refer to different speakers, outcomes and contexts. They cannot be combined into one performance narrative.
- Ryan's 5%, 500% and order-of-magnitude language is rhetorical and numerically non-equivalent. Preserve it as emphasis rather than corrected or validated performance data.
- High viewing does not imply replies or bookings. Pete explicitly separates viewing from response, and an attendee reports no response to custom videos.
- Ryan shifts between hard selling and relationship building depending on audience and secondary goals. SMB-versus-enterprise differences are his experience-based tendencies, not universal rules.
- Advice to target tightly later gives way to broad, candid exploratory outreach for founders who do not yet know their market. The boundary condition is the stage of customer discovery.
- Ryan nearly stopped pursuing Pitch Lane; documented feedback from a trusted early user changed his decision. Pete acknowledges the limited sample and potential outlier problem while defending action under uncertainty.
- The episode contains no Adam/RB2B ARR, revenue, profit, cash, funding or customer-count figures, and no exact Pitch Lane team size. Bootstrapping discussion does not include a substantive VC comparison.
- Automatic captions vary RB2B and Pitch Lane spellings and garble some names, places and the leave description. Uncertain details have not been silently converted into verified facts.
- Publication, capture and event dates are distinct. Relative references such as yesterday, next Tuesday and tomorrow morning remain anchored to the recording rather than assigned calendar dates.
