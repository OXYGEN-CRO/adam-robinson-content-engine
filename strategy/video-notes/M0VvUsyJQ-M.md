Tom Hunt explains his observational approach to LinkedIn growth: human photographs, simple curiosity hooks, widely relatable topics, short stories, easy reading, engaging endings and controversy that invites substantive comments. Despite the opening promise, the episode provides no attributable revenue or customer-acquisition result from Tom's largest viral post.
