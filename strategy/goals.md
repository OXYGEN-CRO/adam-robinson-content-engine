# Goals

## Summary

The user requested a sourced second brain. Adam's business ambitions and observed content habits are recorded below as evidence; they do not set this workspace's publishing targets or calendar.

The subsequent brief asks us to develop the author context. **Proposed editorial objective:** become useful and trusted to the founder making a difficult product, distribution or operating decision. A secondary objective is to help relevant GTM readers understand an implementable method. [[strategy/editorial-direction]] turns this objective into specific angles and brief criteria.

## Proposed content outcomes

| Outcome | Evidence or signal to inspect | What it does not prove |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Attract the intended founder/operator | Responses that reveal a relevant stage, problem or use case; audience fit where analytics are available. | A high impression count is not verified buyer fit. |
| Earn trust through useful judgment | Readers can describe the lesson, ask a substantive follow-up or return for a deeper explanation. | Trust has no single complete per-post metric. |
| Help a reader act | Use of a relevant resource, attendance, or a clearly described implementation step. | A download or registration is not paid adoption. |
| Support a relevant commercial path | Qualified signup, activation, payment and retention tracked as distinct events. | A post, visitor signal and later email can all contribute to the same customer. |

The first outcome follows Adam's [March 2024 audience strategy](../raw/sources/2026-09-08-linkedin-rb2b/posts/2024-03-28-7179219737141231617.md). His [June 2024 reflection](../raw/sources/2026-09-08-linkedin-rb2b/posts/2024-06-11-7206396119432482817.md) explicitly resists attributing every activity's effect. This measurement plan is our proposal: preserve business relevance while acknowledging what cannot be isolated. Baselines and numerical targets remain unset.
