# RB2B operating system

## Summary

The useful interpretation is a small product core supported by specialist contractors and shared Retention.com resources. Adam's $9.5m ARR / three-FTE claim is dated August 18, 2026; it is not evidence that total labor is three people. The detailed graphic in `output/rb2b-operating-map/` reconstructs the system with dates and public source links. It is working research, not a formal reporting chart or approval for publication.

The September 2025 roster names Adam for distribution/customer conversations, Tate for development, and Robb for AI support/docs/UI prototypes. It separately names Alec Paul, Kyle Williams, Taylor Haren, Understory, Sam Dunning, NoBoringDesign and Samu Kovács as contractors; Ebere/Angel, Keleigh and Tyler/Nikola/David provide shared design, operations and data. The August 2026 combined MoltSets/RB2B roster adds Ebru in success and shared Cecilia in UI/UX and Kayley in flat-file sales. Product allocations are not supplied. See [[identity/company-map]] and [[identity/proof]].

## Implementation distinctions

- Engineering: the August 2025 founder stack names Jules AI/RubyMine and Claude validation. Robb names Lovable, Upvoty, Postman, New Relic and Logz.io in his own toolkit. No production framework, CI/CD, automated test coverage or deployment cadence was verified.
- Support: Robb reports a documentation update requirement before merging user-facing PRs, manual review of low CX scores, and weekly escalation-theme review. This connects customer experience to product changes; it is a stated process, not an inspected repository rule.
- Outbound: Taylor owns the email program. The October 2025 visitor workflow names RB2B → Clay → HubSpot customer suppression → Instantly. Salesforce/Salesloft appear in the January 2026 inventory; do not infer a migration or sole CRM.
- Video: Samu / KS Media produces Adam's owned YouTube; Understory operates paid YouTube remarketing. Partner appearances are a third distribution category. Shared KS Media clients do not prove that every partner's channel generates RB2B sales.
- Search: Sam Dunning / Breaking B2B targets vendor/category/alternatives intent with content, refreshes and technical work. Keep signup-page conversion distinct from all organic-visitor conversion.
