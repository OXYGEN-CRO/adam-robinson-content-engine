# Go-to-market

## Promise and boundary

Show founders how a distinctive offer, trusted distribution and clear communication become customers. Adam repeatedly requires disruptive product/offer **and** disruptive marketing. Neither replaces the other. The latest version emphasizes founder content, agent contact data, creators and practical live education. [P5] [P10] [P36] [P55] [P57]

## Current direction and attribution

The August 25 MoltSets plan puts LinkedIn at the center, complemented by UGC, email, SEO and YouTube. The June 28 show requires free Claude skills and implementation understandable by nonengineers. Live education is both a reader benefit and distribution mechanism. [P10] [P44]

Adam is often curating/hiring expertise rather than personally building every system: Michel Lieben's APIs, Understory's campaigns, Sam Dunning's SEO and Samu's videos. Their client results, technical throughput and pipeline do not become his own proof. [P6] [P78] [P159] [P195]

He predicts spam fatigue, criticizes poaching commenters and still promotes high-volume cold email. Preserve this situational tension. A reaction, site visit or comment is not consent to outreach. [P29] [P43] [P94] [P121]

## Inbound routes and measurement

The January28 continuation names his “Magic Triangle”: content creates awareness/traffic, outreach converts visitors, and retargeting brings back those not ready. This supports the relationship between channels, not an additive attribution model. December27 explicitly combines organic founder content with low-price freemiumPLG and possible data/network advantages. [C7422337587291320320] [C7422337880775311360] [C7410750629234769921]

Observed routes include event registration, course/video, requested deck, waitlist and product signup. Match the destination to one post's job. A free founder course reaches people beyond the contact-data buyer. [P18] [P44] [P103] [P188] [P189]

Measure attention, qualified people, registrations, activation and revenue separately. The source does not establish causal contribution: cold-email share and “LinkedIn alone” may describe the same funnel. Do not add them or infer revenue from views. [P29] [P171] [P194]

## Audience quality before channel volume

In [March 2024](../../raw/sources/2026-09-08-linkedin-rb2b/posts/2024-03-28-7179219737141231617.md), Adam contrasts generic follower growth with content intended for founders, sales and marketing readers, direct participation and useful commercial outcomes. His [January 2025 diagnosis](../../raw/sources/2026-09-08-linkedin-rb2b/posts/2025-01-07-7282507888328167424.md) adds a stage boundary: a founder without customer/offer clarity is not necessarily ready to copy the acquisition channel.

These sources support two practical questions for a GTM brief: who should care about this, and what makes the method appropriate at their stage? His [June 2024 account of hard-to-isolate content effects](../../raw/sources/2026-09-08-linkedin-rb2b/posts/2024-06-11-7206396119432482817.md) prevents reducing that judgment to a per-post revenue score. See [[strategy/editorial-direction]] for proposed angles.
