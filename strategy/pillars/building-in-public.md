# Building in public

## Promise and boundary

Give founders a candid view of the choices, emotions and imperfect evidence behind a profitable company. This pillar is the ongoing story, not simply a revenue screenshot. Adam explicitly favors his emotional journey over a changelog, while acknowledging that unusual results and prior credibility made his own build-in-public more effective than a beginner's. [P101] [P122]

## Narrative progression

November 2025 considered an expanded visitor workflow and OEM identity products; January planned more RB2B product lines. March reversed the broader outbound idea, reasoning that complexity and channel degradation would hurt the business. April introduced MoltSets and limited-human-time RB2B; June/July exposed data-quality and launch delays; August launched beta. Preserve changing decisions rather than imply the current plan always existed. [P184] [P130] [P94] [P85] [P86] [P37] [P18]

## Proposed brief structure

What happened → how it felt → possible cause → remaining uncertainty → next experiment or lesson. Use a new real author event rather than fabricate the next installment. A public target stays a target until a dated milestone proves it; the latest collected RB2B number remains “about to cross $9.6m,” despite earlier $10m predictions. [P2] [P61]

Keep public-use boundaries on family, sobriety, layoffs and narrated private conversations. Public source visibility does not itself approve another first-person retelling.
