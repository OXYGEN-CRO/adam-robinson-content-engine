# Middle of funnel

## Summary

**Proposed job:** make a useful method understandable, show its assumptions and build trust in the quality of Adam's operating judgment and the experts he hosts.

## Methods and evidence to show

The June workshop brief asks guests to teach practical GTM workflows to non-engineers and give away usable skills. The August MoltSets plan explains acquisition, pricing, product-led and sales-led buyers, support, churn and team assumptions. Together these support demonstrations, operating breakdowns and tradeoff discussions. A guest demonstration remains the guest's work; their result is not Adam's proof. [Workshop brief](../../raw/sources/2026-09-08-linkedin/posts/2026-06-28-7477048608886812672.md), [operating model](../../raw/sources/2026-09-08-linkedin/posts/2026-08-25-7498049303853191168.md).

## What the explanation needs

Use a specific question from [[audience/pains-and-questions]]. State the starting conditions, the choice made, the mechanism Adam believes mattered, and what remains unproven. The [January 2025 post](../../raw/sources/2026-09-08-linkedin-rb2b/posts/2025-01-07-7282507888328167424.md) is useful for explaining why discovery precedes acquisition tactics. The [February 2024 operating update](../../raw/sources/2026-09-08-linkedin-rb2b/posts/2024-02-06-7160748181696188416.md) is useful for showing why a narrower ICP does not automatically cure churn.

A finished explanation should give the reader a diagnostic question or a test they can understand. It should also explain when the method is a poor fit. Adam's experienced team, earlier capital and existing audience should remain visible when they materially enabled the example.
