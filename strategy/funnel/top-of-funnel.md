# Top of funnel

## Summary

**Proposed job:** help the right founder recognize a problem, feel understood and become interested in Adam's perspective.

## Topics and formats

Use business setbacks, uncomfortable tradeoffs, founder freedom and an honest account of a decision. A July 31 source opens with a poor purchase/cancellation week and ends by explaining the choice to preserve RB2B's simple scope while pursuing MoltSets. Its relevance comes from a concrete experience and unresolved questions, not merely a provocative hook. [Source](../../raw/sources/2026-09-08-linkedin/posts/2026-07-31-7488990110525353984.md).

The September 3 reflection treats continued publishing as a practice despite widely varying reach. His autobiographical and performance claims remain dated evidence, not guaranteed outcomes for other creators. [Source](../../raw/sources/2026-09-08-linkedin/posts/2026-09-03-7501296198624907265.md).

## Practical brief choices

Begin with a recognizable founder tension: initial revenue without product-market fit, progress that still feels stuck, or the cost of an ambition that looked attractive. The [November 2023 origin story](../../raw/sources/2026-09-08-linkedin-rb2b/posts/2023-11-11-7129220348616593409.md) supplies an explicit audience intent: help the founder experiencing the same difficulty. The [May 2025 plateau post](../../raw/sources/2026-09-08-linkedin-rb2b/posts/2025-05-31-7334641032967454724.md) pairs advice with Adam's admission that he is also stuck.

The desired response is recognition and interest in the next explanation. Choose a real incident, one emotional contradiction and a useful takeaway. A broad founder story can succeed at this job without carrying a product pitch.

## What to measure

Proposed measures include relevant reader responses, profile interest and movement to a useful next piece. Reach can indicate distribution, but it does not establish buyer fit or revenue. No target or tracking configuration is approved.
