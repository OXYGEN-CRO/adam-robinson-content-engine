## Business and writing references named in the older archive

In his [November 4, 2024 reading-list post](../raw/sources/2026-09-08-linkedin-rb2b/posts/2024-11-04-7259270932546936832.md), Adam gives the following influences. This table records his interpretation of them; it is not an independent review or summary of the books.

| Reference | What Adam takes from it | Use in this workspace |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Steve Blank, *Four Steps to the Epiphany* | Customer discovery and building a buyer audience before launch. | Ask for buyer evidence before proposing a scalable acquisition play. His zero-failure rhetoric is not a guarantee. |
| Dave Gerhardt, *Founder Brand* | Founder content as a trust-building practice; he explicitly notes his favorable alignment with his ICP on LinkedIn. | Keep audience/author/product fit visible when adapting the method. |
| 37signals, *ReWork* | A model of software entrepreneurship outside institutional venture funding. | Explore operating choices without importing every view from the reference. |
| Y Combinator, *Startup School* | Growth-oriented thinking, balanced against the previous reference. | Preserve his stated preference for finding a middle position; this entry is a course, despite the post's “books” heading. |
| Ben Horowitz, *The Hard Thing About Hard Things* | A realistic account of the difficult CEO role. | Use actual operating dilemmas rather than generic founder inspiration. |
| Matt Mochary, *The Great CEO Within* | Different leadership lessons become relevant at different stages; support through mentors and peers. | Match the lesson to the founder's current problem. |
| Alex Hormozi, *$100m Offers* | An offer whose value is compelling to its intended buyer. | Test the offer against customer context; the book title is not Adam's result. |
| Seth Godin, *Purple Cow* | Differentiation that makes a product worth discussing. | Ask what is meaningfully different and why a buyer would care. |

The post also promotes a then-upcoming Dave Gerhardt event. The invitation is historical and does not establish current access to that event or permission to reuse its materials.

## Discovery method he describes

Review leading creators' recent successful posts, collect the questions people ask in the niche, and separate hooks, structures and endings in a swipe file. He recommends bringing one's own story to the structure and eventually moving beyond templates. This is Adam's stated method, not verification of the performance of third-party tools he mentions.
