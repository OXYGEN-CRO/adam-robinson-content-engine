His recurring argument combines differentiated products, distinctive marketing, delayed fundraising, constrained hiring and founder freedom. The advice does not claim all firms can avoid capital.
- His [August 21 workweek](https://newsletter.rb2b.com/p/what-does-the-ceo-of-a-32m-arr-business-do-all-week) (W05) explicitly scopes $32m ARR to **RB2B + Retention.com + MoltSets**. It places him in Aspen. An [undated bio](https://newsletter.rb2b.com/authors/adam-robinson) (W22) still says Austin; prefer the dated Aspen statement for present context.
- [August 29](https://newsletter.rb2b.com/p/how-to-bootstrap-to-24m-arr-with-5-employees) (W06) puts RB2B at $9.5m ARR. MoltSets reaching $24m ARR with fewer than five full-time employees is a future two-year model, not a result. Shared staff and other business lines are part of that model.
- The [official Retention history](https://retention.com/about-r) (W03) now describes an identity/data company with multiple applications, names three co-founders, dates GetEmails to 2019 and RB2B's launch to 2024. Do not flatten the parent company into the old ecommerce product or portray Adam as sole founder.

- W01/W18: Personal finance-to-software history is supported. The [2020 interview](https://membermouse.com/podcast/adam-robinson-robly/) includes Rice, graduation in 2003, departure from finance in 2011, inspiration from Vimeo/CollegeHumor roommates and substantial founder savings. Its description of ten years trading does not cleanly fit those dates; avoid invented precision.
- W19: [The 2024 primary interview](https://www.sramanamitra.com/2024/09/09/best-of-bootstrapping-bootstrapped-to-exit-and-bootstrapped-again-and-again/) supports a 2021 Robly sale and incubation of GetEmails inside Robly. Customers using the identification feature with other email tools shaped the spinout. It explicitly withholds the sale price.
- W17: [The March 2024 presentation page](https://founderpath.com/resources/adam-robinson-retentioncom-keynote) offers historic $22m Retention ARR context and audience-assisted discovery/pricing. Its editorial $10m Robly exit figure needs reconciliation with W19, not automatic reuse.
- W20: [The May 2025 article](https://theadamrobinson.com/founders-stuck-between-1m-10m-arr/) connects stalled growth to weak differentiation, unclear messaging, unfocused ICP, unit economics, weak word of mouth and churn. It acknowledges RB2B's own churn problem.
- W21: [The January 2025 ownership story](https://theadamrobinson.com/startup/) contrasts the founder's desired company with pressure to maximize valuation. It reports a single $1m-profit month, not annual profit.
- W02: The [June 2026 current-work page](https://theadamrobinson.com/now/) supports the four themes but references 2025 experiments. Its update stamp does not date every experiment to June 2026.
- W08: [The June 2026 motivation essay](https://newsletter.rb2b.com/p/why-i-still-build-startups) frames businesses as creative expression that funds a chosen life. Lean operating design, aligned teammates and a sustainable schedule matter more than retirement or scale for its own sake.
