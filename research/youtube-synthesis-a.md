## Initial cross-source implications

1. Recent autobiography ties financial ambition to freedom, family, health and time autonomy. Lean teams are a lived response to Robly's painful call-center operating model, not simply an efficiency slogan.
2. Building in public is both value and GTM strategy: candid failures cultivate trust among the B2B audience that became RB2B's market. His audience did not simply follow his original Shopify intent.
3. His own December2025 pillar vocabulary is building in public / industry future / contrarian takes. Four wiki pillars requested by user are a useful proposed taxonomy with GTM and SaaS-building as related mechanisms, not an exact quote of his taxonomy.
4. Current GTM combines founder content, product-led/free adoption, outbound where it works, retargeting, and visitor identity. Absolute headlines about "cold email dead" or "only way" should be checked against conditional in-video advice.
5. AI-enabled tiny teams depend on detailed maintained context, customer data, human review and escalation; "AI ran the whole company" headlines omit this preparation.
6. Recency improves metric relevance but does not remove contradictions. Explicitly unresolved: six vs sixteen weeks to RB2B $1m, apparent launch-year caption errors, Retention $25m annual revenue vs later $21m ARR, and historical combined-three-company vs current-two-company totals.
