# I manage support, AI training, onboarding, QA, product design, testing, and a half dozen other things at RB2B.

I manage support, AI training, onboarding, QA, product design, testing, and a half dozen other things at RB2B. Here's what's in my toolkit.

➜ Intercom is the mission control.
I triage tickets, train the bot, push proactive nudges, and manage live chats; all from one screen. It's the pulse of RB2B support.

➜ Lovable saves me from dev backlogs.
We're using it to design V3 of RB2B: mapping features, user flows, and UI without writing code. It's where ideas become functional blueprints.

➜ Supademo kills friction.
Instead of hand-holding new users, I ship interactive product tours that onboard at scale. One demo replaces 10 DMs.

➜ Upvoty is my feedback brain.
Every upvote, comment, and request tells me what to build next. It's how I turn user pain into AI workflows and messaging plays.

➜ Postman keeps integrations honest.
When integration API calls go sideways for a user, Postman helps me take a look under the hood and find out why.

➜ Tolt runs our referral engine.
Tracks referrals, manages payouts, and keeps our growth loop running without manual oversight.

➜ ChatGPT is my daily editor.
Process breakdowns, copy rewrites, bot response ideas; this is where they start. GPT gives me speed. Not fluff.

➜ Claude brings tone balance.
When I want a softer angle or a second take on phrasing, Claude is where I go. The contrast helps sharpen our messaging.

➜ n8n for behind the scenes workflows.
We use it to link our AI agent to our ESP. So, if an email bounces, the bot knows before support ever sees a ticket.

➜ NewRelic and Logz.io are my panic buttons.
If something breaks, I need answers fast. These tools tell me where the fire started. And whether it’s still burning.

Anyone have an inside line on a tool that I should be using but aren't?
