Is RB2B SOC 2 compliant?
RB2B and its parent company Retention.com are SOC 2 Type 2 compliant.
SOC 2 Type 2 documentation can be acquired by filling out the request form at
https://retention.securitypal.com/
Do you have a trust portal that we can review?
Yes, RB2B has a trust and assurance portal available at
https://retention.securitypal.com/
Is the RB2B script secure?
Yes. The RB2B script is securely served over HTTPS from a dedicated Amazon CloudFront CDN (Content Delivery Network). This ensures fast, reliable delivery with built-in protections including TLS encryption, DDoS mitigation, and global edge caching for enhanced security and performance.
