## Specific implementation detail and boundaries

### Cold email

- [G03] July 20, 2026: Adam says Taylor has run RB2B email for years and attributes **42% of revenue** to cold email. No attribution model/window. That same post's **1m emails/month, custom Claude waterfall skill, Halo, and nontechnical markdown management** describe the **planned MoltSets launch**, not a confirmed RB2B rebuild.
- [G04] October 31, 2025 full transcript: prelaunch content awareness for three months and a waitlist → announcement email; pure outbound from Apollo; identified-site-visitor follow-up via RB2B/Clay/HubSpot/Instantly. Taylor owns both sending infrastructure and copy (02:43–02:57). The three campaign types should be distinct in the graphic.
- [G04] The source first says **160,000 emails/month** (02:48), later **160,000/day** (09:22). G01 independently says 160k/month; G14 later says >200k/month. Do not print a precise current send volume.
- [G04] Intro says 32% revenue / $1.9m; at 02:37 it says 42% **eight months ago**; end says at least 20% of new revenue daily. July 2026's 42% is a later dated claim, not a reconciled last-touch partition. The campaign attribution shares, 30% positive reply rate, 12.7% signup conversion, and 10.4% visitor-campaign conversion have different/unclear denominators. Do not turn them into a pie chart.
- [G05] July 12, 2025 says one TAM email every other month and 40% of paying customers had received email; G04 later says the Apollo list is emailed twice per month. The frequency and population changed or conflict. Receipt of an email does not establish causal revenue.
- W03 verifies Taylor's business as Sales Automation Systems. Current Taylor profile discusses Halo/Outfound migration across agency clients, but does not name RB2B as a migrated client in inspected material. **Rockstar affiliation not established.**
- **CRM scope/date disagreement:** G04 (Oct 31, 2025) specifically names **HubSpot integration for excluding existing customers in the website-visitor email workflow**. G02 (Jan 23, 2026) and G18 (May 15, 2025) list **Salesforce as RB2B's CRM**, plus Salesloft for CRM/sequencing/booking and Clay for filtering/routing PLG signups. These could coexist, or the video could describe a narrower example; there is no proven migration sequence. Graphic wording: “customer suppression (HubSpot in Oct 2025 walkthrough)” in that historical workflow, and “Salesforce / Salesloft (Jan 2026 reported stack)” in the operating tool inventory. Do not replace HubSpot with Salesforce inside the claimed demonstrated workflow or infer HubSpot is their sole/current CRM. G17's rebuilt HubSpot and Salesforce integrations are **customer-facing product integrations**, not direct evidence of internal CRM choice.
