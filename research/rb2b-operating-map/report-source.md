# Inside RB2B's $9.5m ARR operating system

Audience: founders and GTM operators. Research date: 8 September 2026. Requested deliverable: detailed editable graphic covering people, contractors, shared resources, acquisition, conversion, engineering and support. This is a third-person reconstruction from public disclosures across 2025–2026, not a private org-chart or systems audit.

## Direct answer and company boundary

Adam reported $9.5m in annual recurring revenue and three FTE for RB2B on 18 August 2026. The operating model combines a small product core, specialist contractors, a wider Retention.com organization, a shared identity-data foundation and self-service software. Treating this as a company whose total work is done by three people would omit material dependencies. [Adam, ARR/headcount disclosure](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/retentionadam_me-in-2019-i-want-a-25m-arr-biz-w-under-activity-7495491952788787200-5HT0).

The explicit September 2025 core is Adam Robinson (distribution, sales clone, customer conversations and marketing site), Tate Blahnik (CTO/development), and Robb Clarke (Head of AI, documentation, support escalations and UI prototypes). Adam names seven contractors: Alec Paul (LinkedIn advice, not ghostwriting), Kyle Williams (guide delivery/outreach), Taylor Haren (cold email), Understory (paid retargeting), Sam Dunning (SEO), NoBoringDesign (website) and Samu Kovács (YouTube). Shared resources are Ebere and Angel (front-end design/HTML), Keleigh (COO/back office), and Tyler, Nikola and David (data). [Adam, full roster, 30 September 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/retentionadam_whenever-i-say-rb2b-is-at-63m-arr-in-18mo-activity-7378873027557523457-XVJ9).

Product-level allocation is unknown. In December 2025 he credited 33 people across Retention/RB2B. Mike Cottenden's published role is group SVP Product and Engineering; the wider group leadership is not demonstrated as dedicated RB2B labor. [Combined roster](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/retentionadam_within-2-years-we-will-bootstrap-moltsets-activity-7498049303853191168-QHBA), [group acknowledgment](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/retentionadam_i-sit-here-and-wind-people-up-about-how-we-activity-7412229091862581250-FKhv), [official team](https://retention.com/about-r).

## Engineering and AI support

Reported development tools are Jules AI with RubyMine and Claude validation. Robb turns conversations into Lovable prototypes; Upvoty collects feedback; Postman diagnoses integration calls; New Relic and Logz.io diagnose failures. He explicitly owns QA/testing alongside support and product work. These accounts do not establish automated test coverage or autonomous production releases. [Adam, 29 August 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/retentionadam_heres-our-ai-agent-stack-that-will-get-rb2b-activity-7367217721387769856-zltc), [Robb toolkit, 18 August 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/robbclarke_i-manage-support-ai-training-onboarding-activity-7363218952698650626-90Kz).

Current disclosures identify AWS hosting/storage, CloudFront script delivery, RedisLabs caching, monitoring/logging, Inspector vulnerability scanning, Stripe billing, SendGrid delivery and PostHog analytics. They are published service categories, not an inspected architecture. [Subprocessors](https://www.rb2b.com/sub-processors-list), [security](https://www.rb2b.com/security), [script delivery FAQ](https://support.rb2b.com/en/articles/8999958-security-faqs).

The July 2025 support architecture separates Delphi for pre-signup questions from Fin for logged-in users. Fin receives account status, plan and credit context. Intercom also handles workflows, proactive messages, live chat and activation. n8n links the AI agent to the ESP for bounce context; Supademo provides tours. Robb writes answers from recurring questions, uses clear headings/lists and text instructions, and uses ChatGPT to reformat for human and AI reading. Missing knowledge leads to new docs; ambiguous answers lead to revisions; unnecessary questions lead to UI improvements. [Delphi/Fin](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QqNhenP7jao), [UI and documentation loop](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S3fI3M3ckVE), [AI-readable docs](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZtJaI-5_sVk), [starting from recurring questions](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_3zVR0AQLhc).

Robb reports manual review of every CX score ≤3/5, weekly escalation-theme review, same-day documentation changes, customer-discovered gaps addressed within 24 hours, versioned docs and documentation updates before user-facing PR merges. Some escalations require human judgment. [Robb, 3 February 2026](https://robbclarke.com/applied-ai/how-we-got-our-ai-support-agent-to).

Adam reports Fin handling 98.9% of tickets and escalating 1.1% in May 2026, with Robb spending at most 15 minutes daily on escalations in that account. Intercom's March 2025 case reports 65% automatic resolution. These are different dates and definitions, not a controlled before/after comparison. [May founder account](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/retentionadam_a-few-hours-ago-rb2b-crossed-9m-arr-i-don-activity-7463630759023529985-QMxX), [March vendor case](https://www.intercom.com/blog/videos/creating-a-lean-scalable-support-system/).

## Synthesis and limits

The useful mechanism is a constrained product plus distribution specialists and shared data, with human judgment concentrated in exceptions, product decisions and documentation. Adam also reports 9%+ monthly churn in May 2026; low core headcount does not solve retention. His claim that shared data creates negligible marginal fulfillment cost does not establish zero labor, infrastructure or acquisition costs. [May founder account](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/retentionadam_a-few-hours-ago-rb2b-crossed-9m-arr-i-don-activity-7463630759023529985-QMxX).

Missing evidence: current individual labor allocations, all partner-channel contributions, current campaign configuration/budgets, repository host, production framework/versions, CI/CD, tests, release authority and deployment cadence. No internal audit was performed. The inaccessible 99.3% Substack headline is excluded after a bounded retry. Research stopped when each requested function had primary implementation evidence and remaining gaps required private/current configurations. Full source provenance remains in the lane records and the consolidated claim ledger; the graphic uses short descriptive hyperlinks near claims.
