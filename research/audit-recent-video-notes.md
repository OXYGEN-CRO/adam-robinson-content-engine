# Audit of the newest 17 video extractions

## Scope and method

Bounded review on 2026-09-08 of recency ranks **1–17**, April 16, 2026 through November 19, 2025. This is a semantic spot audit against important original caption passages, supported by earlier complete reads of five of these sources. It is **not** a claim that a second reader independently reread every line or validated every extracted claim in all 17 videos. The primary per-video workers received each full transcript; those original results, raw captions and provenance JSON were left unchanged.

For every examined video, the independent verifier's `audit_note` function was run read-only: source/prompt hashes, identity, terminal success/output equality, evidence timestamps, declared caption coverage, metric caveats, promoted wiki content and links all passed. It produced no heuristic coverage flags for these 17. Most are short solo videos; the December 3 course is the substantial long-form coverage case in this set. A clean automated result does not certify factual correctness or exhaustive semantic coverage.

The review then compared selected learning claims and their caveats with exact caption windows, checking whether summaries preserved conditions, actual versus hypothetical results, ownership and changes of mind. Five complete sources previously read were revisited through their original notes: `eUzqVJpZPP4`, `F-WwDP1d-pM`, `aaBZ3f5TViQ`, `AdFLL-bFEFI`, `r47Q4Cfit5s`.

## Defects, qualifications and corrections

**No high-confidence unsupported extraction claim was found in the specific passages checked.** This is not a zero-defect certification of all claims or all 100 videos. No raw files, model responses, structured extraction JSON or provenance were changed.

The main integration risks were broader than individual extraction defects, and were addressed in [[strategy/learnings]]:

1. **Latest video is not latest company state.** March/April video narration is supplemented by later LinkedIn decisions: March 19 reverses the RB2B outreach-suite expansion, May 22 explains its unusual data/distribution advantages, July 31 accepts a possible product ceiling, and August 25 gives the MoltSets plan with shared contributors. Historical $6m/$8m examples cannot become current-company proof.
2. **"Never VC" is an incomplete representation.** Profit/freedom remain consistent preferences, but May 13 discusses preserving options if capital is needed and September 1 permits necessity-driven fundraising. Named outside companies and quoted founders remain attributed.
3. **AI support still has human work.** The February source explicitly retains daily content correction and direct customer learning. May's higher automation claim still includes escalations handled by Robb; August describes core and shared staff. The compact map does not claim an unsupported all-company autonomy percentage.
4. **Headline rules contain conditions.** Customer discovery precedes scaling, cold-email tactics change, and the historical ABM result depended on specific market timing and staff. The map carries these qualifications rather than repeating a universal recipe.
5. **Disclosure is both useful and costly.** The notes preserve executive/employee objections. July 6 illustrates a more recent concrete failure disclosure; it does not prove that public candor always improves outcomes.
6. **Metrics are unresolved where sources disagree.** Six versus sixteen weeks, apparent launch-year errors, different company scopes, course-era audience figures and shared-staff exclusions remain in [[identity/proof]] and [[identity/source-conflicts]]. No cleaner numbers were manufactured for the learning map.
7. **AI-assisted writing is task- and channel-specific.** An April suggestion to generate alternative endings does not establish that he personally uses that exact workflow, that all LinkedIn posts are generated, or that his scripted YouTube process is identical to LinkedIn authorship.
