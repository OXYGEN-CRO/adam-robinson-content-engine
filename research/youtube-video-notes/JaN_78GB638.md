## Summary

Moah describes building Boomerang with modest outside funding, reaching founder-salary profitability, surviving platform competition and revising team size and distribution. Her account covers voluntary subscriptions as pricing research, values-based hiring, universal support training, targeted outbound, product differentiation and philanthropy funded by profits. Adam contributes his own experiences with premature hiring, support automation, overseas teams, engineering management and customer account audits. Both discuss the social pressure to equate headcount or fundraising with success, while Moah explicitly allows that capital-intensive businesses may require outside funding. The final third adds customer-led discovery, AI product opportunities, staffing discipline and Adam's stated interest in using his platform to show alternative founder paths.
