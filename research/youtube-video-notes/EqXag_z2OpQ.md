## Summary

Adam recounts building GetEmails, later Retention.com, through provocative Facebook advertising, word of mouth and a small outbound team, then losing profitability during an aggressive expansion that failed to resolve churn. He explains the move into a separate B2B business, how LinkedIn audience fit enabled customer discovery and influenced a free-product strategy, and why founder credibility can strengthen acquisition and activation. The Q&A adds important qualifications about retention benchmarks, product-market fit, reviews, channel experimentation, overseas staffing and external financing. The spoken account does not establish that LinkedIn alone produced the revenue suggested by the title. Publication is dated, but the recording date and several historical reference years remain unspecified.
