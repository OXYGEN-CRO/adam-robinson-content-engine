# Close reading of the last 100 hooks

The hook is taken as the first three non-empty lines or roughly the first 210 characters, whichever is shorter. Likes are the capture-time snapshot from 2026-09-08; comment-gated posts are marked G. Tags and notes are my reading, not the author's; the numbers are one profile's record, not a rule. Adam says himself that after 70m impressions he cannot predict what will work [#8].

## Tag legend

- **A**: Adam is the subject of the first clause
- **T**: temperature word in the first hundred characters
- **C**: contradiction inside the visible text
- **V**: explicit verdict
- **N**: announcement, invitation or pitch language in the first clause
- **P**: a third party or company is the subject
- **H**: a "Here's…" promise
- **Q**: a question before see-more
- **D**: dialogue format
- **R**: recency anchor
- **X**: reaction to an artifact or image
- **L**: numbered list promise
- **S**: textured specific
