## Documentary chronology and defects to preserve

1. **Upload is not recording time.** At 30:03–30:24 Adam explicitly introduces a Q4 2022 update and upward 2023 guidance; at roughly 2:19:25 he says “it is 2023.” An early host introduction also places the $50m goal “by 2024.” These anchors support treating much of the film as 2023-era footage. They do not prove every scene's exact recording date. The description's “building a $50M business” framing cannot convert a target into an outcome.

2. **The film corrects its own hypergrowth story.** At 1:18:39–1:19:32 he explains that Q1 led the team to assume product-market fit and repeatable sales, then April stopped them. At 1:56:37–1:58:04 he distinguishes velocity from FOMO, admits that the $50m goal caused poor decisions and calls for grounded optimism. At 2:22:09–2:23:49 he regrets declaring the goal and reaffirms profitable growth and good treatment of people. Earlier billion-dollar valuation/sale dreams, the later montage's billion-dollar revenue language and the $50m ARR target have different units and are all prospective. No unicorn exit or $50m achieved ARR is supported here.

3. **A separate goal document remains separate.** At 1:06:04–1:06:53 Adam reads an April 2017 Lead.com statement targeting $2m MRR by his 42nd birthday, says that birthday has passed and still hopes to reach it by the next. This is not an established correction to the 2015/2016 freedom-letter dates already in the wiki; the documents may be different. No birthday date or current age was calculated from it.

4. **Failed sale identity/date remains bounded.** At 04:49–05:16 he says a buyer walked “last year” from the then-current business. This is not evidence that the completed Robly sale failed or that it occurred in 2024. The buyer and exact year are not established by that passage.

5. **Retrospective growth numbers do not reconcile exactly.** At 1:58:38–1:58:48 he reports 75% growth over twelve months and $12m to $20m ARR. Those rounded endpoints imply a different percentage. Preserve his language without repairing it or adding a validated growth row to the proof ledger. Financial snapshots throughout the film belong to their historical scenes, not a March 2025 or September 2026 measurement.

6. **Customer and partner claims remain attributed.** The Dr. Squatch example mixes an October $1.2m/$20k retelling, a November/Black Friday context and the customer's own statement about a segment's revenue share. It is not Adam-company revenue or independently demonstrated incremental ROI. The conference “$1m ARR from $35k investment” conversation at 1:24:37 onward is an on-site estimate. Private board-meeting speculation does not establish official Klaviyo approval or investment.

7. **Speaker and caption uncertainty persists.** Santos supplies much of the philosophy and growth theory; Kyle discusses the DTC field-sales role; family and customers speak for themselves. The closing fragment at 2:29 is not automatically Adam's current priority. Spellings such as Santos Sharan, Klaviyo and Dave Rogenmoser require contextual normalization; raw captions are unchanged. No direct audio, imagery or on-screen financial tables were audited.
