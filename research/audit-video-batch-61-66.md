The recording describes RB2B as two months old, with 6,500 users, 78% activation and $350k ARR, and says the intended paid feature set does not yet exist. The small-team proof is explicitly qualified: $12m ARR with one salesperson and six core people **plus a Philippines prospecting group** of unknown size/status. Six is not all-in labor. Historical six-to-fifty expansion, recording-time sixty employees and an eight-person coaching group concern different scopes. Jasper’s growth and unicorn status are third-party anecdotes, not Adam-company achievements; the source inconsistently gives December 29 and December 9, 2020 for the message he recalls.

The source supports an evolving ambition: Adam first favored several smaller cash-generating businesses, felt limited by Robly’s prolonged plateau, saw former office peers build Jasper, pursued a larger outcome, then regretted the speed of organizational expansion. At 36:14–38:38, he still acknowledges that a small-company portfolio can be a reasonable strategy. This cannot become a fixed anti-growth or anti-VC doctrine.

His cold-email position is also conditional. At 15:11–15:38, he concedes possible overcorrection and allows outbound while it works. At 19:20–19:40 he explicitly says RB2B uses it.
