# Reader language and objections

## Summary

The archive gives us Adam's descriptions of founder problems and his reported conversations. It does not provide a verified voice-of-customer interview corpus. Preserve that distinction when using a phrase in a brief.

| Exact short phrase | Who says it in the source? | Useful interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “can I talk to your cold email guy?” | Adam reports a founder's request in [January 2025](../raw/sources/2026-09-08-linkedin-rb2b/posts/2025-01-07-7282507888328167424.md). | A desire for a working channel before the offer and discovery process are ready. |
| “the same but better” | Adam's description of stalled founders' differentiation in [May 2025](../raw/sources/2026-09-08-linkedin-rb2b/posts/2025-05-31-7334641032967454724.md). | A useful way to frame the switching problem; not a measured segment label. |

## Objections to answer with evidence

| Proposed objection | Why it is plausible | Editorial response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| This worked because you already had money and an audience. | Later products use established distribution and shared resources; [[identity/company-map]] preserves that context. | State the starting conditions and isolate the part a beginner can actually test. |
| Content is an expensive distraction from the product. | Adam's [July 2025 CFO story](../raw/sources/2026-09-08-linkedin-rb2b/posts/2025-07-19-7352407363783438336.md) reports precisely this internal challenge. | Explain the hypothesis, opportunity cost and evidence available; do not treat retrospective success as guaranteed payback. |
| We already have revenue, so the product must be working. | His [May 2025 post](../raw/sources/2026-09-08-linkedin-rb2b/posts/2025-05-31-7334641032967454724.md) describes plateau and churn after initial traction. | Separate initial interest from recurring customer value and retention. |
| I need more reach before I can attract buyers. | His [March 2024 audience strategy](../raw/sources/2026-09-08-linkedin-rb2b/posts/2024-03-28-7179219737141231617.md) explicitly prioritizes reader fit and trust. | Define who the piece should matter to, then evaluate relevant responses and downstream behavior. |

These objection wordings are proposed paraphrases, not quotations from customers. Use a new author's actual incident or approved source in public copy; do not fabricate dialogue to make an objection feel real.
