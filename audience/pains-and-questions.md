# Reader pains and questions

## Summary

Use this map to turn the ideal follower into a concrete editorial brief. Pain descriptions reflect Adam's public accounts; the questions and useful outputs are our proposed teaching angles. They are not verbatim customer-research findings or approved post copy.

| Reader situation | Proposed question | What a useful piece supplies | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Prospects do not understand the product | How can I explain what we do in one sentence that starts a useful conversation? | A before/after explanation and the customer context that makes it clearer. | January 7, 2025 [S1] |
| The target is “small businesses” | Which specific problem and buyer should we learn about first? | A narrower use case and a discovery hypothesis to test. | [S1] |
| The product sounds interchangeable | Why would someone switch from what they already use? | A concrete difference tied to the buyer's job, with evidence required to support it. | [S1]; May 31, 2025 [S2] |
| The founder wants an ads or outbound shortcut | Are we trying to scale before we know why anyone buys? | A stage check and a manual learning task before channel expansion. | [S1] |
| Initial revenue has stopped growing | Is the constraint our customer, message, economics or product? | A diagnostic sequence, distinguishing an observation from a possible cause. | [S2] |
| Signups obscure churn | Does our value last, or is it bought for a temporary need? | Acquisition and retention considered separately, including category/seasonality limitations. | February 6, 2024 [S3] |
| Public posting feels like an endless obligation | How can I start without turning the company into a content production project? | A realistic account of founder involvement, effort and opportunity cost. | April 7, 2023 [S4] |
| The CEO role feels isolating | Who helps me make decisions without placing the whole burden on my family? | Adam's dated account of coaching and peer support, without diagnosing the reader or claiming health outcomes. | April 6, 2024 [S5] |

## Desired change

A reader should leave with a sharper decision, a more specific hypothesis, or a useful question for a customer. Emotional recognition matters too: Adam's recurring admission that he remains stuck gives the teaching a peer's perspective. Content should not imply that confidence, harder work or one tool guarantees the next milestone.

## Choosing an angle

Start with one row, one reader stage and one source-backed incident. The beginner's messaging problem and the established founder's churn problem can share a pillar while requiring different advice. Do not stack all eight pains into a single post. [[strategy/editorial-direction]] connects these questions to the four existing pillars and funnel jobs.
